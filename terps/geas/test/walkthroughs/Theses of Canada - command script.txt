Theses of Canada by Linus (QDK Lite 4.03, ASL 400)
- raw command script for geas_walkthrough_runner.
Game system: Quest 4.  Marker: "historians forgot about these folks".

NOT A WIN -- the game has no ending.  All 90 lines of it are five rooms, one
readable Textbook and three professors (Creighton, Innes, Turner) standing in
the rooms named after their theses of Canadian history.  There is no score, no
"playerwin", no flag and no object to carry anywhere; the Room of the Forgotten
People, reachable from any of the three thesis rooms, is as far as the game
goes.  So the marker is that room's description, the furthest reachable state,
and the script's job is to prove every room and every implemented verb still
works rather than to finish anything.

Worth knowing while reading the transcript:

  * The Classroom lists its three neighbours twice -- once as compass exits and
    once as "You can go to LR Laurentian Room, SR Staples Room, or FR Frontier
    Room."  That is not a decompiler artefact: for ASL below 410 Quest reads
    "place <LR; Laurentian Room>" as prefix "LR" plus destination "Laurentian
    Room" and prints the prefix ahead of the bolded room name (see the place
    list built in V4Game.Part2.cs:7790-7818).  The author evidently meant the
    prefixes as clickable abbreviations.
  * Professor Donald Creighton has no "look" property, so LOOK AT him gives the
    defaultexamine message.  Turner and Innes have one.
  * The game block defines "verb <open>" and "verb <read>" as global fallbacks,
    which is why READ TEXTBOOK reaches the object's own read property instead of
    "I don't understand your command".
