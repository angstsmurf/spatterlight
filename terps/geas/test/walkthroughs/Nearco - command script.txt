Nearco -- Jhames, QDK Lite 4.1.5, asl-version 410.  In Spanish.

Nearco cannot be finished, and the reason is an engine rule rather than a
missing room: the three doors that lead to the rest of the game are opened
with a statement that, from ASL 4.10 on, cannot open them.  This script
therefore plays the game out to its furthest reachable state and stops there.
Everything up to that point works, and there is a lot of it -- a desert, three
storeys of cave maze, an archer who chases you, two fights, a palace, and all
three of the stone cylinders that are supposed to do the opening.

THE WALL.  Each of the three coin rooms sits behind a blocked doorway declared
in the room block like this (PBE2, and the same line in PPE6 east and PSG2
west):

    north msg <Parece como si en esa direccion hubiese una puerta.  Pero no
               consigues ver la forma de abrir la losa que bloquea el paso
               hacia la otra estancia.>

and each is meant to be opened by pushing a stone cylinder in the palace,
three levels up, whose selection script runs

    create exit north <PBE2; PBF2>

From ASL 4.10 on that cannot work.  A direction's exit is a long-lived object
and re-creating it reuses the old object rather than building a new one --
RoomExits.SetDirection says why in as many words, and RemoveExit repeats it
(RoomExits.cs:20-33, 575-591):

    Don't remove directional exits, as if they're recreated a new object will
    be created which will have the same name as the old one.  This is because
    we can't delete objects yet...

AddExitFromCreateScript rewrites the statement into the tag `north <PBF2>`
(RoomExits.cs:199-256), AddExitFromTag finds nothing after the ">" and so calls
SetToRoom and never SetScript (RoomExits.cs:161-192), and Go tests for a script
before it looks at the destination (RoomExit.cs:240-259).  The declared refusal
message is that script, it is still there, and it still runs.  The new
destination is dead weight.  Below 4.10 the same statement would have worked:
there are no exit objects, ExecuteCreateExit assigns r.North.Data and stamps
r.North.Type back to Text (V4Game.cs:5429-5450), wiping the script -- which is
how "Bear Campsite" (ASL 400) opens its way past the grizzly.  Jhames wanted
that behaviour out of a 4.10 game and could not have it.

geas is faithful here, and it has to be: Tim Hamilton's "The Maze" (also ASL
410) holds a 15x20 hedge maze in a single room whose four compass exits are
scripts that adjust %xpos%/%ypos%, and it destroys and re-creates those exits
on every move -- passing "<Maze; Maze>" -- purely so the walls show up in
#quest.doorways#.  If the create replaced the script, that game would become
one room you can never leave.  The two games want opposite things from the same
statement, and Quest 4.1 answers the way that suits The Maze.  Pinned by
fixtures/createexitscript410.asl and its 400 twin.

So: the cylinders are pushed, the message says "Aparentemente has abierto algo
en algun sitio", the wav plays, and the losa still will not budge.  The marker
is the resurrected Persian's farewell speech in PBE2, the last thing the game
has to say to a player who has done everything that can be done.  It is worth
reading: he shakes your hand, says he has enjoyed working with you, has to go
because it is his wedding anniversary, and asks you to put in a word with the
CAAD so he gets nominated for best supporting actor for his death scene in the
cave -- treading on his own big toe, he feels, lent it credibility.

WHAT THE ROUTE DOES.  Nearco is a soldier of Alexander the Great, sent to find
the treasure of Darius III Codomannus.  He starts in the desert with a helmet,
a sword and a handkerchief.

  * The desert is a four-step tool chain.  USE HUESO ON PANUELO wraps the bone
    in the cloth and gives an unlit torch; USE PIEDRA ON ROCA strikes a prism
    out of the rock; USE CASCO ON PRISMA fits the prism into the helmet; and
    USE ANTORCHA ON CASCO CON PRISMA focuses the sun through it and lights the
    torch.  Desierto's north exit is `if got <Antorcha_encendida> then goto
    <Cueva_con_Luz> else goto <Cueva>`, so without the torch the cave is dark
    and there is nothing to do in it.
  * The bottom storey (PB) is a 6x6 grid of one-way-ish corridors, walked in 27
    moves to PBE2, where a Persian archer starts shooting.  The room script
    flags persatesigue, marches you through PBE4 to PBD4, dumps the archer
    behind you, and hands you his bow and four arrows.
  * The middle storey (PP) ends at PPE6, where two Persians are playing dice.
    One USE ARCO ON SOLDADO PERSA spends two arrows, kills both, and runs
    `create exit west <PPE6; PPE5>` -- a direction the room block never
    declared, so that one really does open, and it is the only way on.
  * The top storey (PS) is drawn on the diagonals: almost every exit is ne, nw,
    se or sw.  Persa4 in PSD4 dies to USE ESPADA and opens southwest to PSC3,
    again an undeclared direction.  The bracelet in PSC3 is loot, nothing more.
  * The palace above is two long ranks of corridors.  Three rooms (A19, B19,
    C19) hold a stone cylinder sticking out of the wall; EXAMINE CILINDRO DE
    PIEDRA runs the object's `action <examine>`, which `choose`s the selection
    that pushes it.  Answer Si.  Pushing the first one also swaps the dead
    archer in PBE2 for the resurrected one who wants his award.
  * Then back down, and the two losa doors within reach are tried once each --
    PSG2 west and PBE2 north -- so the transcript records both refusals.  The
    third, PPE6 east, is the same statement.

WHAT IS BEHIND THEM, for the record.  Each coin room holds one coin, and each
of PBD4, PPD4 and PSC3 holds a slot (ranura) on a pedestal "like a Persian
Coca-Cola machine".  The coins go in in any order; the third one to be
inserted makes a chest appear in that room.  The chest yields a parchment whose
writing has faded, and PSE6's fireplace has soot in it: USE PERGAMINO ON
HOLLIN dusts the soot over the vellum, it catches in the grooves the scribe's
goose quill pressed into the skin, and the text comes back.  EXAMINE PERGAMINO
then hides the pushed cylinder in B19 and reveals a "ranura circular" in its
place; examining that offers the last selection, and Si drops you down a stone
chute to EndCapitulo, `playerwin`, and the closing text -- your next step is to
meet the god RAR, who will ask you a question so you can go on playing the
adventures of NEARCO.  The goose feather lying in PPD1 is a red herring, but a
fair one: it is a hint at the quill.

QUIRKS WORTH KNOWING.

  * PANUELO is spelled with an n-tilde, and geas passes story-file bytes
    through untouched rather than transcoding them, so the command that uses it
    has to carry the same raw CP1252 byte (0xF1) the .cas does.  Line 5 of the
    route below does; a UTF-8 n-tilde would not match.
  * `define options abbreviations on` is set, so n/s/e/w/ne/nw/se/sw/up/down
    all work, which is what makes a 250-turn maze crawl bearable.
  * USE A ON B tries A's `use on <B>` first and only then B's `use <A>`.  USE
    ANTORCHA ON CASCO CON PRISMA lights the torch; the same two objects the
    other way round get a refusal.
  * The game block declares `verb <examine> msg <No puedes examinar eso.>`,
    which is a catch-all that beats an object's `look`.  Objects with their own
    `action <examine>` still answer, which is exactly how the cylinders and the
    revealed parchment are meant to be operated.
  * Selections come up as numbered menus; the answer is not a turn.  Si is
    accepted as well as 1.
  * PSA7 is a one-way trapdoor: walking in drops you through a stone chute to
    PBD5, two storeys down.  It is the only quick way back to the bottom, and
    the route uses it to reach PBE2 at the end.
  * The startscript's `wait` after the intro does not consume a script line.
  * Boton3 is declared with `suffix <un>` where the author meant a prefix, so
    C19 reports "There is cilindro de piedra un here".  Harmless.

Route: desert tool chain -> PB maze and the archer chase -> up to PP, kill the
dice players with the bow -> up to PS, kill Persa4 with the sword, take the
bracelet -> up to the palace, push all three cylinders -> down to PSG2 and try
the losa -> PSA7 trapdoor to PBD5 -> PBE2, try the losa, and hear the Persian
out.

to reach.
