Assassin (Mocha Man and Eager Elmer, QDK 3.12, ASL 311, version "1.0- silent
death", 2002-03) - raw command script for geas_walkthrough_runner.
Game system: Quest 4.  Marker: "the corruption was left to the Mafia".
135 turns, no timers, so no --tick.

An open-map mafia game: 104 rooms laid out as a real street grid (Elm, Main,
Hilton, Print, Grey, the Ave, plus a gravel back road), and four missions handed
out one at a time.  The credits admit it "was based on a game written for a
graphing calculator (sad but true)", which shows in the structure: almost all of
the map is scenery with a joke room description, and the mission chain touches
maybe fifteen rooms of it.  The route below is the shortest path through that
chain with no exploration and no wasted turns.

THE ECONOMY IS NOT A PUZZLE.  The help text advertises four ways to make money
-- burgling the four lockpickable houses and selling the loot at Paul's Pawn
Shop, gambling at Lucky's Casino, street racing the Tattoed Man, and the mission
payouts -- and the walkthrough uses only the last one.  `cash` starts at 20;
joining pays 500, mission 1 pays 1000, mission 2 pays 1000.  That is 2520, and
the only two things you have to buy are the Uzi (2000, leaving 520) and the
silencer (200, bought after mission 3 has paid another 4000).  So Louie's Easy
Lockpick, the four houses, the vase/stereo/camera/laptop, the two cars, the
casino and the race are all optional -- which matters here because `gamble` is
`$rand(1;2)$` and the street race ends in `playerlose` if you turn up on foot.

WHAT GATES WHAT.  Every step is a `got <...>` check on an invisible marker
object in the hidden `Mission Status` room:

  * Vinnie will not appear in the Tavern once you hold the Mafia Member Card;
    the Tavern's entry script only moves him in on the *un*carded branch.  So
    the very first thing that happens in the game has to be SPEAK TO VINNIE.
  * `create exit east <Fake Intro Club; N. Gravel>` is what lets you out of the
    club at all.  The Fake Intro Club has no exits of its own -- decline the
    card and DeStefano teleports you out instead.
  * `More E Elm`'s west exit is `if got <mission 1> then msg <The crowd is too
    big. You'll get caught.>`, so the walk out to the Red Jacket Man is a
    dead-end corridor and the trip back to Vinnie has to retrace east and then
    up the gravel road.  That is the long stretch of `east`s below.
  * `SafeSmart Bank`'s north exit needs mission 3, `W. Hilton 1`'s west exit
    needs mission 4, and `Mayor's Front Garden`'s west exit needs the Keycard.

FIVE WAYS TO DIE, ALL AVOIDED BELOW.

  1. The Police Woman.  Shooting the Red Jacket Man moves her to S. Gravel, and
     S. Gravel's entry script fires her interrogation the moment you walk back
     through it.  Answer 1 ("I, uh... didn't hear anything, officer.");
     answer 2 is "RUN!!" and she shoots you in the back.
  2. The mission-2 policeman, likewise parked in Ave Parking by the explosion
     and likewise fired from the room script.  Here it is the *polite* answer
     that kills you -- "No, but whatever it was, it wasn't me." leads to "can I
     see some ID, sir?" and BUSTED.  Answer 2, "Shoot the cop".
  3. The Angry Guard.  Entering the vault swaps the Tied up Guard for an Angry
     Guard back in the Security Room, and the Security Room's entry script
     execs SPEAK TO ANGRY GUARD for you on the way out.  Surviving needs
     `got <Bulletproof Vest> and got <have uzi>` -- both, not either.
  4. The upstairs hallway.  `W. Hilton 1`'s west exit sets `security; active`
     as it drops you in the garden, and the hallway's entry script is
     `if ( #security# = active ) then ... playerlose`.  You must FLIP SWITCH
     downstairs first.  (Also: never SPEAK TO the Mayor Guard standing in that
     hallway.  He is described as "facing the other way, unaware of your
     presence"; talking to him sounds the alarm.  Walking past him is fine, and
     USE PISTOL ON GUARD would work too -- it is just two wasted turns.)
  5. Shooting any mafia member -- either Vinnie, either Delsa, any of the four
     DeStefanos, either Louie, or the Mafia Salesman -- is an instant loss, with
     a hand-written death message per pair.  There are twelve such handlers on
     the Pistol alone.

THE PASSWORD IS TIFFANY.  You learn it in the duck-pond cutscene: the woman who
killed Louie has "a small piece of paper that says 'TIFFANY'" on her.  The
mansion's own `hint` command points back at it ("do you remember what the slip
of paper said on Louie's Killer?").  FLIP SWITCH uses `enter <password>`, so the
answer is its own script line and costs no turn.

THE VAULT LEVERS.  `#lev1#`, `#lev2#` and `#lev3#` are never declared as
variables; the only thing that ever initialises them is SafeSmart Bank's entry
script, and only on the `if got <mission 3>` branch -- it sets all three to
"up".  The vault door wants up/down/down, so exactly two flips are needed.  Two
notes on typing them: a lever is flipped by TAKEing it (the room description
says so, and the room's own `command <mission>` repeats it), and all three carry
an ordinal alias -- `<Lever 1>` / `alias <1st Lever>`, `<Lever 2>` /
`alias <2nd Lever>`, `<Lever 3>` / `alias <3rd Lever>` (assassin.asl:2426-2452).
The alias is the only name the parser knows, and the game is ASL 311, so there is
no partial matching to fall back on either (Quest matches a word from the middle
of a name only for ASL 391 and up: Disambiguate, V4Game.cs:4762-4772;
fixtures/abbrev.asl).  TAKE 2ND LEVER works; TAKE LEVER 2 does not.  The same
goes for the target of the first shot: he is `define object <Red Jacket Man>` with
`alias <Red Jacket>` (936-937), so the command is USE PISTOL ON RED JACKET.

TWO SEARCHES.  The Molitov Cocktails and the Bulletproof Vest are both behind a
room-level `command <search ...>` rather than any property of the object you are
searching, and in both cases the object's own `look` denies having anything:
the Lost and Found "smells like gasoline... there probably isn't anything you
need", and the Coat Rack has "a few jackets... nothing you need most likely".
The bomb is in "EDUCATIONAL territory" per the mission envelope -- the
Elementary/Middle School's lost and found, not the High School.  The vest is
hinted twice: by Liz in the Loomis Offices (menu option 3) and by Zeke's last
menu line, "Mah brother Paul said somethin' once about hiding a bulletproof
vest in a coat rack somewheres".

AN AUTHORING BUG WORTH KNOWING, because it explains why the route never picks a
fight with a cop.  Buying a gun does `clone <Uzi; Buy Uzi; Guns>` + `give <Buy
Uzi>`, so the thing in your inventory is called "Buy Uzi", not "Uzi".  The Uzi
purchase separately hands you the invisible marker `have uzi`, and the Angry
Guard checks *that* -- which is the only reason a bought Uzi works at all.  The
`police` selection, which is what you get if you shoot the Snobby Lady and then
meet one of the two cloned Angry Cops, instead checks `got <Uzi>` and `got
<Shotgun>` directly.  A purchased weapon therefore never satisfies it and the
"fight" branch always ends in "Arrested!!".  Nothing in the main line goes near
that selection.

The Spy Facility takes `use molitov cocktails` with no target: it is a
room-level `use <Molitov Cocktails>`, which is also what the room's `hint` tells
you ("try USEing it on its own").  DeStefano at Main and Ave and the Bad Cop at
the club are both spoken to for you by the room's entry script, so neither gets
a line here.
