TARDIS Escape v.002 by Jim R. Harbis (QDK Lite 4.1.1, ASL 410)
- raw command script for geas_walkthrough_runner.
Game system: Quest 4.  Win marker: "Thank you".

Six rooms, one linear chain of unlocks.  The Doctor has locked you in his TARDIS;
GIVE TRENCHCOAT TO THE DOCTOR outdoors calls playerwin.  DIAL PHONE at the TARDIS
Door forfeits (playerlose) and is the only other ending.

The chain, all of it in the Console Room to start with:

  * LOOK AT COAT RACK runs "show <trenchcoat2>" -- the coat is hidden by the
    startscript, so nothing else reveals it.
  * The Sonic Screwdriver is nested two containers deep: pocket inside
    trenchcoat, screwdriver (and the first half of the note) inside pocket.
  * USE SONIC SCREWDRIVER ON WARDROBE STAIRCASE unlocks up, USE ... ON ZERO
    CHAMBER DOOR unlocks the Wardrobe's west, and the Zero Chamber holds the
    crowbar.
  * USE CROWBAR ON FLOOR GRATING unlocks down to the Maintainence Area and the
    TARDIS Key, and USE TARDIS KEY ON DOOR at the TARDIS Door unlocks the way out.

Worth knowing while reading the transcript:

  * LOOK AT TRENCHCOAT and LOOK AT POCKET are load-bearing, not flavour.  Quest
    only lets you reach a container's contents once the container has been seen:
    contents are available if the parent is a surface, or is (open or transparent)
    AND seen (V4Game.Part2.cs:7694), and looking at it is what sets "seen"
    (V4Game.cs:2141-2146).  Both are declared "opened", so no OPEN is needed --
    but without the two LOOK ATs, TAKE SONIC SCREWDRIVER answers "Sorry... you
    never acquired that..." and the game looks unfinishable.  LOOK AT CLOSET
    upstairs is there for the same reason, ahead of OPEN CLOSET.
  * The game renames most of Quest's stock refusals ("Stop being selfish!",
    "Look in your inventory, you greedy pig!"), so an error message here rarely
    looks like an error message.
  * The Console Room's exit is written "west <TARDIS Door; This door is
    deadbolted...>" without the "locked" keyword.  From ASL 4.10 on that second
    field is a lock message on an *unlocked* exit, so it is never printed and
    WEST simply works; before 4.10 the same line would have walked you into a
    room named after the message.  geas used to do the latter for every game.
