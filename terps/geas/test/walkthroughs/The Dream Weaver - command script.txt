The Dream Weaver -- Bad Omen, 2001, ASL 217 (QDK 1.02)
======================================================

A stoner dream-quest in seventeen rooms, and one of the more sincerely strange
things in the Quest 4 corpus.  A wizard has told you that the only way to stop a
recurring dream is to kill the Dream Weaver inside it, "he didn`t say how to do
it though" -- and indeed the game never finds out either.  Every puzzle in it is
solved by packing a hooka with a Good Nugget and smoking it.

The game cannot be won.  There is no `playerwin` anywhere in the file; the only
`playerlose` is in an unreachable procedure; and the last room, Mystic Ruins,
describes paths leading in seven directions and declares none of them.  So this
is a furthest-reachable-state script.  It ends on the Blue Flamed Torch, the last
object the game will ever give you, and the marker is its take message: "you
could almost swear you say a skull in the flame".

The engine fix this game needed
-------------------------------
`take hooka` runs

    define procedure <~Internal Procedure 2>
     msg <You take the hooka and put it in your coat pocket>
     give <Hooka>
     hideobject <Hooka>
    end define

which is the standard Quest 2.x idiom: hand the player the *item* and hide the
like-named scenery *object* it was standing in for.  Below ASL 2.80 those are two
unrelated tables.  PlayerItem only sets `Got` on the item and never touches an
object (V4Game.Part2.cs:6658-6669); SetAvailability only clears `Exists` on the
object and never touches an item.  From 2.80 on they are the same thing: give
moves the object to "inventory" and lose moves it back to the current room
(ibid. 6604-6647), and `if got` becomes `(ContainerRoom == "inventory") & Exists`
(V4Game.cs:7370-7378).

geas moved the object at every ASL version, so `hideobject <Hooka>` looked like a
held object being destroyed and took the item with it: the hooka vanished on the
turn you picked it up and the game was unwinnable from its second puzzle on.
Both halves are now version-gated -- give/lose only touch an object from 2.80 up,
and `got` at 2.80 and above additionally requires the object not be hidden, so
the two answers agree with the inventory listing.  test/fixtures/itemhide217.asl
and itemhide280.asl are the same game at the two versions and pin both.

Nothing else in the file depends on the split, but everything does: the Hooka,
Chest, Key, Glowing Mushroom and Small Torch are all taken by that same
give-then-hide pair.

The Good Nugget economy
-----------------------
`items <Good Nugget; Hooka; Lighter; Chest; Key; Locket; Glowing Mushroom; Good
Brazilian Kind; Blue Flamed Torch>`, and you start with the Lighter.  The nugget
is the game's currency and there are three places that grow one:

    Concack Beach (north)         `command <take pot plant>` -> proc 19, refillable
    Sleeping Forest (North West)  `command <take pot plant>` -> `give <Good
                                  Brazilian Kind>`, a *different* item, with no
                                  message at all -- it just silently appears
    Concack Beach (north)         procs 0 and 10 are byte-identical copies of 19
                                  that nothing calls

Three things spend a nugget, and each is a puzzle:

    Small Hut, `use hooka`        proc 4 -> proc 7: reveals the Sign and the Key
    The end of the world          proc 8: the only way out of the trap room
    Large Hut, `use <hooka>`      proc 21: `lose <good nugget>`

Only the first two are reachable, because "use hooka" in the Large Hut matches
the room's `command <Use hooka>` first -- ExecCommand tries user commands at
V4Game.Part2.cs:4208 and does not reach the built-in `use ` handling at 4428 --
so proc 20 runs and proc 21 never does.  That is a mercy: proc 21 prints nothing
and would silently eat a nugget.  (It would not actually eat one in Quest
either.  It says `lose <good nugget>` and the declared item is "Good Nugget";
below ASL 2.80 the item lookup is case-sensitive, so Quest logs an error and
nothing happens.  geas matches case-insensitively here, a divergence left alone
because geas does not track the declared item list at all.)

The Small Hut sequence is the game's one real puzzle.  Bring a nugget, take the
hooka, smoke it, and proc 7 does `lose <Good Nugget>` then `showobject <Sign>`
and `showobject <Key>`.  The sign, which was "writing in some forign tounge that
you don`t understand", is now Robbert's letter to Cathrine, drafted into a war
and leaving her "little joey and the crop to make the money off of".  The key
opens the chest in the Sleeping Forest.  It is the only piece of the game where
the plot and the mechanic line up.

Two rooms with no exits
-----------------------
Both are reached by walking in an inviting direction and both are permanent.

    The end of the world      south from Concack Beach (south).  The room
                              description is a full loss narration -- "You have
                              just lost although it doesn`t say so there is
                              nothing you can do but watch as the stars pass you
                              by" -- and it is not a `playerlose`, so the game
                              stays running and the parser keeps taking input.
                              `use <Hooka>` with a nugget is the escape hatch
                              (proc 8, back to Concack Beach (south)); without
                              one you get "You have nothing to smoke in that"
                              forever.
    Sleeping Forest (North East)  northeast from Sleeping Forest (North West).
                              A single paragraph about vines grazing your back,
                              no exits, no objects, no commands, and no way out
                              at all.  The script below does not go there.

The route
---------
    Concack Beach -> N -> (north) -> N -> (north)2 -> N -> Small Hut
    -> S -> swim to island -> Small Island -> N -> Large Hut
    -> S,S,S -> (north) -> S -> Concack Beach -> S -> (south) -> S -> the end of
    the world -> [smoke] -> (south) -> N -> W -> Sleeping Forest
    -> N -> (North) -> follow deer trail -> (North West) -> W -> (North West 2)
    -> Mystic Ruins

Forty-one turns.  Notes on the odd steps:

    SWIM TO ISLAND        Concack Beach (north)2 declares three commands for it
                          ("swim to the isle", "swim to the island", "swim to
                          island") and no exit; the island is "about 700 meters"
                          away from the beach below and "about 10 feet" from
                          here, which the game notices out loud.
    USE HOOKA (Large Hut) proc 20: you share the pipe with Cathrine, "you feel an
                          inner bond", and `showchar <Cathrine>` finally makes
                          her visible -- she was in the room description from the
                          start.  Understanding her does not let you talk to her:
                          she has no `speak`, so SPEAK TO CATHRINE is "She says
                          nothing."
    TAKE POT PLANT (2nd)  the nugget for the trip off the end of the world.
    USE KEY ON CHEST      proc 16, `showobject <Glowing Mushroom>`.  The Sleeping
                          Forest declares `use <Key>` at room level too (proc 18,
                          a byte-identical copy), so plain USE KEY works as well.
                          Do not TAKE CHEST first: proc 12 pockets and hides it,
                          and then there is nothing there to unlock.
    W into (North West 2) the room's `script` fires `if got <Glowing Mushroom>`
                          and the mushroom heats up in your pocket -- the game's
                          one bit of foreshadowing that works.
    USE GLOWING MUSHROOM  proc 22: the cap fits the "inward hemisphere" in the
                          stone carving and the wall opens.  The room also
                          declares `northeast <Mystic Ruins>` outright, so the
                          puzzle is optional and the mushroom is not consumed.

Loose ends
----------
Seven of the twenty-four procedures are unreachable: 0 and 10 (copies of 19), 3
(empty), 5 ("Me casa Su casa" / "Wow you must be higher than you though",
presumably Cathrine's first intelligible words), 9 (`say <Hey how did you get my
bong this was mine and joey`s>` + `lose <hooka>`, clearly meant for the Large
Bong, which has no scripts at all), 11 (following the beach footprints to a
`goto <Marijauna Field>` -- a room that does not exist, where "a woman and a
child" are harvesting, i.e. Cathrine and Joey), and 1, the only `playerlose`:
swimming out and opening your own thigh to bring the sharks.  Nothing calls it,
so the game has no losing ending either.

`define text <win>` and `define text <lose>` are both present and both empty.
The Ancient Scroll on Robbert's tombstone in the Sleeping Forest (North West) has
no `look` and no `take` -- "Nothing out of the ordinary." -- which is the closest
the file comes to admitting it stopped.  The Locket on the beached whale's tusk
and the Good Brazilian Kind are likewise collected and never used; `collectables
<>` is empty, so they are just inventory.
