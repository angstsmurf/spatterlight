Skate ur @SS off -- Wilson Trent, v0.4a, ASL 410 (QDK Lite 4.1.2)
=================================================================

A skateboarding trick simulator with no adventure in it: four rooms, no puzzle,
no score threshold and no `playerwin` or `playerlose` anywhere in the file.  The
whole game is fifteen `command` blocks in `define game` driving four one-second
timers, and the only thing to do is chain tricks for points.  This script is
therefore a demonstration run, not a walkthrough -- the marker is the 720 spin,
the biggest single trick the game can score (+100), not a win.

How it plays
------------
    OLLIE                   status -> in the air, air time 5, "Come down tick" on
    LEFT / RIGHT            +/-90 degrees while airborne; 180/360/540/720 score
                            10/25/50/100
    KICKFLIP, IMPOSSIBLE    +20 / +50 while airborne; on the ground you fall
    GRIND RAIL              only in the air (the rail is in Training 1)
    SKATE UP QUARTER PIPE   the other way into the air
    MANUAL, then UP / DOWN  alternate them to keep a manual alive, +10 a tick
    LAND                    ends a manual or an air trick cleanly
    REVERT                  a flat +10, any time, any status
    RESET SCORE             score to 0, and `inc <rank; 20>` (see below)
    TUTORIAL                prints `define text <intro>` again
    CHANGE / STOP / RESUME SONG    three `playwav` tracks and a `define menu`

The timers all re-arm themselves from inside their own action:

    define timer <Come down tick>
        interval <1>
        action { dec <air time left>  ...  timeron <Come down tick>  ... }

and Quest's `timeron` sets BypassThisTurn, so a timer never fires on the turn it
was switched on.  Re-arming every tick therefore halves the clock: an air time of
5 lasts about ten turns, which is why eight RIGHTs fit inside one OLLIE.  The
countdown messages in the transcript come out on alternate turns for the same
reason.

LAND during a grind only half-works.  The Grind action leaves `status` alone (it
was already "in the air") and starts `grind tick` beside the air timer; LAND
looks at `status`, switches off "Come down tick" and reports the score, but never
touches `grind tick` -- so the grind keeps paying out +10 a tick while you skate
around, which is visible in the transcript as "4s left to grind" arriving after
the landing.

The spin scoring, and the engine fix it needed
----------------------------------------------
    command <right> {
        if ( #status# = in the air ) then {
            inc <degrees turned; 90>
            select case <%degrees turned%> {
                case <180> { msg <180 spin.  +10pts.>  inc <score; 10>}
                ...

`degrees turned` is never declared -- there is no `define variable` for it, only
the `set numeric <degrees turned; 0>` the landing timer does.  Quest handles that:
ExecuteIncDec reads the variable with GetNumericContents, gets the -32767
"no such variable" answer, clamps anything at or below -32766 to zero, and stores
0 + 90.  geas was missing the clamp, so the first RIGHT left -32677 and none of
the four `case` labels could ever match -- every spin in the game scored nothing.
Fixed, with test/fixtures/undefnum.asl to pin it.  The same clamp is why RESET
SCORE's `inc <rank; 20>` now creates `rank` at 20; nothing in the game displays
it, so that one is invisible either way.

Half Pipe Central
-----------------
The Skate Lobby declares

    place locked <Half Pipe Central; You have yet to unlock this Skate Zone.>

which QDK Lite emitted and Quest has no idea what to do with.  Quest matches the
line on the `place ` prefix alone, reads the parameter, splits it on the
semicolon and takes the *second* half as the room name and the first as a
prefix -- so a real Quest player sees "You can go to Skate Shop, Half Pipe
Central You have yet to unlock this Skate Zone., or Level 1", and GO TO HALF PIPE
CENTRAL is rejected because the place is now filed under that whole sentence.
geas drops the malformed place instead and answers "You can't go there.".  The
room is unreachable either way -- which is just as well, since its nine objects
(two half pipes, three rails, stairs, two ramps and a side walk) have no
descriptions and no actions at all.

The shop that cannot sell anything
----------------------------------
`define variable <Credits>` starts at 0 and nothing in the game ever incs it, so
the clerk's "If you have Credits you can buy boards and unlock more areas to
skate in" is the whole of the economy.  Six of the eleven decks (Mana Palation,
Happy Trails, Sonata Burst, Element, Sonata Classic and Amplitude Greatness) are
`invisible` with nothing anywhere to show them.  Of the five you can see, four
have a bare `take` and the Darkstar Deck has none -- but its `action <equip>`
still fires, so EQUIP DARKSTAR DECK works on a board you do not own.  Equipping
only sets the `Skateboard deck` status variable; no deck changes anything.

The Default Deck is `drop nowhere <YOU CANNOT DROP YOUR SKATEBOARD!>` and is
handed over by the startscript along with the Music Player, which is the one
object in the Skate Lobby.

The run below
-------------
Tutorial and music, a manual chain, a REVERT, a deliberate KICKFLIP on the ground
(the -100pt fall, whose three recovery ticks are spent shopping), the shop and
the clerk, the locked place, the Training Area's quarter pipe and rail, then RESET
SCORE so the finale stands alone: OLLIE, eight RIGHTs for the 720, LAND at
225pts.
