World's End! (worldsend/world's end.asl, v1.0, by GAMER!, QDK Pro 4.03,
ASL 400) - walkthrough.
Win marker: "slumps to the ground dead", the opening of the game's `define
text <win>`; the run ends on its "(to be continued)".

The first input is the "tester" that answers the startscript's `enter <name>`
prompt, not a command.

This is the only script in the suite that needs all three runner extras, so
run_walkthroughs.sh passes them (and GEAS_SEED is fixed, but the seed does not
decide the outcome -- --save-scum does):

  * --tick, for the dynamite.  `define timer <dynamite>` has interval <5> and
    starts disabled; lighting the stick planted by the spaceship turns it on,
    and the explosion hides the spaceship, which is what lets "examine crop
    circle" in the fields reveal the sleepville security chip the game needs
    later.  Without ticks the fuse never burns down.
  * two --fight specs, for the two fights the game decides with $rand(1; 2)$:
      - the hovering cube in the sahara desert.  Each vial does 5 of its 10
        health on a coin flip, so both have to land, while `define timer <cube>`
        (interval <1>) takes 10 health off you every other turn.  A missed
        vial1 is caught and can be thrown again; a missed vial2 prints nothing
        at all.
      - the alien warlord, 10 damage each way on the same coin flip.
  * --save-scum, which is what actually wins those two: a losing streak of
    flips can kill you outright, so the runner restores and throws again.

Start: hometown (the room prints its description, not a name).
