The Awakening Dead 2 (QDK 4.1.5, ASL 410)
- raw command script for geas_walkthrough_runner.
Game system: Quest 4.  Win marker: "hand Big Joe the can".

Nine rooms around a crossroads, one puzzle chain, one ending: GIVE CAN OF BAKED
BEANS TO BIG JOE in the Boarded Up House calls playerwin.

The chain is three "use X on Y" scripts, each of which moves the next link out of
the hidden holding room:

  * USE KNIFE ON LOOTER on Vesterlit Avenue (the knife starts in your car, and
    has to be taken -- it is not in your inventory) kills him and drops the
    First Aid Kit there.
  * USE FIRST AID KIT ON WOUNDED POLICE OFFICER, same room, trades it for his
    Pistol, which the script also gives you directly.
  * USE PISTOL ON ZOMBIE at the Gas Station leaves the Can Of Baked Beans the
    zombie was standing over.

Route: OUT of the car to the Street, NORTH to the Crossroads, then EAST for the
avenue and WEST-SOUTH for the gas station, and finally NORTH-WEST from the
crossroads for Big Joe's house.

Worth knowing while reading the transcript:

  * Nothing here is a container and nothing is hidden behind a LOOK AT, so the
    only sequencing rule is that each "use" needs its object in your inventory:
    TAKE FIRST AID KIT before using it, and the pistol arrives already held.
  * SPEAK TO BIG JOE and SPEAK TO THE POLICE OFFICER are what tell you what they
    want.  They are pure flavour for the runner, so the script skips them.
  * The Mattress's "action <sleep on>" is empty, and the game's own "verb <sleep
    on>" answers "You can't sleep on that." -- resting in the house is scenery,
    not an ending.
