Where's annabel? (QDK 3.53 unregistered, ASL 350) - raw command script for
geas_walkthrough_runner.
Game system: Quest 4.  Win marker: "YOU HAVE FOUND ANNABEL".

Run with --tick.  The game has two timers and both of them are death clocks, so
ticking makes it harder, not easier; the script defuses each one on the very next
turn.  219 commands, no RNG, wins in 209 turns.

A private detective is hired by Griswold to find his fiancee Annabel, last seen
in the village of Insania.  Twenty-one rooms: a garden with a well, the village
square, an inn, the Warden's office, a herb garden, two market stalls, a building
site, an amphitheatre with a prop shed, four forest quarters, a theatre and Chad
the Bad's secret hideout.

THE SPINE.  Everything funnels into one lock: `use <Small silver key>` on the
Large Chest in the Prop Shed (annabel.asl:1194) is the win.  The key falls out of
Chad's pocket, Chad is only reachable with the Secret Map, and Baggie the barmaid
only hands the map over when you are carrying cheese, an onion, a potato and salt
all at once (Baggie2's `speak`, 423-431).  Those four are the four separate quest
chains:

  * POTATO.  Cut the well's mesh (USE WIRE CUTTERS ON WELL), which leaves a piece
    of wire behind; the wire cutters are in the Warden's tool box.  Buy the
    turnip, mask it, let Baggie turn it into a Bobo Head, plant the broom's pole
    in the mud and top it with the head -- the scarecrow distracts short-sighted
    Jumbo.  Separately, USE TROWEL ON MUDDY PATCH digs up a muddy nugget, and
    washing it in the well makes it a potato, not the gold you were hoping for.
  * CHEESE.  Sprinkle the itching powder (from Bobo's joke stall) on Bob the
    builder's Big Mac, then USE FULL WATERING CAN ON BOB: he thinks it is
    raining, puts the coat on and runs off scratching, leaving his lunch box.
    TAKE CHEESE SANDWICH then EXAMINE CHEESE SANDWICH splits it into cheese and
    white bread -- and brings Bob straight back, so the order matters.
  * SALT.  Robin Hatz is superstitious: three pieces of bad luck (the skull from
    the stocks, the tarot card, and a stuffed cat fired at the closet door) make
    him throw salt over his shoulder and leave the shaker behind (`luckout`,
    2080-2087).  The same procedure also does `show <Usher>`, so the drama
    group's ticket seller does not exist until Robin has fled.
  * ONION.  Buy the ticket for 5 gold, which `create exit`s the Theatre both
    ways, and give Anna the blue topped mushroom from the split tree stump.

Along the way the Warden wants a sandwich (a mud-dipped white loaf as
"wholemeal", a headless rubber chicken dipped in the same mud as the "roast
rabbit", swapped for the real rabbit while Jumbo is talking to the scarecrow,
plus mint), which pays out the wanted poster, which is what tells Baggie who Chad
is.  Basil and Rosemary pay 3 gold three times over (ring, luggage label, grey
flowers) and are the game's only real income.

Gold, in order: 2 from Griswold, -1 licence fee, +3 ring, -1 turnip, -2 rubber
chicken, +3 luggage label, +3 grey flowers, -5 ticket, leaving 2.  Note the
licence fee is `if is <%Gold%;2> then dec <Gold; 1>` (495) -- an exact-value
compare, so it is only ever charged when you happen to hold exactly two coins.
Paying it first, as the script does, is the expensive way to do it.

THE TWO TIMERS
  * `thief`, interval 15 (2108).  TAKE TOMATO arms it and it ends the game in the
    stocks.  Only the Barrel of Tomatoes turns it off, either by putting the
    tomato back or by USE RED NOSE ON BARREL OF TOMATOES, which is what the
    script does on the following turn.
  * `chad`, interval 13 (2118).  Agreeing to tell Chad why you have come starts
    it; he shoots you when it fires.  USE BRUSH ON CHAD (the broom's brush, sold
    to him as a hedgehog) is the only `timeroff`, again on the next turn.

WHY EVERY NOUN IS SPELLED OUT IN FULL

The game is ASL 350, and it gives nothing an `alt` list -- not one object in
2100 lines.  Quest only matches a word taken from the middle of a name for ASL
391 and up (Disambiguate, V4Game.cs:4762-4772; see fixtures/abbrev.asl), so the
only string that reaches an object here is its alias, in full.  That is why the
script says LOOK TOOL BOX, TAKE TRADING LICENCE, USE RED NOSE ON BARREL OF
TOMATOES and GIVE BLUE TOPPED MUSHROOM TO ANNA rather than the shorter forms.

The aliases are doing real work, because this game builds every state change out
of clone objects: `<Bobo1>`/`<Bobo2>`, `<Warden1>`..`<warden4>`, `<Baggie1>`/
`<Baggie2>`, `<Basil and Rosemary>`/`<Basil2>`/`<Basil3>`, `<Bob1>`/`<Bob2>`,
`<Chad>`..`<Chad4>`, `<Well>`/`<Opened Well>`, `<Pole>`/`<Pole2>`,
`<Lunch1>`/`<Lunch2>` -- each pair or family sharing one alias, exactly one of
them visible at a time.  So WELL means the closed well before the wire cutters
and the open one afterwards, and BASIL AND ROSEMARY means whichever of the three
couples is currently on the bench.  geas will also answer to the raw ObjectName
(BOBO1), a divergence Quest does not have, and no line here relies on it.

Three name spellings look like mistakes and only one of them is:

  * `<Meryy Menfolk>` (1503) really is misspelled, and it has no alias, so the
    typo is the only name that works: GIVE BOBO'S CARD TO MERYY MENFOLK.  The
    author spelled it "Merry" correctly in every message around it.
  * `<Gaint turnip>` (877) is misspelled too, but it carries
    `alias <Giant turnip>`, so the alias silently repairs it and the script uses
    the correct spelling.
  * BOW AND ARROW AND STUFFED CAT is not a mouthful for its own sake.  The
    Closet has two separate `use` scripts (1210, 1223): `use <Bow2>`, whose alias
    is the whole phrase, is the one that scares Robin, and `use <Bow and arrow>`
    -- a different object, with the plain name -- just prints a "nice shot" and
    counts for nothing.  Only the long form advances the luck counter.

LOOK AND EXAMINE ARE NOT SYNONYMS HERE

The game's own information screen says so ("Remember to LOOK AT and EXAMINE
objects as they may produce different results"), and it means it: `look` is the
script that reveals hidden objects on the tool box (987), the notice board (1166)
and Bobo's pile of jokes (759), while `examine` is the revealing one on the
broom, the small crate, the thyme, the wheelbarrow, the camp, the tent and the
lunch box.  Hence the mixture of LOOK TOOL BOX and EXAMINE SMALL CRATE.

Two related habits: the Photograph is shown with USE, never GIVE (`give to
anything` refuses it, 127, and the information screen spells this out), and
TAKE BROOM has to come before EXAMINE BROOM -- examining it breaks it into the
pole and the brush, and the `if got <Broom>` branch (1127-1137) only puts the
pieces in your hands if you were holding it.

One flourish the script skips: four different characters accept flowers, and
giving all four colours away summons a fairy and one of the four "secret gems"
(`fairy`, 2097).  The gems are pure score and the win does not need them.

The two leading LOOK commands are free turns -- the intro room's four `wait`
calls take no input, so nothing is eaten and the game starts in the Garden.
