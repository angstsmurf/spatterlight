Red Sauce Monday, aka "A day at the redsauce office" (QDK Lite 4.1.2, ASL 410)
- raw command script for geas_walkthrough_runner.
Game system: Quest 4.  Win marker: "You beat the game".

An in-joke office game with one puzzle: a nine-link chain of fetch-and-give.
Every character wants what the previous one handed you, and the chain has to be
walked in exactly this order:

  fridge -> CAN -> Tom -> BANANA -> Amy -> BANANA SKIN -> Chris -> BANJO
  -> Tim -> MAGAZINE ON TWITTER -> Simon -> CUP OF COFFEE -> Roger
  -> ANIME DVD -> Selena -> M&MS -> Milton -> CHEEKY POSE -> James
  -> RUBBER BAND -> crocodile.

Three gates that are easy to miss:

  * SPEAK TO TEAM is what introduces the cast.  Until then the individual
    characters do not exist, so nothing can be given to anybody.  It also walks
    you to your desk, hence the SOUTH on the next line.
  * LOOK AT FRIDGE is what reveals the CAN.  There is no other way to get one.
  * Opening the front door conceals JAMES, so he is never listed in the room --
    but he is still reachable by name, and USE CHEEKY POSE ON JAMES is the only
    thing that produces the RUBBER BAND.  (The game also defines a verb spelled
    "use cheeky look on james" for this, but it is dead code: "use ... on ..."
    is a built-in command and parses first, giving "You don't see any cheeky
    look.")

The football table is a joke - CHALLENGE CHRIS and CHALLENGE TIM can never be
won, they just bounce you back to the office.  Both are included below anyway,
along with a SPEAK to every character, because those lines are what the game
uses to hint the chain and they are worth exercising: the SPEAK responses were
all being swallowed until the `speak` property lookup was fixed (see
fixtures/speak.asl).
