Shiversword 2 (CJ, QDK Pro 4.02, ASL 400) -- winning command script
===================================================================

WHY THIS GAME IS IN THE SUITE

  It is the corpus's only walkthrough that is *unwinnable without timers*.  Not
  "harder without timers" -- flatly unwinnable.  The `photo` timer is the sole
  writer of `edison = 7`:

      define timer <photo>
        interval <10>
        action { set numeric <edison; 7>  timeroff <photo> }
        disabled

  and `edison = 7` is the only state in which Edison hands over his camera.  No
  camera means no photograph of Simon, which means no fifth song, and
  `comppart1` refuses to start the talent contest while `songsknown <= 4`.  So
  the whole back half of the game -- contract, clippers, wool, dress, disguise,
  seance, Royal Court -- hangs off one `interval <10>` firing.  Run this script
  without --tick and it stalls at "It seems he is too busy photographing the
  lightning to pay any attention to you."

  Two more timers matter, and both are `interval <7>`:

    * `glove` takes the gardening gloves back off your hands seven turns after
      you put them on.  You need them for exactly three turns on the Lightning
      plain (ram the rod in, clip the battery to the rod, pick the battery up),
      so this is pressure rather than a puzzle -- but it does mean you must
      travel to the plain *before* wearing them, not after.
    * `elliottq` is a genuine deadline: once you are sitting in the medium's
      chair in disguise, Elliott asks his question and you have seven turns to
      `read biography` before he gives up and leaves.  The script answers on
      the very next turn.

  Beyond the timers this is a broad, well-behaved exercise of the ASL 4 feature
  set: a World Map hub built entirely out of `create exit` (eight destinations
  appear one at a time), `choose` selections whose choice bodies `do` procedures
  that themselves contain `choose` (Sharon -> comppart1 -> comppart2 -> three
  nested `choose <perform>` calls inside a single turn), `ask` yes/no prompts
  guarding money, `enter` free-text input at the Oracle, and the
  worn-clothing-as-separate-object idiom (`cloak` / `cloak (worn)`,
  `gloves` / `gloves (worn)`, `green wool` / `green wig (worn)`,
  `red wool` / `red wig (worn)`, `dress` / `dress (worn)`) with room `script`
  blocks that silently swap the worn form back when you leave.


THE SHAPE OF THE GAME

  You are Shiversword, a bard.  John Lemon died leaving you a map to the Magic
  Harp.  There is no Magic Harp; there is a Magic Carp, and it has ended up in
  the Queen's palace by way of Elliott the treasure hunter.  Getting Elliott to
  tell you that requires impersonating his medium, which requires winning a
  televised talent contest, which requires knowing five songs.

  Songs are the spine.  `songsknown` starts at 2 (Yesteryear, Mysterious Wench)
  and each new one is written when something inspires you -- but only once the
  Oracle has given you the songbook, which he does when you hand him back the
  blank `book1` he gave you for playing him Mysterious Wench:

      3  look at light        (the shaft of light in the dark passage)
      4  ask Olive about Elliott
      5  use camera on Simon

  `songlistmenu` picks which selection `play lute` shows, so the choice numbers
  shift as you learn songs.  At the point this script plays for Edison the menu
  is `songs3`: 1 Hopelessly Devoted, 2 Ray of Light, 3 Yesteryear,
  4 Mysterious Wench.


THE GOLD LEDGER IS EXACTLY BALANCED

  Eight gold pieces exist in the game and eight are spent.  There is no slack,
  and the order matters because two of the purchases have hard minimums.

      +2  sell the map to the Oracle (you have already used it)
      -1  Simon's entry fee for The Eggs Factor
      -1  the clip holding the opera glasses in the theatre box
      +1  return Elliott's wallet to Olive
      -1  Madam Mayi's reading
      +2  sell Simon's autograph to the Oracle
      +2  bring Al Chemist his five snails
      +1  give Edison his camera back (battery removed)
      -5  Bing's sachet of red hair dye

  The red dye is the crunch: `if is <%gold%;gt=;5>`, and the only way to be
  holding five at once is to have banked every single source above it.  In
  particular the camera must be handed back *before* you shop, which means the
  Emporium door must already be open, which means Cherie must already have
  given you the dress.


THE OPENING: TEMPLE, ORACLE, VILLAGE

  `read map` opens Shurlee Temple.  In the ruins, `look at mausoleum` then
  `push iron gate` opens the way down; the chamber holds an iron `rod`.  Take
  the rod back up to the temple and `use rod on head` to lever the statue's
  head aside -- that punches the light shaft that makes the dark passage
  passable and, incidentally, opens the `hole` that later hides a snail.  The
  rod is *not* consumed here, which is the only reason the game is finishable:
  the same rod becomes the lightning rod two hours later.

  Down in the crypt the dais holds a `cloth` with a coat of arms.  Showing it
  to the Oracle at Fax By The Sea puts Elliott's Bottom on the map; selling him
  the map funds Simon's entry fee.


THE MIDDLE: FOUR FAVOURS, ALL INTERLOCKING

  Olive at Elliott Manor wants the cloth, then the wallet (theatre box, behind
  a cushion, reached with the `box key` swiped off her desk while she is out
  buying a show ticket -- and she only leaves the desk if you give her the
  leaflet after playing her Hopelessly Devoted).

  Edison in the pasture wants, in order: a song about light (`edison` 1 -> 2),
  a scout with the opera glasses (2 -> 3, which puts the Lightning plain on the
  map and moves him there), a lightning rod (3 -> 4, `use rod on burnt patch`
  while wearing the gloves the sheep was standing on), and a charged battery
  (4 -> 5 -> 6, which starts the `photo` timer).  Ten turns later he is
  `edison = 7` and gives you the camera plus the business card whose `read`
  action adds Burningham.

  Al Chemist in Burningham wants a replacement microscope lens -- the opera
  glasses, which is why they must be spent on Edison first -- and pays in a
  mortar and pestle.  Grinding grass in it yields the `green paste` (wig dye)
  and breaks the pestle into the `clay bowl` (fish net).  Then he wants five
  snails, which only exist once `snailhunt` is on:

      examine hole        Shurlee Temple
      examine altar       the ruins
      examine dais        the crypt
      examine valve       the sheep dip
      examine long grass  the pasture

  Madam Mayi wants a gold piece.  Pay it, pick any of the three `askme`
  questions (all three end "She's right!"), and she leaves -- hanging her cloak
  on the chair on her way out.


THE EGGS FACTOR: 25 IS THE ONLY WINNING SCORE

  Bing Harbor scores 24 out of 30 and `comppart2` needs `%xfactorscore% gt 24`.
  The fresh-performance marks are Yesteryear 5, Ray of Light 4,
  Mysterious Wench 7, Hopelessly Devoted 8, You're So Vain 10; repeats are
  scored 2, -1, 2, 1, 3.  Three distinct songs, best three: 10 + 8 + 7 = 25.
  The next-best combination is 10 + 8 + 5 = 23.  So the script's answers -- 5,
  3, 2 -- are not one winning line, they are the *only* winning line.

  All three `choose <perform>` calls happen inside the single turn spent on
  `speak to sharon`, along with the `ask` that starts the contest, so that one
  command consumes four script lines.

  Winning hides Simon, Sharon and the Bouncer, moves Bing to his barber shop,
  opens Burningham's east/west exits, confiscates your pass, and sets
  `olive = 7`.  Note the ordering trap this creates: Simon is gone afterwards,
  so `use camera on Simon` -- the fifth song -- has to happen before you ever
  speak to Sharon.  If you lose, `comppart2` clears the played flags and lets
  you try again, so the contest itself is forgiving; the *setup* is not.


THE ENDGAME: ONE FLEECE, TWO WIGS

  There is exactly one sheep and therefore exactly one fleece, and it has to be
  green first and red second.

      contract -> Bing -> clippers -> shear the sheep -> plain wool
      green paste on plain wool -> green wool  (Madam Mayi's hair)
      camera on the shorn sheep -> poodle photo -> Cherie -> collar and lead
      collar and lead on the sheep -> Cherie's shop, cherie = 4
      speak to Cherie -> party dress, and the Emporium doors unlock

  The fake crystal ball is the other half of the disguise: the contest prize is
  a crystal bowl, `remove battery` splits the camera into an empty camera and a
  charged battery, the battery lights the bulb scavenged from the lighting rig
  under the stage, and the glowing bulb dropped in the bowl is a mystic's orb.
  Cloak + green wig + orb on the table, `sit on chair`, and Elliott walks in.

  Answer his question from the biography and the seance runs: there was never a
  harp, the carp is at the palace, and the ball smashes back into a plain bulb.
  Now the fleece can be recycled -- `turn valve`, `use bleach on tank`, wash the
  green wool clean, dye the plain wool red -- because the barn's entry script
  demands both the dress and red wool before it will let you stay:

      script {
        ...
        if got <dress> and got <red wool> then { ...wear them... }
        else { msg <You decide to retreat...>  goto <Royal Court> }
      }

  Scoop the carp out of the press with the clay bowl, which gets you spotted
  (`followersmad`), then `speak to magic carp` -- the wish that gets you out is
  the one John Lemon promised.  Slip northeast, north, and hand the fish to the
  Queen.


AUTHORING QUIRKS WORTH KNOWING

  * QDK emitted several handlers twice, with the *second* copy carrying the
    extra line that actually matters.  `biography`'s `action <read>` appears
    twice, identical except that the second ends `do <reading>`; the Mystic's
    chair has two `action <sit on>` blocks, only the second reaching
    `do <chairsit>`; the business card has two `action <read>` blocks, only the
    second creating the Burningham exit.  Anything that keeps the *first*
    definition instead of the last makes this game unfinishable at three
    separate points.
  * The barn's exit is written with an empty `then`:
    `northeast { if flag <followersmad> then else { ... goto <Royal Court> } }`
    -- an inverted guard spelled the lazy way.
  * `command <remove battery>` is one command with two independent `if got`
    blocks, so holding both the camera and a glowing bulb would take both
    apart in a single turn.
  * The Chic Boutique, Bing's Barbers and the palace have no `look` at all, so
    entering them prints exits and objects and nothing else.
  * `Theatre` exists only to `goto <auditorium>` from its script -- you never
    see its own description twice.
  * The Oracle's `obj` procedure `enter`s a free-text subject and string-matches
    it; every branch is a pure hint, so the script never uses it.  Asking about
    "snails" is how a player is expected to learn that snails hide in places
    already examined.
  * The Statue in the Royal Court "smells faintly of mashed potato".  There is
    nothing to do with it.
