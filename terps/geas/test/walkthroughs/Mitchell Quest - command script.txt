Mitchell Quest -- command script
================================

Game:      Mitchell Quest, version 1.1  (games/MitchellQuest.cas)
Author:    Robert "Torgo" Sedler
Copyright: (c)2003 Torgoesque Games
Engine:    ASL 311, "Created with QDK 3.12 and a 12 pack of Schlitz"
Marker:    "You wonder if Burger King is still open"
Length:    245 turns (246 script lines -- one of them answers a disambiguation menu)

WHAT THIS GAME IS
-----------------
An MST3K fanfic in Quest form.  You are Mitchell -- the greasy, perpetually
drunk cop from the 1975 movie riffed in the final Joel episode of Mystery
Science Theater 3000 -- and you have come to Deaney's mansion to shoot Deaney.
Roughly thirty-five rooms: the mansion above ground, then a cave system, a
river, a torture chamber and finally Hell, where Deaney turns out to be Satan.
The narrator spends the entire game insulting the player, and the game insults
itself just as often ("this stupid, poorly described river").  Nearly every
noun answers LOOK, EXAMINE and SPEAK TO separately with three different jokes,
so this script examines and talks to a great deal of scenery on purpose.

The `define game` block installs *twenty* `error <...>` overrides -- badcommand,
badthing, badplace, itemunwanted, defaultuse, cantdrop, noitem, badexamine,
badgo, baduse, badgive, badtake, baditem, baddrop, badpronoun, defaultdrop,
defaultexamine, defaultlook, defaultspeak and defaulttake -- which is more of
the parser's error surface than any other game in this suite replaces at once.
That is most of what makes this game worth having as a regression: every
unrecognised or default-handled command in geas has to route through a
game-supplied string rather than the built-in one.

THE CRITICAL PATH
-----------------
Everything is `use <thing>` on `<thing>`; there are no verbs to discover.

  1. Mitchell's Ford LTD (start): TAKE CROWBAR.
  2. Front Entrance: TAKE KEY, TAKE REMOTE- LATTICE, TAKE REMOTE- FRONT GATES.
     USE KEY ON LOCKED DOOR opens the Gun Room to the west.
  3. Gun Room: USE CROWBAR ON FANCY CABINETS.  Mitchell smashes every gun in
     the house to splinters and leaves exactly one box of .38 AMMO intact.
     TAKE .38 AMMO -- this is the win condition, three hundred turns early.
  4. Bathroom: TAKE PENCIL.  The remotes have recessed buttons, so
     USE PENCIL ON REMOTE- LATTICE is what actually opens the Cedar Lattice
     (an exit west from the Upstairs Hallway into Deaney's Bedroom).
  5. Kitchen: USE CROWBAR ON REFRIGERATOR reveals the CAN O SCHLITZ.  Take it.
     It is never consumed, and it is needed three separate times.
  6. Computer Room (east of the Upstairs Hallway): EXAMINE PILES OF JUNK
     yields the RUSTY KEY.
  7. Garage Bay 2: USE RUSTY KEY ON CHEST yields the BFH (Big Effing Hammer).
  8. Basement: USE BFH ON SMALL DOOR opens south into the Crawlspace, and the
     Crawlspace opens south into the Well Room -- the whole underground half of
     the game hangs off that one hammer blow.
  9. Well Room: TAKE GOLD KEY.  Torch Room (west): TAKE COIL OF ROPE,
     TAKE TORCH STUB.
 10. Upstairs Bathroom: USE GOLD KEY ON MEDICINE CHEST yields MATCHES (the
     object is `Medecine chest`, but its alias spells it right).  The
     torch stub is too damp to light, so USE TORCH STUB ON MICROWAVE (Deaney's
     bathroom has a microwave next to the toilet; the Bathroom downstairs
     complains about *not* having one) to get a DRY TORCH STUB, then
     USE MATCHES ON DRY TORCH STUB for the BURNING TORCH.
 11. West River Bank (east of the Well Room): EXAMINE FOOTPRINTS is the only
     thing that creates the exit north, and LOOK AT FOOTPRINTS is not enough.
 12. West River Ledge: USE COIL OF ROPE ON HOOK swings you to the East River
     Ledge.  The same command on the far ledge's own hook swings you back, so
     the rope is the river's only crossing in both directions and gets used
     four times over the course of this script.
 13. East River Bank -> east -> Torture Chamber Entrance -> east -> Stone
     Passage -> east -> Sign Room -> east -> Evil Darkness.  Going SOUTH from
     Evil Darkness while carrying the burning torch drops you into the tunnel
     maze; without it you pass out and wake up back in the Torch Room.
 14. The tunnel maze is four rooms, most of whose exits loop back onto
     themselves.  In: SOUTH, WEST, NORTH, WEST.  Out: EAST four times, then
     NORTH.  SPEAK TO CRAZY OLD BASTARD at the Torture Chamber Entrance while
     carrying the Schlitz gets you the *exit* half of that ("I can't tell you
     how to get through the tunnels, I couldn't do it!") and nothing else.
 15. Teleportation Room: EXAMINE WEIRD MACHINE reveals the FUEL FUNNEL, and
     USE CAN O SCHLITZ ON FUEL FUNNEL teleports you to the Satellite of Love.
 16. Satellite of Love: SPEAK TO CROW makes Crow run off and drop his
     screenplay, EARTH vs SOUP.  Take it.  USE CAN O SCHLITZ ON ANOTHER FUEL
     FUNNEL returns you to the tunnels.
 17. Deaney's Bedroom: GIVE SCREENPLAY TO LINDA EVANS.  She is too stoned to
     notice she has dropped a TINY KEY.  Take it.
 18. Torture Chamber Entrance: NORTH now works.  TAKE HARPOON GUN.
 19. Benton's Room: USE HARPOON GUN ON BENTON THE LOUSY BUTLER, then
     EXAMINE BENTON'S BODY to be handed the AMULET.
 20. Entrance to Hell: NORTH is blocked by an invisible force until you hold
     the Amulet.  In Hell, TAKE MITCHELL'S GUN and USE .38 AMMO ON MITCHELL'S
     GUN to get the LOADED GUN.
 21. Throne Room (east of Hell): USE LOADED GUN ON DEANEY.  `playerwin`.

There is a single `playerwin` in the file and it is that line.

SIX WAYS TO DIE (thirteen `playerlose` statements)
--------------------------------------------------
  * The Street.  USE PENCIL ON REMOTE- FRONT GATES opens the front gates, and
    then going WEST from the Driveway walks you into the road at night and
    under a car.  The whole room is nothing but a `script` that prints and
    loses -- there is no way to enter it and survive.  This script opens the
    gates anyway (it is a joke worth firing) and simply never goes west.
  * JUMP ACROSS / LEAP ACROSS / JUMP ACROSS RIVER / LEAP ACROSS RIVER at
    either river bank.  Both banks define all four phrasings, all eight
    identical, all fatal -- Mitchell sinks like a boat anchor.
  * USE HARPOON GUN ON CROW or USE MITCHELL'S GUN ON CROW.  You miss, you
    puncture the hull of the Satellite of Love, and it implodes.  (USE CROWBAR
    ON CROW is safe and the game congratulates you sarcastically for thinking
    of it: "MY GOD! That is SO clever!!!!!! But it's also pointless.")
  * USE MITCHELL'S GUN ON DEANEY before loading it.  Same text as the win up
    to the trigger pull, then "THE GUN ISN'T LOADED!!!"  Load it in Hell.
  * USE HARPOON GUN ON DEANEY.  You miss.  Deaney does the MANOS hand gesture.

AUTHORING QUIRKS
----------------
The Gun Room defines *three separate objects all named `chalk outline`*, with
no aliases and no distinguishing names, so EXAMINE CHALK OUTLINE raises a
three-way disambiguation menu whose options are identical.  The script answers
`1`.  This is the only prompt in the run, and forgetting it silently eats the
following command as the menu answer (geas parses menu input with atoi, so
"examine fancy cabinets" becomes 0, clamps to 1, and picks the first outline).

`examine bar` in the Living Room is not an examine at all: it gets Mitchell
"Dudley Moore bombed" and `goto`s him to the Bathroom, which is where the
Pencil is.  It is the intended way to find the Pencil, and it is the only
teleport in the mansion.

The Key carries a stray `action <key> create exit west <; Gun Room>` tag -- a
verb literally named "key", with an *empty* from-room in the create-exit --
alongside the locked door's own `use <Key>`.  Only the door's version is
reachable by normal play; the `action` tag looks like a first attempt left in.

The running fly gag is a four-room relay of five separate objects.  A `fly`
buzzes in the Teleportation Room; teleporting to the Satellite of Love hides
it and shows `fly2` in the Computer Room, on the far side of the map ("Hey,
where did that fly go?").  CATCH FLY in the Computer Room hides that one and
shows `fly3` in the Well Room; CATCH FLY there shows `fly4` in the Hallway;
CATCH FLY in the Hallway finally gives you a `dead fly`.  This script chases
it through all four rooms, which is why the route doubles back through the
Crawlspace twice -- it is show/hide/reveal churn across four rooms and the
best exercise of that machinery in the corpus.

`Another Fuel Funnel` on the Satellite of Love has `alias <Fuel Funnel>`, the
same name as the hidden object in the Teleportation Room, and the Weird
Machine's examine is gated on `if exists <Fuel Funnel>`.  The two never share
a room, so the collision is harmless, but it is a real name clash across rooms.
The Weird Machine is the same trick (`Another Weird Machine` / `alias <Weird
Machine>`), and so is the bathroom fixture (`plain toilet` / `alias <toilet>`
downstairs against `Toilet` / `alias <Toilet>` in Deaney's).

Where the alias differs from the object name, the alias is the only thing the
parser answers to, and at ASL 311 there is no partial matching to fall back on
(Quest matches a word from the middle of a name only for ASL 391 and up:
Disambiguate, V4Game.cs:4762-4772; fixtures/abbrev.asl).  Two of those bite:
the bathroom cabinet is `define object <Medecine chest>` with `alias <medicine
chest>`, so the walkthrough must spell it *correctly* even though the game
misspells it, and the television is `<Goldstar Television>` with `alias
<Goldstar>`, so TAKE GOLDSTAR TELEVISION finds nothing and TAKE GOLDSTAR works.
The torch is a pair like the funnels: `<Torch stub>` and, after the microwave,
`<dry torch stub>` with `alias <torch stub>`.

Other flavour deliberately included: the Empty Schlitz Can in the LTD is dry
("dryer than a covered bridge"); OPEN SMALL PRESENT and OPEN BIG PRESENT under
the Christmas tree are game-level commands yielding panties and a GoldStar
television; EAT SAMMICH; USE COIL OF ROPE ON WELL fishes a Hoyt Axton record
out of the well; USE CROWBAR ON SUGGESTION BOX in the Torture Chamber yields a
paper; SPEAK TO BFH and SPEAK TO CAN O SCHLITZ both have custom replies.  The
win text ends "You wonder if Burger King is still open.  THE END".
