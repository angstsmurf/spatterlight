Hospital Visit (Hospital visit.asl, by Katamari, QDK 4.1.4, asl-version 410)
===========================================================================

You are visiting Robbie, a colleague who was thrown out of a 34th-floor window
for losing the 2007 credit adjustment figures, and you have his get-well card
and cake.  He is in Room 101 on the thirteenth floor.  GIVE CARD TO ROBBIE is
`playerwin`, and the win text is worth reading: Robbie disappears out of his
window while you wait in the corridor, and "Looks like we need to buy a new
present" -- which is the marker this script checks for.

The whole game hangs on one object.  There is exactly one "Get well soon!" Card,
and three characters have a script for it:

  * Robbie, in Room 101: `give <"Get well soon!" Card> playerwin`.
  * The Shady Man, in the thirteenth floor hall, who asks for it outright:
    he burns it and calls `playerlose`, which prints the (long, and much better
    written) losing text in which you and Robbie go over the balcony together.
  * The dying Female Patient in Ward 1, whose script would trade it for her
    granddaughter's Last Card.

Only the first two can actually happen.  The Female Patient's line is written
`give to <"Get well soon!" Card>`, and in Quest "give to <X>" on an object means
"when *this object* is given to X" -- so hers only fires if you hand the old
woman to the card.  ExecGive looks for the recipient's `give <item>` first, then
the item's `give to <recipient>`, then the two `anything` catch-alls, and none of
them match here (V4Game.Part2.cs:4724-4780), so she answers with the standard
"She doesn't want it." even in the real engine.  The script speaks to her, and to
the Shady Man, and hands the card to neither.

The two gift exchanges that do work are the other way round:

  * GIVE CAKE TO RECEPTIONIST -- the cake's own `give to <Receptionist>` fires
    (the receptionist's `give to <Cake>` is written backwards the same way the
    Female Patient's is, so it is his cake-side script that never runs), and you
    get a Box of Cigars.  Note the win text says "You hand the presents you have
    over to Robbie", but the cigars and the cake are not checked for anything.
  * GIVE FOLDABLE WHEELCHAIR TO OLD MAN in the Disabled Toilet -- written the
    right way round, `give <Foldable Wheelchair>` -- gets you the Music Box.

Everything else is scenery, and the script tours it: both other wards (Ward 2's
1938 patient records and its dead flashlight, Ward 3's vending machine that only
takes money you do not have, and the cat), the cafeteria menu and Dinner Lady
Dorris ("No food left. Goodbye!" plus three stray quote marks the author left
in), and the Mad Patient in reception.

Two things that look like bugs and are not:

  * NORTHWEST from the Hall is the stairs, and they are locked forever: "A
    maintence sign is covering the door", with no key anywhere.  The lift is the
    only way up, which is why the script goes NORTH then UP.
  * The Thirteeth Floor Hall (the author's spelling) has no `look` or
    `description` line at all, so arriving there prints the exit list and
    nothing else.  Four of its five doors are locked with a flavour message
    apiece; only SOUTHWEST, Room 101, opens.
