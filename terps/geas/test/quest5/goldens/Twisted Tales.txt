Twisted Tales
The Crimson Path
By Craig Dutton (c) 2013

Winter walks its bitter path across the land as the small village of Darkmoor shivers for a different reason this night.

Every year a sacrifice has to be made.  At the Offering Ground, one person volunteers to sacrifice them self to the terrifying resident of the Dark Forest.

In 1750, the village was founded by your grandfather, Elric Darkmoor. At the turn of the new century the werewolf came and killed the Anderson family. A deal was made between the villagers and the creature.  Only one person should be sacrificed each year otherwise all the villagers would be killed. Nothing has changed and no one has every tried to find and kill the beast...

Tomorrow is your birthday and your brother Klaus volunteers to be an offering. Celebration is the furthest thing from your mind.

Your name is Katherine Darkmoor, descendent of Elric Darkmoor. Your vociferous protests fall on deaf ears as your brother is led to the Offering Ground. Tonight is a full moon. You are taken back to your home and sit in the darkness... waiting.

The howl of a wolf emanates from beyond the forest depths and you jump out of your skin. You can't wait any longer and rush to the Offering Ground.  Only a single shoe and a splatter of fresh blood greet you. You feel sick and alone.

Whatever it takes to avenge your brother you will do it!

However, you need knowledge. How do you kill a werewolf? You have heard of a book belonging to the Notary that could tell you. But he's dead and his house is all locked up. But isn't Father Abanon the keeper of the Notary's house key? He won't give it to you easily.

As the early rays of the sun peer over the snow-laden trees you wrap yourself tightly in your red hooded cloak.  The cold morning air bites your bones and you start to shiver. If Death shadows you this day then let him fall at your feet so you may step over his even colder bones...

However, I had an affair with another woman; Father Abanon's sister in Kelbury and she found out. I didn't know she was a witch and she cursed me. I became the werewolf. My bloodlust was uncontrollable and I killed the Anderson family. The next day, on a search party, I found young Samuel Anderson hidden nearby his house. I took him to Elsa, Abanon's sister and she raised him. When he came of age he left and learned the ways of the forest and fighting. He stands before you now.'

You look at the Huntsman and tears fill his eyes.

'I'm sorry, Samuel. You have a right to end this and I will not stop you.' says Elric and closes his eyes. You intervene but Samuel doesn't make any attempt to kill Elric.

'I too have killed. I'm sorry but I knew the truth about your grandmother and it was either her or me. She wasn't the person you though she was, I'm sorry.' weeps Samuel.

You can't believe what you're hearing. Your whole life was a lie.

'Don't blame your mother, she didn't know. I told your father and he told Klaus.' says Elric, 'They did it for your protection.'

Samuel decided not to kill your grandfather and left the forest forever. Your grandfather, due to his unique condition, lived in the forest until his 118th year.

You continued to live at Darkmoor and married a Miller called Jonas. You had twins, a boy named Klaus and a girl named Helga.

Peace came to Darkmoor at last. In fact, you could say, you lived happily ever after...
