For almost a year, every morning has begun the same way. Feed Felix. Try the Wi-Fi. Look for someone outside.

The snow always drives you back. No neighbours answer. No engines start. No planes cross the sky. At night, the food you have eaten returns to the cupboards.

Before all this, there were sirens. You remember that much. Then silence, and snow.

Today you are going farther than the garden gate.
