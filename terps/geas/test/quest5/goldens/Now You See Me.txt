INTRODUCTION.
Even though it's your twentieth time in the audience of the Duchess Theatre, you're watching the man on stage with bated breath, not daring to move a muscle, not even blinking. You don't want to miss even a second of Tony Stein, the best, most awe-inspiring magician in the country. Maybe the world.

It's almost the end of the first half of the show, and you've been mouthing along with his pre-trick speeches and jokes the whole time. But although you know his words, no matter how many times you watch him, you still can't figure out Tony's tricks. You're a bit of an amateur magician yourself, so if anyone could spot the sleight of hand and the subtle misdirection, it would be you. But no such luck. You're burning with curiosity. You have been for years.

That's why tonight, you've got an idea.

See, Tony Stein's son Oliver used to be his apprentice, but has decided to move on to another career. And even though before Olly, Tony had never ever taken on an apprentice before... maybe he's feeling a bit lonely. Maybe he needs someone to share all his secrets with.

The intermission begins, and you make your way into the foyer with the crowd. But instead of following them to the bathrooms and concession stands, you slip your way past the ushers into the corridor backstage. You tiptoe up a stairwell and soon find yourself at Tony Stein's door. Here goes nothing. You knock, noticing as you do, that there is a small dog sitting by the door.

Suddenly, everything goes black.

From somewhere around you, you hear a voice. The amazing Tony's voice. “I've seen you here before.”

He sounds a lot more menacing than he does on stage.

“You're trying to steal my tricks. You work for my competition? Well, I'm not going to let that happen. If you think you can figure out my secrets, try to figure your way out of this!”

The darkness vanishes. Tony Stein is nowhere in sight…and neither is the door you knocked on. Or the hallway. Or the stairs. You're now inside a room with no obvious way out.

Ahead you see a box of the type magicians use when sawing someone in half. This one is unusually small. Trapped inside is the dog that you saw at the door. It seems uncomfortable and is shifting around restlessly. Maybe Tony Stein isn't so whimsical and wonderful after all. Maybe you need to get yourself and the dog out of here!
