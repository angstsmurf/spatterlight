Dr. Science Mouth

by Farley Sweet

Street Corner
You can see your friends and the Sampton Warehouse in the distance.
You can go north.

It was Halloween night and the town was finally quieting down. The parade was over, children had returned to their homes with their spoils, and only the occasional snicker or cheer could be heard in the distant before the patrolling police cars scared any stragglers off.

One of those left over groups was you and your friends. They were ready to conclude the night with a movie but not you. You stood at the street corner, leaf sprinkled field ahead of you with your destination in view—the Sampton Warehouse.

“Ho ho, something tender this ways comes, eh?” The man at the end booms his honeyed voice out towards you, “come over and give me a hug!”

“There’s nothing more jolly golly than a god damned hug! Ha!” You swear something it about to break but he releases you, “I am Dr. Science Mouth and I humbly welcome you to my domain.” The odd man walks off the way you came, “I’ll be with you in a moment, friend. Set yourself up at the end of the hall.”

Dental Room
You can see a dentist chair, a skeleton and a cabinet.
Here there are light blue walls and a white tiled floor. There are curtains to a window with bricks covering the outside. Between a grey cabinet and a countered sink is a dentist chair.
> sit on dentist chair

> sit on dentist chair
You lie down in the chair and a moment later a dozen claps of iron swing across it and lock you tightly into it. You struggle but it’s no use—until Nurse Namanda enters the room.

“I shouldn’t have let you even come this far,” she says with tears in her eyes, “you should have never of come here. You don’t know who…what he is.” The nurse activates something behind the chair and the claps return. You’re free!

“NAMANDA!” Dr. Science Mouth’s voice calls out from down the hall.

“Don’t fall for his tricks and traps. Get out of here as soon as you can,” Namanda clicks something and you feel the room ascend. She rolls out the door and you see her drop down into the fancy hall—you’ve elevated away from the first floor.

Street Corner2
You can see an officer, Dr. Science Mouth, Sampton Warehouse and a vehicle.
A police car pulls up to you, slamming on the brakes. You fall onto the hood in distress, inching your way around it as the black officer steps out.

The officer has his hand on his gun and raises up the other to you, “What’s going on? Are you in—” his eyes flicker from you to the now dangerously close Dr. Science Mouth, “Drop the weapon!” Dr. Science Mouth reaches the home stretch to the street you’re on, “I SAID DROP THE—” a warning shot is sent into Dr. Science Mouth’s shoulder but does nothing to impede him!

“FEEEYYYAOOORRR!” Dr. Science Mouth yells while the officer unloads several more shots into his chest—none doing any true damage bar the one that hit his wrist and sent his own chainsaw into his own neck! “Geaghthh!” Dr. Science Mouth falls to his knees with blood spraying from his neck, his chainsaw falls to the blacktop, and the officer lowers his gun—out of ammo.

A final moment goes by with nothing but the sound of the wind passing by before more silence. It’s over. Dr. Science Mouth has bled out on the ground and the officer opens the backdoor to his vehicle to you, “why don’t you wait in the back before I get some backup to arrive.”
