 The House of Dom Orre.

Above the village stands the mysterious house of the eccentric scientist, Professor Dom Orre. A reclusive character, Dom Orre visited the village on rare occasions, but he has not now been seen for some weeks, not since terrifying screams had been heard from the house. Rumours abound concerning his fate, the nine fabulous artefacts he is reputed to have accumulated during his travels and an experiment he has been working on.

As the ace reporter of the local newspaper, you have been sent by your editor to investigate the situation and, incidentally, to gather what  treasures you can find.
