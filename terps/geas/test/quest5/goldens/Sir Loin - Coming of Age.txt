IN THE BEGINNING...

A long, long time ago, somewhere between the dark ages and the light ages...in some sort of dawny age... Come-Here-A-Lot Castle sat proudly on English soil, somewhere near Bognor Regis. That was where King Arnold reigned, aided by his loyal and self-righteous Knights of the Brown Table.

Many people longed to become Knights of the Brown Table. One such person was Loin, a small orphan boy brought to the castle by the kindly Quartermaster, Stan.

As Loin lies sleeping in the hay in the Royal Stable, he dreams that one day he will wield the sword of the Knights and serve his King.

And today may just be the day...

...or maybe tomorrow?
