Hilltop View
You are standing in a clearing at the top of a steep hill surrounded by trees. Below you to the north lies a wide, shallow valley. There is a large forest in the valley. It fills the whole valley from where you are standing to the northern side of the valley, and stretches far into the west. Huddled within the forest is a small village. To the east of the village is a large lake. Beyond the lake, far in the distance, is a steep, dark mountain. Although the weather here is clear, the mountain is covered by dark clouds and rainfall. The highest peaks of the mountain break through the clouds.

A small, winding path leads north down the hillside.
