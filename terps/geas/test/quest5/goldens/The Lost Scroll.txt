The Lost Scroll

by peter edwards

 Introduction.

You are an investigative journalist with a high reputation based on your success in solving the mystery of Dom Orre.
You are summoned to the home of an old man, John de Sward, on his 100th birthday, who has a mission for you.
You are in the home of Mr de Sward.

"It's my 100th birthday today," he tells you. "I've had a long life and no regrets, save one.  My grandfather, Sir Peter de Sward, used to be a favourite of the King who often used to stay at his home, the stately house just outside the village.

"One day he was entrusted by the King to look after an important scroll until such time as it was needed. Sadly, soon afterwards he was tragically killed in a riding accident. No sign could be found of the scroll, either on his person, or in the house. It was assumed that he had either carelessly lost it, or traitorously disposed of it.

"Nothing was proved, but my family fell out of favour, and we lost our wealth. The house is now the property of a national heritage organisation, and is open to the public.

"We have always believed in his innocence. If only the scroll could be found..."

You are determined to help if you can, and make your way to the old House.
