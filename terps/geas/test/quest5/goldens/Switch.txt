"Not any more, my time travelling days are over," Veronica sighs and adopts a contemplative gaze.
"Was this your last assignment?"
"No one ever told me that... I didn't decide that but... well, let's put it this way, the future that I come from has been altered so tremendously by what I did in making sure that little old you remained little old you, that I'm pretty sure I don't belong there anymore."
"But, what do you mean?  you're not going back?"
"The door that would normally take me home - I'm pretty sure it won't lead there this time. Do you think they'll risk me bringing something of you back with me? No. I know them. You could say I've been quarantined - for ever!"
"But how can you be sure? That means you'd have to stay here forever, well, I mean till you die."
"There wouldn't be anything there for me anyway; the world that made me never existed, the block I grew up in never needed to be built, the government department that sent me here never needed to be created. I could go on. I'm homeless, jobless and have no family."
Richard absorbed Veronica's words and his face candidly mirrored the devastating shock he felt inside.
"You've... sacrificed everything... and it seams it's all my fault."
"I've FIXED everything. And you HELPED me, remember?"
"Well then.. I suppose I'd better buy you a coffee."
