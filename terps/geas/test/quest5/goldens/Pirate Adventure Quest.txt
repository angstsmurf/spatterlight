Pirate Adventure Quest

Arrrrrr!  Your dream of being the captain of your own pirate ship is about to come true!  You will be free to hunt treasure and plunder all the swag there is!
You are on board a pirate ship. The captain and the other crew went on shore to secure supplies.  You were trusted to guard the ship along with your trusty pet monkey. You know the captain has a treasure map and you just know it has to lead to some vast fortune.  Now is your chance to get it all for yourself!  Find the map and steal the ship while they are away.  Hurry before they come back!
It would be useful to keep a map as you explore an island.
