As you move forward  you hear loud coughing. Pigears staggers from the wreckage.

"WELL DONE YOUNG SHODDY!" he booms. "COME ON, LET'S GET OUT OF HERE. WE CAN GET DOWN TO THE STATION AND BACK TO TOYTOWN IN NO TIME. THEN WE ARE OFF FOR A LONG VACATION IN FLORIDA.
