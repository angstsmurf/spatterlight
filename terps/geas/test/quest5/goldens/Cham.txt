Cham is from the kingdom of Sag. All was peaceful much of the time but for once every few years when an enormous black tower materialised out of thin air, bringing with it an army of foul beasts from goblins to dragons. They plundered all the wealth they could and then vanished back away to whatever place they dwelt.
One day while walking along, a mermaid jumped into Cham's arms (Cham was the one walking) and offered to fulfill any wish if Cham would deposit her back in the sea.
"Aye!" Cham did sayeth, "And you will fullfill my wish by depositing me wherever be the black tower, and I will crush every stone thereof and all the black hearts therein!"
So this the mermaid did...
