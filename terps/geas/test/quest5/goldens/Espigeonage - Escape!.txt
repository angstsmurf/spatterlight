Espigeonage - Escape!

by Marcus Corby, Ethan Tobey, Ethan Tan, Cameron Celebuski

You, America’s #1 spy pigeon, were assigned a secret mission to gather classified Soviet Union data. You have been captured! Now you must find a way to escape the confinement of the Soviets.

You’re stuck in a cold, damp, cramped prison cell. The Cage Bars are barely wide enough to stick a human arm through. How will you escape?

You enter into a small prison block lined with neighboring cells.

You see three other inmates occupying cells near yours, who you nickname Grumpy, Sneezy, and Sneaky

You notice an Upper Platform where the prison guards oversee the prison block. There is also a locked door leading to a guard room on the upper level, but it’s completely inaccessible from the ground.

"I learned an important piece of Soviet intel, but it's only one part of a larger scheme. The code word I found was PIGEONS"

"Also, on your way out, try to look around to see if you can find any other information."

A Locked Door sits in front of you, with an old fashioned Lock. You remember that you passed through a guardroom, and two other rooms when you were initially brought into the prison block.

You Win!

Since you have all the code words, you were able to piece together a Soviet plot! The documents were chronicling the Russians’ attempts to stop you and your spy pigeon friends. That must be what the “pigeon” codeword meant!

The “threat” codeword probably was talking about the threat that you pose to the Soviets. After all, they have no way of telling you apart from regular pigeons, and even when they offered a cash reward for pigeons to report their suspicious peers they still couldn’t find you.

By that logic, “failures” probably references all the failed attempts to catch you. They almost got you with a robotic arm last year, but you escaped by landing on Comrade Gorbachev’s head and confusing the targeting systems, leaving him with a nasty scar. And you still have nightmares about the nuclear-powered net they used in Chernobyl.

But what does “falcon” mean? Wait, you got captured because a falcon grabbed you! You thought that was just bad luck, but what if the falcon was a Russian spy? You have to get this info to the CIA right away!
