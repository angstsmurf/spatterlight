You give the comb to the mermaid, who accepts it with joy!

"I'm so happy," she says. "not for getting my comb back, but because you have passed my test. You have shown yourself to be kind, heroic, trustworthy and resourceful - the sort of person I need to help me. By telling you my story I am making myself very vulnerable.
"Many years ago," she continues, "as humans reckon time, I was trapped in this cave by some evil pirates. I can only be rescued by my own kin, and to summon them the Great Conch is required. This conch was stolen by the pirates and is my only hope. Sadly, I am now too weak either to search for it or to use it.

Will you help me?"
You readily agree.

"Thank you." she says simply. "You will need this." She hands you a tiny shell.

"Remember," she adds, "letting go can sometimes be more rewarding than hanging on..."

As you watch you realise that some are real horses and are ridden by many mermaids and fierce-looking mermen. They ride beneath the cliffs, and reappear carrying the little mermaid. As they disappear into the foam, she turns briefly and waves.

You drop the conch. It vanishes! You feel that the memory of the mermaid, however, will not disappear so easily...
