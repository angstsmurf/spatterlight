With Alexendre Bartolomeu defeated, complete anarchy reigned over the Shadow Masters. They have wrecked havoc all through the little towns South and West of the Dark Hills in an attempt to seek revenge on the one who abandoned them and then returned to defeat their leader. Luckily for you, after winning your battle with Bartolomeu, you managed a narrow escape and have been hiding in a cave on just the other side of the Dark Hills for the last couple of weeks. During the time you've been sitting in the cave alone, you've thought about everything that has gone on. Somehow, you feel that the recent destruction is all your fault and that you have a responsibility to fix all of the wrong which has been done. You know you can't stay in hiding for the rest of your life so you decide that you must embark on a new journey to confront the Shadow Masters and remedy all the problems which you have caused.
