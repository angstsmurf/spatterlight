Welcome to The Devil's Bargain.  In this text roleplaying game you portray a common thug living life on the streets.  You can play this character in many ways:  a thief, a nice guy, a drugE, or something else.  Whatever you choose I'm sure you will take full advantage of your resources as you try to build your fortune in this game. 

Press any key to continue...  




Objective:  Buy a Guitar (A $1000 Ibanez) from the Pawn Shop on Maple Avenue so you can start an amazing band that will rule the world with it's amazing sound!

Losing:  Their are numerous ways to lose, dying or being thrown in jail without bail money are the most common. 

Press any key to continue...
