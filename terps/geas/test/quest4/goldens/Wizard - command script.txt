Wizard v1.3 (by Wonderjudge, ASL 410, distributed as an obfuscated .cas)
- raw command script for geas_walkthrough_runner.
Game system: Quest 4.  Win marker: "You have finished Wizard!!".
Needs --tick: almost every gate in the game is a `define timer` with a turn
interval, and geas only advances timers when the runner ticks them.  Needs
GEAS_SEED=1 for one reason only (see IDIOCY below).
No score in this game; the win is `playerwin` from the endgame.
1088 turns, 1218 script lines - the extra lines are menu picks, `ask` answers
and `enter` answers, none of which the runner counts as turns.

You are a month-old apprentice at the wizards' tower by the sea.  A summoning
has gone wrong, the grand wizard seals your bedroom door "for your own
protection", and then screams start coming down the hall.  By the time the seal
fades the tower is empty: the students are dead, the masters are prisoners in
the castle across the sea, and Grackon - one of your own elders - has sold the
order to the Red Scorpions.  The whole game is about the spellbook.  You start
with three spells and finish with twelve plus their reverses, and every single
new spell has to be found on a scroll, scribed, and then usually reverse-learned
before it becomes the tool that opens the next area.

THE SPELLBOOK IS THE MAP.  `memorize` and `rmemorize` both open a numbered menu
over the twelve spellbook slots, so the script is full of bare numbers:
  1 Illuminate  2 Unlock  3 Scribe  4 Detect magic  5 Levitate  6 Enfeeble
  7 Bremoval    8 Charm   9 Bind   10 Enchant      11 Stabilize 12 Shield
`rmemorize` on the same number gives the reverse - Deluminate, Lock, Gravitate,
Enlighten, Badder, Unbind, Disenchant, Destabilize.  You may hold at most five
memorized spells at once (`ns`), casting one frees its slot, and casting is
never gated - only memorizing is.  Slots 4-12 are all acquired in play: Detect
magic from the dusty scroll under the bed, Levitate from the invisible scroll
you REACH IN TELESCOPE for on the roof, Enfeeble from the secret library scroll,
Bremoval from Mercoz's Rainbow book, Charm from the icy scroll in the crystal
cavern, Bind from the crumpled scroll the songbird drops, Enchant from the holey
scroll under the boulder, Stabilize from the shiny scroll the bead table prints,
and Shield from the stained scroll the captured wizards pass through the hole.

IDIOCY IS COMPULSORY, AND IS THE ONLY RNG IN THE GAME.  The secret library
scroll enters Enfeeble into the spellbook with its description set to the
literal string "?", and `rmemorize`'s Enfeeble case refuses while that is still
the description ("You don't know what the spell does yet").  So Enlighten - the
prerequisite for memorizing Bremoval or Badder at all, and for `banish` in the
last scene - can only be unlocked by casting Enfeeble on yourself first.  That
sets `idiot2`, whose `beforeturn ... dontprocess` swallows every command for the
next 45 turns, so lines 167-212 are 46 forced `wait`s.  The game's one and only
`$rand(1; 5)$` is the drooling message picked each of those turns, and roll 5
makes you throw the spellbook on the floor - hence `take spellbook` at line 213.
Under GEAS_SEED=1 roll 5 lands early and the pickup is real; that draw is the
whole reason the seed is pinned.

THE 60-TURN OPENING.  Bedroom1's east exit is the sealed door, and nothing
unseals it: the `door1` timer runs for 60 turns from the startscript and then
prints "Your door suddenly stops glowing!".  Lines 16-70 are that wait, done
after the four things worth doing in the dark bedroom.

THE PORTAL IS THE OVERWORLD.  The summoning lab's Swirling Misty Portal is the
only way off the tower's island.  `portal1` cycles `portal2` every 15 turns
through black(1) - red(2) - yellow(3) - blue(4), and ENTER PORTAL reads the
current value: black kills nothing but wastes the trip, red goes to Forest1,
yellow to the Small ledge, blue to the Lake shore.  The colour is only printed
by the room's entry `script` and by LOOK AT PORTAL - a bare LOOK will not tell
you - so the script probes with `look at portal` and then pads with `wait` until
the wanted colour comes round.  That padding is why the run is 1088 turns; the
wait blocks at (counting from the Start: line) 16-70, 167-212, 294-328,
460-496, 562-606, 855-930, 946-984, 995-1018 and 1035-1048 are all
either timer waits or portal-phase alignment, and they are tuned to this exact
script - inserting or deleting a turn anywhere before them will land the wrong
colour.  Every destination has its
own Misty White Portal home.  Late in the game `stabilize swirling misty portal`
(line 1161) freezes it white forever, which is the fifth destination: the
Outside gate of the enemy castle.

The colour is worth being able to compute, because retuning these blocks by hand
is otherwise guesswork.  `portal2` is never initialised, so it starts at 0, and
`portal1` is `enabled` from the start of play with `interval <15>`, so it fires
on turns 15, 30, 45, ... - a Quest timer counts TimerTicks up to its interval
and then resets, which makes the period exactly the interval and not one more
(Tick, V4Game.Part2.cs:91-129).  A command issued on turn n therefore sees the
value written by the k'th firing, k = floor((n-1)/15), and that value is
((k-1) mod 4) + 1.  So over turns 15k+1 .. 15k+15 the mist is black for
k = 1, 5, 9 ..., red for k = 2, 6, 10 ..., yellow for k = 3, 7, 11 ... and blue
for k = 4, 8, 12 ...; k = 0 is the opening 15 turns, when portal2 is still 0 and
LOOK AT PORTAL prints no colour line at all because no `select case` arm
matches.  In turn numbers, the blue windows - the ones this route wants four
times over, for the sand, the boat, the water bucket and the rope - are turns
61-75, 121-135, 181-195, 241-255 and so on every 60 turns.

THE POTION FACTORY.  Seven of the eight potions are made, not found, and the
mixed ones are made from the made ones, so the Alchemy labratory is visited six
times.  One-off setup: LOOK AT POTION DISPENSER and LOOK AT POTION MIXER (geas
keeps a container's contents out of scope until the container has been looked
at, which is faithful Quest behaviour, and the beaker lives in the dispenser).
A primary potion is eight turns - `use bucket of sand on glass machine`, `take
empty beaker`, `use empty beaker on potion dispenser`, `push red|yellow|blue
button`, `take beaker`, `use beaker on potion mixer`, `pull lever`, `take
<colour> potion` - and a mix is four: both inputs onto the mixer, `pull lever`,
`take <result>`.  The blue button is unlabelled until `use label on unlabeled
button` (line 377); the label itself is inside the tavern's Bottle of sludge and
has to be BADDERed into a "Label2" you can carry.  The Empty bucket from the
closet starts magical, so `use empty bucket on sand` at the lake makes a Bucket
of sand that never runs out - infinite beakers.
The pair table (`define procedure <mix2>`) is: R+Y=Orange, R+B=Indigo,
Y+B=Green, B+Green=Violet, B+Orange=(B+O), Y+(B+O)=(B+O+Y), Indigo+Violet=
(V+I), R+(V+I)=(V+I+R), Green+(V+I+R)=(V+I+R+G), and finally
(V+I+R+G)+(B+O+Y)=Rainbow.  The five combination bottles have no alias, so they
must be typed exactly as they are named: `take potion (v+i)`, `use potion
(b+o+y) on potion mixer`.  Lines 647-768 are the whole Rainbow chain in one
sitting - twelve primaries and nine mixes - because each mix has an "only one of
each potion type" guard and the chain has to be walked in order.
What the potions do: Red = `cr`, cold resistance, for the Snowblown path.
Orange = `bf`, fire breath, for the wall of ice and the spider web.  Yellow =
`bu`, gills, for the flooded cavern.  Green = `sa`, speak with animals, for the
giant mouse.  Blue = `fr`, fire resistance, for Grackon's opening spell.  Violet
dissolves the boulder on the Tiny ledge.  Rainbow = `i1`, 30 turns of
invincibility, the only way past the booby-trapped front doors.

THE SIXTEEN YELLOW BEADS.  Every bead is `alias <Yellow Bead>`, so from the
second one on every TAKE YELLOW BEAD raises a disambiguation menu, and geas
orders that menu by object *definition* order in the file, counting inventory
and room candidates together.  The bare numbers after each `take yellow bead`
were computed that way (definition lines 1368, 1523, 1563, 1696, 1802, 1847,
2929, 3080, 3396, 3466, 3793, 3875, 4002, 4111, 4162, 4385), which is why the
index climbs from 1 to 16 across the run rather than staying at 1.
The beads go onto the Strange Table in the extradimensional Room - a room you
carry in your pocket, because `bremoval broom` turns the closet Broom into a
Room and `enter room` walks into it.  With all sixteen placed the table becomes
a 4x4 lights-out grid drawn entirely in .jpg tiles, i.e. completely invisible in
a text runner; the coordinates are column a-d by row 1-4 and each press toggles
itself and its orthogonal neighbours.  All sixteen start lit, and the shortest
of the sixteen solutions is exactly four presses whose toggle sets partition the
board: C1, A2, D3, B4 (lines 1148-1151).  The table then prints the shiny scroll
= Stabilize.

  1. The tower (lines 1-98).  Illuminate, dress, the dusty scroll under the bed
     (Detect magic), Scribe, Unlock, then the 60-turn door wait.  Up to the roof
     for REACH IN TELESCOPE (Levitate); `unlock door` and into the closet for
     the Broom and the Empty bucket.  The Collapsed wall in the stairwell is
     passed with `levitate collapsed wall` + yes, which also moves the wall to
     the far side, so every later trip through the stairwell needs another
     Levitate (lines 843 and 940).  The library's chandelier is reached from the
     balcony one floor above it: `gravitate chandelier` at line 93 brings it
     down, and EXAMINE BROKEN CHANDELIER in the library below is the first bead.
  2. Library and barracks (99-157).  The control desk is a menu-driven
     catalogue with three options, all three of which are used.  Option 1 wants
     a row (1-2000) and a book (a-e): 1112 and "c" is a hollowed-out book with a
     bead in it, and that call number is written on Mercoz's smudged note, which
     this route does not pick up until line 154 - the script simply uses it
     early.  Option 2 with the topic "portals" is the in-game explanation of the
     colour cycle, including "never enter when the mist is black".  Option 3 is
     the librarian insisting there are no secrets in the library, and it has to
     be asked six times (lines 106-115) before it gives up and produces the
     secret library scroll = Enfeeble.
     The Main Entrance's south door is blocked by two Giant statues: EXAMINE
     GIANT STATUES + yes opens a `repeat until` over six flags (each statue's
     arm, shield and head), and every control locks or unlocks one of the
     others, so it is not six moves but ten.  Lines 124-133 are a
     breadth-first-search solution: 1, 3, 5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 1, 4, 2.
     Behind the door Delbat is the one other surviving apprentice; Mercoz's body
     in the dormitory's south wing carries the Rainbow book (Bremoval), the
     smudged note and a bead.
  3. Enfeeble, Enlighten, loadout (158-227).  Scribe both books, cast Enfeeble,
     sit out the 45 idiot turns, pick the spellbook back up, `rmemorize` 6 for
     Enlighten, cast it, and use the 40-turn `int1` window to memorize Bremoval
     and Badder - the only two spells whose memorization checks `int1`.
  4. First red portal (228-286).  Fill the bucket at the lake on the way past,
     then the Alchemy lab for Red and Orange, drunk immediately for `cr`+`bf`.
  5. Yellow portal: the mountain (287-345).  Snowblown path needs `cr`; the wall
     of ice needs fire breath; `bremoval beast` turns the Beast into a Beas...
     into nothing and re-creates the destroyed east exit of the Small cave (the
     startscript destroys it at load).  The Crystal cavern has the icy scroll
     (Charm) and a bead but no exit - `deluminate` is the way out, so the trip
     is ordered to cast Bremoval first and free the slot for it.
  6. Red portal: the town (346-424).  `bremoval boars` gives the Oars.  The
     small boy has to be Charmed before he will talk, and his puzzle is
     ten-peg solitaire in the game's own move order; the answer sequence is
     remove peg 2 then choices 1,1,1,1,2,1,1,1, which yields the bag of
     marbles, two red marbles and a bead.  Charm the bartender twice for the
     bottle of sludge, LOOK AT the bottle before TAKE LABEL (the seen gate
     again), then `badder label`.  The stables hide a bead in the hay and the
     Oat that becomes the Boat.
  7. Blue and red portals: bind, purse, shells, church (425-461).  The songbird
     in the oak drops the crumpled scroll (Bind).  `bind shady looking man`
     stops the mugging that otherwise robs you; his purse holds a copper coin,
     and the coin must be taken into inventory proper, because the street
     merchant's `got <Copper coin>` does not see a coin sitting inside a carried
     container.  The shell game is rigged in your favour by `levitate ball`
     before picking a shell.  In the church, `detect magic` is what reveals the
     bead in the basket.
  8. The lake and the sunken temple, first visit (462-544).  Yellow potion for
     gills, `badder oat` for the Boat, `unbind rope` to open the trap door, and
     then the flooded cavern east to the temple.  `badder rain` in the north
     wing turns the endless rain into the Brain and permanently drains the
     temple (`flood1`), which is also what makes the cavern walkable later.
  9. The Rainbow potion (545-797).  Violet for the boulder on the Tiny ledge
     (holey scroll = Enchant) with a red-potion trip up the mountain, then the
     long uninterrupted chain to Rainbow.
 10. The mouse hole (866-962).  Green potion for `sa`, Indigo to shrink, then
     the Enormous Area behind the closet mouse hole.  All eight of its exits
     loop back to itself and the hole to the Large mouse hole only exists if you
     entered from the Closet, so this has to be a dedicated trip up the tower.
     Ask the giant mouse for its third option after pushing the yellow ball, and
     the `shrink` timer (80 turns) dumps you back out with the bead.
 11. Bucket of water and the ent (963-1024).  `disenchant bucket of sand`,
     empty it, fill it with lake water, `enchant bucket of water` - the Fire
     checks `property <Bucket of water; magical>` and a non-magical bucketful
     just wastes the bucket.  Putting the fire out makes the ent clear the
     spiders, which unlocks Forest3 south; behind the web (burned with fire
     breath) are the last outdoor bead and the stone Head.
 12. The golem (1051-1106).  Two red marbles for the eye sockets and the Brain
     for the skull, then `use head on giant statue`.  SPEAK TO GOLEM + yes
     swaps your mind into it: your inventory and your memorized spells are
     parked in the `wstuff` room and the golem's hands are too big to pick
     anything up except objects typed `golem` and the Strange machine.  As the
     golem: smash the small shelf, carry the timber to the east wing, feed the
     furnace, carry the machine back to the main hall, smash the stone coffin,
     then SPEAK TO YOURSELF + yes to swap back - which is also what moves the
     bead the golem could not pick up into the room you can reach.  With the
     furnace lit, LOOK AT STRANGE MACHINE offers the lever puzzle twice: 1,3,1
     drops the Powerfull scroll (Banish) and 1,3,1,2 drops the sixteenth bead.
 13. The bead table and Stabilize (1107-1164).  `bremoval broom`, in, sixteen
     beads, four presses, out, scribe the shiny scroll, and freeze the portal
     white.
 14. Reconnaissance (1165-1188).  The Iron gate is magical from the start, so
     `unlock iron gate` fails until `disenchant iron gate`.  `gravitate
     drawbridge` sets `draw1` permanently.  Then swim the moat to the cracked
     wall and SPEAK TO HOLE for the stained scroll (Shield).  This trip has to
     happen before the lever is pulled: once `end1` is set, the Moat's entry
     script drops piranhas in it and kills you outright.
 15. The assault (1189-1220).  Memorize Shield, Enlighten, Gravitate, Levitate
     and Destabilize - the full five - and carry the Powerfull scroll, the bag
     of marbles and the Blue and Rainbow potions.  Drink Rainbow on the castle
     grounds (the gargoyles at the front door kill anyone without `i1`), Shield
     and Enlighten and Blue in the cell corridor, then PULL LEVER + yes.
     From there it is a five-move chase, and the sequence is forced by `end2`:
     `drop bag of marbles` (which is scripted to reset `end2` to 0, and is the
     only thing that stops the scorpions in the long hallway), `gravitate giant
     chandelier` (drops `end2` again and carries you to the grounds), `levitate
     drawbridge` (strands them and carries you to the gate).  Quest evaluates a
     room's `afterturn` against the room you were in when the command started,
     not the one you moved to, which is exactly why this chain survives.
     At the gate `ending1` counts up one per turn: 1 is Grackon's flames
     (survived with `fr`), 2 is his magical darts (survived with `shield1`), 3
     is the long complex spell, and 4 melts you if he is still there.  `banish
     grackon` only works while `ending1` is exactly 3 and only while `int1`
     still holds, which is why Enlighten is cast eight turns earlier and not
     sooner.  Banishing him lets the rest of the Red Scorpions through the gate,
     so the portal home is no longer an escape - `destabilize swirling misty
     portal` blows it up instead, the scorpions take you, and the order is safe.
     You lose, and you win.

No geas divergences turned up in this game; it exercised a lot of the engine
(nested selections, `repeat until`, `for each object in <room>`, `enter`,
container `seen` gating, timers under `--tick`, definition-order
disambiguation) and matched throughout.
