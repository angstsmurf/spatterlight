Mario Is Missing 2: Ultimate Quest (Jordan, QDK Pro 3.53, ASL 350)
==================================================================

Part 2 of a fan-made three-part Paper Mario pastiche.  You are Luigi, hunting for
the kidnapped Mario across 86 rooms of Glitzville, Toad Town, Hooktail Castle,
Shy Guy's Toy Box, Goomba City, Dry Dry Desert and the Boo Mansion, mostly by
whacking bosses in the correct order.  Nearly every puzzle is "hit the right
object with the right verb"; the interesting part is that the wrong verb usually
calls `playerlose` on the spot, and there are 21 such instant deaths.

Two endings, both `playerwin`:

  * Secret Room- Ending -- at Fahr Outpost, `speak to x naut` and answer his
    password menu with choice 3, "There there now password outpost".  (Choice 4,
    "Hurry hurry now Luigi password outpost", is the decoy and kills you.)
  * REAL Ending -- the Thousand-Year Door, reached by showing the Bob-omb at Fahr
    Outpost the Bob-omb doll.  `take thousand year door`, say yes, and the game
    prints "(Congratulations on finding the secret ending!)" -- the author labelled
    the *canonical* ending "secret", which is what this script's win marker
    matches.

This route takes the REAL Ending, because it needs one more item than the
password does and so covers strictly more of the game.

Verbs
-----
`take X` is the all-purpose attack, `speak to X` the all-purpose conversation, and
`examine X` is a *different* action from `look at X` -- both are defined
separately on many objects and only one of them advances the plot.  In the
training room the chain is deliberately one of each: `look at bench` reveals the
seat, `examine seat` reveals the bathroom, `speak to bathroom` reveals the enemy,
`take enemy` starts the first fight.  In the Shy Guy Army fight, phase four is
cleared with `examine shy stack` and nothing else.

Almost all of the bosses fall to the Glitz Pit stage lights, picked up with `take
lights` mid-fight against Giga BowZer and used as `use lights on <boss>`.  They
are confiscated on entering the Boo Mansion foyer (`lose <lights>`), which is
also the point at which the vacuum cleaner takes over as the weapon -- so the
whole Dry Dry Desert chapter has to be finished before the mansion.

Item and flag dependencies (the real map of the game)
-----------------------------------------------------
  Stone keys      Petalburg, `speak to mayor kroop`     -> Stone Warp Pipe works
  Radio           Hooktail Castle- Secret Room          -> Dull T trades the Toy Box
  Toy Box         Dull T, menu choice 2                 -> `use toy box` enters it
  20 Gooms        dropped by Commander Murder           -> Goomba Gate, Yellow Binary
  Yellow Binary   Dull T, menu choice 5                 -> Yellow Pipe to Dry Dry
  bow tie         Luigi Duplighost puzzle
  party hat       GBL Duplighost puzzle                 -> both free the Mayor
  Dry Dry Hammer  dropped by Ultima Bones               -> smashes the Forever Gate rock
  Vaccuum Cleaner Boo Mansion side room "Room"          -> Boo fight, hidden doors
  Bob-omb Doll    `use vaccuum cleaner` in "Room"       -> Fahr Outpost -> the door

Goomba Gate has no exit into it: the way in is `use mushroom`, the free red
mushroom from the Shroom Market, whose use script teleports you there.  Getting
back out to Toad Town needs the `out` exit that the Highway's own `north` handler
creates -- `north` there prints a paragraph and creates three exits without
moving you, so it has to be typed even though it looks like a no-op.

The two Duplighost puzzles are coin flips with a death on the wrong side.  Hit
`luigi` (not `luigi?`) and `gbl?` (not `gbl`); the giveaway is in the two
selection menus, where the fake answers the "if I asked the other guy" riddle the
way a liar would.  Each correct hit drops the hat or tie, and taking it warps you
back to the City Hall, whose description re-runs and frees the Mayor once both
are held.

Timers -- why this script runs with --tick
------------------------------------------
Three timers, and the game cannot be finished without them, so the runner needs
`--tick` (one tick per turn, as the geasglk frontend does):

  * Cannon My Way Out! (interval 5).  Armed by `take cannons` in Shy Guy's
    Playground and again by `take cannon` in the War Room; if it fires you are
    strapped to the cannon and killed.  Both times the very next command fires the
    cannon and disarms it.
  * Blast, The Nuclear Blast Is Bad! (interval 15).  Armed by `take nuclear
    reactor` in the Doopliss fight.  The escape is six moves -- staircase,
    weapons research, Goomba City, west, out, `take bomb shelter` -- and taking
    the shelter is what disarms it and opens the Forever Gate.
  * Wow... It's COOL In Here! (interval 60).  Armed by `speak to bootler` in Lady
    Bow's Room and the *only* way to reach Fahr Outpost, hence the 60 `look`s
    near the end of this script.  There is nothing to do while the Cool Moon
    rises; Bootler even says so ("About... 1 minute").

Deaths this script threads past
-------------------------------
`take machine gun` (Magnus 2.0), `use lights on hooktail`, `take the general`
before firing the cannons, `take general tank`, `take man` or `take woman` at the
Dry Dry shop stands, `take king koopa`, `take ultima bones`, `take boorawn`
bare-handed, going `west` from the Goomba Highway after the tanks activate, and
`take luigi?` / `take gbl` in the Duplighost puzzles.  Also note `take box3` is
the *orange* box: the ten colour-coded boxes on Hooktail's third floor are a Half
Life joke and only the orange one goes on the invisible pedestal.

Authoring quirk this game exposed in geas
-----------------------------------------
Bare `use <item>` with no target (fixtures/bareuseroom.asl, bareuseroom410.asl).
Quest checks the current room's own list of bare-use handlers before the item's
own `use` action -- but only below ASL 410 (ExecUse, V4Game.Part2.cs:5348-5368);
from 410 on the room's list is never consulted for a bare use.  geas checked the
room first at every version.  This game is 350 and depends on the room winning
twice: the vacuum cleaner's own `use` only comments on the noise, while the dusty
side room's `use <Vaccuum Cleaner>` uncovers the Bob-omb doll and the third
floor's uncovers the door to Lady Bow.  Without the room-first half neither the
doll nor Fahr Outpost exists and the game is unwinnable; without the 410 gate,
geas would run a room handler that Quest 4 ignores.
