A day at the redsauce office -- QDK Lite 4.1.2, asl-version 410.  The file is
game329.asl.

A companion piece to Red Sauce Monday, which is already in the suite: the same
Stoke web agency, the same staff, written as an in-joke for the team.  Tom
introduces it himself -- "This is a standard text adventure, so remember to LOOK
at things, SPEAK to everyone and GIVE things to them when necessary" -- and that
is the whole game.  There is a crocodile sitting at your desk and nine
colleagues between you and getting rid of it.

There is no playerwin and no score.  The win is a message on the rubber band's
`use on <CROCODILE>` -- "You beat the game, so well done.|n|nNow, whose turn is
it to make the teas?" -- and that is the marker.  The game keeps running
afterwards, because nothing ever ends it.

THE CHAIN is strictly linear and every link is a GIVE except the last two:

    can of pepsi -> TOM       -> banana
    banana       -> AMY       -> banana skin
    banana skin  -> CHRIS     -> banjo
    banjo        -> TIM       -> magazine on Twitter
    magazine     -> SIMON     -> cup of coffee
    cup of coffee-> ROGER     -> anime DVD
    anime DVD    -> SELENA    -> M&Ms
    M&Ms         -> MILTON    -> cheeky pose
    cheeky pose  -> JAMES     -> rubber band     (USE, not GIVE)
    rubber band  -> CROCODILE -> the ending      (USE ON)

Each colleague's SPEAK text is the hint for what they want, so the intended way
to play is to talk to all nine and work out the order.  The route below just
does the chain.

THREE THINGS THAT ARE EASY TO MISS.

  * NORTH from outside does not get you in.  The Front door room's description
    is the whole hint -- "Try opening the door first." -- and it is the door's
    `action <open>` that runs `goto <office>`.
  * The can of pepsi does not exist until you LOOK AT the FRIDGE: the fridge's
    look script ends with `reveal <CAN>`.  It is the only object in the game
    hidden behind a look, and the chain cannot start without it.
  * The last link is USE CHEEKY POSE ON JAMES rather than GIVE.  James declares
    `use <CHEEKY POSE>` and the pose has no `give to` at all, so GIVE POSE TO
    JAMES gets nowhere.  (The game block also declares a
    `verb <use cheeky look on james>` that does the same thing, which is
    presumably where the author started before moving it onto James.)

AUTHORING QUIRK, visible in the transcript.  The door's open script conceals the
entire cast so that the office listing is not a spoiler, but three of the
conceals -- magazine on Twitter, banana skin, banjo -- are written *after* its
`goto <office>`.  The goto prints the room first, so the first look at the
office lists three objects that are gone by the time you look again.  That is
faithful: a script keeps running after a goto, it just runs too late to affect
the description the goto already printed.

The rest is scenery with jokes on it: the garden south of the office tells you
off for not working, the football table lets you CHALLENGE CHRIS or CHALLENGE
TIM and then admits you have no chance and bounces you back, the shelves
remember Selena's doweling faux pas, and SPEAK TO FRIDGE gets "You say 'Open
Sesame', but nothing happens.  Because this isn't Kings Quest".

Verified: 17 turns, marker "You beat the game", seed 1.
