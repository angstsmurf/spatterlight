Broken Mirror: The Screaming Fountain -- command script
Derived from BMTSFD.asl (ASL 310).

A demo, so the ending is a `displaytext <win>` plus `playerwin` rather than a
real conclusion; the win marker is "completed the rather short demo".

Two things in the map are not exits and cannot be walked to:

  * Subway Entrance -> Street happens inside the Two Men's `speak` script
    (a `goto <Street>`), so talking to them is mandatory, not colour.
  * The pub is reached with `place <THE ENGLISH>`, which makes a "go to" exit
    named after the room's alias -- hence "go to the english pub".

The last three moves are the whole puzzle. Examining the Old Guy is what
`show <Rope>`s the rope he is twiddling with; the Brothel Advertisment on the
bar is what makes him leave, but only via his `give` script's `if real <Rope>`
guard, so it has to be examined first; and the rope can only be taken once he
is gone (`take` checks `not here <Old Guy>`).

Talking to the Woman for the Card, and the extras in the subway and the pub,
are not needed to win. They are here to walk a little more of the demo than
the shortest path does.
