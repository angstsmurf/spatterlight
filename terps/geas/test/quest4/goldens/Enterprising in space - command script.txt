Enterprising in space
Enter.asl, v1.0, ASL 311, "Created with QDK 3.12", author FireJuggler, 2003

A Star Trek parody in which you are Captain Cork and the entire goal, stated in
the intro text, is to bed Lt. Youhera; everything else on the ship is a step
towards that.  Thirteen rooms, no score, no inventory puzzles worth the name.
It is an adult game and the prose is pornographic; nothing in this file
reproduces it.  172 turns below, visiting every room, every object and every
one of the author's joke responses.


THE SHAPE OF THE GAME

There are no flags and no variables anywhere in the file.  Every piece of state
is an object being hidden and another being shown: Youhera is eleven separate
objects (Youhera, Youhera2 through Youhera11, Youhera13 and Youhera14 -- the
numbering skips 12), the Ships Mate is three, Nurse Baker is seven, Nurse Foster
five and Nurse Kelly five.  All the variants of a person share one `alias`, so
SPEAK TO YOUHERA always addresses whichever one is currently visible, and the
game is a state machine you advance by talking to people in the right order.

Movement is entirely by `place`: the Turbolift links to all seven decks and the
rooms link back to it.  Where a room has an `alias` you must type the alias --
GO TO CREW'S QUARTERS and GO TO YOUHERA'S QUARTERS, with the apostrophe; GO TO
CREWS QUARTERS is "You can't go there."  The three epilogue rooms created later
have no alias, so those are GO TO FOSTERS QUARTERS and so on.


THE YOUHERA LADDER

  Youhera    -> Youhera2   carry the Dildo.  The Bridge has both a
                           `beforeturn override` and an `afterturn` doing
                           `if got <Dildo> then hide <Youhera> / show <Youhera2>`,
                           so the swap happens the moment you walk in with it.
  Youhera2   -> Youhera3   SPEAK TO YOUHERA, answer the ask; she takes you to her
                           quarters.
  Youhera3   -> Youhera4   USE DILDO ON YOUHERA.  This also reveals Nurse Kelly,
                           Nurse Baker and Nurse Foster in the Hospital, which is
                           the only thing that starts the nurse chain.
  Youhera4   -> Youhera5   SPEAK TO YOUHERA.
  Youhera5   -> Youhera6   carry the Chocolate (its `gain` script does the swap).
  Youhera6   -> Youhera7   SPEAK TO YOUHERA, answer the ask.
  Youhera7   -> Youhera8   GIVE CHOCOLATE TO YOUHERA.  USE CHOCOLATE ON YOUHERA
                           only tells you to give it to her instead.
  Youhera8   -> Youhera9   SPEAK TO YOUHERA.
  Youhera9   -> Youhera10  carry the rope (its `gain` script).
  Youhera10  -> Youhera11  SPEAK TO YOUHERA; she walks to the Turbolift and you
                           have to follow her there.
  Youhera11  -> Youhera13  SPEAK TO YOUHERA in the Turbolift.
  Youhera13  -> won        USE ROPE ON YOUHERA in the Captain's Quarters.

So the three things that actually gate the game are the Dildo, the Chocolate and
the rope, and two of the three are gated by `gain` scripts rather than by
anything you type.


WHERE THE THREE OBJECTS COME FROM

The Dildo is the easy one: in Engineering, SPEAK TO SHIPS MATE and say yes to
"Will you order her to her knees?".  Engineering's `beforeturn override if here
<Dildo>` then swaps in Ships Mate2 -- it fires while the Dildo is still on the
floor, i.e. on the very turn you TAKE it, which is why she has already changed
by the time you look at her again.

The rope is a prop left behind by a hollow-deck glitch.  You need Nurse Kelly's
program card, which you get by feeding her, which means finishing the nurses.

The Chocolate is at the end of the same nurse chain:

  SPEAK TO NURSE FOSTER          reveals the Regulations Manual -- in the
                                 Captain's Quarters, a room the game has given
                                 you no reason to visit yet.
  TAKE REGULATIONS MANUAL        its `gain` swaps in Nurse Baker2.  This one
                                 worked even before the engine fix below,
                                 because the author wrote it as
                                 `take give <Regulations Manual>` and the give
                                 statement runs gain scripts correctly.
  SPEAK TO NURSE BAKER           -> Nurse Foster2
  SPEAK TO NURSE FOSTER + ask    both nurses go to the Captain's Quarters
  SPEAK TO NURSE FOSTER + ask    (there) -> Baker4 / Foster4
  SPEAK TO NURSE FOSTER          -> the combined object "Nurses Foster and Baker"
  TAKE NURSES FOSTER AND BAKER   -> Nurse Kelly3, and
                                 `create exit <Crews Quarters; Fosters Quarters>`
  SPEAK TO NURSE KELLY           (Hospital) -> Nurse Baker5, in the Galley
  TAKE NURSE BAKER               -> the Sandwitch and Nurse Baker6
  TAKE SANDWITCH                 its `gain` -> Nurse Kelly4, in the Exam Room
  TAKE NURSE BAKER               -> the Chocolate, and the Bakers Quarters exit
  TAKE CHOCOLATE                 its `gain` -> Youhera6
  GIVE SANDWITCH TO NURSE KELLY  (Exam Room) -> the hollow deck program card,
                                 and the Kellys Quarters exit
  USE HOLLOW DECK PROGRAM CARD ON CARD READER   -> the Virtual Dominatrix, and
                                 the card is consumed and replaced by a
                                 "Useless card" you keep for the rest of the game
  TAKE VIRTUAL DOMINATRIX        -> the rope

Note the ordering constraint that is easy to get wrong: the rope's `gain` swaps
Youhera9 for Youhera10, so it does nothing if you pick the rope up before you
have talked Youhera up to state 9.  The script below finishes the Chocolate leg
and the two conversations after it *before* going to the Hollow Deck.

The three "quarters" rooms -- Fosters, Bakers and Kellys -- are pure epilogue.
They define no exits of their own; the only way back out is the reverse half of
each `create exit` pair, which the author did remember to write.


THE ASK PROMPTS AND THE ENDING

Five `ask` prompts drive the plot, and geas renders each as a two-item selection
(1) Yes (2) No; the script answers them with the word "yes", which works.

The last one is the win itself.  USE ROPE ON YOUHERA ends with

    if ask <You have won would you like to stop playing?> then playerwin

and then, either way, the author's thank-you note and paypal address, followed by
`show <Youhera14>`.  Saying yes prints `define text <win>` and stops.  Saying no
leaves you in the Captain's Quarters with Youhera14, a repeatable epilogue scene
-- and no second chance at the win, because Youhera13 has already been hidden.
The script says yes.  `define text <lose>` is empty and there is no `playerlose`
anywhere: you cannot lose this game, only fail to finish it.


ONE ENGINE FIX CAME OUT OF THIS GAME

Pinned by the new `gainscript` fixture.

An object's `gain` script must run when the object is taken.  Quest's ExecTake,
for a bare `take` or a `take <message>`, prints the message and then calls
PlayerItem(item, true), which moves the object to the inventory and runs its
GainScript (V4Game.Part2.cs:5205-5215 and 6631-6634).  geas dispatched that
through the two-argument `run_script` overload, whose signature is
(script, &return_value) -- so it ran the object's *name* as a script line and
dropped the gain script into the return value, silently.  Only a scripted take
that hands the object over with `give <self>` still worked, because the give
statement has its own correct implementation, and that is exactly the form the
Regulations Manual uses, which is why the game looked healthy for its first
forty turns.  The Sandwitch, the Chocolate and the rope are all bare takes, and
all three gate the rest of the game: without the fix the Exam Room is empty,
Youhera never leaves state 5, and the game is unwinnable.


ODDS AND ENDS

  * Every inanimate object has a joke `speak` response -- the Dildo, the
    Sandwitch, the Chocolate, the rope, both cards, the Regulations Manual.  The
    script talks to all of them.
  * Dr. Boner is dead in an insta-freeze chamber and Solo and Checkup are busy
    with each other; the author's stated reason for both is that it removes them
    as competition.
  * Nurse Kelly3's look, take, speak and examine tags each carry a stray
    `hide <Nurse Baker2>`, as does the Sandwitch's gain -- the author cleaning up
    after a state he had already left.
  * The Ships Mate, both nurses and Kelly all have repeatable final states, so
    the game has more content after the win than before it.
