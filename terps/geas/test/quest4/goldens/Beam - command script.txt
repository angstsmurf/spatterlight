Beam (Madrone Eddy, 2006, QDK Pro 3.53) - raw command script for geas_walkthrough_runner.
Game system: Quest 4.  Win marker: "safe on Earth".  REQUIRES --tick (heavily timer-driven).

Derived from the "bare minimum walkthrough" in the hint file, fixed for this .cas build:
  * AIRLOCK CYCLING: you enter from the south (south door open); the north door only opens once
    the south auto-closes (Door Airlock South timer, 5 ticks). So wait ~4 turns in the airlock,
    then "open door" -> it pops a choose-menu ("Which door?"); answer "1" (North Door).
  * AIR: opening the north door starts a ~90-tick hypoxia countdown; the maze to the machine is
    short enough to beat it without the optional air bottle.
  * WIN TIMING: inside the machine, the launch sequence is Launch(30)->Space(12)->Atmosphere(12)
    ->Earth(10), and each step costs interval + 1 turns, not interval: Quest's SetTimerState only
    flips TimerActive and raises BypassThisTurn, never resetting TimerTicks (V4Game.Part2.cs:561-574),
    so the tick that turns a timer on is spent on the bypass flag instead of counting.  The
    "safe to exit" window therefore opens 31 + 13 + 13 + 11 = 68 turns after you climb in and stays
    open the 13 turns Machine in Atmosphere Exit(12) needs to close it.  Wait 68 turns then "out".
    Exiting during space/atmosphere = instant death.
