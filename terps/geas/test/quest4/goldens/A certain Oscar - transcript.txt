
A certain Oscar., v1.00 Ido Angel

A tingling sensation at the end of your forehead wakes you up. Your ears seem to have catched the last moment of a dissolving echo, an eerie sound of metal crashing against the ground... but by the second you think you recognise the meaning of it all - all disappears.

Quiet.

It seems as though the world just stopped: The wind froze dead in mid gust. Birds paused their flapping, people snapped still - their sound echoes hanging motionless in the cold, wintery, windless air.

And then the pain. A slow yet sharp pain, eating you up like a hungry virus from your heart and up through your throat, into your mouth, numbing your tongue... and thrn up to your head. You try to shut your eyes but you can't.

And as if out of sudden concern, the frozen world kindly folds himself into an invisible little pink dot, and hides behind the leg of the letter "P" in the word "SHAKESPEARE", that is hanging in the air motionless, caught on it's way from one forgotten mind to another. The Word, now the only existing thing, other than the Darkness, dances happily around you. It is playing with you. You try to reach for it but it flys away, glittering with an endless sense of childish semanticism. You tire of it and finaly shut your eyes, only to realise that they have been shut the whole way through. "SHAKESPEAR", dissappointedly, fades away.

And you remain alone.
