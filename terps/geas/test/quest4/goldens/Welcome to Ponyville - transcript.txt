Status are measurements in your different abilities. All abilities are divided into these categories.

STR - Strength is how tough you are. The higher it is, the more likely you are to win battles and move heavy objects.
DEX - Dexterity is how skilled you are. The higher it is, the more likely you will be able to craft and manipulate objects.
KNO - Knowledge is book smarts. The higher it is, the easier it will be to learn new things from books and pass test.
AGI -  Agility is how fast you are. The higher it is, the more likely you are to win races and escape danger.
WIS - Wisdom is your magic ability. Believe it or not, even pegasi and earth ponies have a small amount of magic.
