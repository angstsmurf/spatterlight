
The Maze, v1.1 Tim Hamilton

The last thing you remember is hitting on this really pretty girl at a party. Then you suddenly have woken up in the middle of the night in the local hedge maze with the front gate shut and sealed. You need to find a way out of here before daylight hits and you are arrested for tresspassing.
Press any key to continue.

The Maze!
By Wonderjudge.
I left the debug windows on in this one so you could see that the game was made all in one room. I warn you though the only way you can lose is by using the debug window to cheat.
