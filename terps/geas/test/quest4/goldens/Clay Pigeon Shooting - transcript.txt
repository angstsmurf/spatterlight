CLAY PIGEON SHOOTING

Clay pigeon shooting, also known as clay target shooting, and formally known as inanimate bird shooting, is the art of shooting at special flying targets, known as clay pigeons or clay targets, with a shotgun or any type of firearm. Scoring of each target is 3 points for a first barrel kill, 2 points for a second barrel kill and 0 for a miss. Make sure BOTH your barrels are loaded before shooting. This is standard procedure.

This interactive fiction game can be played with either the keyboard or the mouse. When using the keyboard, you will have to type the commands.

When using the mouse, you can access shortcuts to the commands by clicking on the alternatives presented in the window menu "game commands", which you can find at the top of the screen, or using the panes on the right hand side of the screen.
