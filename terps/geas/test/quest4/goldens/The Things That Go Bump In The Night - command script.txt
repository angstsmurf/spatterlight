The Things That Go Bump In The Night, by Tim Hamilton (QDK 4.1.6, ASL 410)
- raw command script for geas_walkthrough_runner.
Game system: Quest 4.  Win marker: "Not on my watch."

318 turns, and by far the largest Quest 4 game in the corpus: 87 rooms, four
separate buildings, a dozen keyed and coded doors, and five timed set-pieces
that kill you if you spend a turn in the wrong place.  You are the lone night
security guard at a smelting complex; something has turned the fence live,
knocked out the water pump, filled the site with chemical mist and let a great
many things out of their cages.

Nearly every room is `properties <dark>`, so TURN ON FLASHLIGHT on turn 8 is
load-bearing for the rest of the game -- several exits check
`is <#Flashlight:suffix#;(providing light)>` and simply refuse to let you leave.

Things worth knowing before reading the script:

THE FIRE EXTINGUISHER IS AMMUNITION.  `fe` is a 0-5 gauge on the small
extinguisher (0-10 on the large one) and every spray decrements it.  It is spent
on the corridor creature, the vine and the black mass, which empties it -- so the
Smelter Lunch Room has a *second*, fresh one behind the glass of a Fire Cabinet
(USE HAMMER ON CABINET breaks the glass).  That refill is not optional: the
octopus fight below needs `%fe%` to be at least 5 when the fifth spray lands.

FOUR DEAD FUSES OUT, FOUR GOOD ONES IN.  The water pump in the Pump House will
not reset until fuse1..fuse4 are all parented to the Fuse Box, and the box's
`add` script counts what is still parented to it and refuses a fifth.  The four
good fuses are scattered across the site (New, Good, Dusty, Wet -- the wet one
comes out of the pump's own drainage valve).  This is the game that pinned down
geas's implied-REMOVE-before-TAKE bug: geas used to take a parented object
without clearing its `parent` property, so the box stayed "full" of fuses it no
longer held and the pump could never be reset.  See fixtures/takefromcontainer.

THE GAS CAN IS A THREE-STATE MACHINE.  `gascan1`: 0 empty, 2 oil only, 3 gas+oil
mix, 1 straight gas.  The chainsaw wants the mix and the Makeshift Bomb wants
straight gas, so the oil goes in *first* (while the can is still empty), then the
Fuel Pumps, then FILL CHAINSAW empties it, and only then is it refilled with
plain gas at the pumps for the bomb three hours of game-time later.

THE OCTOPUS (East Smelter Courtyard).  Eight tentacles.  Three chainsaw slices
take out most of them, but the third throws the chainsaw over the fence, which is
why the fire extinguisher is dropped in this room *before* the fight starts -- the
menu's "pick up the extinguisher" choice needs it here.  Then the flashlight
blinds it and five sprays finish it.  On the fifth spray the `arms=0` branch wins
the race against the `f=5` (empty extinguisher) branch, which is what lets the
extinguisher survive the fight to be thrown at the beast at the end.  The menu
answers below are option *numbers*, and they do not count as turns.

EXEC RUNS THE TURN HOOKS AGAIN.  Quest's `exec` is not a dispatch shortcut: it
hands its parameter to the whole of ExecCommand, so an exec'd command gets its
own `beforeturn` and its own `afterturn` on top of the outer turn's.  Three of
this game's set-pieces are timed off `afterturn` and three of its commands are
written as `exec`, so the route below is built around which commands tick the
clock twice.

THE HUGE SPIDER (Smelting Plant).  The plant is a 3x3 grid tracked in
`location2`; `beforeturn` recomputes the spider's approach square (`spider3`)
from your *pre-move* location and `afterturn` either moves it there or, if
`spider4` is set, burns a stun instead.  SHINE LIGHT ON SPIDER and USE RADIO are
both `exec`.  SHINE LIGHT ON SPIDER sets `spider4`, the inner `afterturn` sets
`spider5`, and the outer one clears both: one still turn, nothing carried over.
USE RADIO sets `spider4` *and* `spider5`, so the inner `afterturn` clears them
and the outer one lets the spider move -- it is not a stall at all.  The only
stun that carries is USE FLASHLIGHT ON SPIDER, a plain object `use` with no
`exec`, and it only bites when the spider is on an adjacent square.  So: three
radio turns walk the ladle to the centre (Neva reverses every direction you give
her, which is why the answers below read as "east" and "west"), one adjacent
flashlight stun buys the turn needed to let the spider come round to the centre,
and SHOOT CHAIN finishes it -- that one is `flag on <spider4>` followed by
`exec <use hunting rifle on chain>`, so it satisfies the "spider must be
stunned" test inside its own turn and no separate stun is needed at the end.

THE CLOWN (Recycle Work Lab).  USE LEAF BLOWER ON BALLOON ANIMALS is what sets
`search1=1`; without it the five searchable spots only ever get `search1` to 5
and the Button never appears.  So the blower has to be fired *before* the
searches, and re-fired between them, because each round the balloons drift back
and the clown closes in.  USE FLASHLIGHT ON CLOWN is the reset that puts `clown1`
back to zero before it reaches the value that claws you.  The searches are
written as X and not EXAMINE on purpose: the game defines
`command <Examine #@ob#> exec <x #@ob#>`, so every EXAMINE advances `clown1`
twice and you get clawed on the second search of each pair.  Five spots (lab
benches, x-ray machine, microscope, furnace, eye wash station), then PRESS
BUTTON, then the Door Handle is under the benches.

THE RING OF KEYS (Refinery Courtyard) is a four-step Rube Goldberg: LOOK AT TABLE
reveals the keys on a stone table on the far side of an electrified fence; the
Magnet from the Smelter Storeroom taped to the Mop Handle from the Maid's Closet
reaches them, but the magnet touches the fence and the keys are flung up into the
elm tree; SHAKE BRANCHES drops them into a pile of leaves; the Rake from the
Work Shed digs them out.  Note that CLIMB TREE is refused both while the keys are
still on the table and once the leaf pile exists, so the climb has exactly one
turn of opportunity.

THE BLOB (Refinery Main Floor).  LOWER LEVER in the control room drops the chain
hoist so it can be climbed; the Makeshift Bomb (large extinguisher duct-taped to
a full gas can) is thrown at the blob and shot from up on the chain, and it only
works when `%fe%` > 5 -- entering the control room for the first time sets `fe`
to 10, so the large extinguisher must not be sprayed on anything first.  LOCK
DOOR on the way out is the non-obvious one: Refinery Main Floor's entry script is

    if flag <chainlift1> and flag <kp11> then { ...the chain rises... }

so if you leave the control-room door unlocked, Dave gets in behind you and
raises the chain, and there is no way off the floor.

THE BAT AND THE HOSE.  You cannot carry the Ladder and the Hose at the same time,
and the roof needs both: the ladder goes on the Hooks to open the ceiling hatch,
you fetch the hose, then the ladder is pulled up after you (which destroys the
DOWN exit) and laid across the Gap to the lab roof.  USE HOSE ON PIPE ties the
hose to the fire-suppressant machine first -- the bat carries you off no matter
what, and holding the other end of that hose is the only thing between you and
the ground.  The grab also costs a point of bloodloss, and the moth swarm and the
two blows the bear lands during SPRAY BEAR (another `exec`, so the fork lift's
`afterturn` runs its attack twice a turn) have already spent three of the four
you get, so USE GAUZE goes in just before the crossing.  `rg` is a five-use
counter and this is its only use in the route.  The hose is also, later, what
pipes the suppressant into the ventilation
system once the chemical valve behind the lab has been shut with the Wrench.

THE GLASSES SWAP is the ending.  Neva has been your sidekick since the gypsum
pile; she is also the one holding a gun to your head in the Secret Labratory, and
the gremlins are keyed to whoever is wearing the signal glasses.  So you give her
the plain Safety Glasses from the Supply Room and wear the pair she "dropped"
behind the lab.  Get that backwards and the gremlins eat you.  After that it is a
strict turn budget: `throw hammer at fire alarm` (which needs Hose2 parented to
the Ventilation System, or the alarm sounds and nothing happens), retrieve the
hammer -- the throw drops it -- then two turns through the Underground Tunnel
(`tunnel1` > 2 and Dave escapes) and one on the runway.

Two lines below look like mistakes and are not.  Line 314 (I) is a deliberate
inventory check inside the octopus aftermath; and LOOK in the Secret Labratory is
a sacrificial turn, because that room's `beforeturn` is `if here <Dave> then
dontprocess` -- the first command you type there is swallowed by design while the
Neva-betrayal scene plays out in `afterturn`.
