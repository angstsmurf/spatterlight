
The Mansion, v1.0 | Nick Dablin

It's 3:30 in the morning. The phone's ringing. Who could it be at this time of night? You stretch, yawn, and reach for the receiver lying on your bedside table.
"Hello. Who is this?" you ask impatiently.
A voice at the other end says that it's the police. Your uncle, a noted scientist, has been found dead.
Shocked, you quickly pull on some clothes. It's quite a drive to the station, but you get there and identify the body. You feel quite sick to the stomach. Your uncle was your only living relative. Something isn't right though. Apparently, he died of a heart attack. But he was only 55, and had never had any history of heart problems.
A few days later, you learn that you have inherited everything your uncle owned - his wealth and his huge mansion in the country. Wearily, you pull up on the gravel drive outside the house. Time to investigate the house for any clues to his death...
