Blight of Elantria
By Craig Dutton (c) 2011

A dark veil hangs over the land of Elantria.  Xanthus is a demon who resides in the Southern Peaks and has plans to conquer the lands with an undead army.  You are an adventurer and have been assigned to stop Xanthus before it's too late.  

You know of only one way to defeat him - the Pandemonica.  It's an ancient tome that holds the secrets to the underworld.  Acquiring this will surely give you knowledge on how to destroy Xanthus.  Strangely, Xanthus keeps the tome near him.  It's protected so it doesn't fall into the wrong hands, but hopefully will fall into yours.

Good luck on your mission!
