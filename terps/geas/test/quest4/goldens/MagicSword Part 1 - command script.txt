MagicSword Part 1: Legacy of Earth -- Dom, QDK Pro 1.02, asl-version 217.

A big, sprawling, half-finished sword-and-sorcery game: five towns and a
forest maze drawn with real care, a hand-rolled combat system built on
seventeen collectables, and then a door at the end of it that the author
never got round to putting anything behind.  It is worth being clear about
what "winning" means here before the route below, because the ending is not
a climax.  Walk into the gatehouse beyond Hallucination Forest, talk to the
Guardian of Maren, and he tells you so himself (procedure 102):

    "Yet again this is the end of the line #name#.  You can go no further.
     There is nothing beyond the door leading outside.  It has not yet been
     created.....  Still your path has not been particually difficult, and
     you probably got here with ease."

He then asks whether you are finished or wish to return.  Say yes and you
get `msg <For now:|n...You Win!>` and `playerwin` (procedure 204).  That is
the game's one and only playerwin, so it is the marker this script is
verified against -- but it is an author's bookmark for a Part 2 that does
not exist, not a victory.  Everything before it is a real game, and the
illness plot does resolve properly on the way.

THE ILLNESS PLOT.  You are a graduate of a magic academy; the Head Luridii
sends you to the village of Fennat, where people are dying of a disease
that arrived with a mist over Hallucination Forest.  The mist is made by an
Evil Druidess in an underground den, from the spores of a creature called
Sporogia which her sister used to help her stir into gas.  Kill Sporogia and
kill the druidess and %Illness% flips to 0.  This matters mechanically as
well as narratively: GH1's gatehouse place is

    place <the; Gatehouse> if is <%Illness%;1> then do <~Internal Procedure 291> else goto <FGH>

so while the illness is live the gatehouse only tells you Fennat is in
quarantine.  The endgame is unreachable until the plot is finished.  There
is no way to shortcut it.

THE FIVE KEYSTONES.  Getting to the druidess means opening the stone in the
deep forest clearing (FD1), which has five hollows.  Each `use keystone on
stone` runs procedure 111 -- decrement Key, increment Unlocked -- and the
fifth calls procedure 110, which prints "and a door opens!!!" and does
`goto <FDO>` on the spot.  Exactly five keystones exist, and this route
collects all five:

  1. The Archer Sprite east of the forest edge, killed by throwing the
     dagger and picking it up again (procedure 119).
  2. The Entangled Sprite, freed by cutting its thorny bush with the dagger;
     it hands you one in thanks (procedure 133).
  3. The three Warsprites in FI2, fought one at a time with the sword; the
     last one's death conjures a keystone (procedure 194).
  4. The Oggy-Waggi plant's bush, where a previous victim left one
     (procedure 148).
  5. The pair of demons guarding a keystone on a table in FD2.  Taking it in
     the open runs procedure 124 and the demons take it back off you and
     hurt you; cast the fog spell first and procedure 123 lets you sneak it
     away.  The fog lasts a handful of turns (Fogtimer), so cast it in the
     room you intend to steal in.

Two more keystone-granting procedures are dead code -- 139 ("The sprite
falls.  He drops a keystone...") and 170, the entire Axe Sprite encounter --
neither is referenced anywhere in the file.  So is FI3's alternative north
exit:

    north if has <Unlocked;=6> and is <%Illness%;1> then goto <FDO> else goto <FD1>

Unlocked can never reach 6, because there are five hollows, five keystones,
and the fifth one teleports you into FDO anyway.  Which makes FDO a one-way
door: it has no exit back to the forest, and its only place is the
Underground Den.  Do not go into the stone clearing until you are ready to
finish the illness plot, and do not expect to come back out for supplies.

COMBAT.  Every fight is the author's own script, and each one wants a
specific weapon; the wrong one is either refused or costs you Health.  In
practice: flam spell on plant and tree enemies (demon tree, tree demon,
Golomoshk), air rush spell on airborne ones (smoke whisp, Sporogia), light
flash spell on the wraith, sword on ordinary bodies (bandit, sprite,
warsprites), thrown dagger on the ranged ones who otherwise shoot you while
you close (the Sniper, the Archer Sprite -- throw, then TAKE DAGGER, then
throw again), and dagger on the Oggy-Waggi plant's tendrils and body.  The
counts below are the exact number of hits each enemy's HP needs at seed 1;
a surplus cast just says "You don't see any...", but a shortfall leaves the
enemy alive and hitting back.  Health is bought back three astrals at a time
by taking a room at an inn -- the Sword and Staff in Calamatra, the Jolly
Woodman in Fennat -- which is why the route detours to them twice.

AZAMBRI NEVER SHOWS UP.  Procedure 79 wants an ally for the Golomoshk
fight and does `showchar <Azambri@GProad1>`, which cannot resolve: below ASL
281 Quest's SetAvailability does not accept a room qualifier on a character
that is not defined in that room, and Azambri is defined elsewhere.  geas is
faithful here (verified against QuestViva's pre-281 SetAvailability), and it
does not matter, because the author wrote his own fallback into the same
procedure: with no ally the fight simply runs solo.  Golomoshk takes seven
flam casts alone.  She turns up again in the wake-up scene at the end
regardless, since procedure 181 shows her by name with no room.

ENGINE NOTE -- the game-block `use <X>` fallback.  This game is unwinnable
on a geas that lacks it, and it is the reason the interpreter grew
find_game_block_line.  Dom declares four use scripts in the *game* block
rather than on the objects:

    use <Sword>
    use <Staff>
    use <Immune Potion> do <~Internal Procedure 242>
    use <Potion> do <~Internal Procedure 241>

Below ASL 280, Quest's ExecUse (V4Game.Part2.cs:5472-5497) searches the
room's use list, then FindLine over the game block, and then gives up with
the defaultuse error; the object's own use action is not in the chain at
all.  geas used to stop at the room, so USE IMMUNE POTION answered with
Dom's defaultuse text, "You might want to, but you can't."  That potion is
the witch's gift and the only thing that sets %Immune%, and Hallucination
Forest kills anyone who enters without it (procedure 103: you cough, sit
down, and die of the disease).  Without the fallback the forest -- which is
to say the rest of the game -- is sealed off.  The route below drinks both
game-block potions, the Immune Potion at the witch's house and the doctor's
healing Potion before the last fight, so it exercises the path twice.

QUIRKS WORTH KNOWING, all of which cost turns to discover:

  * `place` exits answer to GO TO <name>, not to the bare name.  Typing
    "study" or "training room 1" gets "I don't understand your command".
  * A plain (unscripted) place answers to the destination room's *alias*,
    not to the text in the place line.  `place <the; Doctors>` leads to a
    room aliased "the Doctors House", so it is GO TO THE DOCTORS HOUSE.
  * `ask` prompts are numbered menus in this runner: answer 1 or 2, never
    yes/no.  There are five of them in this route, and two of them
    (procedure 47's "Are there any last things you want to do here?" and
    procedure 102's "Are you finished now?") branch on the answer.
  * The academy will not let you leave until you have spoken to the Head
    Luridii, been to the training room for both teachers, cleared the
    training gauntlet, and then returned and spoken to him twice -- the
    first time to decline his offer of more time (answer 2), the second to
    be sent on your way.
  * The first command of the game is not a command: the start script asks
    for your name with `enter`, so the script's first line is the name.

Route: academy (name, Head Luridii, both teachers, training gauntlet, heal
gem, back to the Head) -> Calamatra road (tree demon, bandit, Golomoshk) ->
Calamatra (sorcerer's spell, inn) -> north to Fennat (Sniper on the way,
witch's Immune Potion) -> forest sweep for the five keystones, with inn and
doctor stops for Health -> stone clearing -> FDO -> Underground Den
(druidess's sister, Sporogia) -> den again (she takes your staff) -> dagger
on the Evil Druidess -> Ferran House 2, illness cured, staff back -> back
across the forest to GH1 -> gatehouse -> Guardian of Maren -> yes.

Verified: 170 turns, `--win "You Win!"`, seed 1.
