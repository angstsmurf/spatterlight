Uranus or Bust! Episode 1: Escort (uranus.asl, by Sam Bickley, ASL 210) -
walkthrough, derived from the game source.
Win marker: "BOOOOOM".  Episode 1 of a series, so the ending is a "Continued
in..." card rather than a real conclusion.

Two things can kill you, and both are the *first* answer to a yes/no question:
saying yes to "Try and destroy?" at the asteroid blows up the squad, and
attacking the Fatties without the Fighting for Newbies Book gets everyone shot
(procedures 7 and 16).  Answers are menu numbers -- 1 is Yes, 2 is No.

Every "go to asteroid surface" below is a different room: the game chains five
of them (Asteroid Surface, Surface2 ... surface5), all aliased "Asteroid
Surface", each with a single place exit to the next.
