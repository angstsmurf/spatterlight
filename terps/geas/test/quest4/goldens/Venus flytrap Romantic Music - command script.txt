Venus flytrap: Romantic Music -- Nicky Leech, v1.0 (musicvf1.cas, ASL 210)

A one-house sex farce in thirteen rooms.  You are Leonard; you want the lodger's
Nat King Cole CD; the lodger is Linda Venus, and she is in the shower.  Episode
one ends when a bus pulls up in the street.  Its win text, in full, is "End of
episode one." -- there is no episode two.


The route

Everything hangs off one chain, and every link is a flag set by a procedure:

  READ RED BOOK      "You Too Can Have A Photographic Memory"  -> photo=y
  READ GREEN BOOK    "How to Juggle by I. Dropballs"           -> juggle=y
                     (readgreenbook checks photo first: green book read on its
                      own prints "You read the green book." and sets nothing.
                      The joke is that you cannot remember how to juggle unless
                      you have already read the memory book.)
  JUGGLE BALLS       only in the street (confirmjuggle tests
                     #quest.currentroom#), only with juggle=y (otherwise
                     juggleup drops the balls on the pavement), and only pays
                     out if `here <hat>` -- so the hat has to be lying in the
                     street, not on your head.  DROP HAT first, then juggle: an
                     elderly woman puts a GBP 2 coin in it, and yesmoney flips
                     to n so it happens once.
  BUY CONDOMS        in the public convenience, spends the coin.
  TAKE ROMANTIC MUSIC in the lodger's room, holding the condoms.

The balls are in the attic, and the attic is reached by the steps from the
kitchen -- carried to the landing, dropped, and then LOCKed before being
CLIMBed.  CLIMB STEPS on unlocked steps runs climbdie, which is `show
<steps.gif>` and `playerlose`: the only fatal move on the critical path that
isn't about clothes.

Take the music without the condoms and vertakemusic sends you to takemusic ->
sleeplinda, which ends `if got <everyday clothes> then do <clothesdie> else do
<noclothesdie>` -- two different scenes, both finishing in `pounce`, which is
`show <faint.gif>` and `playerlose`.  With the condoms you get sleeplinda2
instead, which is the same scene up to "Do you want the music or not?" and then
runs clothesdrop: `lose <everyday clothes>` plus `moveobject <everyday clothes;
#quest.currentroom#>`.  Your clothes are now on the lodger's floor.  TAKE
EVERYDAY CLOTHES again before you leave the house, because the street's
description procedure begins

    expocheck2: if got <everyday clothes> then do <expocheck> else do <police>

and police is `show <exposure.gif>` and `playerlose`.  expocheck then tests
game=y -- set by takemusiccondom along with lb=n (Linda is out of the shower for
good, so the bathroom finally opens) and bd=c -- and hands off to endpartone,
which reveals Haidee at the bus stop and calls playerwin.

"go to" wants the bare room name, not the `place` prefix: GO TO PARENT'S BEDROOM
works, GO TO YOUR PARENT'S BEDROOM does not.  That is Quest's behaviour too --
Disambiguate strips a leading "the " and matches the alias, and the
prefix-tolerant abbreviation pass is gated on ASLVersion >= 391 (V4Game.cs:4599-
4790).  At ASL 210 nothing here is prefix-aware.

The script below takes the long way round on purpose, to walk over the parts of
the game that are easy to get wrong: the attic before the steps ("You cannot
reach."), the bathroom while Linda is still in it ("The door is locked."), both
books examined before they are read, the shoes taken so the dog shit in the
street lands on `tread1` rather than on bare socks, and the bathroom revisited
after the scene to show it has unlocked.


The engine fixes this game needed

Three, and it loaded into a corpse without any of them.

1. A stray `end define`.  Line 517 closes `define procedure <noclothesdie>` a
   second time.  geas's section scan let the block depth go negative there, and
   from that line on the `depth == 1` test that decides whether to record a
   top-level block was never true again -- so two thirds of the procedures, the
   whole of the standard library the author had pasted in at the end, and
   `define text <win>` were all silently dropped.  The game printed its first
   room and then no command did anything at all.  Quest's own scan just steps
   over the line (V4Game.cs:1745-1787); the one thing that objects is
   CheckSections, which calls it a fatal error -- except that Quest skips
   CheckSections outright for three files it names in its source, and this is
   one of them:

       if (filenameNoPath == "bargain.cas" || filenameNoPath == "easymoney.asl"
           || filenameNoPath == "musicvf1.cas") _skipCheckFile = true;

   Quest carries a second hardcoded concession to this game as well -- `if
   (filenameNoPath == "musicvf1.cas") _useStaticFrameForPictures = true;`
   (V4Game.cs:1727), because the author expected the .gifs in a popup window.
   Pinned by test/fixtures/strayend.asl.

2. The Quest 2.x "name@room" reference.  This is a 2.x game all the way through:
   `give <X>` adds to the item list without moving the world object, so every
   pickup is a pair, `give <steps>` followed by `hideobject
   <steps@#quest.currentroom#>`.  geas passed the whole "steps@kitchen" string
   through as an object name, matched nothing, and hid nothing, so everything
   you picked up went on being listed in the room you took it from.  The same
   form is how both characters are staged -- `showchar <Linda@lodger's room>`,
   `showchar <Haidee@street>` -- so neither of them ever appeared.  Below ASL
   281 SetAvailability splits at the "@" and matches the half after it against
   the object's ContainerRoom (V4Game.Part2.cs:2994-3068).  Pinned by
   test/fixtures/atroom.asl.

3. Not this game's, but found next door and needed by the same batch: GIVE's
   second word order (fixtures/givethe.asl).


Odds and ends

Every room in this game carries a `description` tag, most of them `description
do <proc>` dispatching on flags, which is how the landing manages four
different paragraphs and how the street manages to be a checkpoint.  Two of
those procedures move you: atticdes sets bd=at and goes straight back to the
landing, bathdes does the same while lb=y, so GO TO ATTIC and GO TO BATHROOM
before their time are answered by the landing's description with an extra line
tacked on.  Being sent elsewhere from inside a room description leaves the exit
line printed twice, which is why the transcript has two "You can go to your
bedroom, ..." lines for those turns.

`attic2` is both a room (the real attic, with the balls in it) and a procedure;
`goto <attic2>` at line 699 gets the room and `do <attic2>` at 782 gets the
procedure.  Neither of the second pair of attic branches can fire, though:
they switch on `ad`, which startscript sets to n and nothing ever sets to y.
`changelb` is defined and never called.  `checkbd2` reads `if is <#bd#; at>
then <attica2> else do <ldd2>` -- the `then` branch is missing its `do`, so the
one path through it that matters does nothing.

The dog shit is a four-way gag: treadchk picks tread1/2/3/4 by what is on your
feet (shoes, socks, both, neither), sets tshit=y, and every later visit to the
street prints `smell` instead.
