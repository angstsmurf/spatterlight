You are about to embark upon a mysterious and amazing journey through Tai & David's Amazing Maze. To your left is a place called the cafe, where you may go to interact with many items and people. The cafe is like a safe house, or home base.

The maze portion of this game is the portion that you will be in the majority of the time. What is your goal? Your goal is to find all 10 Magic Artifacts.

Good luck, and one more thing- this is REALLY important. If you would like to save, make sure you are not in the middle of the maze, because SAVING WILL REDIRECT YOU TO THE BEGINNING(The main room. So, enjoy, and talk to the gate master (the man by the entrance to the start of the maze).
