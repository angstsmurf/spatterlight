You awaken in a bed, not having any idea who you are, where you are, or why you might be there...

No, wait a minute.  You're pretty groggy when you first wake up in the morning.  After thinking about it briefly, you know exactly who you are, where you are, and what you're doing.  Your name is Pat Conroy, and you are staying in a hotel room at the Bayside Resort--although most people call it the Lazst Resort (either affectionately or disdainfully, and it can be hard to tell which) after its proprietor, Ray Lazst.

Every autumn, Ray hosts a murder-mystery weekend, where every guest who is at the hotel is competing to see who can solve the mystery first.  But these are no ordinary murder-mysteries.  Ray prides himself on his complicated, multi-stage puzzles that must be solved, and he is particularly renowned for his electronic and mechanical genius:  previous retreats have featured working secret passages, electronically-coded locks, and an assortment of other strange goings-on.  Such puzzles require intense preparation, and the Bayside Resort suffers as a result of it, but since Ray has long been independently wealthy, it doesn't matter to him much.  The murder-mystery weekend is only one of a few in the year when you can be sure the hotel is fully booked.  This is Ray's thirteenth year of running the game, and you have attended every year since the second one, except in 1995, when you had pneumonia and were in the hospital.

Ray serves as the puzzlemaster for the weekend, distributing clues and otherwise tending to the brainteasers.  He also gleefully serves as the murder victim, complete with grotesque stage makeup, relishing the screams from the guests when he is initially discovered.

It is Saturday morning at 7:45.  The game typically starts around 8, and you were told to expect to receive instructions in your room around then.
