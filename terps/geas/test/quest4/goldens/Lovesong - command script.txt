Love Song. (Mihalis "DarkAng3l" Georgostathis, 2001, QDK 1.0 Beta 3A, ASL 210)
- raw command script for geas_walkthrough_runner.
Game system: Quest 4.  Win marker: "*THE END*".

Thirty-one commands, no timers, no RNG and no prompts.  A teenage metalhead has
to write a song for Christine and play it at the school gig.

THE DREAM.  The game opens in a forest that turns out to be a dream, and it has
to be played out before the real day starts.  TAKE VINES, north to the chasm,
TIE VINES TO TREE (a room command, not an object action -- it makes a rope down
to the flower), TAKE FLOWER, then back south and west and north to the small
tree, where GIVE FLOWER TO CHRISTINE moves her to the waterfall.  Follow her
south and south, and UNDRESS: you jump into the lake and wake up in your bedroom
with your mother emptying a bucket over you.  There is no way to skip the dream
and nothing in it carries over.

THE SONG.  LOOK NOTE is the to-do list (write a song; rehearse in David's
garage).  TAKE BOX `showobject`s the playboy magazines that were `hidden` under
the records; TAKE PLAYBOY MAGAZINES drops a photo of Christine out of one and is
what makes you "very inspired"; TAKE ELECTRIC GUITAR arms you.  PLAY GUITAR then
writes the song -- procedure 36 is `if got <magazine> and is <#organ#;guitar>`
(lovesong.asl:539-542), so without the magazine the same command only prints
"After playing a few chords you quit."

THE GIG.  South three times, west and south twice to the garage, GIVE SONG TO
DAVID for the rehearsal, then back north twice, west to the school, north to the
cafe and west onto the stage -- whose description is gated on
`got <guitar> and here <David> and got <song>` (188).  PLAY SONG runs the gig and
`goto`s backstage; EAST into the shadows and TALK TO SHADOW is the win, a
`speak playerwin` on the shadow object (227).

TWO NAMING QUIRKS, WHICH TOGETHER MAKE THE SCRIPT LOOK INCONSISTENT

  * The game is ASL 210 and QDK 1.0 gave nothing an alias, so an object has to be
    named in full: TAKE PLAYBOY MAGAZINES and TAKE ELECTRIC GUITAR, never TAKE
    MAGAZINES or TAKE GUITAR.  Quest matches a word taken from the middle of a
    name only for ASL 391 and up (Disambiguate, V4Game.cs:4762-4772; see
    fixtures/abbrev.asl).
  * PLAY GUITAR, one line later, must use the *short* word -- because it is not
    an object lookup at all.  Every room defines `command <play #organ#>` (45,
    127, 189), and `#organ#` is a text placeholder compared as a string:
    `if is <#organ#;guitar>`.  PLAY ELECTRIC GUITAR fails that compare and does
    nothing.  (The guitar object is not even in the room by then: taking it hands
    you the *item* `guitar` and `moveobject`s the object to a junk room, 482-486.)

That item/object split is the ASL 210 world all through -- `items <guitar; song;
magazine; flower; vines>` in the game block is Quest 2's parallel item list, so
`got <guitar>` is an item flag and `electric guitar` is a separate object that
gets swept into `junk` the moment it is taken.  See fixtures/items210.asl.
