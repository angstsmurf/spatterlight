Naturally this made you very unpopular around town. Upon reaching the age of 18 your widowed mother decides to send you off to the wizards who live in the tower by the sea. The master wizards there find you to be an apt pupil with a great amount of power and you learn quickly.
You have been here almost a month now. The grand wizard flings open your door in haste one morning and tells you that a summoning has gone amiss and that he is magically hiding the entrance to your room for your own protection. He then says he must do the same for the other students.
You hear him shut your door and mumble an incantation. A few moments later you can hear a scream from somewhere down the hall.
Startled you jump out of bed!
