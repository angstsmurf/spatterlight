 Created using ALAN 2.6 
 
 You wake up in your home on an ordinary day. 
 
 Ordinary, you hope... 
 
 The last year and a half has been a nightmare. It all began with the death your daughter, Patricia. She was only ten years old when she died in a car accident. You were driving her home from school when you crashed into another car. You escaped unharmed. Patricia was not so lucky. 
 
 You began to act strangely after her death, having strange hallucinations and visions. You had a severe mental collapse and was committed to a mental institution for one year. 
 
 You have just been released and have gone back home to recuperate. 
 
 You feel the time at home will help set your mind at ease...

You are standing in your bedroom. An old dresser is next to your bed. Plaster is peeling from the walls of your room. Your closet is directly across from your bed and a mirror is located on the door. 
 
 An overhead lamp is hanging above your head. The window to your room is plastered shut, and there are bars outside the window.
