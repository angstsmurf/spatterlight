 Interactive Suspense 
 
 An introductory level adventure 
 
 Written by Michael Zerbo 
 Created Using ALAN 2.6 
 
 Your name is Charles Browning and you are the personal servant to Robert Harris, a rich businessman living in a suburb of London. Recently an odd occurrence had happened to you; one of the groundskeepers, Jim Devlin, approached you with a plan to kidnap one of Harris' daughters in exchange for a ransom. Now you have known Jim for quite a while, and he always plays practical jokes, so you assumed he was kidding. It has been on your mind though, and you can only hope that it was just a bad joke...

>  Alice takes a strawberry from you and thanks you. "They are my favorite,", she says in a sweet voice. You tell her she can have more if she enters the house. She gets up and follows you. 
 
 "Thank you Charles, I didn't know how to get her in," Mrs. Harris remarks. After several minutes the Harris' get ready for the concert. They leave on time, with plans to spend most of the evening out. 
 
 You check that the front door is unlocked and the children are in bed, before you go to your room for the night. You awake very early the next morning. The sun is just rising and you hurridly rise from your bed, hoping to start a great day. Your hopes are smashed as you look down at the floor. Alice is lying there, dead.
 
 You fall to the floor in horror. You try to make some sense of this, when you hear the maid outside your door. If she catches you with the body, you will be blamed for the murder. You must do something, and do it fast.
