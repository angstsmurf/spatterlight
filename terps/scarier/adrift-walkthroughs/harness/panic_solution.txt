# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# What this game found: the 3.9/3.8 immediate-restart fixup ate StartText
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
# Panic! leans on always-restarting one-turn events for its atmosphere -- the
# priest's cough, the stigmata opening, the wraith crossing the room.  All
# three are `RestartType=1` (restart immediately) with `Time1=Time2=1`, and
# two of the three carry a StartText and *no* LookText (`texts=S--` in
# scdump.cpp's EVENT line), which is what makes them decisive.
#
# Against the author's transcript, scarier printed each of them exactly ONCE
# and then fell silent for the rest of the game:
#
#     published   ours (before)   ours (after)
#       66             1              66     "The old priest's sudden rattled
#                                             cough is gathered by the heavy
#                                             air..."
#        9             1               9     "The palms of your hands begin
#                                             to ache..."
#       13             1              13     "Siezed by a sudden
#                                             uncontrollable shaking..."
#       21             1              24     "The translucent shimmer of a
#                                             wraith filters into the room..."
#
# The cause was evt_fixup_v390_v380_immediate_restart() in scevents.cpp, which
# for a pre-4.0 taf restarted such an event by hand -- state to RUNNING, clock
# to one-less-than-a-fresh-roll -- instead of calling evt_start_event().  The
# event therefore cycled correctly (SCR_TRACE_EVENTS shows a finish every
# turn, and its TaskAffected did keep firing, which is why nobody noticed)
# but its start actions, StartText included, ran only on the very first start.
# Fixed 2026-08-04: the fixup now calls evt_start_event() and then takes the
# one silent turn off the clock, rather than open-coding a silent start.  The
# length roll has to come from evt_start_event() alone -- rolling a second one
# in the fixup churns the RNG stream and breaks three combat walkthroughs.
#
# Corpus effect: 13 of the 202 rows gained text, every one of them a pure
# addition of an ambient or plot line that had been silently swallowed --
# troll's "Sid sips from his mug.", haunt's grandfather clock, wrecked's
# seagulls, spirits_flight's wind, tq3's rattlesnakes, colony's lightning,
# secret_of_lost_world's "It starts to rain." and "The volcano is erupting.",
# marooned's rescue ship coming over the horizon, and the whole paragraph in
# alices_restaurant where Obie takes your wallet and your belt at the station.
# No route broke and no win marker moved.  thetest, gateway and inverness --
# the games whose always-restarting one-turn events were probed live in
# run390 for RUNNER_TESTS_TODO.md §2 -- are byte-identical, so this does not
# disturb anything that was arbitrated against a real Runner.
#
# One residual divergence, left alone and recorded: the wraith fires 24 times
# for us against the transcript's 21.  The three extra are turns 44-46 (`u`,
# `x eyes`, `roll eyes`), which is the stretch spent up the rope on the
# statue; the published run suppresses the event there and we do not.  That is
# an event-visibility question (evt_can_see_event and where the player counts
# as being while climbing), not a restart question, and it wants a live probe.
