EPISODE 5: The Final Battle
The war between the humans and aliens comes to a climax, with a battle where
one of the two sides will be finally wiped out.

*****************

The traitor's work had almost undone all the efforts by the allies in the war
against the aliens. The knowledge gained from the betrayal had given away
many secrets, but the aliens still didn't understand the individuality of
humans, even psychically-enhanced humans such as yourself. The traitor had
not known every secret, and could not predict every reaction.

The modified cold suits are now even more powerful than when the traitor
last saw them. More capabilities have been added to the robotic armours.
There are three suits remaining. All piloted by teenagers, but you are all
psychically enhanced unlike the others. You are the oldest at sixteen, and
the leader of this select group. Your psychic abilities allow you to
communicate more closely with the other pilots, so there is more
co-ordination to every attack than there could be otherwise. The enemy is
even more co-ordinated though, so this advantage is not sufficient. The main
advantage is that you can psychically control the cold suit.
The cold suit's cockpit
You are leading the allied forces through a valley. On the display are two
modified cold suits in support, just behind you. More normal cold suits
provide further back-up, but they were not designed for this combat. The
constant snow here makes it difficult to judge the enemy's location, but you
expect that they are somewhere to the north. There is not much else in this
cockpit, as all you have to do is think a command for the suit to attempt to
carry it out.

A voice in your mind says, "You're in the lead, Marcus, so make your move."

>The modified cold suit moves to the north, at your command.
The cold suit's cockpit
Dots representing the enemy on the screen have surrounded your forces. The
aliens think that they have ambushed you, but you will show them that they
are instead the victims. A modified cold suit belonging to the enemy is also
on the display, which must be the traitor's suit. All the old weapons such as
the shoulder mounted machine guns, electrifying grappling hooks and the
diamond edged pickaxe are in both of your suits. However, your suit boasts a
new weapon, with a psychic disruptor. You can activate any of these weapons
with just a thought.

>You activate the grappling hooks. They fly out of your suit and latch on to
the traitor. Your aim has been improving with practice. Now you just have to
wait for your suit to charge up.

>You activate the psychic disruptor, hoping that your plan works. The
disruptor makes every enemy nearby and yourself stop moving on the display.
You cannot control your suit, but it has already been pre-programmed. The
traitor cannot remove the grappling hook with no controls and electricity is
soon sent through the cable. The traitor's cold suit is too well built to be
destroyed by the attack, but you can see on the display that it has been
damaged. What looks like a panel on one side has been knocked loose and
fallen off. You have created an entry through the powerful armour.

>You activate the machine guns and they begin spraying bullets wildly. The
traitor dodges the initial bullets, but several then hit the suit. Most do no
damage, but a couple hit the damaged section. The damage is worsened and the
suit staggers backwards. You continue firing, aiming more carefully, until
suddenly the traitor's cold suit erupts in flames, as you hit something
critical. The suit burns up, finally killing the traitor.

The momentum has been shifted by your actions. An even contest between the
two warring sides is soon turned into a rout. Careful use of the psychic
disruptor helps you to disable groups of enemies, allowing the other forces
to move in and hack away at the aliens. Eventually the aliens are defeated.
The allied forces have won and all of the surviving forces head back to the
allied base.

When you reach the base and exit your suit, you are suddenly thrown from your
body.
