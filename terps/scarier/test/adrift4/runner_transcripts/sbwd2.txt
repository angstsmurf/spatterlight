The Greenwood Docks
You are standing on a rock shingle facing out over a vast expanse of open ocean. You can catch the scent of fish mingled with a hint of salt as you stare out over wave after wave of water rolling its way to the rocky ledge at your feet. Straining your SpaceVision to its limits, you can make out the hazy forms of other islands dotting the horizon far, far out to sea. Not knowing how your SpaceFlight Powers would effect the atmosphere around you on this alien world, you don't dare to fly to them ~ your only hope is to find some sort of floatation device to get from island to island…

You can here the song of water gurgling along a streambed to the northwest.

> unfold raft
You flip the raft over and, with a mighty tug; you pull the tab causing the raft to slip open for use. You carefully slip it into the water where it bobs on the oceans surface obediently awaiting your commands.

> enter raft
Not wanting to take an unnecessary salty swim, you carefully slip down into the boat barely fitting into the tight seat.   
In The Raft
You are in the raft - this will be the only sorce of travel between about 4 different islands during the rest of the game.

All you need to do is enter your coordinates, and the raft will do the rest.

> turn dial to A1
You flip the dial to A1, and the raft begins to sail slowly away from the Docks. As you move out of the no wake zone, the raft picks up speed rapidly zooming across the oceans surface towards a small island to the north.
