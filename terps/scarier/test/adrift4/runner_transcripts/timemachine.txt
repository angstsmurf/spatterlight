The Time Machine
You are stood inside the Time Machine. Most of the screens are blank and cracked, and there is the faint smell of burning from the dials and switches. > enter 9217
You type the code. The Time Machine whirrs into action - clunking and grinding as it seems to build to a frenzy of noise. Suddenly, you feel your stomach drop down towards your feet - stars and planets whizz past your head. You black out . . . . . .

You wake with a start. The Time Machine has clearly moved again, but this does not look like the Professor's laboratory.
