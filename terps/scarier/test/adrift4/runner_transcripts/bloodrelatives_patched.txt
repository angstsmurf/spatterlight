> x henry's painting
Uncle Henry's painting is just a simple one: Henry smoking his pipe, smiling, humming a tune...
   Humming a tune? You can hear him humming!
   "Indeed you can, my dear," says Uncle Henry from the painting. "I have important news for you and felt this was as good a way as any of passing it onto you."
   "But what if I had never examined this painting?" you ask.
   Henry frowns. "Then you would have wound up dead. But you did examine the painting and so therefore you shall not die." He pauses a moment and amends that to, "or probably not. Or actually, you probably will still die but at least this way you'll have a more sporting chance. Anyway. Listen carefully. Go to my room and lift my bed. I've hidden something very important for you there."
   "Y1r room?" you say. "I never even knew you had a room here."    Uncle Henry sighs. "West from the top of the stairs. Honestly, child, I've never known anyone as unobservant as you. Go to my room and find the item I've left for you."
   "What is it?" yu ask and wait patiently for a response. Which you fail to get. When you look carefully at the painting again you see the reason for this. It's just a painting once more. Any hint of the magic that animated it is gone.
    "Really, Sophie," Father remarks. "Y1'll have to do something about this talking to inanimate objects problem of yours."

Your room

Your roomYou are in your room. If there were prizes given out for having the untidiest bedroom in the world, you'd win it in a second. Clothes are strewn everywhere, as are empty beer cans, books, posters, foreign coins of less value than the metal they are minted from, and lots of other items which don't really warrant a better description than 'lots of other items'. Amidst the carnage here is your bed and also a window although the location of either is a mystery to you right now. Exits lie south.  Also here is a gold coin.

> tidy room
You decide to tidy your room. Alas, exhaustion gets the better of you after six hours or so and you have to give up the whole idea as a bad job for another decade or two. On the plus side, however, you did find your bed.
