> use fan
Whoosh!  As you near the back of the fan, you find yourself swirling uncontrollably around, around, around... time seems to slow as you speed toward the whirring blades... and suddenly you're sucked in, tumbled through, then spit out on the other side, and the whirring noise cuts out.  You feel....  a little dizzy.  But quite refreshed, actually.  Maybe even a little stronger...

> use pencil

She waits for acknowledgement, then smiles and leaves after the child's nod.  The child, apparently "Sammy", walks through the door, and conveniently leaves it open wide enough for you to fit through comfortably.  Now if only you could be sure that door wouldn't shut after you go through it...
