Tremendous explosions beat the earth into sludge & spit up horrifying plumes of smoke, fire, and debris.  In pauses between certain flashes, there is no light.  At other times, feeble explosions in the air cast haunting, miscoloured glimpses of No Man's Land.

Corporal Butcher shouts, "Die, damn you, die!"

> wait
Time passes...


Death comes whistling down from the night skies over your trench, everywhere blowing new grave pits into the ground.

A shell explodes in a fireball nearby, throwing you flat onto your stomach & blasting away your perception that Smythe was once a single, solid, and living thing.  Shrapnel drums on your helmet.

Corporal Butcher shouts, "Why-- won't-- you-- die!?"

> wait
Time passes...


The seeming last of the salvo ceases... and a hush falls over the trench.

> wait
Time passes...


A minute, two, three of silence drags on in tense breaths until blare of the gas horn fills the trenches.

The noise stuns you & your mind blanks.  Where is your gas mask?  Panic sets in and all you can think is you don't have it, you don't have it.

Your gas mask is GONE and YOU ARE GOING TO DIE...
