PROLOGUE  
It is the Galactic Civil Year 3937, in a galaxy nowhere near Earth.  The league of solar systems known as the Third Galactic Union hasn't even been dreamed.  It's every planet and sentient being for itself as the Third Galaxy writhes in chaotic misery.

Luckily, you thrive on chaos.  You wouldn't be a man of your times if you didn't.  You are Greph Quik, and you have long made your living in shades of moral grey.  No job is too dirty for you, so long as you can clean the money.

Last month you finished hunting down and delivering the crew of the I.S.C. Dog's Head to a mob boss on A-48.  The merits you earned have all but spent themselves since then.  You wouldn't mind getting, for once, a fat paycheck that allows you to kick back for a few months, maybe even a year or two at a time.  These petty scores are for the birds.

Upon hearing hints of new developments between the warring planets Ghlarsk V and Zo, you raced over to their sector of the galaxy.  The score from a planetary government contract could be just what you're looking for...
