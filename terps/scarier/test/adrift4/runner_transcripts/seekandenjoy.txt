3) About half way through the track Knees up Mother Brown is played at a high frequency on a vibraphone (Vibes).

4) if you play the tune in a spectrograph ( www.sonicvisualiser.org/) there is a picture of a pig (a reference to DJ Ham). The pig appears at 3:31.

5) At the end of the track you can hear a number being dialed. At the end of the video the website http://www.dialabc.com/sound/detect/ is flashed up. If you upload a wav of the number being dialed to that site it will give you a number to ring. If you ring the number you will hear an excerpt of Billy Bunter of Greyfriars school.
