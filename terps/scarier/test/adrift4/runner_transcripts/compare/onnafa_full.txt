  scarier  Interaction with the characters in ONNAFA - of which there are a considerable amount - is necessary to complete the game. There are several characters who it is essential to have follow you, and quite a few others who have items you need. And then there are the ones who are just thrown in for good measure. Deciding which is which is all part of the fun. Conversation in ONNAFA is handled for the main part by typing "Talk to [somebody]" which will open up a set of dialogue options from which you can then pick what you want to say. For example: >talk to rancid "My name's Rancid. I'm your butler. Your poor, overworked, underpaid, underappreciated butler, that is." 1) "What's it like being a butler, Rancid?" 2) "Quit your complaining, you whining sod!" 3) "Yeah, yeah. Tell me something useful." 4) "I'm bored talking to you right now." Depending on your responses, you might get another set of dialogue options pop up, or the character might fly into a wild rage and attack you for insulting you. Needless to say, insulting a character is more likely to turn them hostile than being nice to them is. Of course, some characters are just plain hostile through and through and no matter how nice you are to them they won't respond with anything other than mindless violence (unfair? Hey, this is a text adventure! What do you expect?) The characters you meet during the course of your adventures will carry a wide variety of items. To get an item from a character just type "ask [character] about [item]". Of course, whether you get the item or whether they decide to keep it for themselves is just something you'll have to deal with. Another bad thing about being nobility is that you have to act as the nobility would. Note: this is how people believe the nobility should act (i.e. good and fair and full of knowledge and understanding for the common man) and not how the nobility really act (i.e. "I'm richer than everyone else so I matter and they don't" sort of thing). As such you have a reputation to maintain. Do enough evil deeds and you'll find it plummeting; do good deeds and it increases. A low reputation might see you being barred from Korbintown (a potentially fatal eventuality depending at what stage you're at). Check out your reputation at any time by typing "reputation" or "rep" for short. If you get stuck, try typing "help" or "hint". It might (or might not) provide you with a clue. Intro O.N.N.A.F.A.
