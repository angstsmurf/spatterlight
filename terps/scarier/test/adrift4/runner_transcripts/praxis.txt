In Your Office
A desk, a comfortable leather chair, and the usual sofa. A humble place, but thine own. How many souls have been cured within these four walls, you often wonder as you stare at the many framed academic degrees and other trophies that surround you here? You are tempted to while away the time before your patient's arrival by making a brief survey of the room's contents.
