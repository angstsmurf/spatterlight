Epilogue

The shuttle docks at your home colony-ship, A-48, but relief is still as far as your living chamber.  The colony seems abuzz about something; there are almost never this many people at the docking bay so late and never any so lit up.  

Has there been a parade?  A riot?

You can barely take two steps from the gangplank before the Sector Head waves you down.  

The Sector Head looks pale and keeps making quick wipes at his forehead.  You cannot discern whether his dilated pupils shine frantically or wildly.

He rambles, "While you were working... while you were working, we started to get some weird signals in from satellite number four at your station... messages from the one called Earth, but they don't make any sense... they're too... we can't tell... it's... 

"Come on," he says, grabbing the sleeve of your spacesuit, "you'd better have a look at this..."
