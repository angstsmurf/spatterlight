> turn cap clockwise

'Kath, d'you think the Commisson will ever send any inspectors down here?' Bels asks. 
'Down here?' I scoff 'They never go below top-side - no it would be the police, not the inspectors.' 
'Oh, that's great,' Gripper says staring at his screen morosely. 

Gripper belches long and loud. Simultaneously Bels and I grimace to ourselves.

> turn dial

Bels sighs loudly and thwacks some papers on one of the desks.

> examine screen

'Bels, Gripper, get your arses over here.'

'What the hell?' says Gripper.

'Oh my God!' says Bels.

'This is just what we've been looking for.' I say.
