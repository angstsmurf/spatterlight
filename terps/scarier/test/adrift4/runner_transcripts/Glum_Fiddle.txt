A sign in front of the cottage has two boards on it, one pointing at the cottage saying 'Gait-Huose', and the other pointing to the east toward the entrance to a large cave, saying 'Caive'. The signs were painted in a rough hand with black paint.

Cave entrance
At least 20 feet high, the entrance to the cave has a massive wrought iron gate that spans it's width and reaches far above your head. The interior of the cave is pitch black, in stark contrast to the bright day outside.

The tiny gatehouse is west, and giant's valley is east.  An Ogre sits gloomily, his eyes shining redly.  

The Ogre blinks, and the reddish light disappears for a moment.

Then he opens his eyes again and makes a sickening slobbery sound.

'Little person,' he rumbles. 'How are you to-day.' He doesn't ask it as a question, but says the words as if reading them off a prompt (as if Ogres can read).

'W-well, thank you.' you manage to stammer. 

'I live here. Welcome to my home. Mr Fiddle will see you soon. First you must listen to my poem. I have to finish it right before you can go on.'

'Oh, okay. So... you wrote it?' You try to sound enthusiastic as wafts of vile-smelling body odour assails you every time the thing moves around.

'No, of course not.' suddenly the Ogre seems to be speaking in his own, normal voice. 'If I had my way, I'd just rip your arms off and suck the blood off the ragged ends. But...' and here he's back to his recital. 'Mr Fiddle is bigger than me, and kindly lets me live here. Here's the poem.'

Pile of boulders
Glum Fiddle's valley is a large space between steep cliff faces that reach up to the peaks of the Bawaire Mountains. Here, near the cave entrance, is a mass of boulders, of all sizes, some deeply embedded in the ground, some piled up on top of each other. It would seem that all the boulders in the valley have been collected and dumped here. In a clear patch of earth, a large ring of stones encloses a smoldering fire.

There's a pigsty to the north, a briar patch to the east, a patch of forest to the southeast, and a swamp to the south. The cave entrance is to the west.  

The giant is loitering around, examining his domain.  

As you and Glum face each other over the pile of boulders, he gives a mild grin and flourishes an arm. 'Please, sit in my parlor!' He exclaims, loudly enough to be heard as far as the great city. You try to find a boulder that isn't too sharp, but it's nearly impossible. Glum continues to stand.
