Park
Here is a small but most unusual park, perhaps a hundred yards wide. The entire effect is one of mind-reeling surrealism. CLOCKS are draped over marble slabs like pancakes, and all of them read 6:46. A giant fried EGG the size of a circus tent is propped up at each end on two tall poles. At the other end of the park, to the north, the stairs to the citadel await you.  A large glass EYEBALL the size of a volkswagen is suspended from a dead tree by a string no thicker than a human hair.    You can move north, south, southeast and southwest.
