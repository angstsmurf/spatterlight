> n
As you stagger up the path towards No. 5 the front door slowly creaks open.  You pause on the threshold.  The world seems to be coming to an end behind you and yet still you hesitate.  Cradled in your arms Cathy moans and reaches out a hand towards the darkness framed by the open door.  With no other means of escape from the disintegrating road in sight you run headlong into the house.  

The door slams shut and the floor pitches forward causing you to drop Cathy.  You try to regain your balance but the hallway tilts further until you find yourself lying on the ceiling.  As the hall shakes and rocks crazily it feels as if you are in free fall and you choke back rising bile as motion sickness causes you to retch miserably.

The hallway.  You stand upon the ceiling as the house sways and rocks from side to side.   Above your head is a long uncarpeted passage ending in rickety looking stairs.  A small wooden stool stands seemingly glued to the floor.  There are doors to the east and west.  The front door is behind you.

Yours and Cathys.  The sound of your screams shatter the vision.
