Bedroom
Our bedroom - supposedly the room of passion, or at least of contented companionship, for married couples. The bed is dishevelled - I obviously slept in here at some point in the past few days, although I can't remember doing so. Satin sheets and that damned expensive silk bedspread. Flaunting all the rules, I had not bothered to place it carefully at the foot of the bed before going to sleep. 

The room is small, and, like the living room, decorated with cream carpet and white walls. A pine dresser faces the bed, at it's foot, and a matching nightstand is next to the bed. The door to the bathroom, to the west, is ajar.

> make bed
I pull up the sheets, smoothing them and even folding the corners. Plump up pillows and draw shamefully exotic bedspread over the lot - done.

> look under bed
I lift up the bedspread, move the sheets aside, and finally the pillows. There's something here, something I want, I just can't quite remember what. Ah, that's it. Reaching under the far pillow, I pick up a letter.

> look behind dresser
For some strange reason, I decide to look behind the dresser. Why, I'll never know. Pushing the chest back from the wall, so I can see the floor down its length reveals a small photograph amid lint balls - it must have fallen down there ages ago. I gingerly pick it up.

> read letter
19/04
Cairo

Dear M (it says)

My ravishing darling, delight to me. I can't think of you without feeling so lost. I want to see you now - this very day, this very hour! Tell me you are coming back soon.

My work is going well, although the oil rigs are soon to be closed, and I am to move to Brazil for the new company. Perhaps you have a trip planned to Brazil soon? Let me know, then I have something to look forward to, dearest.

I haven't heard from you for 2 weeks now - I worry and wonder what you are doing all this time.

I'm sorry if my english isn't good today - you must teach me how to speak better, so I can express myself to you in passionate words.

Your only lover,

C

Somehow, this letter does not surprise me, and somehow it does not make me angry. This 'C' is Marta's only lover, I now realise. At least, there's no competition from me. I want to feel angry though - I want to feel wounded so I can have some reason to pity myself, instead of just loathing myself.
