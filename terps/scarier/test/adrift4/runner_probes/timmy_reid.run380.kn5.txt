\pard\sl-240\slmult1 \cf1\lang1033\fs28\f0 The Timmy Reid Adventure (The Jonny Reid Adventure - Part II)\fs24 \par
\cf2 All that you remember is looking back at Jon as you scramble to hold on...  You begin to plunge into the swirling image of the cottage.  Your ears start to pop and you hear what sounds like ocean surf.  Your face is brushed with a salty mist.  You fall for what seems an eternity...\par
\par
Suddenly, all is bright as your limp carcass slams onto the hot pavement.  The exquisite pain confirms that you aren't dead!  Just before your world goes black, you collect the strength to raise your head from the searing blacktop.  Your eyes roll up into their sockets and you think you see the cottage at Old Orchard Beach.  Darkness...\par
\par
You took a nasty fall, but with some effort you manage to pick yourself up and brush off.  You unconsciously start picking small pebbles from your cheek and shoulder and discover that your shirt is stained with blood.  The sun beats down on your head and neck causing you to start sweating.  Your ears suddenly pick up a faint sound like a mosquito's buzz.  The sound gets increasingly louder and you realize that it's not a mosquito but a scream and it's coming from above you.  You shift your feet and look up just in time to see a body hurling downwardly toward you.  Instinctively, you raise your arms to protect yourself and crumple to the ground after being slammed by the body of a small boy.  You sit up stunned and turn toward the boy.  You both scream at the top of your lungs as you realize that the boy is your brother, Jon.\par
\par
So begins "The Timmy Reid Adventure"\par

There is a yellow bug light above the kitchen window and a clothes line running from a large silver colored pulley up to the telephone pole near the road.\par
