The Lounge
You are in Marks lovely lounge, well no it's actually horrible.  It seems to be the morning but you can only just tell that through the dirty windows. A pair of limp unhappy looking curtains sit either side of the window.   In the middle of the room stands a lopsided coffee table.  To the west you can see what looks like the hall.  The room has the usual things a bachelors place has namely an uncomfortable settee, a battered old armchair, narrow screen television and what looks like a dvd player.  John is currently slumped in the armchair.  

"I'd better go and find Mark, to ask him about my keys," you think to yourself.
