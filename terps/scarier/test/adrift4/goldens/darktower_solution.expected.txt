The Dark Tower

You were working late one night at your new job at A-Z Graphics, a graphic
design company.  Your boss just got a new client, and was trying to impress
him by beating the deadline for the project.  That of course, meant that you
were the one who had to work overtime to do it.  It was around 8pm when you
