The First to Arise: Alone with a Pug
It was a long restless night. Greg was busy preparing for today's journey,
and Selana was as well. You woke up late to find the house sounding
completely abandoned. Where is everybody? Where did they go?

As you haul yourself out of bed, you wonder what's going to happen to you
today.

In your bedroom
This is your bedroom. It's a fairly nice little room, cramped but homey. In
true Alex Thassylian style, the walls are almost bare and scarcely any of
your personal effects are out on display. A window in the north wall admits
midmorning sunlight from outside, and a door to the east opens onto the
second floor landing.  Your bed is still rumpled from a restless night,
sheets tangled and pillows astray.

coverlet. Checking under the bed, you notice an old lockpick lying there that

In the kitchen
The kitchen. You don't often come in here -- to you, this is really just the
place from which the servants magically produce food. It's fairly standard,
as kitchens go. There's a magically-activated stove, a workbench for
preparing food, a cupboard built into the wall, and a water barrel. The
sitting room is through the door to the south, and the scullery is to the
east.

> light stove
You speak the simple magic word that turns the stove on. The hotplates start
to heat up, giving off a pleasant warmth.

> open cupboard

> take bacon

> fry bacon
You place the frying pan on the stovetop, giving it time to heat up, then
carefully slip the rashers of bacon in. The bacon fat sizzles and spatters.
Gingerly, you reach in and turn the rashers over. There, they're done. You
fish the bacon out of the pan and turn off the stove.  Flash trots in from
the south.
Her ears suddenly prick forward, and she sniffs the air intently. Uncannily,
she zooms right in on you and plonks herself on the ground right in your
path, gazing up at you soulfully. I love you, Alex, she says, hopefully.

> feed flash
Flash holds very, very still as you dangle a rasher of bacon over her head.
Her eyes follow it back and forth, making you feel like a hypnotist in a
street show. Then she springs up and neatly snatches it from your hand. The
other two rashers aren't far behind. Flash blinks, looking noticeably more
alert now that she's had breakfast. Oh yes, now I remember! Greg said that he
left the front door key on the nightstand in his room!  Flash runs out to the
south.

> fill teapot
You dip the little teapot into the water barrel, filling it to the brim.

> boil water
You put the teapot on the stove, and wait. After a few minutes, steam starts
to rise from the teapot's spout, and you can hear the water starting to
bubble inside. And then you jump, startled, as the teapot squeaks, "Hey, it's
getting hot on this stove! Tip me over! Pour me out!" You hastily grab the
teapot and carry it into the sitting room, where you plonk it down on the
coffee table.

> search nightstand
Remembering what Flash said, you hesitantly run the tips of your fingers

> sweep floor

In the spare room
This is the spare room, once Selana's convalescent room, and then her

> pick lock on chest
You take the lockpick from your pocket and crouch down next to the chest.

Hmm. Anyone even slightly less perceptive than you might have missed it, but

As you do, you remember Danthil's powers -- the ability to heal, to make

Patting yourself down, you make sure that you've got everything you need.
Your purse is in your pocket, and Greg has left your pack on the front step.
Pulling the front door closed behind you (and slamming it quite
vindictively), you shrug your pack on, double check that Danthil is in your
belt pouch, and head off down the street to your next adventure.
