House of the Damned
Terror grips your mind. All you can think of is to run, run... The wolves
behind you are getting closer, you are certain of that. You can hear their
delighted howls as if they are just behind you. You almost slip on the wet
grass. It had been raining all day, but now at night it has increased to a
torrent.
   You curse the engine failure in your car that forced you to come out and
look for help. It was already darkening when you left the safety of your
vehicle, and now it must surely be close to midnight. The wolves began
chasing you at eleven, and for nearly an hour you have been running. Soon,
surely, you will collapse...
   Hope! Through gaps in the trees you catch sight of a window, and in the
gloom, can just make out the shape of a house. With a superhuman effort you
reach the doorway, throwing it open, and once inside lean against it,
panting. You hear the wolves bay outside, then the slow patter of paws as
they slink away, defeated. You feel you have won.
   If you had known what would befall you in that house of the damned, you'd
have run back outside and prayed for the wolves to come back...
Thunder rumbles ominously.
