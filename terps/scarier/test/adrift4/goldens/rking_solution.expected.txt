lll Shelf lll

A shelf you are on. And a dusty shelf at that. Whoever cleans this place
should be hung (and, knowing Verenor, probably has been). The shelf extends
to the east and the west but as it appears to be the same in either
direction, there's precious little point in moving any further along it.

The rest of the room looks blurry from here (no doubt your lack of body isn't
helping your visual capabilities any), though you fancy you can make out a
desk in the distance.

The edge of the shelf looms to the south.

> roll
No sooner has the thought entered your

(no, not your mind because you don't have one. But your)

consciousness, you find yourself rolling. Unfortunately, while you can make
yourself roll with just a moment's thought, directing that rolling is
somewhat more problematic. You don't have feet. You don't have hands. You
don't even have anything to control direction or slow you down. All you have
is-

The edge of the shelf.

Getting closer.

And closer.

And-

And then you're falling.

Under other circumstances, you might be winded. But one of the advantages of
having no wind is that you escape this little bit of unpleasantness. You're
also not even hurt.

You guess that's two positives to lacking a body but you're sure the
disadvantages add up to a lot more.

You peer around. Yes, you're on a desk alright. Right at the very eastern
end. Over to the west there looms the massively foreboding structure of the
bell which High Chancellor Verenor mentioned. Arrayed around it are what
appear to be a selection of quills and some paperwork.

It occurs to you, as you lie there gathering your... whatever passes for
breath in the world of disembodied eyeballs... that rolling would be your
best bet for getting around. [Footnote 1]
