Blast
(a very rushed game) by Richard Otter

[Written for the Ectocomp 2008]

Your day starts with a headless receptionist and then just goes from bad to
worse; don't you just hate Monday mornings.

Reception
A semi-circular reception desk sits just to one side of the room. To the
north is an office and east leads to the toilets. To one side of the room is
a sad looking plant sitting in a plastic pot. The extensive waiting
facilities seem to consist of one plastic seat.  The entrance to the building
is to the south. A trial of blood leads north.

> x desk
The reception desk is semi-circular and is made of some type of wood,
probably oak.  A shotgun is on the reception desk.

> get shotgun
You take the shotgun from the reception desk.

> n
You move north.

Office
A plain office with very little furniture consisting only of a green desk.
The reception is south, Filing to north and the Kitchen east. A trial of
blood leads south.

> n
You move north.

Filing
In front of you is a counter which basically cuts the room into two halves.
Behind the counter are row after row of filing cabinets. Offices are to the
north and south. The Dining room is east. A trial of blood leads north.
A demon is snarling at you.
The demon charges forward and bites you in the right hand. You lose 5 health
points.
The demon charges forward and bites you in the head. You lose 5 health
points.

> kill demon with gun
You pull the trigger and feel the shotgun recoil. The demon loses 5 health
points.

> n
You move north.

Dining Room
Usually it can be one of the busiest places in any organisation; here though
it is strangely quiet. The room is arranged with bench like seats either side
of several long tables. To the south are the kitchens and west is Filing. A
trial of blood leads west.  Demon arrives from the west.
The demon charges forward and bites you in the right hand. You lose 5 health
points.
The demon charges forward and bites you in the right leg. You lose 5 health

Kitchen
After a brief look around you have the impression that no actual cooking
takes place here, as you can see no cooking facilities. The only equipment
seems to be a fridge and a worktop fixed to the wall.  So it is likely that
it is just a food preparation area rather than a traditional kitchen. To the
north is the dining room and west is an office.  A trial of blood leads
north.  Demon arrives from the north.
The demon charges forward and bites you in the right hand. You lose 5 health
points.
The demon charges forward and bites you in the left leg. You lose 5 health
points.

> x worktop
The metal worktop looks very well used as it is criss-crossed with various
scratches.  A plasma torch is on the worktop.
