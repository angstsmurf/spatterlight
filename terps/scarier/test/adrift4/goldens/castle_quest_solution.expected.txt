Castle Quest
by Andrew Cornish

You've heard rumours that great treasure is hidden inside Castle Oggleboggle,
and you intend to find it. The castle is full of deadly traps, but it's worth
the risk for the incredible treasure that awaits you. Wearing a sweatshirt
and a pair of trousers, you march up to the castle and prepare to begin your
quest.
