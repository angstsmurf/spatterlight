The Road Leads to Nowhere

Leaves crush underfoot as you move through the woods. You've volunteered to
gather wood for the campfire as a show of goodwill toward your new friends
Jack, Travis, Susan, and Kelly. You originally planned to camp out here as a
getaway from the city life, but you met those four college kids and they've
been nothing but kind to you. They're really fun to hang around; you wish
there were people like this when you were in college.

You look around the woods, searching for suitable firewood.
