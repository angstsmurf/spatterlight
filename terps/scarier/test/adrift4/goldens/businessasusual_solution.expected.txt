Business As Usual
by Abbi Park

"Ugh, what nimwits.  Dimwits.  Scoundrels.  Nitwits," you mutter.  Yes, you
are quite fond of talking to yourself, especially about the people who visit
the little... er... miniscule... place you work, which is something like a
museum, except  that you only have four, completely unrelated, items.  And
thus you refer to it as the place.

It's not that the visitors steal the items.  That would not be nearly as
annoying, because they would be taken once, and that would be it.  No.
Instead, your visitors actually move the items all over the place, randomly,
throughout the day.  Maybe some of them are trying to be helpful, but you
really wish they wouldn't.

Basically, it's your job to make sure the four "Grand Items" (you take this
opportunity to remind yourself not to worry, not to worry, you didn't come up
with that title for them... you're not that corny, not to worry...) are back
in place by the time your boss shows up at night.

If the object aren't back, well...  that's not something you like to think
about.  You will succeed.
