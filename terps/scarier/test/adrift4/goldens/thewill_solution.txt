# (+10) and `d` down the revealed stairway (+10) ends the game with the
# WinText. Every scoring task in the data fires, 155 in all against the game's
# declared maximum of 150 (27 tasks at 5 plus two at 10), so the summary reads
# "You scored 155 out of the maximum 150." / "You finished -5 points short."
# -- the Runner prints a negative shortfall the same way. The blank line after
# this header answers the intro's "Press any key when ready to play."
#
# Seen objects: things listed only through %in_X%/%on_X% room text answer
# "What do you want to take?" until the holder is examined -- `x cabinet`
# (crowbar), `x safe` (mug), `x table` (lamp), `x grate` (carving),
# `x computer` (crystal disk); the wooden pedestal after the sculpture breaks
# needs a `look`. The pocket watch comes straight out after `open clock`.
#
# Order constraints:
# - Charge the flashlight early: `level cabinet with matchbook` in the shack,
#   `open drawer`, `get matchbook` (it comes back and the drawer stays open),
#   `put flashlight in charger`. The Cellar is dark; the flashlight is lit in
#   the Kitchen and then NEVER switched off. Once `unlight flashlight` has run,
#   an event clears the "light flashlight" task at the end of every turn, and
#   the Foot of Hill's east exit is gated on that task.
# - The carving is taken while the umbrella is still hooked on the steam pipe.
# - `feed puppy steak` at the doghouse, then `get dogbone`.
# - The shovel appears in the Toolshed after `push button` in the Study;
#   `dig soil with shovel` in the Vegetable Garden, `open bag` leaves the
#   rubberband in hand for `wrap band around valve` / `turn valve with wrench`
#   at the Courtyard fountain (pearl).
# - Chasm: carry the lit flashlight east from the Foot of Hill, take the ring,
#   `drop flashlight` (aims it across the chasm), go back round through the
#   Toolshed hole with the oil lamp lit (a lit flashlight burns out going down
#   the hole). At the Narrow Squeeze `drop all` (the crack needs empty hands),
#   `n`, take the nugget, `throw nugget` across, `s`, `get all`, `d` slides
#   back to the Foot of Hill; put the lamp out before `e` (a lit lamp or match
#   ignites the gas), collect nugget and flashlight.
# - River: `open umbrella` at the Cliff drifts down to River's Edge; swim
#   w/s/s/e to the lily and back, `climb up` the vine.
# - "insert" is a game synonym for "put", so the boot disk goes in with
#   `put disk in computer` ("insert disk" becomes "put disk" and matches
#   nothing).
