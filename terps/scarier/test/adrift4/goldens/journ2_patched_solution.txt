# WHY TWO SWITCHES.  They fix two different things and neither fixes the other:
#
#  * SCR_ASSUME_REPEATS=1 gets you out of the Lair.  Task 3 `#6 creature looks`
#    is a one-shot whose last pattern is a bare `*`, and pre-4.0's spent-task
#    claim (run_spent_task_390(), run390 checktask 44B4DD) then answers every
#    later command in that room with "You have already done that." -- the real
#    Runner's own ceiling, 5/90, and Scarier's since 2026-09-13.  No data edit
#    repairs it: tasks 4 and 5, the Creature's one-shot card handover, claim all
#    twenty direction words on exactly the same terms, so neutering task 3 only
#    moves the brick one task down.  The assist is the honest way past it.
#  * SCR_ASSUME_PATCHES=1 gives the female player her +10 in Rage.  Task 24
#    `#12 release pressure (f)` is `where=0` (runnable nowhere) and its command
#    list holds only the author's internal label, and task 22 -- the "you
#    haven't cleared the debris yet" refusal, three slots lower and carrying no
#    restrictions at all -- steals every phrasing of the valve from both twins.
#    PATCH_JOURN2 in sctafpar.cpp puts task 24 in room 9 with the four valve
#    patterns task 25 already has, and gates task 22 on the debris still being
#    there.  Full reasoning is in the comment above the entry.
#
# WHAT THE FEMALE CAREER IS WORTH.  The same 30 either way, which is the point
# worth being clear about: unpatched she can reach Terror instead and light the
# torch (T75, +10) but comes home with nothing, because T75 drops no card where
# the male T74 drops the King of Spades and `EXIT room=20 N gateTask=74` gates
# the Gnarled Woods' only way back on the male task.  Patched she comes to Rage
# instead and banks
#
#   T69  +5   `down` the well, once the rope is tied and dropped
#   T11  +10  `climb` out of Despaire, once `dig` has cut a foothold
#   T12  +5   `chop brush with shovel` at the edge of the pit
#   T24  +10  `open valve`, the female release of the pressure
#
# with three of the game's eight cards in hand -- the Queen of Hearts, the
# Queen of Clubs and the Queen of Diamonds.  The two trips are mutually
# exclusive: Rage is entered by raft from the Reservoir and left only through
# `EXIT room=9 N -> dest=7 gateTask=25 wantDone=1`, which names the MALE twin,
# so the last `n` below is refused with "You can't go in any direction!" and
# she ends in the room where her points are.  Rage goes last for that reason.
#
# FOUR THINGS THE FEMALE HALF DOES GET RIGHT, all of them twins where the
# lower-indexed task is hers, so forward first-match hands her the right one:
#  * T7 (`* dig *`, Queen of Clubs) before the male T8;
#  * T10's climb is restricted on the MALE dig and fails silently, so `climb`
#    falls through to T11, her +10, one slot later;
#  * T4 hands her the Queen of Hearts in the Lair where T5 hands his King;
#  * T24 sits before T25, so once it can run at all it wins the valve.
#  Her +10 also asks for the longer chain -- clear the debris, close the valve,
#  mount the fittings, then open it -- which is the sequence the author's own
#  hint text describes and which the male T25 does not require.
#
