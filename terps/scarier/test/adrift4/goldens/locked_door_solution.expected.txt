KF Mini-Comp 2001 Entry - Locked Door with Water Trap
"What the heck," you hear  yourself saying.  "Sure, I'll go SCUBA diving with
you guys!"  Though you only know the basics of SCUBA, you are up to the
challenge.  You've been waiting for a chance to show off your SCUBA skills
for some time now.

"Great," Steve exclaimed.  "This will be a blast, I promise!"  Steve is that
kind of guy, always excited and always ready to try something new.  His
excitement is usually contagious.  When Steve is around, you are more likely
to do something that seems a little odd.

Sarah smiles, excited as well.  "This will be fun!  SCUBA is great, plus
you'll get in some extra practice!," she says to you.  Sarah is usually quiet
and contained, like you, except when it comes to water sports.  Then Sarah
goes nuts.  Whatever the sport, as long as it involves water, is the greatest
sport in Sarah' opinion.

The three of you drive down to the beach and get suited up.  You strap the BD
Vest to the tank, followed by the regulators, air gauge, and vest inflator
line.  You gas up the lines slowly, then check your air pressure.  "Just
enough for a short dive," you think as you look at the air guage.  You look
up to see Steve and Sarah hauling their gear into the water.  You quickly
finish setting up your tank and follow them out.

You put your gear on as Steve lays out the plan.  "We'll dive kind of shallow
at first, then move a little bit deeper towards the rock tunnels.  Then, if
they look good, we'll go through a couple of the tunnels, but only if they
offer an easy way out.  Then we'll come up after 40 minutes or 500 psi of air
left."  Steve gives the okay signal, while Sarah and you return the same
sign.  Steve puts on his mask and leads the way down.

Press any key to continue.

The begins as a fair standard for the diving in the area.  You see some of
the local fish populus, along with a rare one or two.  You keep track of the
fish you've seen for your dive log, which you'll fill in later.  As the dive
progresses, Steve leads you deeper towards the rock tunnels.  You know
tunnels can be dangerous, but are confident that all of you will only go
through the safest ones.

Steve points to a tunnel, and leads the way in.  Sarah follows after him
slowly, entering after his fins had made it in.  You follow after Sarah,
watching as she follows Steve.  You emerge safely, feeling more confident.

Press any key to continue.

The dive is running short on time, and you check your air pressure.   You
have enough to make it through one more tunnel.  Not to be outdone by Steve,
you decide to lead the way.  You pick a tunnel and point it out.  Steve and
Sarah give you the okay, and start to follow.

You race into the tunnel, turning carefully.  The tunnel suddenly turns up,
and you follow it.  "This tunnel seems too long," you think, starting to
panic.  You start to breath faster.  You check your air supply.  Only 300
psi!  You kick a little faster, then break the surface!  You found air, but
where?

the east is a rectangular doorway.  Projecting from the western wall, set
over the pool is large steel pipe.  To the east is a rectangular doorway,
that is carved out of the soft rock.

There is a heavy wooden table set next to the doorway.

Ladderway
This room is dominated by a large iron door, which has opened into this room.
 In front of the door is a metal grate that acts as a drain.  To the south is
another large room.  There is a steel ladder here, as well, that leads up.

>You climb the steel ladder, reaching a metal grate.  You push the grate out

You look around and see the sandy beach to the west.  There is a lot of
commotion there, as you see an ambulance and police car, both with lights
flashing.  Steve and Sarah are both there, talking to the police.

You run towards the beach, reaching the warm sand.  Steve looks up and sees
you.  "Look," he says to Sarah with a grin on his face.  "Where have you
been," he asks.  "We followed you into that last tunnel, but when we got out,
you were nowhere to be found!"

Sarah knodded as Steve spoke.  Then she says, "We thought you might have come
up, so we came back to the beach.  When you weren't around, we called the
police department.  We're glad you are okay!"

You just smile.  "See if I ever dive with you two again," you joke.  A
paramedic checks your vital signs, then says that you can go home, but get
some rest.  You agree, pack up your stuff and get a ride with Steve and
Sarah.
