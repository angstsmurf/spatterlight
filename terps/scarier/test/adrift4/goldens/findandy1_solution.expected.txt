Find Andy Part 1 (Little Britain)

A fun text adventure trribute
to the classic TV series of Little Britain.
By Ian Thomson

Lou is in a kerfuffle!

Help him find Andy!

Bathroom

There are metal handles attatched to the walls to help Andy use the toilet
safely.
Also here is a Toilet and a carer called Lou.  You can only move west.

> use toilet
As well as the HORROR of the jobby, you also saw a Fatfighters Membership
card bob up and down then flush away !!

> w
You move west.

Hotel Reception

Ray is here.  You can move east and west.

> ask Ray about Andy
answer his riddle by typing "say _ _ _ _ _ " What is he?

> say Jelly
Listen to his directions! He has also given you something!

> w
You move west.

The Park

It has been vandalised recently.  Also here is The Hanging Tree, a person
called Anne and a Park.  You can move east and south.

> s
You move south.

Fete

The Jam tasting tent looks interesting! Ye Old well is here.  Also here is a
Park.  You can move north, east, south, west and down.

> w

Jam Tasting Tent

can only move east.

> give cake to maggy

> e

Fete

> s

Field

> n

Fete

The Jam tasting tent looks interesting! Ye Old well is here.  Also here is a

> d

Bottom Of A Well

Fete

The Jam tasting tent looks interesting! Ye Old well is here.  Also here is a

> e

Community Centre

> use card

> e

Fat Fighters

The class is empty apart from Marjorie who is looking frustrated.  Marjorie
