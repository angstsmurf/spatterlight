Life
In this life sim you must get a job, get a girl/guy and get rich.
Typing 'prepare meal' while in the kitchen will make the player prepare a
meal and then eat it.
Typing 'clean dishes' or 'clean * dishes will make the player clean the
dishes.
Typing 'sleep' or 'go to sleep' while in your bedroom will make you sleep.
How long depends on how sleepy the player character is.
In this game, if you want to enter a house or a shop or something like that,
you will have to type 'in' or 'go in'. In some other games you would
sometimes just type east, west, north or south if you wanted to go into a
house. Just clearing things up for you before you play the game.
If at any time during the game you need help, you can just type 'help' and
you will be given an option of what kind of help you need.

All I can say now is enjoy the game.
