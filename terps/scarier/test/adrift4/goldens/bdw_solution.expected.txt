Brain Dead Weekend
DON'T PANIC!

One must not become confused and think this is a story of Arthur Dent, or
Zaphod Beeblebrox (although through an amazing coincidence no matter where in
the known universe and even the unknown regions, everyone has some connection
by six links of separation to the former President of the Galaxy), or
Trillian (who is the wrong gender for this story), or even Ford Prefect.
Rather it is of one humanoid by the name of Probert Hardfard.  What is
startling is the high number of aliens who look human.  Despite the obvious
alien forms such as the Vogans, G'Gugvuntt, and Vl'Hurg, a large percentage
of life forms have the physical appearance of a human, although the internal
organs may be radically different.  Such is the case of the D'dorgamol.  Here
is a species whose brains and body may separate and often do.  For most life
forms the brain has the judgment to differentiate between right and wrong,
good and bad, and to override the body's basic urges.  The D'dorgamol found
this very inconvenient and decided to let the body have a vacation from the
brain from time to time allowing the body to tell the brain to bugger off
while it has a good time.

The brain of Probert Hardfard has decided it has been without a body long
enough and is urgently trying to reunite itself.
