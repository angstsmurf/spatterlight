> 1

Sophie's Room
This is your room, a merry hodge-podge of bright colours and "enthusiastic"
vibes. Yep, your mother decorated it for you and if there's one thing mothers
don't know it's how to decorate their daughters' rooms. Squinting through all
this uncontrolled "joy" and "happiness", you catch sight of your bed -
complete with a wonderfully vivid quilt - your desk, a window which looks out
over some not very interesting fields and an overflowing bin. There used to
be a television in here somewhere but it seems to have been overwhelmed by
the rest of your "happy" decorations.
   Exits lie: east (to the upstairs hallway).

> e
You move east.

Upstairs Hallway
The upstairs hallway winds around the upper floor of your house and is one of
those places you've been through several times every day of your life without
ever really noticing. Not that there is a lot to notice anyway: wallpaper
which looks like it was put in place by someone with a severe sight problem,
carpet that's coming undone near the walls, and a light fitting overhead that
swings eerily from side to side in a way you imagine most haunted house
designers would kill for. The sound of the tv - a constant that's always
there in your house - drifts from below.
   Exits lie: west (to your room), northwest (to Carla's room), southwest (to
your parents' room), north (to the bathroom) and down (to the downstairs
hallway).

The bathroom door comes flying open and out storms your sister, Carla,
looking less than pleased.
   "Outta the way, brat!" she snaps. "I haven't got time for you now."
   "It's my birthday," you say.
   To which Carla's reply is, "I didn't give a damn last year and I sure
don't this year" before she heads into her room and slams the door shut.
   Typical Carla really.

Downstairs Hallway
The downstairs hallway isn't really a hallway at all, just a wide corridor
with the coatrack on one side and a gaudy mirror Mother bought from a jumble
sale a few years ago. It's not as hideous as the chest of drawers it hangs
over but it's a pretty close thing. The "hallway" ends a short distance ahead
at a front door that could certainly do with a few licks of paint.
   Exits lie: east (to the kitchen) and south (to the dining room).
