Stepping carefully through the door, you walk slowly to the centre of the
room. As you reach the middle, the door behind swings silently closed. You
are only aware that it is no longer open when a key turning in the lock can
be heard.

The Room
The room is about 10m square and seems to be an empty space. For a place with
no windows and no visible light source it is strange that the room is lit by
an odd bluish glow.  The only furniture is an old wooden table in the centre
of the space; the table has a box on it.  On the wall to your left is an old
oil painting. The only visible exit is south, through the closed door.

This time you hear a click not from the box, but from behind.  As you turn to
look at the door, you just catch the sight of it swinging open. Wasting no
time, you sprint for the opening.

Running through the open door a voice from somewhere in the room behind you
shouts, "Get out and stay out!"  The door slams shut.  You are free!

[Press ENTER to continue]

You want to run, need to run, but seem unable to do so. It feels like you can
only crawl. You have no memory of the journey home, only a half thought of it
taking forever.  Staggering through your front door you manage to get
upstairs and crash into your bed.

Afterwards you are never sure if anything about the house, the corridor and
the room was real or just some terrible nightmare. Unlike other dreams this
one never fades from memory.

It seems vital now that you avoid Sycamore Street.  If you should find
yourself anywhere near the old house you cross the street and hurry passed,
your eyes elsewhere.

"Get out and stay out!"
