Coming round you recall that the plane, that you were at the controls of,
crashed through the canopy of trees and seems to be partially buried, nose
down at an angle, in the floor of the jungle that you were travelling over on
your way to Florida.The dry earth fills the space in the front of the
aircraft that used to be occupied by the glass of the windshield.

You gently feel around yourself, and check that all of your limbs appear to
be in working order. Once you confirm that you have survived the crash of
your light aircraft surprisingly unscathed, you survey your surroundings,
unbuckle and remove your seatbelt. Quickly retrieving your loaded handgun
from beneath your seat, you never know who or what you can meet in the
jungle, you try to decide what the best course of action would be.

The cockpit
Luckily the cockpit was robust enough to protect you from the worst ravages
of the crash. The emergency light above illuminates the controls in front of
your seat look that look dead. Near to the seat is the side door to the
aircraft and back, back through behind a curtain is the cargo area of the
plane.

A metal briefcase is slid down beside your seat.

> take briefcase
You take the briefcase.

> back
You move the curtain aside and go back to the cargo bay.

Cargo bay
A metal lined shell provides a good space for you to carry assorted goods
around. You can go forward, through the dividing curtain, to the  cockpit.

Beside the rear cargo door is a small control panel.  There is a bulky crate
lashed up against the side of the plane.

> open penknife
You open your penknife.

> put knife in slot
Your penknife is too big to fit inside the slot.  A quick push and the button
in the slot operates a quick release allowing the cargo door to lower under
it's own weight.
The door hit's the ground with a crash.

> out
You move out.

By the aircraft
The body of your aircraft, partially buried, is beside you. All around are
trees and the sounds of a myriad of birds and animals, many of which you
would prefer not to meet.
