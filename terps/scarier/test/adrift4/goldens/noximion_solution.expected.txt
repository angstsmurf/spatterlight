Noximion

After becoming a Guardian, an official protector of Meho, you felt like you
were a noble figure that society looks upon. Although nothing changed the
instant they made it official. You feel the same. You don't feel younger.
Your teeth aren't any whiter.

But, despite this, you have a world to save.

Meho City (1st Floor) Central
Central is named so because of the amount of activity that takes place here.
Some people hustle and bustle about, while others converse with one another,
sitting on a bench or near the giant fountain in the center of the place. The
area is a small circle from which the upper parts of the city can be viewed.
This little section is the heart of Meho City, and the rest of Noximion as
well.

You move up.

Meho City (2nd Floor) Mall 1
Plenty of people roam the first floor of the mall. The translucent windows
and walls that border the main walk make it easy for shoppers to see what is
being offered.  Military material such as weaponry and armor can be found
here, as well as civilian drugs and foods. An odd mix for sure, but it is
what the Planners designed.

You can move northeast (to the Medicine Shop), northwest (to the Food Shop),
southeast (to the Weapon Shop), and southwest (to the Armor Shop).

Meho City (3rd Floor) Mall 2
The upper half of the mall is the one where the most civilians can be found.
Translucent benches have been put in place along the walkway because of the
many families walking around buying goods. Clothes, furniture, and other
miscellaneous items can be purchased on this floor. A dizzying array of
colored lighting is allowed here, and brand names and commercials are
displayed everywhere. It is also relatively noisy in here, with hundreds of
people chattering and "soothing" background music playing.

You can move northeast (to the Club), northwest (to the Clothing Shop),
southeast (to the Items Shop), and southwest (to the Furniture Shop).

Meho City (4th Floor) Port
The place most familiar to Guardians, the port leads to four different
waiting rooms where you can be ported to places far away from here, or
anywhere outside of the city. They are the only ways into and outside of
Meho.

You can move northeast (to the Zank Port), northwest (to the St. Ness Port),
southeast (to the Alunia/Plantura Port), and southwest (to the Mihokosa
Barrens Port).
