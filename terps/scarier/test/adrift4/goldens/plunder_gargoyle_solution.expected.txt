Ye Olde Pirate Ship
Arrgh!  'Tis ye olde pirate ship, which ye sail with ye pirate buddy Ichabod.
 'Tis a good place to drinketh some grog and telleth ye pirate stories at
sea.  Also, ye liketh to sail around in it plunderin', which is what maketh
ye a pirate in ye first place.  From here ye can moveth east down ye
gangplank to yon beach.  Or, when ye feeleth ready, ye might set sail.

Also here is ye olde riddle scroll and ye cannon.

Lo, ye be Captain Somebody.  Ye looketh mighty piratey, because ye happen to
a pirate.  Yarrgh, me hearties...!  Ye are wearing ye pirate clothes and
carrying ye cutlass.

If ye need some help on how to play ye game, type tutorial.  Remember, if ye
be stuck on how to use something, look at it by typing x followed by ye
object name, me matey!  If ye get really stuck, typeth hint.

helped ye navigate to Loot Island, it sayeth:

^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Findeth ye the Isle o' Loot
And followeth ye sign of yon boot.
Through ye marshes navigate
But toward ye end don't celebrate!
Ye still must enter ye olde ruins,
Discover ye Secret of cursed dubloons,
Then reacheth ye cliff bottom
Where ye treasures await, if ye spot 'em!

What a silly treasureman ye gotteth this from!
