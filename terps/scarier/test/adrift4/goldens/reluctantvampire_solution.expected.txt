The Charter Of The Fearless Vampire Hunters
(3rd edition)

1)  Wherever vampires are found, we shall stamp them out!
2)  Wherever dead bodies show up drained of blood and pale of countenance,
the culprits are undoubtedly vampires!
3)  Wherever innocent men fear to tread in the darkness, there vampires lurk!
4)  Wherever shady individuals hide in the shadows, they are vampires!
5)  Wherever the encroachment of evil is greatest, vampires are behind it!
6)  Wherever vampires are found, we shall kill them!

The FVH are, not surprisingly, no friends to vampires. Their recruitment
criteria are simple: if you're not a vampire, you're in. No questions asked.
Oh, and being a dab hand at stabbing people in the chest with a sharp wooden
implement is also handy.
     They've hounded you ever since that fateful night three months ago after
which you developed a craving for blood, biting people in the neck and flying
around your garden in bat form. So far they haven't made any actual moves
against you - aside from the obligatory chalking of a message on the side of
your house declaring "THE OWNER OF THIS HOUSE IS A MEMBER OF THE UNDEAD!" -
as they operate strictly within the law and at the moment they can't prove a
thing. While they'd prefer to stake every vampire through the heart the
moment they uncover one, they'd also be arrested for murder so they tend to
wait, watch and take notes. One wrong move and it's staking time.

lll Morping Street lll

This uninteresting bit of road right outside the marriage guidance
counsellor's office is known hereabouts as Morping Street for reasons that
are probably better off not known. It's the kind of street that few people
ever have cause to visit as it contains nothing more than the marriage
guidance counsellor's office (to the south), a Christian bookshop (to the
north)  and the town's most unprofitable bank (to the northeast). Even the
homeless have better taste than to try begging for money here. Morping Street
itself runs east and west to more interesting places.
Your arch-nemesis, the leader of the FVH, Norbert van Helsing, is here.

"No doubt you left a dozen corpses in there, you foul spawn of the undead,"
says Norbert van Helsing, speaking in the kind of strident tones that carry
to everyone on the street. "Drained of blood, eyes vacant, bodies twitching
with the last vestiges of lost life."
     Yes, Norbert really talks like that.
