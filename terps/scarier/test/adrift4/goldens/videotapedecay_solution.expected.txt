"Though there are very many nations all over the earth, ...there are no more
than two kinds of human society, which we may justly call two cities, ...one
consisting of those who live according to man, the other of those who live
according to God ....To the City of Man belong the enemies of God, ...so
inflamed with hatred against the City of God."

-- Augustine of Hippo

You enter the threshold of the first city, promptly step onto a random
street, and feel alive with the pulse of Justice. Your mission to correct the
wrongs of the town and its denizens is right and proper.

Within the city, along with countless sin and evil, is your daughter. Your
pure, innocent daughter that must be nearly dead by now.

You must rescue her.

You have done well, son.

A loud voice shatters the silence of the town, and you see everything before
you fade to white. It's as if you were lifted into heaven itself.

You are lifted somewhere. You feel like you're floating above the earth. Your

The voice starts again.

You have done as I have asked without question. I shall reward you for
helping me cleanse that city, that evil city designed by Man. I'm sure you
know Man's evils...and I am glad you have chosen to abandon them. Joyce

The voice comes from nowhere, but is loud and dominating.

And of your daughter, she is with me. I have kept my part of the bargain.

Come. Hurry.

Why must you take your time? Your Paradise is waiting!

Your daughter, your sweet water Lily, she is going to be your daughter again.

Have you no respect?

And you stop at the angry tone of God.

"Where is my daughter?" you ask. He must think you've forgotten.

I told you, she's with me.

"And? That tells me nothing."

Let me remind you who you are talking to. I am your Creator. I can just as

"My daughter is with you, everything is with you. But where exactly is she,

No...

"Then where is she?" Your voice rises.

I'm going to be nice. She's in that transparent relic. That was your
daughter. Her soul was trapped in the relic because she was another of the
unholy, just as with the other colored records, the other testaments of Man.
They were symbols of what Man meant, and your daughter was one of them. She
was entrapped after the Second Fall, after the Purge.

"What? Her soul was in that statue because she was simply a sinner. We are
all sinners. That was your mark of design."

Yes, but she was a testament to Man. She was a visionary that showed
everything that was wrong with the city of Man. She was a sexual deviant. She
committed incest with her cousin. Not stopping there, however. She went on to
spearhead a movement to legalize this abomination.

"But...she is forgiven."

You know, you should have been one of those relics. You are worse than her.

"Then why didn't you make me one."

Because you, Joyce Reveras, represent everything wrong with Man. You are the
individualist, hard-nosed in effort and approach, constantly refusing the
help of others, selfishly looking out for your own interests, never knowing a
single sympathy for altruistic behavior. I thought you could redeem yourself.
I thought you could redeem the human race.

"I thought Jesus did that. Actually...I don't care. This is a bad deal. I
want out."

OUT? You want OUT? You got it!
