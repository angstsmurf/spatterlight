FIVE YEARS AGO
Five years ago you were not the Mould. It was your birthday and you had only
just turned eighteen. The world seemed so full of promise back then. You
thought that you could do anything. That arrogance should have proved to be
your undoing, but you were lucky. He was there to save you.

A dark bridge
It is late at night and you have had a little too much to drink. You are a
bit unsteady on your feet, but you are sure that you can make it home all
right. You live to the north, just past the river rushing furiously in front
of you. Luckily, a bridge provides a safe path across the river. The only
light is from the stars and the bright full moon shining from above you, but
you know your way well enough for this to be more than sufficient to navigate
by.

> n
You stumble forwards and are midway across the bridge when you hear someone
rushing up behind you. You glance back to see a man wearing a balaclava
running at you. Your reactions are slow and before you can move out of his
way, he pushes you over the side of the bridge to fall into the river below.
The water envelops you, and soon all you know is darkness.

heard rumours about, but you have never seen in person. Still you recognise
him instantly. With a bright blue appearance, and yellow underwear that seems
to stretch no matter what he turns into, it could only be the Mould.

The Mould asks, "Are you all right? I was really worried about you for a few
minutes there. After I saw that thief knock you into the water, I wasn't sure
that I could save you."

You croak out, "What happened?"

He replies "I was chasing a thief who robbed a bank earlier tonight. He
pushed you into the river, as he ran past you. Unfortunately I could not both
save your life and arrest him, so I was forced to let him go. It doesn't
matter. I'll catch him next time and a human life is far more important.
Don't try to say anything else right now. I'll take you to a hospital."

You see the Mould shift into a shape sort of like a small blue ambulance just
before the darkness takes you again.

ONE YEAR AGO
You spent the next four years training with the Mould. You are not as
imaginative or have as much control, but you can master many basic shapes for
your hand to turn into. Unfortunately, every time you try to be more creative
you just cannot get it right. Eventually the Mould offered that you could
become his sidekick and you quickly accepted before he could change his mind.
You wore normal clothes and put on a bright yellow mask to hide your
identity. Your new name was Handmade, and you had a lot of fun.

Not everyone rejoiced in your good fortune though. Your parents did not
approve that you dropped out of all further education in order to pursue your
dreams of being a hero. Both of them being scientists, they really
disapproved of this career path. In fact, you can remember going to visit
them on that terrible and fateful day. As you approached the lab you
immediately knew that something was very wrong.

Street in front of the laboratory
The laboratory to the north is burning down rapidly, with a fire spreading
along the top of the complex. There is no siren to urge people to evacuate,
and you can see no one exiting. This is a dangerous situation, made worse by
the fact that your parents are probably still in there. You hope that they
are all right.  You should probably call the Mould on your mobile in order to
get his help. There is no one better at rescuing people from burning
buildings.
