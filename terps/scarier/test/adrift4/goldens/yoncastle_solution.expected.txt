Outside Yon Castle
Ye standeth outside yon castle, which is surrounded by yon moat.  Yon
drawbridge in ye east is up, preventing ye entry into yon castle.  Nearby
there groweth yon nut tree.

To learn how to playeth yon game, typeth "tutorial" and press enter.  Do not
typeth of ye "help", for yon message knoweth not what it talketh about.

Always presseth ye enter key after ye typeth ye commands.

> climb tree

In Ye Nut Tree
Ye standeth within yon nut tree and clingeth to't quite precariously.  Yon
nuts decorate ye nut tree nutfully.  Ye noticeth yon long branch of ye nut
tree extendeth o'er ye castle wall to ye east.

> east
Ye moves east.

Atop Ye Castle Wall
Ye standeth atop ye castle wall, o'erlooking yon castle courtyard to ye east.
 Yon towers loom north and south, and yon long branch of ye nut tree
extendeth from ye west.  A jolly breeze bloweth ye hair about (or tickleth ye
scalp if ye be bald).  Yon drawbridge operating contraption sticketh out from
between a pair of yon crenellations.

> open contraption
Ye openeth yon drawbridge operating contraption.

Ye heareth yon loud, creaking kaboom as ye drawbridge falleth open suddenly.

> west
Ye moves west.
