Prologue

It is the Galactic Civil Year 5012, in a galaxy somewhere far from Earth...

Satellite Control Station XI, a critical junction for observation and
potential communication with the Sol star system in the Parigathon Galaxy,
has suffered a collision with a previously undetected asteroid now known as
Tarsellis Alpha II.  The station's satellites are now out of position and
communications with the Sol system have ceased.  Unfortunately, the systems
administrator for the station was at work when the asteroid hit, so the
Sector Head has assigned you to the station.

As systems administrator for XI, your priorities are two-fold: you must
re-align the satellites to resume communications with the Sol system and
restore the station's switchboard to active status.  The satellites must be
re-aligned before the switchboard can be changed from safe to active status.
So long as the switchboard is in safe mode it will reset periodically to
prevent tampering by space pirates.  Resetting will require the re-alignment
of the satellites.

A class W shuttle will be by Station XI to recollect you as soon as your job
is finished.

Satellite Control Station XI
Even the ultralight titanium shell of the station could scarcely withstand
the impact of Tarsellis Alpha II.  The formidable debris strewn all about the
station, barely anchored by weak artificial gravity, will have to get sorted
by the janitors after you finish.  Appropriately crater-sized dents have
deformed the station's switchboard, but it still appears to function.  In all
cardinal directions are camera stations which one could enter to observe
surrounding satellite positions.

A spark flies up from the switchboard and dissipates.

For help with game commands, type "help" and press enter.  For help with
puzzles, type "hint" and press enter.

lights of a class W shuttle flood Satellite Control Station XI.  The shuttle
docks with the station and drops off the cleaning crew-- it's their turn to
work this place over.

You board the shuttle, ready for the long ride home.

Epilogue

The shuttle docks at your home colony-ship, A-48, but relief is still as far
as your living chamber.  The colony seems abuzz about something; there are
almost never this many people at the docking bay so late and never any so lit
up.

Has there been a parade?  A riot?

You can barely take two steps from the gangplank before the Sector Head waves
you down.

The Sector Head looks pale and keeps making quick wipes at his forehead.  You
cannot discern whether his dilated pupils shine frantically or wildly.

He rambles, "While you were working... while you were working, we started to
get some weird signals in from satellite number four at your station...
messages from the one called Earth, but they don't make any sense... they're
too... we can't tell... it's...

"Come on," he says, grabbing the sleeve of your spacesuit, "you'd better have
a look at this..."
