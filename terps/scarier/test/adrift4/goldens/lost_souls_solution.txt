#                                      [unlock * trunk] [use key * trunk]

#            RESTR type=0 v1=6 (holding the key)   ACT type=0 -> scrap of paper

# phrasing forever and answers "I gave the lid a tug, but it's locked." even

# a player who happens to type "unlock" instead of "open".
