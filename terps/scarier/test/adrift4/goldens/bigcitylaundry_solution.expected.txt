If at any time you need help, then too bad.  This is Big City and laundry is
up to you.

It's Sunday, and you've slept all the way to 8:15!  It's a glorious day
because on Sunday, the time is yours!  You roll out of bed, excited to make
breakfast and coffee, and spend the day playing video games!  First things
first, though!  The floor in here is FREEZING!  Put on some socks!
