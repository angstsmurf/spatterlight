After a number of years your membership of the distinguished club of the
unemployed has finally expired.
In other words, you've got a job! It's not the job you've hoped for but being
honest to yourself you realize that the chances of you becoming General
secretary of the UN was at best non existent.
Instead you've got a job at the local library cleaning up, putting books back
on the shelves and the occasional sweeping of the floor.
When you arrive at work today you never in your wildest dreams imagined that
this day would turn your otherwise peaceful existence upside down.
For a few days your job has been to clean up the cellar under the library, a
dusty place where they keep all the books that are no longer interesting.
When you arrive at the library you quietly walk down the stairs and lock
yourself into the cellar.

Cellar
The cellar is not the coziest of places you've been.
A simple wooden desk with a single drawer is placed almost in the middle
between tall bookshelves which upon closer inspection are nailed to the wall.
A row of fluorescent lamps are hanging from the ceiling lighting up the room
with their cold, but efficient light shining down on the old dusty books.
Along the walls and on the desk piles of books are stacked up high,
threatening to fall over at the tiniest breeze.

> look

Cellar
The cellar is not the coziest of places you've been.
A simple wooden desk with a single drawer is placed almost in the middle
between tall bookshelves which upon closer inspection are nailed to the wall.
A row of fluorescent lamps are hanging from the ceiling lighting up the room
with their cold, but efficient light shining down on the old dusty books.
Along the walls and on the desk piles of books are stacked up high,
threatening to fall over at the tiniest breeze.

> open drawer
You open the drawer.  A map and a box of matches are inside the drawer.

> get matches
You take the box of matches from the drawer.

> pull shelf
You grab the vertical metal bar and pull with all your might, nothing
happens...
Then you wedge your feet against the wall next to the shelf and again pull as
hard as you can...
At first nothing happens and you are about to give up when suddenly it feels
like the shelf is moving...
Slowly the shelf and the wall its mounted on starts to swing out... more...
more... until a crack wide enough for you to slip through has appeared in the
eastern wall.

> go east
Although not too keen on the situation your curiosity gets the better part of
you.
Painfully reminded that you could lose a few pounds you squeeze yourself
through the opening in the wall.

Chamber
You are in an ancient chamber of some kind. Obviously this is all that
remains of whatever building was here many years ago.
The walls are made of huge boulders delicately chiseled into shape so they
fit perfectly into one another. A single torch is placed in a metal holder
high on the wall.
Roughly cut wooden shelves are placed against the wall and a huge oak desk
with an chair is placed near the middle. A faint smell of burnt wood still
lingers in the air although it must have been hundreds of years since anyone
was here.

> get torch
You reach up to take the torch.
As soon as you lift it out of its metal holder a screeching sound makes you
turn around.
The torch falls from your hand as you helplessly watch the wall slowly but
inexorably slide back closing your only exit, and the darkness slowly engulfs
you.
Is it your imagination, or is the air already getting heavier in here?

> light match
You light one of your few remaining precious matches.
The sudden burst of light almost blinds you and by the time you get your
vision back the match has almost burned out.
Before it extinguishes you get a glimpse of the torch on the floor, right by
your feet.

> get torch
With the match burned out the darkness seems even deeper than before. You
slowly kneel, letting your hands slide slowly over the rough floor...
nothing!
Then, just as you are about to give up, your fingertips touches something,
you desperately grab it, hold it tight as if it would wiggle its way out of
your grip. The feeling of the torch in your hand sends a sigh of relief
through your body.

> light torch
You light a match and set it to the oily cloth wrapped around the end of the
torch.
You watch impatiently as the flame slowly eats away the matchstick, getting
closer to your fingers without any noticeable effect on the torch.
With a silent oath you drop the match and the room is once again engulfed in
darkness.

> light torch
You strike yet another match and hold it to the torch. It slowly burns down,
further and further... the flickering flame almost touching your fingers when
finally something begins to happen.
First a tiny flame appears. It slowly grows bigger and bigger as it feeds
from the oily cloths and finally a sleepy dark yellow, almost orange light
spreads throughout the room and the various things stands out in their
natural form.

Chamber
The sleepy golden, orange light from the torch manages to light up your
immediate surroundings.
The flame flickers, casting eerie shadows over the ceiling and ancient walls.
Obviously this is all that remains of whatever building was here many years
ago.
The walls are made of huge boulders delicately chiselled into shape so they
fit perfectly into one another.
Roughly cut wooden shelves are placed against the wall and a huge oak desk
with an chair is placed near the middle.

> sit
You slowly sink down on the chair, leaning back, resting your head against
the back and placing your arms on the armrests.
It feels like the shape of the chair is changing slightly, forming after your
body.
Then an almost unnoticeable shiver, and the chair slowly slides forward
across the floor, not stopping until you can easily reach the desk.

> read book
You press the torch into the hole in the old desk and sit down.
Gently you pull the book towards you and open it carefully.
In letters almost impossible to read the first page starts:
I Merlin, Wizard of Camelot in the forgotten land of Avalon, .... (the rest
is unreadable)
As you turn the page you feel a tiny prick on the tip of your finger. You
look down and see a tiny drop of blood forming... You bring the finger to
your mouth and suck it before continuing reading.
Making out the words is hard work, and a light dizziness overwhelms you. Your
eyelids become heavy, almost impossible to keep open, your head nods, falling
on your chest, and soon you slump over the book, drifting away in a deep
sleep.

Kitchen
You are abruptly awakened by a rather unkind shoe between your ribs.
Still groggy you stumble to your feet only to be sent back to the floor by a
clout on the ear.
As the haze lifts from your eyes and the few remaining stars of the universe
stop rotating you look up. A tall man wearing a white apron is leaning over
you, his face lobster red with rage.
“What are you doing, sleeping while there is work to do!” He raises his hand
again and you instinctively cover your still burning ear.
“King Arthur is celebrating the anniversary of his and Queen Guinevere's
wedding this evening, and you are sleeping?”
He grabs a loaf of bread from a nearby table and shoves it in your arms.
“Here, take this to the King for approval, but make haste you lazy git, or my
shoe isn't the only thing you'll feel.”
He turns and disappears in the crowd.
Press any key

You look around, confused and with your ear still stinging from the rough
treatment.
People are running back and forth, carrying huge trays or pots and pans of
all shapes and sizes. Two lambs are slowly roasting over a huge fire,
supervised by two young children.
Bloody pieces of fresh meat lie on long tables and young girls and fat women
are chopping up vegetables, between the lumps, obviously not caring much
about kitchen hygiene.
The straw scattered on the stone floor does little to suck up the blood and
other liquids.
To the north and east two narrow openings in the walls lead out of the
kitchen.
The northern leads up, and you can see a faint light at the top of what must
be a staircase, and the eastern leads down into darkness.

> go north
You sneak out of the kitchen, up along the dark passage.
After a short while you see a light up ahead, and soon you step out into the
courtyard.

Courtyard
The courtyard is surrounded on all sides by tall walls that seem to go on
forever.
High above them the towers of Camelot can be seen, so high up they almost
disappears in the clouds.
The ground is covered with straw  from a huge haystack in the corner right
next to the stables.
To the west through an arch lies a green garden. Through the arch you can see
rose beds with roses in all colours. To the East lies the entrance to the
castle, guarded by two heavily armed men holding long spears. Behind you to
the south are the stairs leading back down into the kitchen and finally to
the north the heavy drawbridge that spans the moat. Here too, guarding the
huge oak doors and the portcullis another two men are posted. An axe is
leaning against the winch that holds the portcullis.

> go west
A soft stillness engulfs you as you walk through the arch into the rose
garden.
All the noise from the courtyard is muffled to a melodic humming, and you
feel a tranquility overtake you.

The Rose garden
The first thing that strikes you as you enter through the arch is the
delicate scent of roses. Although there are hundreds of different kinds, you
discover that each of them smells differently and each scent is distinctively
caught by you.
A green carpet of grass is spread out before you, only broken by the flower
beds which have been placed in decorative patterns throughout the garden.
Peacocks, pecking for food, wander around a stone bench which is placed under
the protective shade of a small almond tree and a low stone wall surrounds
the garden on two sides, the castle wall making out the third and the wall
with the arch which you walked through to the east, makes out the fourth.

> feed peacocks
You break off a piece of the bread and crumble it between your fingers.
No sooner have you thrown it on the ground before the peacocks come running
over and begin to eat it.
A fight breaks out over the last few crumbs and as the victor proudly struts
away, a lost tail feather lays behind on the ground.

> get feather
You take the tail feather.

> go east
You feel almost sad having to leave this beautiful garden. But you head east
and once again pass under the arch of roses entering the noisy courtyard.

> go south
The sudden darkness of the passage surprises you and you carefully walk down
the steps towards the kitchen, steadying yourself by holding on to the
wall...
The smell of food becomes stronger as does the noise and chatter from below.
Soon you step out into the kitchen.

Kitchen
You are back in the kitchen.
Nothing much has changed since your last visit here, except the kitchen
master isn't here to kick and slap you around. He must be busy tormenting
some other poor soul.
People are running back and forth, carrying huge trays or pots and pans of

> go east
With the light from the kitchen flickering playfully over the walls, you
slowly descend down into a darkness that gets deeper and deeper the further
you go.
The air gets heavier and warmer the further down you go and the walls get
moist... little drops of water trickling down onto the steps making them
dangerously slippery.
As you continue further down, the passage widens a bit and finally opens up
into a large room.

Cave
The ceiling of this huge cave is towering high above you, invisible in the
darkness.
What was in the beginning a natural cave has been carved into a huge cellar
like room deep under the castle
The cave would have been completely dark hadn't it been for the burning
torches mounted high on the walls.
The flickering flame from the torches and the moving shadows makes is
somewhat confusing, but you remember that the passage leading back up to the
kitchen lies to the west, and amongst the erratic moving shadows you notice
that there's an opening in the southern wall, leading down, and a narrow dark
passage in the northern wall.

> go north
After fumbling your way through a narrow windy passage you finally enter a
huge hall.
You slip out from the narrow opening in the southern end of the hall, hidden
by a sharp turn.

The Knight's Hall
The hall stretches out before you seemingly endlessly from one end to the
other.
Furthest away is a huge oak table with twelve oak chairs around it and a
little closer is a long table where trays are placed in rows as long as the
table.
Torches are placed high on the walls, ready to be lit at night but for now
the sun beams in through the tall windows.
Huge tapestries hangs on the walls, reaching from the ceiling almost to the
floor and in the eastern wall is a stairway leading up.
There are two huge fireplaces, one in the center of the hall and one built
into the northeastern corner. Neither of them are lit but both stacked with
firewood.
Behind you, so the south, lies the entrance to the passage leading down to
the cave, and far away, across the hall to the west are the doors leading
out.
To the east, behind the long table, is a dark opening in the wall.

> go east
You enter the passage in the east wall of the Knights Hall.

Passage
The dusky passage winds its way between the walls of the castle, leading up
to a corridor higher up or down to the Great hall.
The steps have been polished smooth and even, so that Queen Guinevere can
walk safely from her chamber to the hall. Even the walls here are different
from those in the other parts of the castle.
The passage is only lit partly by what little light that seeps in from above
and below, hardly enough to allow you to see where you put your feet.
This is no problem to Guinevere who of course is always followed by a maid
holding a torch or candle.

> touch indentation
Running your fingers over the indentation you feel a faint roughness.
You press against it and a faint click is heard somewhere.
Slowly a part of the wall slides aside revealing a dark spiral stairway
leading north.

> go north
Carefully, you step through the opening and are at the foot of a spiral
wooden staircase leading up into the unknown darkness.

Spiral staircase
Looking up along the winding wooden staircase you see... nothing but
darkness.
A high pitched scream is heard, almost inaudible, and then the faint sound of
wings fluttering through the air. Something soft and hairy brush against your
cheek as a bat silently flies by, disappearing into the darkness.
It takes a while before your eyes get used to the darkness, and still you can
only vaguely see the outline of the steps before you leading up.

> go up
With your arms stretched out, your hands running along the stone wall you
slowly climb the stairway, one reluctant step at a time.
After a while the darkness is broken by a faint flickering light and you
quite suddenly bump your face against what appears to be a door.

> open door
Fumbling, you run your hands over the door, trying to keep your balance on
the narrow steps.
Finally, your fingers touches metal, a door handle of some kind.
You reach for the handle like a drowning person would grab a lifebelt. You
turn it and literally fall into the room.
Not quite the dignified entrance you had planned, but it'll have to do.
You pick yourself up from the, not very, flattering position on the floor and
your jaw drop to your knees.

Merlin's chamber
The room is humongous! But this can't be, how can anyone fit a room the size
of the great hall inside a slim tower?
Under a window at the farthest end of the room an old man stands behind a
desk an owl resting on his shoulder.
Behind him is a fireplace with a nice fire going. A cauldron hangs on a sooty
hook, and the cozy sound of something boiling fills the room.
Along the wall tall shelves is filled with dusty books and weird looking
instruments. Glasses and flasks are piled up on a table in a strange looking
experiment.
He looks at you with a knowing smile, and nods... Like he has been expecting
you...

> get book
Merlin comes over and takes the book out of your hands and puts it back down.
“Please don't touch anything. The order here is a very delicate balance.”
Looking around the messy room you find it hard to see ANY balance at all, but
you decide to believe him.
"Let me tell you why I brought you here. Today is the anniversary of Arthur
and Guinevere's wedding.
My gift for them should be hot running water, and every thing is in place
except..."
Merlin pauses and pulls his beard. “I can't get that stupid dragon to heat up
the water! Can you imagine the embarrassment.. The mighty Merlin, not being
able to keep a promise?
Getting the dragon was easy enough,” he continues, “sneaking it into the
dungeons under the castle under the eye of everyone was a bit more
troublesome, but absolutely manageable. But getting that blasted creature to
spew fire and heat up the water, is downright impossible.”
He looks at you with a wicked look in his eyes. “This is where you come into
the picture. If you can get the Dragon to heat up the water before the
banquette tonight I will send you back to your own time, if not you will stay
here as the lowest kitchen helper. I am not usually a mean person, but this
shows you how desperate I am.”
He raises his hands, waves them in an intriguing pattern before your eyes...
“Now be gone. We both have a job to do.”

West end of corridor
You are heading down a long corridor running east west, your steps sounding
hollow against the wooden floor. On your left and right are doors leading
into the chambers occupied by people of the court.
A soldier is guarding the door on the right side of the corridor whereas the
door on the opposite side is not guarded at all. Further down you see other
doors and at the far end of the corridor to the east you can make out a small
room with stairways leading up and down.

> go east
Your steps sound hollow against the wooden floor as you walk down the
corridor.

East end of corridor
You are heading down a long corridor running east west. Behind you to the
west at the other end of the corridor is the stairway leading down to the
great hall. Ahead of you to the east is a small room in which you can see a
stairway leading up and down.
On your left and right are doors leading into the chambers occupied by people
of the court.
Here too torches are placed in metal holders on the wall, none of them lit
though, leaving the part of the corridor gloomy and dusky.

> open left door
Putting up an attitude that shows you have every right to be here, you
quickly reach out and open the left door and slip inside.
Unfortunately your attitude, however splendid wasn't fooling anyone, and
certainly not the guard.
He shouts angrily at you as you close the door behind you. You are safe for
the time being but you realize that it's only a matter of time before he will
have reinforcements up here.
You quickly look around, wondering how much time you have before the guards
will come.

Queen Guinevere's chamber
This must be Queen Guinevere's chamber. It's very luxurious with a huge bed
raised upon a small platform and a silk canopy above it. The bed is placed so
the first light of the sun will shine directly on it, waking the Queen
gently. The scent of cedar, mixed with delicate perfume lingers in the air
giving the room a truly feminine touch.
A table with a candelabra is positioned under the window and a comfortable
chair is standing near by.
A huge closet takes up part of the western wall and between it and a cedar
chest is a door.
The room is kept neatly clean and tidy, heavy velvet curtain and beautiful
tapestries on the wall.
A door in the western wall leads into another room and to the north an
opening, covered with a semi see-through silky curtain, leads into yet
another part of the Queen's chamber. Behind you, to the south is the door
leading out to the corridor.

> go north
You gently push the silk curtains aside and walk into the adjacent room.

Bathroom
The beauty of this room is absolutely fantastic.
The stone floor is covered with heavy mats, woven from the finest and softest
wool in all of England. A small table filled with bottles and jars of all
forms, shapes and sizes are neatly lined up against the wall under a huge
metal plate, polished like the clearest glass, and a small stool is pushed
under it.
What surprises you the most is what appears to be a modern bathtub placed
against the western wall, discretely out of sight from the entrance in the
southern wall, leading into Guinevere's chamber, and a shiny faucet
protruding from the wall a little above it.
A modern bathtub in the midst of the darkest medieval age? This must be
magic...

Also here is the dragons delight, a arthurs pleasure, a summer breeze and a
moonlight at midnight.
You hear noise from the corridor as a squad of guards is approaching.

> use dragons delight
You dab a few drops of the Dragons delight onto your neck and wrists.
The heavy scent engulfs you completely as the warmth of your blood evaporates
the perfume.
The door is slammed open and the guards rush into the room.

> go south
Slipping through the flimsy silky curtain once again, you leave the bathroom
and enter Queen Guinevere's chamber.

Both guards grab your arms and they drag you though the castle corridors down
to the kitchen where they dump you at the feet of the kitchen chef, who
immediately slap you across the face, sending you tumbling through the room.
The guards laugh scornfully and return to their duties..

Dragon's Lair
The first things that strikes you as you enter this cave, is how in the

Squeezing it through the fairly small opening in the cell at the end of the

The lair is enormous, as is the dragon laying in the middle of it, on top of

The cave is dry and warm, the warmth adding to make the foul smell of the

High above the dragon, way out of your reach, a huge metal tank is hanging

> tickle dragon with feather
As you get closer the dragon lifts his huge head and turns it towards you.

You slowly edge closer, and closer... without making too much noise you climb

At first nothing, then a sound like a baby's giggle emerges from this

Little puffs of smoke rises from his nostrils and suddenly a roar of flames

The water inside the tank starts boiling, the bubbling sounding like sweet
music to your ears.
You suddenly feel dizzy … The hankie drops from your powerless hand and you

A deep darkness engulfs you and you sink to the floor.
