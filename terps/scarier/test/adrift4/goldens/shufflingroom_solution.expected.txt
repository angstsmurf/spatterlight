> release hands
The hands take some struggling to remove, the arms tremble as they sense
becoming unguided. You prise the hands off and fall from between the two
people before they can link you in their chain again. You land on the floor.

> feel darkness
Arms out in front, searching for anything - yes! anything - and carefully
stepping through the darkness. Your hands soon come upon a wall.

> feel wall
Exploring the wall, its rough grain, you happen upon a small metallic box
that feels like it may be a lightswitch.

> use switch
The switch, at first, resists your efforts.

> use switch
The switch, this time, doesn't resist your efforts. Light fills the room...

Once slithering out of its mother the child crawls to the woman's teat
suckling on the nutritious flap.
   One of the children, grown up now, leaves its mother's breast and stands
by the circle of men. On his face you can see the concntration as the child
counts the rhythm within the footfalls before he finds a place into which to
slip, his arms fixing themselves upon the shoulders of one man while another
takes charge of the boy.
  The number of footfalls increases and the tempo of the shuffling decreases
as if relaxing due to the additional footsteps.

> up
As you ascend the stairs the light from the shuffling room begins to fade as
you climb further and further away from its madness...and then the footsteps
stop, no hobbling or shambling - just silence.
 From the room below they come...slithering masses of children , dried up
women, and tired men, blindly reaching forward taking hold of the stairs of
the walls, of you...
   And then they pass, one child being born as its mother races up the steps,
its soft head taking a beating from each step as its mother drags it
umbilically.
   At the top of the stairs there is a door which all those that have passed
you have opened. Stepping into the room beyond you are not surprised to see
nothing but a bitumen void, the door behind you vanishes, and once again you
are trapped in darkness with the soft shuffle of feet massaging your ears.
A chorus of voices duet with the shuffling of feet: "Join us, join us, join
us - there is no need for radicals."

> join circle

   You praise yourself for such invention as your role becomes apparent to
you. Without wasting any more minutes? days? years? you hold out your arms
and, counting the beat of the shuffling, find your place within the circle of
men.
  A bizarre feeling sweeps over you; that despite this being the same group
of men you had escaped from downstairs they are somehow a new circle of men.
 And so you wander around in circles, holding on to the man before you for
guidance, and, in turn, guiding the man behind you. Only now do you
understand how great a role you were born to play in the shuffling room. And
you are thankful for it as the people of the shuffling room are thankful for
your powerful discovery.
   Such self-appraisal is, however, not eternal. Eventually all thoughts of
the shuffling room vanish from your mind as you blend with the circular
patrol and understand nothing but the regular shuffle of the footsteps of
man.
