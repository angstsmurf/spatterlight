Tenebrae Semper
An Interactive Fiction Formed in
Exactly 3 Hours for EctoComp 2010

By Seciden Mencarde

WARNING: This game contains scenes
which may be considered violent and

sort of material, please do not continue.

(Author's Note: The game claims that there are five possible endings, but
only three have been implemented in this version - endings 2, 3, and 4 - due
to time constraints.)

The moonlight barely illuminates the dirt trail cutting through the forest.
You're on your way back to the dorm, cutting through this trail to get there.

You hear a scream, and it stops you. "Help!" It's a girl.

You run toward the scream through the darkness, leaping with each step to
avoid tripping over any roots or rocks. The darkness grows darker, and the
scream never gets nearer...

Something is beeping, very loud, piercing into your ears. You open your eyes
slowly. Oh. It's your dorm. You've probably overslept again.

The blaring noise starts to piss you off, so you slam your fist around trying
to find the sleep button. You eventually hit the alarm clock, but too hard -
it falls off your desk and hits the tile floor, cracking into pieces. Well,
at least it stopped.

You growl as you slide out of bed. Tuesdays suck. You grab your clothes, take
a shower, and come back to the room. You say to yourself, "All right, get
ready. Probably only a few minutes till class."

You enter the stairwell and climb to the upper floor. You open the door

You hesitate; your mind must be playing tricks on you. Probably best if you
don't step through the door... but you're already on the path. The stairwell
- and the rest of the science building - has vanished.

Time passes...
You continue traveling northward through the path, and it all still looks
very much the same, until you come across a faint outline on the ground.
Looking closer, you see that it is a girl: you can see the slender figure and
the long, flowing hair. She looks a lot like Lara.

Something grunts in the darkness beyond.

A creature steps forward from the shadow, as if made of it, crawling on five
thick legs that whisp like tendrils of darkness. Its eyes glow red and lock
onto you. A sound half like a chuckle and half like a roar echoes around the
forest as if there are several of these beasts. It stands and waits for you
to react.

> shoot beast
You point the gun at the beast and pull the trigger. The beast dissipates
into the air like it were only black fog.

> x pillow
You look closer at the stain on the pillow. It's very dark and caked on. You
immediately think it's blood, but it can't be. You touch it and your mind
clicks like you've reached the solution to a problem you've been working on
for eternity, throwing your consciousness, blanking the world before your
eyes and flashing memories like dreams before you...

Again, the forest path, but the beast is gone. Laura lies on the ground. Yes,
it is Laura, and it is not now. You begin to remember your previous school,
your love, her betrayal...

And here you stand, answering the call. You ran to the scream like any
courageous young man would, hoping to save whoever might be in danger. But
the man holds a gun to her head, a hand to her hip. The sight's details defy
description; you haven't the stomach to even think about what you're seeing.

Now what do you do? What did you do? What is this guilt inside of you that
builds like a sickness?

She betrayed you, and you won't ever forgive her for that. Is she worth
risking your life? You hold a gun in your hand; he holds a gun in his. The
path is still open to the south, still beckoning you to safety.

What now?

> shoot man
You point the pistol at the man's head and pull the trigger. He drops Lauren.
She scrambles over to you and grabs hold of your leg as the man falls to the
ground. You look down at her, her face buried in your leg, and then at the
now-still man collapsed before you.

You open your eyes to see Steve walking out of the dorm room. "Lauren?" you
say.

He turns around and lets the door shut behind him. "Who?"

You shake your head and sit up. You're in your bed, the sheets ruffled
wildly. Was it all a dream? No, your hand is still shaking from the recoil of
the gun, and your sweaty palms smell of metal from gripping it too tightly.

Steve walks closer to you. "Are you all right, man?"

You shake your head. "Don't be late for class."

"I was heading out early, anyway. Who's Lauren?"

You look at the blank wall opposite the end of your bed. That's a very good
question. "I... don't know."

"Well, when you figure it out, let me know. Must be important if you're
dreaming about her."

You shake your head again and try to picture the events of the past day: the
empty classroom, the beast in the forest, the blood on the pillow. A strange
experience, more than a dream, but...

Who is Lauren?
