The Lounge
You are in Marks lovely lounge, well no it's actually horrible.  It seems to
be the morning but you can only just tell that through the dirty windows. A
pair of limp unhappy looking curtains sit either side of the window.   In the
middle of the room stands a lopsided coffee table.  To the west you can see
what looks like the hall.  The room has the usual things a bachelors place
has namely an uncomfortable settee, a battered old armchair, narrow screen
television and what looks like a dvd player.
John is currently slumped in the armchair.

"I'd better go and find Mark, to ask him about my keys," you think to
yourself.

> west
You move west.

Hall
You are in Marks small hall.  The front hall in a house is usually a warm and
inviting place.  It is usually the first room visitors see and makes a good
impression.  Well Mark's hall is nothing like that as it is cold, uninviting
and dirty.  The front door is north and next to it is a small table.  You can
see the lovely Lounge to the east and from the smell of it the kitchen is to
the west.  Some stairs lead, well up.

You can see a pair of boxer shorts.

> west

The Kitchen
You are in Marks reasonable sized Kitchen.  It is nice to see that the theme
starting in the Lounge is being continued here as the Kitchen is horrible as
well.  You look around and can see a single lonely kitchen unit next to the
cooker.  An old fridge seems to be leaning against one wall with a washing
machine next to it.  The window over the kitchen sink is just as clear as the
rest of the windows in this house.  A table is positioned in the centre of
the room and on the floor near the back door is a small dog basket.  The hall
is to the east, the door to the back garden south and what looks like a
cupboard under the stairs is southeast.

Under the Stairs
