        Welcome to Hotel con Fuego, a beautiful tropical resort on a secluded
peninsula in the Caribbean Islands where the wealthy come from all over the
world to find peace and relaxation.  With its fifty luxury suites (all with
ocean views), three different pools, spa, gym, tennis courts, golf course,
three private restaurants and a Cabaret, the Hotel con Fuego is perhaps the
most luxurious resort on the island.  What nobody knows, however, is that the
hotel will be nothing but smoldering embers in just twenty-four hours.
Nobody, that is except for Joe Park.

didn't reduce his stress.  It was meant to be just a vacation, but he had
such a horrendous ordeal getting through airport security on the way here
that he was afraid another flight might be the end of him.  So he stayed
right where he was.
