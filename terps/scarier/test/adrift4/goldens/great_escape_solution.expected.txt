The Great Escape
Introduction
Hi. This introduction is for those who haven't played that many text
adventures before. This game is set purposefully on Earth, not in space, or
on Mars, or under the sea or something stupid like that. The game is set in
England in the small (and completely fictional village) of Piddlington. This
game is unlike others in that it is possible to have a proper conversation
with the characters. To do this you can use the 'converse with' command (the
'talk to' command will not work) and this will usually let you conduct a
conversation with the said character. These conversations will usually give
you a few hints although you can probably only do this once. The characters
can also be asked about things although their responses to this action are
pretty limited. This game is not completely realistic or serious but very
cynical. Try things that sound completely ridiculous. Most objects have uses
although some are just used for show. I also have to say that it is
(probably) impossible to achieve the total score although it would be
interesting to see what the highest percentage is. Finally if you can't work
out how to complete the game there is a built in walkthrough in the game
although you will have to discover this for yourself.
If you have any questions, comments, ideas, suggestions, conspiracy theories
etc. please send them to me at kevkov@hotmail.com. Thankyou.

The story so far...

pizza delivery boy. It continued when you couldn't pay your rent to Mr
Malloney, your landlord and local hard-man. You have been having nightmares
and Malloney has threatened to send men round to 'sort you out.' You dream of
getting away from it all, escaping. One night you are having an even more

to shout but to no avail, you're sinking further and further...

sleep-walking, again. You remember the times when, as a child, you used to

feeling swells up inside you, you need to get out of this place, you need to
escape. In that moment you decide that today is the day that you are going to
leave. There, you've said it, but first of course you could do with some
clothes...
