You are Ferdinand, son of Alfonso, the King of Naples.

You are on a sea voyage with the King, his brother, and several other
counsellors, including in particular Antonio, the current Duke of Milan.

Funny thing about Antonio.  His brother, Prospero, formerly the Duke,
disappeared mysteriously about twelve years ago and has never been heard of
since.  His infant daughter, Miranda, vanished with him.

Anyway, you've been having a great time on the voyage.  Terrific party last
night, despite the storm that started to blow up.  You got rather drunk,
though, before you came to bed.  You seem to remember doing something rather
silly with the key to your cabin, because your drinking mates were
threatening to  steal it and lock you in for the duration.  But you can't
remember quite what you did with it...
