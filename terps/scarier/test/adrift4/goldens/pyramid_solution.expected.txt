been going through a bad patch and really needs a discovery. So here you are
wandering the Egyptian desert when you find an unknown pyramid. You rest on

Entrance Chamber
You are just inside the pyramid.  Beside you is the stone which opened and

around, by the light of the torch that you produced from your pocket, you
find yourself in a narrow corridor sloping southwards from the way you
entered.  At the southern end of the corridor is a stone wall with a carved
