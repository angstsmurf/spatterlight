Forest Clearing
  Soft light flows through the canopy above and illuminates the clearing in a
mystical sort of way, entrancing all your senses. A water basin, namely the
basin of life, stands in the center of the round clearing. The air carries a
fain, yet noticably sweet, odor of the flowers that grow only here around the
basin of life, relaxing you despite your knowledge.
  Other than the basin you can see only one few objects in the clearing, a
pool of water. Beyond the pool of water you can see the dense forest of the
Timeplane.
  There are no visible exits from the clearing.

> x basin
