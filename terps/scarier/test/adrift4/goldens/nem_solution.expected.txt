Welcome to this exciting adventure where you must find out what is your
purpose and reason for existence. You will find your self in a cave not
knowing how you got there.

You are soaked through to the bone and have received a knock to the head
which accounts for your loss of memory. It's up to you to explore the caves
and determine what you have been brought here for.

At any point you can type 'hint' to help you out of sticky situations but you
will lose points for doing this. Type 'help' at any point to see a list of
the main commands you can use. Type 'use x' (where x is an object you are
carrying) to use any of your carried objects in any way - this may or may not
work depending on whether it's helpful to use the chosen object. Use the
'options' menu above to view the map as you progress and be sure to try out
the cool little control panel that makes things easier. Click on the help
button for more help on using the game engine and further hints and advise on
making it through the five missions.

Let the adventure begin......just type 'look' or click on 'look' in the
control panel to start....
