DANNY

You are wading through a clear mountain stream. It's just like the one you
knew as a child back on Earth. Brown trout shimmer and twitch around your
legs and you hover, bent over, estimating distance, water resistance, the
speed of your own muscles, the pattern of the trout school. With pure
instinct, you plunge your hand into the stream and it comes out holding a
wriggling, muscular brown fish - about 6 inches long now, but it'll grow with
lightning speed, you think, chuckling. You are about to throw it back, when
it looks at you with its cold round eyes, gasping in the alien air, and
starts making an ear-spitting, rhythmic screech. You drop the fish and open
your eyes at the same time.

Cursing, you jolt awake and the first thing you do is look over at the
Captain's chair. Mercifully, she isn't there and didn't see you nod off on
watch. Miss Deleanora and Mrs Williamsby are talking loudly and excitedly to
each other. The red alert alarm is blaring.

You look at the view-screen. Stars, moving normally along. But there are two
conspicuously bright ones, not moving. Quite still, unblinking.

'Danny McPhee, will you please wake the hell up!' Miss Deleanora shouts. The
Captain bursts in.

'Who activated the Red Alert!' She hisses with uncharacteristic sharpness.
'Only the Captain has that prerogative. Why didn't you call me?' She says
this last to you, and you stammer without actually saying anything. Miss
Deleanora glares at you.

The Bridge
The red alert beacon flashes, giving the room an eerie, almost disco-like
atmosphere. Your chair sits near the middle of the room, facing the giant
view-screen. Stretching across the room in front of your chair is the control
board, with the piloting controls at hand, the information computer panel in
front of the Captain's chair to your left, and the weapons controls further
left.

Behind and to the left is the communication officer's station, and next to
her, the systems monitor's station.

To the right of the Captain's chair is a double-lift, one leading down to the
engine room, and the other is the Captain's personal access up to her
quarters. Another door leads east to the Briefing Room.

Captain Sun ponders the view.  Miss Deleanora mans the system monitor
station.  Mrs Williamsby concentrates on the airwaves traffic through her
padded headphones.
