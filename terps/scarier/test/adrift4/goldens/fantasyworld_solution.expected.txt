The Story

When you were young, you were orphaned by your parents and left to be raised
by the elder of a small village in the mountains.  As you grew into manhood,
your life was pretty simple but you seemed destined for greatness.  Then,
last year, the Dragon came.

At first, the Dragon simply harried travelers but then, it started scattering
the sheep and killing the cows.  The villagers grew fearful, afraid to leave
the village and slowly, the food and water stores dried.  Messagers, sent to
the King who resided over the mountains, never returned and now, it has come
down to you.

You have come into your destiny and your quest will be to see the dragon
slain or driven off so that your village may live in peace.  Unfortunately,
things are not what they seem in far away lands.

Our story begins in the hamlet that you've called home for as long as you can
remember.  Your best friend, Aline, has just informed you that the Elder
wishes to speak with you.  Your quest begins...
