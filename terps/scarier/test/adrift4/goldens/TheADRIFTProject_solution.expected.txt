“Brring Brring”

Leaning over the edge of your bed, you tilt your alarm clock, and a
nickel-cadmium accumulator rolls off onto the nightstand.  You can see that
it is just past 3 am.

“Bbbrrrrring  Bbbrrrrring” The phone rattles, with almost a more urgent tone.

Reaching over, you clumsily bring the receiver to your ear.

“Hello?”

“I have an urgent assignment for you, and there isn’t any time to waste.
We’ve just launched the ADRIFT Project, and something has gone terribly
wrong.  The ship will be over your house in 5.3 minutes to beam you aboard.
We need you to test all the major areas to make sure we’re not getting false
readings.  It is a very difficult task and Drifters around the world are
depending on you.  I know you can do it. You will be the only human on board.
 Be on the lookout for DARWIN.  I think it’s the cause of the false readings
we’re getting.  It may have tampered with the ADRIFT-O-Sweep too.  I knew
that robot should have been tested more.”

“What?  Can’t this wait until morning?”

“It is morning, by my calculations.  We don’t have any time to spare.  The
next ADRIFT release is due and the Drifters are restless.  One more thing,
Luna, the Holographic Informational Assistant should be able to help you out
with some things.  Just press HIA on the control panel.  Well, time is up.
Once you have everything fixed, you should be able to contact me through the
Drift-O-Com.  You might want to take the nickel-cadmium accumulator with
you.”
_____________________________

Before you can object any further or refuse, your body becomes a glittery
swirl and the phone falls from your now semi-transparent hand.  You quickly
grab the nickel-cadium accumulator off your nightstand with your other hand,
before it is too late.

Twisting in every direction, every particle of your body is dematerialized.
You never been transported before, and certainly had no intentions of having
it done now.  You’ve seen the transporter tests conducted back at the main
lab and were horrified to discover that upon materialization, some of the
testers body parts were put back in the wrong places.  You can only hope that
it doesn’t happen this time.  You certainly don’t want to have your hand
sticking out where your nose should be.

A rush of warmth washes over you as you are materialized on the bridge of the
Beta-Drifter. You quickly run your hands across your face and discover that
you are in one piece, letting a sigh of relief escape you.
