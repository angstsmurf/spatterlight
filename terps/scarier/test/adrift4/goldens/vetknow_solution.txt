# Veteran Knowledge (vetknow.taf) -- ADRIFT 4.00, English, 52,248 bytes.
# 43 rooms, 359 tasks, 83 objects, 15 NPCs, 38 events, 2 variables.
#
# The full-length rewrite of *Veteran Experience* (`veteran.taf`, 12,043 bytes,
# 4th 1-Hour Comp), which is already wired as row `veteran_solution.txt`.  Same
# author, same premise, same finish -- a washed-up wrestler crowbars his way
# back to the World title -- but where the 1-hour game is just the arena, this
# one puts a whole town in front of it.  The one thing worth carrying over from
# `notes/Veteran_Experience_walkthrough.md` is the shape of the endgame: take
# out the other contenders one at a time, then win the match with a weapon from
# under the ring.
#
# Result: WIN, **50/50** -- and 50 really is the ceiling.  Summing every
# `ACT type=4` in the file gives 2 + 8 + 10 + 4x3 + 8 + 10 = 50, and the route
# below fires all eight of them, so there is no scoring action left unclaimed.
# WINTEXT is EMPTY, so the win marker has to be prose from the endgame text --
# `AND THE NEW WORLD CHAMPION IS`, which is the ring announcement.
#
# PROVENANCE.  No published walkthrough: David Welbourn covered the *earlier*
# game (http://www.plover.net/~davidw/sol/adr2nd3hr04.html#veteran) and nobody
# covered this one.  It does not matter, because **the game ships its own hint
# system and `SCR_DUMP_TASKS` prints it**: the per-task `HINTQ=`/`HINT1=`/
# `HINT2=` fields are the author's own walkthrough, one entry per puzzle, and
# every load-bearing step below is quoted from them.  (In play the same text is
# behind `hint`, but that prompts `[Y/N]` and echoes its prompt twice, so it is
# no use inside a strict-diffed golden -- read it out of the dump instead.)
#
# THE EIGHT SCORING TASKS, in the order the route fires them:
#   T46  `swim`                room 17  +2   wash off the alley
#   T49  `paint *trunks`       anywhere +8   the Veteran's old ring gear
#   T63  `pour *oil *driveway` room 5   +10  writes the Brawler out of the show
#   T111 `light*firecracker`   room 29  +4   scares the Evil Twins off your crowbar
#   T124 `hit*monster*crowbar` room 27  +4   and drops the bottle of acid
#   T135 `get *mask*`          room 39  +4   unmasks the High Flyer
#   T138 `throw *acid*youth`   room 26  +8   and starts the title match
#   T260 `attack*star*crowbar` room 42  +10  the only `ACT type=6 v1=0` in the file
#
# THE FOUR TIMING TRAPS, all of them things that cost a replay to find:
#
# 1. THE FLYER LANDS AT THE END OF TURN 3.  `EVENT 1 [Flyer arrives]` is
#    `start=4..4`, so `take flyer` on turn 1 is "You see no such thing".  The
#    three `z`s at the top are not padding -- they are the wait.
#
# 2. THE BRATS DO NOT MOVE INTO THE PARK UNTIL THE BEER IS DRUGGED.  The north
#    end of the park (room 18) is empty at the start; `talk to brats` there does
#    nothing and no amount of waiting helps.  What puts NPCs 4 and 5 in it is
#    **T57 `east` out of the bar**, gated on T54 `put pills in beer` being
#    complete.  So the pills have to be stolen from the store and dropped in the
#    Brawler's beer BEFORE the walk to the park, which is why the route crosses
#    town twice.
#
# 3. TAKING THE OIL BUYS YOU A PILEDRIVER AND A `<waitkey>`.  Grabbing the can
#    off the red shirted brat brings the Monster down on you; the scene ends in
#    a "(Press a key)" pause, which is why the row carries `SCR_SKIP_WAITKEY=1`.
#    You keep the oil, and the game gives you a grudge to spend later.
#
# 4. `LOOK UNDER RING` IS TWO DIFFERENT TASKS AND THE ORDER IS FORCED.  T126
#    (tacks + ladder) has `RESTR type=2 v1=139 v2=1` -- task 138 NOT complete --
#    and T127 (steel chair + crowbar + fire extinguisher) has the same reference
#    with `v2=0`, complete.  So the ringside trip for the High Flyer's props has
#    to happen BEFORE the acid, and the trip for the crowbar AFTER it.  Get that
#    backwards and the High Flyer is unbeatable.
#
# THE ARENA, which is where the game actually starts.  Walking in triggers T79
# `east`: a white crate falls and jams the stairway door.  T86 `move crate with
# crowbar` levers it clear -- and the Evil Twins jump you, take the crowbar, and
# dump you unconscious in the Mysterious room (`ACT type=1 v1=0 v3=28`).  That
# is not a losing state, it is the intended way in:
#   touch east wall   T95 -- a hairpin, in the dark, from the author's own hint
#                     ("TOUCH EAST WALL then head WEST to escape")
#   west              picks the closet lock with the hairpin and drops you in
#                     the lower south corridor (29), where the Twins are playing
#                     cards in the storeroom to the west with your crowbar
#   talk to guard     T104 -- "He is actually very willing to leave if you TALK
#                     TO GUARD".  He goes to the car park to smoke, and takes
#                     the red cigarette lighter with him; the fireworks
#                     storeroom to the south is now unguarded.
#   pick lock         T106, with the hairpin -- one firecracker.
#   THEN WALK INTO HIS ROOM AGAIN.  Stepping into the north corridor makes the
#   guard bolt back to his post, and he DROPS THE LIGHTER in the north end of
#   the car park on the way.  That is the only way to get it: there is not one
#   task in rooms 36/37, and `take lighter` while he is still holding it is
#   "Take what?".
#   light firecracker T111 in room 29 -- the Twins run upstairs and leave the
#                     crowbar behind in the empty storeroom (33), west.
#
# THE MONSTER'S ROOM IS DARK AND HOLDING THE TORCH IS NOT ENOUGH.  The
# flashlight is in the locker in your own locker room (23) -- `open locker`
# first, `take flashlight` alone is "Take what?" while it is still shut -- and
# it has to be switched on: `turn on flashlight` fires T67 and, conveniently,
# also carries the pending `east`, so the transcript reads "You move east and
# turn on your flashlight."  T122/T124 both carry `RESTR type=0 v1=13 v2=1`
# (flashlight held) on top of everything else.
#
# THE FINAL MATCH IS ON A CLOCK.  `throw acid at youth` teleports you into the
# ring with EMPTY HANDS, and 38 events start firing: `star attack 1..8` at turns
# 3-4, 7-8, 11-12 ... 29-30, and `star gets out chain` at 36.  The seven-command
# finish below lands the win on match turn 7.  `spray star` sets the `blinded`
# variable, which `EVENT 14..20 [unblind star]` clear again **4 turns later** --
# T260 needs `blinded == 1`, so the crowbar has to swing on the very next turn.
# The author says it plainest: "You need to go outside, look under the ring, get
# the fire extinguisher and crowbar, go back inside, spray Star, and hit Star
# with crowbar."
#
# 120 commands.  No RNG is consulted on this route, so no `SCR_SEED` is needed;
# `SCR_SKIP_WAITKEY=1` is mandatory (three `(Press a key)` pauses: the Monster's
# piledriver, the Promoter's street-fight offer, and the celebration).
