The Magician's Niece

   Uncle Maynard was the only magician in our family - heck, our extended
family for four generations.  In fact, we were the only family in town that
had a mage of any rank.  Shortly after I turned 12, Uncle said that I might
have the talent also - would I like to learn magic?  Would I?!  Of course I
jumped at the chance and at 16 I am doing very well at elemental spells (not
elementary spells - I finished them at 13!), and can cast more advanced
spells with a quick read in the Magician's Tome.  Oh, by the way, if you have
any questions, just "ask Ariana about..." - that's me of course!

   Today is Saturday and I had finished my homework (from high school that
is) and am just enjoying the sunshine pouring into my bedroom.  Not sure what
I want to do yet.

Bedroom
  Dad knew that a magician sometimes practically lives out of their study, so
he knocked down the wall between two bedrooms to make my bedroom-study.  I
have my bed against the closet wall corner and the night stand right next to
it.  Under the window to this bedroom, I have my study desk - I know, mom
says I daydream too much looking out the window, but it's so nice to look out
at the backyard when studying gets too much.  Between the windows on this
eastern wall is my palantiri. The opposite closet was converted into recessed
bookcases for all my books; not just magic, but all my books.  In that corner
of the room is the Magician's Tome on its book stand.  It gets just the right
amount of light from the windows especially with a full moon to have that
magical aura.  The bedroom doors are opposite the windows and I have my
dresser and brass coat rack located between them.
  What's cool about the doors is that Uncle and I made them into magic
portals - I just have to know where I am going, otherwise it's out to the
hallway.

Hallway
This is the upstairs hallway.  Pictures of me and my brother when we were
younger are along the stairway down.  My room doors are on the eastern wall,
the bathroom door to the north, and my brother's room to the west.

  “Mom!”, I yelled down the stairs.

  “Ariana, have you made your bed yet?”

  “Moooom!  Uncle just called, and I need meet him!”

  “I suppose that your curfew is out of order tonight then.”

  “I don't know how late I'll be.”

  “That seems to be the general way for you magicians.  Alright, be safe and
try to let us know if this is an extended mission.”

  “Love ya!  Gotta go!”

Mage's Hall
  From what I've experienced, most magicians disdain a fancy appearance, so
this hall is kinda flashy.  It has eight sides with marble pillars at each
corner supporting a ring of low windows under a copper dome.  The windows
cast light on the middle of the room where a brass sundial sits.  Alcoves are
in the north wall - for the communal palantiri - and the northwest wall - for
the Magician's Tome.  The northeast wall has the exit to the street, and the
door looks like it's out of a medieval movie.  The other walls lead to
classrooms and offices.  Both wooden and marble benches form two concentric
circles around the center of the room.

  “…Yes, what you are saying is true…    …Fine, but I'd rather her be a
normal teenager and enjoy her youth before sending her on tough missions.
…She is skilled, more so than most of the other students, but…   …Alright,
but I still don't know why you want me to…   …(Fragglesticks!  Diplomacy my
ass.)…  …I'll be there right after I brief Ariana.”  Uncle is leaning against
the palantiri, staring into the communal crystal ball - yep, this one really
is crystal - but I don't think he is still in communication with the council.
 I politely wait for him by one of the marble benches.

  “Ariana!  Sorry to drag you down here on a Saturday!”  Uncle runs over and
grabs me in a bear hug, which I give him one back.

  “You know I like doing magic and running errands.  Especially missions!”

  “But you are still young and should be with your friends.  Maybe even a
boyfriend?” he says with a smirk.

  “Uncle!”  I start blushing.

  He sits down on the bench and gestures next to him, “Sit with me, child”  I
don't take offense at him calling me that because I know he says that only as
a term of affection, not that he thinks I'm just a kid.  I sit next to him
waiting for him to talk.  Uncle sighs and stares off in the distance, not
saying anything for a while.

  “Uncle, why do you always say 'fragglesticks' instead of swearing?” I ask
to satisfy my curiosity.  He has a quick shake of the head, grins, then
bursts out laughing.

  “You know, I never really thought about it.  Swearing is a bad thing to do,
but 'fragglesticks'…”  He shakes his head and rests his hand on his chin.
“My mentor says it, and his mentor said it.  I suppose someone somewhere just
came up with the word!  Means nothing.”  Uncle turns his head and looks me
right in the eyes.  “You are an amazing young lady, Ariana.
  “And that is why I have to send you out on a mission for me.”  His face
quickly turns somber.  “I have been very suspicious of the activities of Mage
Dresdan.  I cannot put my finger on what he is up to, but I believe many
things are coming to a head tonight.  The counsel disagrees with me and wants
me on what they call a 'diplomatic' mission.
  “I fear that sending you to continue to investigate Dresdan may be
extremely dangerous, and would rather you enjoy your youth.”  I start to
argue, but he continues to talk.  “I also know you very well,” he says with a
slight smile, poking a long finger on my nose. “You have just the right
amount of confidence in your talents - not overconfident to put yourself into
trouble, and not under confident to be hesitant to take action.  You have the
imagination for new spells, like that PDA of yours.  Maybe we can implement
that for the younger generations - though we still insist that all students
have a hard copy of the Magician's Tome.”

  Uncle places his hands on his lap, then swings a leg over the bench to face
me directly.  I turn to face him sitting cross-legged.  “I am not able to
approach Dresdan's home directly as he has a powerful ward blocking any magic
from entering except himself.  All I know is that I have seen several
disreputable characters coming and going.  It is my thought that since you
are young and not as powerful in magic, but imaginative, that you might find
a way to find out what is going on inside.  Don't risk a direct
confrontation!  Even if he is not doing any harm, he still might be annoyed
enough to hurt you.  Yes, I said to come quickly, but don't act rashly.
Remember your own wards, attacks, spells, and incantations.”
  He looks at me as if debating if he is going to tell me more.  “I need to
teach you another spell.  You know how to shape-shift into animals you've
imprinted on.  Now you need to know how to shape-shift into another human.”
Uncle jumps up and gives me a hand up from the bench.  “First you need to
know that the person you are copying must be asleep or unconscious.  You do
know the sleep spell?”  I nod my head.  “Concentrate on everything you see
about that person - don't forget the clothes!  We don't want you going around
starkers.”  We both laugh at the mental image.  “Picture that firmly, just
like when you imprinted on the animals, then close your eyes and place your
fingers on their forehead.  Then recite the phrase 'her2mind'.  If you don't
know their name, make one up that you will remember.  You now have the
imprint for the shape-shift.  To make the change, remember the imprint with
your eyes closed and fingers on your forehead, reciting 'me2her'.”  He sighs,
and places his hands on my shoulders.  “Try to charge up on magic as much as
you can.  This spell does take a lot out of you and does not last as long as
an animal shape-change.”

  Uncle starts walking to leave the building.  “We both need to go.
Dresdan's home is in the downtown quarter near the old firehall.  You can't
miss it with the 'sight'.”  He closes his eyes with a slight smile, “I have a
feeling you will do fine.  Godspeed, Ariana.”  He then leaves me to walk out
of the building onto whatever mission the counsel sent him on.

  I think mom was right - this is going to be an all-nighter!
