With a flourish, as always, Boss hushed the audience with a finger-to-lips.
When they finally quieted, the boss announced the next act:

"With the help of a little elf, I will pull a rabbit out of this hat!"  So,
Boss reached into the top hat and shouted, "Arbadacarba!"

That was my cue!  Of course, everyone expects the bunny...

Inside the Top Hat

A bunny twitches its whiskers at me from down here.
A rainbow necktie lies twirled all upon itself.
Also, a mouse trap lies about (y'know, to keep mice out).

Boss's hand rummages around inside for something, but can't seem to reach.
Also, there's one of Boss's old socks in here.

I pop up for a second of fresh air.  Boss looks horrified, but the audience
goes crazy, clapping and laughing.

"Oh, hi, folks!" I smile, waving ecstatically.  Then I see Boss.  "Gee, you
don't look too happy, Boss."

"What are you doing up here?" Boss asks.

I try to explain that Frubbles is tired of the old show, but Boss won't have
any of it.

"The bunny!  I need the bunny!"

I pop up for another second of fresh air.  Just one more.  I also try to
explain for a moment just how hard it can be to find a rabbit in a hat.
These magic hats are awfully roomy.  Awful lot in 'em.  Did Boss know how
long the socks had been in there?

The audience's chuckles throughout, but the boss's eyes roll at the ceiling.
With a sigh, Boss repeats the order to get that darn rabbit and shoves me
back down!

I leap out of the hat to get just one more word to Boss, but trip on the brim
on my way out and send the top hat careening to the floor, spilling out all
its contents to the bemusement of the audience.  Boss and I take our bows,
but then he sends me to clean it all up.

I love this part.  The spotlight's on me as I stuff Boss's things back into
my house, and waddle offstage sideways, bowing (and thanking) and bowing (and
thanking) and bowing...

But will the next show go the same way?
