House Of Horror

Local legend ha it that this old house is haunted. The story goes that the
last owner was a mad scientist, obsessed with his own immortality. He used to
experiment on anything he could find, including other people.
They say that one day he succeeded, but before he had time to reap the
benefits of his labour, he came to a mysterious and bloody end. To this day,
they say you can feel the cold, chilling presence of his ghost, trapped
forever in this house of horror, and if you listen very carefully, in the
dead of night, the groans and moans of his last victim echo out into the
night.
You on the other hand think this to be a load of old bull. The only stories
that interest you are the ones saying he was stinking rich.
So here you are, in the middle of a stormy night, torch in hand, ready to
nick anything of value.
