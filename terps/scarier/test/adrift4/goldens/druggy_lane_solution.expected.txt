DRUGGY LANE

Welcome to DRUGGY LANE. This is a simple game based on the very popular game
DOPE WARS.The object of the game is to repay your debt of $25000 in 30 days,
starting with $5000, and get as much money as you can by buying and elling
drugs. Here are the different commands you can do.

[Press any Key to Continue]

You can:
+Buy [number] [drug]
+Sell [number] [drug]
+Take out [number] loan
+Pay back [number] loan
+next day, or end day, or day
+deposit [number]
+withdraw [number]

[Press any key to continue]

I hope you have a fun time in DRUGGY LANE. You can make a transcript, and
email it to me if you do very well. Also, if you find any bugs, or have any
ideas, comments, questions, or thoughts, you can email me at
pauldboswell@yahoo.com

THANK YOU, AND HAVE FUN!
