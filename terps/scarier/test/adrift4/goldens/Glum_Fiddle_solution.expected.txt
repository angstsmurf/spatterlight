Tiny Gatehouse
All around you gorse, gnarled trees and tough, spiky grass struggles to grow.
A tiny cottage nestles between a couple of large boulders. Blackberry vines
trail across the boulders and the mossy roof of the cottage. A sign in front
of the cottage has two boards on it, one pointing at the cottage saying
'Gait-Huose', and the other pointing to the east toward the entrance to a
large cave, saying 'Caive'. The signs were painted in a rough hand with black
paint.

Glacier
A small glacier has slowly and achingly pushed its way down the mountain
face, ending here just east of the pigsty. Dirty crags of snow jut up between

ground.

Your parents are sitting on a furry rug on the ground, bound hand and foot

Dear Diary,

Today I rescued my parents from a giant. Glum Fiddle, as he turned out to be
called. First I met a very strange dog indeed...

...

...and after an uneventful trek through the forest, and a ride in the back of
a baker's waggon, we made it home with time for mother to whip up some

After the adventure of today, I can safely assume that Price Charmless will

as soon as possible. Of course, he will not be allowed to come and visit
until the awful splint is taken off my father's arm, the wooden brace from my
mother's neck, and the bandages around my ribcage, but I am happy to wait.
