Where is Richard?
Written by Rich Dersheimer rich@dersheimer.com

You are worried about your friend Richard. It's been over a week since you
have seen or talked to him. He wouldn't leave town without telling you, and
he usually calls you on the phone at least once a day. You've left several
messages on his answering machine, and you even sent him a couple of e-mails.
Now that the weekend is here, you have time to drive over to his house and
see if he's there.

His car is in the driveway, and you park behind it. You are about to knock on
the front door, when you notice that it is a little bit open, just a crack.
With a sinking feeling, you push the door open and stand in the doorway.

"Richard?" you call out. "Are you home?"

The house is quiet. You take a step into the living room, and as you do, the
door swings shut behind you and the deadbolt slams into place. You try to
turn the deadbolt and open the door, but neither will move! After a brief
moment of panic, you start laughing.

You shout, "Hey Rich! Good one buddy! You had me going there for a sec."

There is no answer...

> pull handle
You pull on the handle and there is a blinding flash of light. When your eyes
recover, you notice that you are now in a different room. Rich is sitting on
the floor, grinning at you.

"Hey bud! What kept you?"

Rich laughs as your jaw drops and you fail to come up with a snappy answer.

"Just kidding, man. It sure is great to see you. I knew you would come
looking for me if I went missing. But, as you can see, I'm having some
trouble getting around."

You notice that Rich's right leg has a makeshift splint on it. The look of
worry is apparent on your face.

"Hey, I'm okay. Really. I just banged up my knee when I was running back here
from outside."

"But Rich," you say, "What's going on? Where is this place? How did we get
here? Can we get back home? WHAT is going on?"

"I'll explain everything when we get back. And we WILL get back. But there's
just one little problem. I brought this device with me, it's a recall beacon,
and we need it to get back home. The thing is, see, I must have dropped it
outside somewhere, 'cause when I made it back here, I didn't have it. So...
you have to go out there and find it, bring it back here, and then we can use
it to get home. It shouldn't take you very long to find it, I didn't go far.
Just don't mess with the giant spiders. That's what got me in trouble. I was
trying to get a good look at one of them, and I think I pissed it off. It
chased me all the way back here."

"Uhh, err," you stammer, "what if it's still out there?"

"Well, it's been a while, I'm hoping it wandered off. Regardless, we've GOT
to have that device to get home. I'll draw you a map of the surrounding area,
and I'll mark where I think I dropped the beacon."

He pulls out a marker and a scrap of paper, scribbles for a few moments, then
hands you the map.

"Let me warn you, though, it's kind of weird out there. It's not like
anything we're used to. Just stay focused, get the beacon and get back here,
and we'll be home before you know it. You made it this far, and I know you
can do this. I'm going to try to rest and conserve my strength."

He smiles confidently, and suddenly you know everything is going to be okay.
Just get the beacon, and then go home. A piece of cake...
