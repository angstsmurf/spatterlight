# Glum Fiddle -- C. Henshaw, ADRIFT 4 (Writing Challenges Comp 2006).
# WON, 100/100 -- all ten of the game's ten-point tasks fire.
# No author walkthrough exists; Key & Compass publishes a map only.  Route
# derived from SCR_DUMP_TASKS.
# The opening is a menu: "game" starts play (the other options are the
# introduction, the credits and RESTORE).
# The dog asks three riddles.  "say cow" / "say sandwich" / "say ache" answer
# them, and TASK 10 opens the iron gate -- but that is NOT enough to go east.
# TASK 13 ([e/east] in the Tiny Gatehouse) also requires TASK 18, the Courier
# encounter, and the courier is only summoned by TASK 11 [say %text%], the
# *wrong-answer* task.  So "say hello" (any wrong answer, after the three
# right ones) is mandatory, and the courier turns up three turns later.
# At the Pile of boulders Glum stands over his belongings; TASK 19 refuses to
# hand anything over while he is in the room and TASK 20 makes him snatch it
# all back if he catches you holding it.  He walks off to the swamp on the
# fifth turn after you arrive, which is what the four "wait"s buy.
# The hessian sack is the counter-measure: TASKs 21/23/25/27/29/33 all carry a
# "NOT inside container 0 (the sack)" restriction, so anything stowed in the
# sack is safe from him for the rest of the game.  The plank has no such
# restriction (TASK 31), which is why it is dropped across the crevasse at
# once.  Objects are pulled back out of the sack one at a time, immediately
# before they are used.
# "take branch" has to reach TASK 44, not TASK 19, or the branch is picked up
# unlit and the lamp can never be lit; taking it on the turn you arrive (while
# Glum is still present) is what makes TASK 19 miss.
# The five chase counter-measures, in the order the pursuit needs them:
# doily -> unravel -> tie to the stalagmites at the Cave entrance (else TASK
# 70 #Trip kill); swamp slime in the teapot -> poured on the Sloping tunnel
# floor (else TASK 71 #Slime kill); china cups dropped on the Mountain Ridge
# (else TASK 74 #mountain kill); silver tray dropped at the Cliff (else TASK
# 76 #cliff kill); cushion thrown off the Cliff (else TASK 90 kills you on the
# way down).  Each is on a 3-4 turn timer once Glum trips, so the endgame runs
# without a spare move.
# "open gate" in the Pigsty is the tenth scoring task and easy to miss -- it
# lets the pigs out, which is the lie you tell the Ogre at the stalagmites.
# The eight "wait"s in the barrel cover EVENT 13's 5-8 turn random delay
# before Glum steps on the cracked plank and falls into the chasm.
# Blank lines are <waitkey> presses: one before the menu, two after
# "say sandwich" (the dog encounter re-prints itself), one on the third
# "wait" (the courier), two after "jump" (the diary and the score).
