Outside the Medical Centre
The Rushmore Medical Centre is situated in a very grim and desperate part of
town. Standing at the end of a long and high brick wall lined alley, it has a
less than inviting appearance. About three floors in height and made from
grey concrete blocks, the centre seems to be surrounded by various mounds of
rubbish. A strong looking security door is to the north and the highway is
back to the south.
A wide-eyed hungry looking security guard is watching you.

> exam rubbish
Hiding amongst of the smelly mounds is an old house brick and a broken knife,
the latter of which you pick up.

> get brick
You take the brick.

> throw knife at guard
Using as much force as you can, you thrust the knife repeatedly into the
chest of the security guard. Making a gurgling sound he collapses to the
ground.

> get key
You take the silver key.

> unlock door
You unlock and open the door.

Foyer

As you enter the foyer you notice that a crowd of people has appeared

> exam someone

> talk to assistant

the floor he says, "I'm sorry I thought, well I thought you might be one of
them."

You are about to ask who one of them are when he continues with, "I'm think
I'm infected, some crazy woman upstairs bit me! I need the cure."

> ask assistant about cure

> ask assistant about artemis
Calming down for a moment he continues with, "We developed a cure for the
pestilence called Artemis.  It is made up of four chemicals, I think the
formula is in the office upstairs."

> talk to assistant
Waiting for he to calm a little you ask, "What happened here?"

"I was working in the lab when everything just went manic. The staff started
attacking each other and destroying everything."

You are about to ask for more information when he continues with, "Some crazy
woman bit me! I'm think I'm infected, so I need the cure."

> talk to assistant

Dining Hall
In your experience the dining hall is a normally a great meeting place. It is
somewhere to take a well earned rest and spend time chatting with friends and
colleagues. Usually it can be one of the busiest places in any organisation;
here though it is strangely quiet. The room is arranged with bench like seats
either side of several long tables. To the west is a corridor and south is
the kitchen.

Kitchen
After the rest of the centre the kitchen seems to be a very pleasant
surprise. Rather than depressing it has a light, bright and warm feel to it.
After a brief look around you have the impression that no actual cooking
takes place here, as you can see no cooking facilities. The only equipment
seems to be a fridge and a worktop fixed to the wall.  Above the worktop, at
about head height, is a small mirror. So it is likely that it is just a food
preparation area rather than a traditional kitchen. The dining hall is north.
