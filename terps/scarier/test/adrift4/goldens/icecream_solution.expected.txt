  Entry for the One hour Mini-comp.

  You are a new employee in the Ice Cream Shoppe. Your job is to scoop the
ice cream, put it onto a cone and give it to the customer. Simple, right?

(Please press any key)

Ice Cream Counter
  You stand behind a small counter in the ice cream store. A long line of
people extend away from the counter and out the front door. They all want ice
cream.
  A box of cones is nearby, and your boss Ronald watches you.
  You have an ice cream scoop in one hand and a slightly sodden, chipped
empty cone in the other.
  Ronald is here.
  A customer stands before you, holding a mighty dollar and waiting for your
attention. You look at him with vacant eyes, and as you wipe the drool from
your lower lip, he says, "I'd like an ice cream cone, please."

> take cone
  You already have an empty cone.

> scoop ice cream
You reach down and get a scoop of ice cream.
  "What's taking so long up there? I've got to get to a funeral!" some guy
shouts from the end of the line.

> place ice cream in cone
  Holding the cone in one hand, you place the scooped ice cream carefully on
the top with the other.
  The ice cream falls on the floor!
  "Hey!" the customer shouts. "I'm waiting here. Can'tcha be a little more
careful?"
  "Wilbur! Clean that up!" Ron shouts.

> give cone to customer
  You reach out to give the customer an empty cone. This infuriates the
customer and he leaves in a huff.
  Ronald scowls.

> quit
Do you really want to quit? [Y/N]
