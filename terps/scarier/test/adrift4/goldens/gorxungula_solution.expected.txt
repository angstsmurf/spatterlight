Yes, Lophese... above the absurd voids have you lived a happy life in faraway
Rabbits until that day when a misspoken word birthed the spirit of
Gorxungula.

With one magical word, Gorxungula stole your voice and began his reign of
terror over beloved Rabbits.

Clever Lophese, there is only one cure for the evil curse-- you must bring a
sacrifice of spirits and gold to Elder Moose, the Great Antlered One who
lives beneath a new state of being.

Find these items and put them in Elder Moose's revered wooden tub to purge
Gorxungula's curse!

Rabbits
In your beloved home a fluffle of familiar leporids float through the void
like lepidopterans, each blindly cavorting over or around the other.  The
rabbits take deep notice of your presence and occasionally giggle.
Gorxungula hovers just inches over your head, seething with translucent
blue-black steam.

The wretched spirit begins to chant some sort of horrible incantation at the
rabbits.  "Behold-- I am your master, Gorxungula, Lord of the Void and Taker
of Voices!  None have such aweful power as mine!  Silence thy will, for
nothing is thine!"

A New State of Being
In this new void, the purest sense of newness overcomes you and your skull
flushes with a dizzying plurge of new thoughts.  Uncertain toast is in the
background to the east and, if you so wished, you could descend from this new
state down to the state of being in the Void of Elder Moose.  Some new tug in
your gut tells you that you oughtn't to go west.

Uncertain Toast
The exact implication of "toast" in this context is unclear, therefore all
usages are prohibited until further research into its intended meaning allows
unanimous agreement upon one of the following definitions:

~v.: to heat thoroughly, esp. until crisp and brown as or by a Maillard
reaction
~v.:  to use a toaster or toaster oven
~n.:  sliced bread browned on both sides by heat (perhaps in a toaster),
generally eaten by itself or as an open-faced sandwich with butter, cheese,
beans, eggs, or any variety of jellied fruitgoods
~n.:  any of a variety of stylistic variations of the aforementioned food
item, including French, Melba, Texas, bagel toast, or milk toast (whose
blandness inspired the term "milquetoast," referring to a timid, apologetic
character)
~n.:  a person or thing to which a ceremonial drink is proposed or the act of
proposing such a drink
~v.:  to propose or partake in a ceremonial drink
~interj.:  a call to incite such an event as a toast
~n.:  one that is highly admired ("the toast of the town")
~n., slang: a thing unclear in meaning ("vague as toast," a spoof of Texas
toast supposing origins in Las Vegas, Nevada)
~n., slang: finished; done with; at a conclusion; terminado

     "What are you doing with that moose, Lophese?"
     The doctor's voice shocks you.  You don't recall being in the waiting
room, but there you are.  You try in vain to explain what has happened, about
the birth and defeat of Gorxungula.
     "That's quite interesting, Lophese," says the doc.  "Are you finished
with those papers?"
     You are.
     "Excellent," drones the doctor.  "Then you're discharged.  Been fun
working with you, but we're all done.  It's time to go home to your rabbits,
now."
     You hand the discharge papers to the doctor and absently find your way
out the revolving doors of the hospital.
     Reviewing your papers the doctor (for whatever reason, if any) mutters,
"Toast."
     Odd, not even the doctor knows why.
