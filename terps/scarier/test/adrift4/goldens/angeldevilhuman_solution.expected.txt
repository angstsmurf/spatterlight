The Angel, the Devil and the Human

By Benjamin Samuel

This is a twist that I hope you'll enjoy on the Fox/Chicken/Corn puzzle,
except you have to move the three characters from hell to heaven, instead of
from one riverbank to the other.

Instructions

To win, you must take the three main characters, Devil, Angel and Human, from
their starting point (hell), to the finishing point (heaven). But there's a
catch! You mustn't leave Devil alone with Human or Human will sell his soul
and you mustn't leave Angel alone with Devil or Angel will smite Devil. You
must find a way around these problems.

Controls

To make one of the characters follow you, simply type "take [character]". To
leave them in the place you want, type "leave [character]". You can only
leave the character in either heaven or hell. There's a middle area (earth)
where you can't drop the characters. You move by typing "up" or "down".
