An exciting in-media res intro would be here if this was a work of
interactive fiction. But this is most decidedly not interactive fiction.
Rather, this game is a hoary old-school text adventure where you solve
puzzles, collect and store treasures, and try to earn a perfect score. Press
any key when ready to play.
