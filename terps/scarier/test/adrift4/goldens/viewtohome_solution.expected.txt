A View to a Home
Welcome to A View to a Home. Your mission in this adventure should you choose
to accept it is to collect the gold, silver, and bronze medals and place them
in the trophy case. One word of advice, if in doubt, use the 'examine or x'
command to obtain tutorial advice on object usage and 'look or l' command to
get location and movement directions. Also, all the verbs and subjects used
in this adventure are mentioned in the adventure text in one form or another.
