The bell stopped ringing only a few seconds ago, but I've already made my way
to the front of the school.  I'm hurrying out of school even more than usual
because today I get to finish my project in the park.  As I run past the
other kids, I imagine the fortress on the cliff.  It's a fortress I built of
leaves and twigs and other stuff at the park, and it sits on a huge
moss-covered rock above the creek.  The fortress is over 12 inches tall and
has little shacks in the courtyard and even a tower.  Anyway it took me two
afternoons to build the fortress on the rock, and now that it's built, I get
to install the best part!

...

The drawbridge!

...

All I need is some kind of string or tiny rope or something and I can attach
it to the bridge. If I can find something like that, then the bridge will
actually raise and lower!  I bet I can find something that will work in my
secret stash in the park, and now that the rest of the fortress is finished,
today is the day to look!

"I don't have any lunch money.  We already had lunch today."  I try to sound

Vick smiles at me, still holding out her hand.  "Come on kid.  I don't
believe you got no money...why don't ya just give us what's left of your
lunch money?"

Baxter and Theodore bend over to inspect the fortress and I say to her "I
promise I don't have any.  Could you pl..."

"OH YEAH? Then show me your pockets!" she demands.

> show pockets

show them, I pull my pockets inside out.

"Haha, just trash!" says Theodore.

"Aw, that's really too bad." says Baxter.

Then Vick takes a step backwards, crushing the front of the fortress.
"Oops!" she says, pretending that it was an accident.  Baxter pushes her.
"You clumsy kid!" he says, and he kicks the fortress' tower, sending all the
pieces flying out over the creek.

"Stop it you guys!" I shout, but as if the fortress weren't damaged enough,
Baxter and Vick step aside and Theodore does a somersault landing right on
top of it. He giggles stupidly as he crushes it...

I stand there with steam coming out of my hot face for a second until Baxter
says "Go home kid.  Bring us your lunch money tomorrow and..."  but I can't
listen to another word...

"NO!  Instead!  I challenge you to a fight!  To avenge my fortress!  At the
playground, tomorrow after school...YOU BUTTHEADS!"
As soon as I finish speaking the words, I can feel my knees shaking...

"Haha, okay."  Baxter doesn't seem scared.
"Alright, kid, you got a deal.  We'll invite everyone!" says Vick, and they
turn to leave...
"You're dead meat, shrimp," calls Theodore as they exit the park.

I sit for a minute at the rock with the wrecked fortress, to calm down and
get ready.  I have a long afternoon ahead of me.  The only thing left to do
now is head home and get ready for tomorrow.
