"THE FOGGY BANANA ADVENTURE"
Three simple game controls:-

TALK (name)

INSPECT (name)

USE (name)

You are an old warehouseman working on the docks. It is early evening, and
the foghorns are hooting mournfully across the RIVER.  A ship's crew of MEN
are unloading banana BOXES from their ship and wheeling them into your old
cobwebbed warehouse ROOM...

> talk men
The MEN are surly and rough, and are reluctant to chat. They seem very
anxious to get rid of their cargo of BOXES. As they leave, you overhear one
say that they never wish to see banana BOXES again.

> inspect boxes
This box looks fine - there are many bunches of fat firm bananas.  The waxy
tropical yellow of the bananas contrasts with the dusty grey of the warehouse
ROOM.

> inspect boxes
You INSPECT more banana BOXES. As you open one, something furry and black
skitters out of the box and runs across your hand. It vanishes into the
SHADOWS.
SPIDERS start to crawl from the BOXES.  Big black tropical ones!  INSPECT the
ROOM to find a quick means of dealing with them.  You will have to be quick
(10 turns max)!

> use hoover
The old hoover starts up, the noise filling the warehouse ROOM.  How could
you ever think that this small old HOOVER could clean such a dirty old
warehouse?

> use hoover
You use the HOOVER to quickly suck up and thus capture all of the
lethal-looking tropical SPIDERS.  Maybe the authorities should be alerted
now?

> use phone
You pick up the old-fashioned PHONE.  There is a dial-tone.  But who would
you call?

> use phone
You dial the operator and TALK to the local ZOO. It's late evening, and a
weary voice answers. You tell him about the large tropical spiders and their
strange red markings.  He becomes excited.  Will you TALK to this man at the
ZOO further, or INSPECT the HOOVER to check that some of the spiders are
still alive?

> talk zoo
You TALK further with the man at the ZOO. He is a researcher working late in
the offices. You are startled to find that you are offering him a colony of
the rarest SPIDERS in the world, the wild Peruvian banana spider. He suggests
it would be fair to offer $10,000 to have the ZOO buy them from you, since
they are so dangerous to capture.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The rare Peruvian banana SPIDERS have been captured by you and sold to the
ZOO at a huge profit!  And without a single one biting you! Treat yourself to
a banana!  You can afford a new BROOM now!
