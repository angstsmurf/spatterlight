Terrified
A traditional text adventure by Eric T. Dorrath

Written for the NaAdWriMo (National ADRIFT Writing Month) Competition 2007.

Pausing in the middle of the street, nearly bent double, hands resting on
your knees, you try to catch your breath.

Pounding hard and irregular your heart feels as if it is going to explode
from your chest.  Light-headed and slightly disorientated, it feels as if you
are going to be sick.

You seem to have been running forever and have lost all knowledge of time.

Bailey Road
Being one of the more run down areas, Bailey Road is a rough, crime-ridden
part of the town. Lining both sides of the road are a series of rundown
houses, and although not that old they are showing years of neglect.
Depressingly the road runs southeast and west. Just to the north is an
alleyway called Thompson's Alley.

> n
You move north.

Thompson's Alley
The alley is just wide enough for two people to side by side, if they are
thin. Lined on either side by high sided plain brick walls, you can see no
windows, doorways or other openings. The ground is rough, broken concrete and
in the dark you would need to be very careful walking along here. The air
feels damp and has an unpleasant smell. The alleys leads north onto Devon
Road. To the south is a lonely street and east is a passageway known locally
as the Pit.

> e
You move east.

The Pit
As wide as one person the Pit has brick walls and an arched roof. A scary
place at the best of times it seems even more frightening today for some
reason. Used as a public toilet by the local drunks, it is pitch black.  The
light from the alleyway to the west and the small park to the east just does
not seem to penetrate very far.

> e
You move east.

Small Park
The park would probably once have been a pretty little place for a picnic or
a Sunday walk.  It is now overgrown and derelict, a dangerous place with any
original features such as the fountains and walks lost to decay. To the west
is the dark passageway and the south is a courtyard. You can still make out
an old gravel path to the north.

> s

Courtyard
The courtyard is enclosed on the three sides by various buildings and is open
to the sky. To the north is a small park. You can see a sad looking bench.
The building on the eastern side of the courtyard has a window, which has
been boarded up.

You can also see an old brick.
An old man is here.

An old man with a dog resting on a bench, stands up for a moment and looks at
you. His face changes from a blank expression to something like terror. He
leaves to the west as quickly as he can.

He did not like something about your appearance.
