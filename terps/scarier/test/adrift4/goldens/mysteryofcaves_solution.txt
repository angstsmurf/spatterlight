# The Mystery Of The Darkhaven Caves -- David Whyld, ADRIFT 4
# (Summer Comp 2004).  125/125, "Godlike Adventurer" -- the game's own maximum.
# The route is the game's built-in "short" walkthrough (type "walkthrough" then
# "short" at any prompt; "clues" gives a staged hint list), with one command
# removed: the author's list gives "give necklace to cyana" twice, and the
# second copy only answers "Give what?".
# Two <waitkey>s, the title and the instructions screen; there are no others
# in the game.
# 2026-08-16: the row sets SCR_SKIP_WAITKEY=1, so the pauses are transparent.
# They never were answered by the blank lines here -- until the fix, the pause
# read did not honour the "#" comment convention and ate this header instead,
# which is why the blanks below reached the game as ordinary empty commands.
# They are turns the route was derived with, so they stay.
# Scoring: 21 tasks award +5 each, and leaving with the diamond, ruby, pearl,
# jewelled rod and jade sceptre in hand adds another +5 apiece (TASKs 138-142,
# run by TASK 137 on the second "s" at the Entrance).  TASK 143 then picks a
# rank band; 125 hits TASK 144, the top one.
# The three jewels are bought from the gnome, so the run has to end with at
# least 120 gold: 55 from the treasure trove, 25 from the freed prisoners, 15
# from the young woman in the cage, 15 dropped by the dwarf when he sees the
# helm, and 5 each for the cello and the guitar given to the mad harpist.
# Two of the wandering hazards are disarmed by rings rather than solved: the
# Blocker's Ring (hidden grotto, behind the boulder-door -- "knock on door")
# stops the trap that makes the Painted passageway unreachable, and the
# Scammin's Ring (in the chest on the island) stops the random teleports fired
# by TASKs 9-12.  Wear both as soon as you have them.
# The dwarf of EVENT 0 steals the sword every time you enter the Painted
# passageway and dumps it somewhere else on the map; wearing Cyana's helm
# (its "exbsg sfqfmmfs" is a Caesar shift of "dwarf repeller") makes him drop
# it again along with 15 gold, which is why the helm has to come before the
# first trip east to Sir Pargus.
