Report Espionage
Welcome to Report Espionage, a dynamic adventure set within Piopio College, a
school principaled by Brian Tegg. Reports are due to be issued soon and
your's is hardly the stunning accomplishment your parents are expecting.
Obviously the only way for you to save your own skin is to collect the
various parts of your report and somehow destroy them. This task will not be
as easy as it sounds for you will have to play to the whims of the characters
as well as your own needs.

Good luck. You may just need it.
