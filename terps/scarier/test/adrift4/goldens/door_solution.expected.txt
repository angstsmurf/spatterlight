Door
A traditional text adventure by Eric T. Dorrath

Written for the InsideADRIFT Summer Competition 2008.

Your objective is simple.  Escape from the kitchen.

The Kitchen
You are in a reasonable sized Kitchen.  You look around and can see a single
lonely unit next to the cooker.  An old fridge seems to be leaning against
one wall with a washing machine next to it.  The window over the kitchen sink
is difficult to see out of.  A table is positioned in the centre of the room
and on the floor near the back door is a small dog basket.  On the wall you
can see a fuse box with a single switch. The door to the back garden south.

> open unit
You open the unit.  A packet of coffee and a glass jar are inside the unit.

> get jar
You take the glass jar from the unit.

> unlock door with jar
When is a door not a door?  When it is a jar!!!

You hear the back door unlocking.

> open door
You open the door. I guess it is now ajar.

> south
You head south. You have escaped.

THE END

You now have the option to either restart the game, undo your last move,
restore a previous saved game position or quit.
