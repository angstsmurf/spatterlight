You must escape!

Now!

Maybe this hadn't been such a good idea after all. Next time you want to
sabotage an enemy base, you should be more careful. Although only if you can
get past the guards during your escape and there is a next time.

The laboratory
You have destroyed all of the experiments in this laboratory. There is
shattered glass and pools of liquid on the workbenches and floor. Ashes from
the burned notes drift around the room. You have succeeded in your mission,
but unfortunately you have set off an alarm,  which you can hear ringing
loudly. Enemy agents are searching for you right now, and this room has only
one exit to the west.

> west
When you leave the room, a guard spots you. Your plan is becoming more
disastrous by the minute. There is nowhere to escape, so you will have to
fight your way out of here.

(Press a key)

The guard collapses dead on the ground. One enemy down, too many to go. You
cannot keep fighting your way out like this. You have to find another way,
but how? Well, you are confident that you will figure it out soon and survive
this experience. Until you do though, all of the guards should watch out, as
nothing is going to stop you from escaping.

As you pause, you notice that the alarm has stopped ringing. You are not sure
why, but you may have more time now.

Southern yellow hallway
You have noticed during your mission that the complex is colour coded, with
this area having a hallway that is painted yellow. Why there are different
colours, you do not know. Maybe it was to prevent the guards from becoming
lost. The corpse of the guard you just killed lies on the floor here. There
are laboratories to the east and west, with the hallway continuing to the
north.

> search guard
You search the corpse and find a yellow keycard, which you take.

> west
You move west.

The other laboratory
You destroyed this laboratory earlier, with the destruction here mirroring
the destruction of the other lab across the hallway. Well, apart from the
minor detail that you did not set off an alarm in here, like you did in the
other room. You have to keep reminding yourself not to get careless near the
end of missions when you start to relax and think it is almost over. The only
exit is back to the east.

You can see a bottle of acid.

> take acid
You take the bottle of acid.

> east
You move east.

You have noticed during your mission that the complex is colour coded, with

> north

Northern yellow hallway
To the north the yellow painted hallway comes to an end appropriately at a
yellow door. This door leads to the centre of the complex and one step closer
to freedom. Next to the yellow door is a switch, and a cabinet that has an
emergency label stuck to it. You an also return to the laboratory area to the
south.

> pour acid on cabinet
You pour the acid on the lock. It dissolves enough to allow you to open the
cabinet. You throw away the now empty bottle of acid.  Inside the cabinet is
a green keycard, which you take.

> turn on switch
You turn on the switch.

> open door
You unlock and open the yellow door with the yellow keycard.

> north
You move north.

Complex centre
This is the centre of the complex, with the four main areas divided by
colours that are shown by the door leading to each. There is  an open yellow
door to the south,  a closed green door to the east,  a closed blue door to
the north,  and a closed red door to the west. You know that the red door
leads to the exit to the complex, as that is where you entered. Before the
alarm went off all these doors were open, but the alarm closed them all
automatically. Keycards have been placed in hard to find areas to minimise
the chances of enemy agents, like you, escaping.

> open green door
You unlock and open the green door with the green keycard.

> east
You move east.

Western green hallway
This hallway is painted green, which isn't a surprise, as it matches the
green door to the west that you walked through initially to enter the
hallway. You think that this is the office area, and there is an empty office
to the south. On the northern wall is a tools cabinet. You can hear voices to
the east, so it would probably not be a good idea to continue walking down
the hallway.

> open cabinet
You open up the tools cabinet, but you are disappointed to see that there are
not many tools left. The only tool you can find is a screwdriver, which you
take.

> south
You move south.

Empty office
By the absence of any papers, books or other office supplies, you guess that
no one has worked here for a while. There is no obvious reason you can see as
to why it is empty, so maybe someone is due to move in here soon. Or possibly
another thief got here first.  There is a table and a bookcase along the
eastern wall.  On the western wall near the top corner is a large closed
vent. The exit is back to the north.

> push bookcase
You move the bookcase under the vent.

> climb bookcase

> open vent
You unscrew and open the vent to allow you to move up into the ventilation
system.

> up
You move up.

> east

> east

Ventilation shaft 3

> open vent

> down
You move down.

Outside the vault
The main feature of this room is a secure looking vault door on the eastern
wall. There are 3 lights on the door, with 1 of them currently lit up. This
is the kind of vault that you would normally associate with top secret
materials. You should know, as your line of work sometimes involves stealing
these materials. Sabotage is more fun though.  A switch is next to the door.
There is also a table under the vent, which leads back up into the
ventilation system. To the north is a closed hallway door.

> turn on switch
You turn on the switch. One of the lights on the vault door turns on as well.

> up
You move up.

Ventilation shaft 3

> open vent

> down
You move down.

Medical office
By the first aid posters plastered over all the walls, you guess that this is
a medical office. There is no one here at the moment, and you hope that there
is not a doctor lurking around the complex. There is a cabinet that is
labelled as medical supplies, and a small bed in here. A closed door leads to
the south. A vent above the cabinet leads up into the ventilation system.

> open cabinet
You open the medical cabinet.  A medical kit is inside the medical cabinet.

> take medical kit
You take the medical kit and apply it to yourself. Soon you are feeling back
to full health.

> up
You move up.

Ventilation shaft 6
Through the vent you can see a medical office, with a closed door. The
ventilation system continues to the east, west and south.  You could also
move down through the vent.

> west
You move west.

Ventilation shaft 8
Through the vent you can see an empty room, which looks like a guard's
bedroom. The ventilation system continues to the east.

> open vent
You unscrew and open the vent to allow access to the room down below.

> down
When you drop into the room, a guard enters simultaneously. His reaction to
the surprise is faster than yours, and he pushes you against the wall. He
then handcuffs you, and tells you that you are going to be locked up for a
long time.

You laugh and kick out at the guard. Finally your years of kickboxing
training can pay off. It might be helpful, but you don't need your hands to
fight. The guard snarls and says that he won't take you in alive after all.
