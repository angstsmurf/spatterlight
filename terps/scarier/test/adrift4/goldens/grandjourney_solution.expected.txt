"Once you know the name of the enemy you have to fight, you can attack them.
You have to type something like: "Cut Mr.Enemy with Sword." Typing something

 You will do damage to the enemy depending on how much stronger than them you
are.
 Battle with enemies is very strategic. You never know how often an enemy
will attack you. Once you attack an enemy, you will be able to tell if you
can defeat him. If the enemy is too strong, you will be told that not much
happened when you attacked them. If you can defeat them, this message will
not appear.
The most important part of any battle is checking your Status. You can check
your Status by typing: "Status" You will be able to see your health(Stamina),
strength(Hit Strength) and your defence power(Defence). This is shown as
numbers. The higher the number, the more chance you have of winning. Your
Stamina is the one you should pay most attention to, because once this number
reaches Zero, you die. You can also check the Status of your enemy to show
the same information for them. You can do this by typing the name of your
enemy, followed by the word Status: "Mr. Enemy Status" This will show you how
much stronger or weaker they are than you. When you finally defeat your
enemy, a message will appear saying that they have been killed.
 How much damage you do to an enemy depends on how much higher your Attack is
to his Defence.  For example,
if your enemy's Defence is 6, and your Attack is 7, you will only do 1 point
of damage. Same as if Your Defence was 0, and the enemy's attack was 6, he
would do 6 damage points.
Be careful when fighting an enemy. Most of the time you should check the
enemy's Status as soon as the fight starts, so you have some idea what you're
up against. You can check Status at any time. It does not interfere with the
battle. Be careful that you don't check the Status of an enemy you have
already killed, as this can be very confusing during a fight. If the enemy
can do enough damage to you in one hit (eg: you haven't got enough Stamina to
survive), you will be killed. If your Stamina isn't full (up to its highest
number), your first move should be to heal yourself using your Magic Seeds.
Winning a batlle relies on your judgement: when to stay and fight, and when
to run away. That decision could be the difference between life and death.
