# Story: it's Sunday, you wake up, and the only thing between you and video
# games is doing your laundry.  Objects are only referenceable once
# something LISTS them (the object-seen model) -- `x desk` before `get
# keys`/`get quarters`, and `x dryer` before `get clothes` on the second
# trip, are both required or the parser answers "Take what?"/"You see no
# such thing."  The in-building washer/dryer cycle is a scripted RED HERRING:
# the washer text explicitly says it comes out "out of commission" and sends
# you to the laundromat regardless of how carefully you run it -- you still
# have to spend all 9 quarters (4+5) and the wash/dry actions to advance the
# plot, then carry the resulting damp-but-dirty clothes out to a laundromat
# and feed its industrial washer 16 more quarters (of the 42 the game hands
# you) before an NPC (the old lady) bails you out and auto-teleports you
# home -- no `east`/`up` needed after `start machine` there.

# FOOTGUN: EVENT 1 "get robbed" is a real-time window (turns 7-75) keyed on

# warning beforehand.  `close back door` after every south/north crossing
# (re-`unlock back door` before the next one; unlocking also opens it, no
# separate `open` needed) avoids it entirely; the front door has no such
# timer.
