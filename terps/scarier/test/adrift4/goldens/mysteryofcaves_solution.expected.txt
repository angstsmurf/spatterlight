The Mystery Of The Darkhaven Caves
Armed with nothing more than a sword and a serious lack of common sense, you
decide to set off one day to solve the mystery of the Darkhaven Caves. For
decades they have been a source of unease in your local village due to the
many, many tales of unfortunates wandering into them and never emerging.
Supposedly a pirate once hid his treasure in the caves in ages past and
people, lacking in common sense perhaps every bit as much as you, have been
trying every since to find that treasure. Until now, no one has succeeded.
And no one has ever been seen again…

But now it's your turn.
