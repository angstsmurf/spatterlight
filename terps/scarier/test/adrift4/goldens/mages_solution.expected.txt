The Mages of Costrador
Welcome to the Mages of Costrador.
Your name is Killarmius the Dragonslayer.  You are living in a war-torn world
of dragons and domination.  Your quest is to find the ancient, magic, Sword
of the Dragons and bring peace to the land. You use this sword to banish all
of the evil dragons back to their native land of Millikari, from which they
escaped.  You will learn more of this as your quest continues.  To find your
total wealth, type "money", to find your magic rating and mana, type "magic",
to find your stats, such as health, hygiene, and hunger, type "stats", and to
carry on a conversation, use the format, "chat with (person)" and they will
give you important information in your quest.  When you kill wild animals and
monsters, you automatically pick up the carcass of the small ones.  You are
also helped in your quest by a loyal friend named Rowep.  He will follow you
anywhere and will help you in any fight you get into.  If there are big
monsters, you can just look at their bodies.  Good luck, and safe tidings,
hero.
