Space Run
You are a secret agent of A.R.M.Y. (Armed Reconnasance Mobile infantrY). Your
orders are to explore a newly discovered space ship that is drifting,
seemlessly lifless though warp space. You step into your uniform, gather your
lazer rifle and enter a drop pod. As you buckle yourself into the 'pod you
look at your comrades; two men and a woman occupy the drop pod. You gaze out
of the nearby viewing window and watch as two other drop pods blast off
towards the nearby floating ship.
 With a roar of thruster engines your drop pod shoots off from the station
and into the blackness of space. The velocity forces you all back into your
seats, you grit your teeth resisting the force of the 'pods speed. Then,
bracing for impact you grab hold of the belt buckles that strap you in by the
shoulders before your pod pierces the outer layer of the ship... you black
out...
 You are shaken up by one of your comrades, he has a slit in his forehead and
blood is trickling from it into his mouth. "The others are dead..." he
utters. "there was an electro magnetic pulse running through the ship. Our
pods retro rockets couldn't fire and we crashed through about five decks!"
You look up and stare into space. "Luckily" the man murmers, "the life
support systems are still online. I've no idea how they could still work
after the e'pulse..." He helps you up and grabs his laser rifle from the
floor. "The name's Adam." He smiles cheekily at the wrecked drop pod. "Let's
go!"
