Three Monkeys, One Cage

    You awaken to a harmony of hooting, grunting, and wheezing, played
against a background of muffled laughter and chitter-chatter in a language
sounding closest to, as far as you can tell, Latin.  These not being the
sounds your ears are normally greeted with in the morning, you let your
eyelids peel open with resignation...to discover that this ceiling is not
your ceiling, this room not your room.  The bed, however, is your bed.
Yawning, you sit up on it and gather in your surroundings.  Then you begin to
wish you could ungather them and go about the whole business a different way.

    It's dark all around, but not dark enough.  Encompassing all sides of you
a vast assembly of alien faces is looking in your direction.  Literal alien
faces, mind you.  Green heads, horns on head, goggly red eyes in triplets,
and every other cliche that goes with the breed.  They are talking,
snickering, shouting, eating live vermin, slapping each other on the back
with slimy tentacles, and generally having themselves a grand time.  It's a
gigantic ampitheatre from another world seating an inumerable host of
grotesque lifeforms originating from a galaxy you're sure no earthly
telescope has ever aspired to pick up!  And all agglomerated together to
watch...you?

    No, not just you.  And then you become aware of your immediate
surroundings, and of why it smells rather like a safari in here.  Your bed is
on one corner of a square dirt surface that marks the floor of a large
enclosure.  Without walls.  No, there must be walls and you just can't see
them.  Because you just hit the back of your arm against one en route to
feeling for your pillow (which is actually an old smelly jersey you stopped
wearing many years ago).  In a situation like this, one can really use a
pillow to bury the face in.

    But wait, I'm not done!  You need to know the reason why you cannot
afford to go back to sleep, no matter how sweet the slumber would be.  I'll
get to the point.  Two others share this space with you, a small monkey and a
big monkey.  The small one does not look like he especially wants to clobber
your face in to the point where an onlooker from behind can start to make out
clear impressions of your eyes, nose, cheeks, and so forth on the back of
your skull.  The big one *does*.  Also, several structures and contraptions
fill the periphery, begging your attention and scrutiny.  The whole affair
reeks of a puzzle.

> jump out
The time has come to fly this cage.
With your friend mattress that you have known to be trustworthy lying on the
ground directly below, your confidence in surviving this last plummet has
taken a gainful rise.  Looking down at the ground from the opening of the
cage, the mattress looks the size of a credit card, but surely it cannot be
difficult to plan your descent so that your major appendages do not miss it.
Yes, you've made your bed.  Now it's time to sleep in it.

Gripping the cord in one hand you make the fateful leap...and immediately
discover that the cord is far too short to cover the length of the drop!  The
wind goes out of you as you reach the end of it, the cord biting into your
chest like a boa constrictor doing the constricting thing, and you discover
yourself fluttering there in the air midway between dear ground and cursed
cage.  The distance you've covered so far is a mere third of the distance to
the ground.  But with your trusty mattress waiting for you below your
confidence and faith is boosted.  You untie the cord from your waste and fall
the rest of the way to where your mattress waits with arms outstretched.
And to the surprise of the entire alien congregation (which you are only able
to discern because of the single unified gasp that meets your ears) you land
right on it!  It breaks the fall completely.  Your bones are merely shaken,
but none broken.  Your breath is gone from you, but you are alive, alive,
alive!  And FREE!

    It has grown awfully quiet all of a sudden.  And what the hey, where'd
all the aliens go?  And the stands that were seating them, for that matter?
All those waving tentacles just flickered to be replaced by tedious blank
walls, as if the end of a cheesy home video had been reached.  You aren't in
an ampitheatre anymore, just an enormous circular room.  I repeat, what the
hey?
    As you are collecting yourself from the mattress you hear something
weighty bounce off it behind you.  It's the chimpanzee!  The drop didn't
injure him in the slightest because his kind learns how to take falls early
on (Juvenile chimps have been observed pushing their friends out of trees
just for the joke of it.  Kids can be so cruel).  The chimp seems to be
chittering and pointing to a small glass door set in the wall that you didn't
notice until now.  You walk across to it and pull it open slowly...to see two
old boys you recognize standing there in white coats.
    "...I didn't think he would make it.  I guess I owe you $10, Dr. Glee.
Oh dear, speak of the devil."  Dr. Wickett clears his throat.  "Er, aherm,

    There's an awkward pause before you put the question forth.  "Wh-where'd
