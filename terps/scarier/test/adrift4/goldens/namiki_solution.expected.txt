A Day In The Life 2: Namiki
My name is Namiki Imagawa, age 17. I am the daughter of a noble house with a
great tradition... well, so I am told every day by my grandfather.
Unfortunately, he's the one who makes the rules inside the house.

Tomorrow will be the first day of my second year in Wakayama High School.
Only one more day to enjoy the holidays. If I can leave the house, that is.
My grandfather is very restrictive.

Bedroom
This is my bedroom. It has the size of eight tatami mats, which is very
spacious. All furniture here is made from hand-crafted cherry wood and lushly
decorated with etchings. Several calligraphies I wrote are on the walls, and
a painting showing a flying crane is directly opposite the door to the east.
A 40 inch plasma screen TV with attached DVD player stands at the northern
wall, next to my stereo. My wardrobe containing my clothing stands on the
southern wall, flanked by a large basket box containing old things I don't
wear any longer and a lacquered chest with my traditional attire. On a nearby
shelf are my CDs and DVDs.
