Apprentice of the Demonhunter
Demons have been around for ages; as long as angels. And, naturally, they are
the polar opposite of angels, and they enjoy nothing better than bringing
hell to the Earth and to mankind.

Demonhunters have been around for almost as long. They enjoy nothing better
than taking that hell and whipping it right back into some demon's face. This
is almost your job. You are an apprentice demonhunter, almost ready to break
free from your mentor and start busting hellspawn to all four corners of the
world. Trouble is, you have to prove that you can, first.

Recently, you've heard of a small trade-route village--demons love these--in
the plains that has been terrorized repeatedly by a "great filthy foul vulgar
vile beast with sulphur for breath", as one smoking villager put it. Your
mentor has decided that this will be your final test, the one that
establishes you as a proper demonhunter. He has sent you off to this village
to kick some scaly tail. Put on a good show!

Outside the Village Wall
Destruction runs rampant here; a gigantic fissure splits the once-strong wall
into two, and the gate is missing entirely. The pitiful remnants of the
village lie north, through the wall.

A Field of Corn
You are surrounded by several acres of corn field. Many years of harvest,
drought, and erosion show here; the soil is crumbly and dry, and shreds of
plant fiber lie around. A nasty aura fills the air here. The remains of the
village are back east.
A serpent-like demon lies coiled here, panting furiously as though out of
breath, gigantic claws poised at the ready, fangs dripping with suphurous
venom prepared to shred somebody.
The demon suddenly spots you. You brace yourself for its swooping attack, and
roll to the side as its claw gouges the earth where you were a moment before.
Springing to your feet, you dash off to the village. Not knowing why, you
find yourself entering the church. The demon is obviously not impressed, and
it enters after you, blocking the exit. Looks like it's do or die.
