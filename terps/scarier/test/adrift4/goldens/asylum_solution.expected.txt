Asylum
Your eyes flicked open...

Who am I? That was the first thought that popped in your head. Everything was
a blank. You tried to think of anything: family, pets, vacations, jobs, but
every thought came back with nothing. You were lying in a strange bed wearing
light blue scrubs. The room around you was milky white, with a small window
sending light across the floor. You sat up on the bed and looked out through
the glass of the window. It was a beautiful sunny day out, but a large stone
fence almost blocked most of your view of the sun. A large plaque sat on the
fence just a few metres in front of your window. Engraved on it were the
words "Morningwood Mental Institution".

something hard, and pull out a screwdriver. It's a risk to carry it around,
but it may come in handy. You slide it into the pocket of your pants.

> use screwdriver on plug

"You idiot, you turned off the damn TV! I was watching that!"

He storms over to the TV, not noticing the unplugged cord, and begins to slam
the sides of the TV to get it working.

> n

leg of the chair. Dr. Walsh yelps as the chair collapses to the ground, and
he lands with a thud on the hard ground.

"Damn, damn, damn. I knew that would happen. I told them the chair was bad
quality, I told them. I'm going to go complain right now, demand for a decent
chair."

Dr. Walsh pulls himself off the ground and storms out of the room, determined
to get a new chair.

Front Entrance
You stand on a small balcony that overlooks the large entrance room of the
asylum. A small staircase winds down the side of the room, leading to the
ground floor.  Located underneath the balacony is a small desk, where a
security guard sits behind sipping a cup of coffee. Two glass doors on the
floor below seem to lead outside the building.

> put pills in coffee
You lean over the railing on the balcony, and aim one of the pills over the
cup of coffee. You drop it, and it bounces off the rim of the cup and onto
the floor. You reach into the bottle, and pull another pill out. Once again
you aim for the cup, and release it. It flies through the air, and lands with
a quiet plop in the coffee. After another few minutes, you have another 3
pills dissolving in the liquid.

A half hour later, you hear the loud snoring of the guard below. You look
over the edge, and see the guard sleeping peacefully at his desk.

> d
With the guard asleep, you slowly sneak down the stairs. When you reach the
floor, you quietly jog past the desk and through the glass doors.

The bright light of the sun blinds your eyes as you exit the asylum.
Everything goes white, and you scream out in pain. The ground underneath you
gives away, you feel yourself falling.

You land with a thud on the ground below. Although the ground is hard, you
seem perfectly fine despite the long drop. You open your eyes. The bright
sunlight no longer blinds. You're in a completely black room, with a faint
light coming from the corner.

A voice echos throughout the room. "Welcome."

You back away from the light. "Who's there?" you scream to the voice.

"I've been waiting for you. Waiting for so long," the voice responds softly.

You begin to get scared. "What's going on? Why am I here!?"

"Please. There is no need to raise your voice. All will be explained in due
time. But first, I need you to answer some questions. Do you understand?"

> yes
"Very good," the voice responds. "Now, answer me this. Who are you?"

> jacob
"Correct. But at the same time, incorrect. Do you understand?" The voice

You respond quickly. "No."

"Then it is time you get the answers you seek."

Your eyes flick open. You're sitting in the games room of the asylum. You
cannot move or speak. You can hear the voice speaking from inside your head.

"This is it. This is your reality. You're not Jacob Tanakian, you never were.
There never was a man by that name. He's in your mind. Everything was. The

"No...you're lying!" You think to yourself.

"You're the one lying. I do not exist, I am a part of your mind. You're
playing a game. This is how you have your fun, entering the subconcious of

Your brain reponds quickly. "But what about all the people I've met! Dr.

The voice chuckles. "Dr. Walsh and Nurse Lois are real. You created them just
like their reality halves. Everyone else was pieced together from things

was a name you heard when Dr. Walsh spoke of an e-mail he recieved, you were

You sit in the chair, perfectly still. Everything connects back in your mind.
You know the voice is right.

"Now, Mr. Jesus James," the voice continues. "Would you like to stay in
