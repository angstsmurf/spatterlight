Where Am I?

Awakening, giving your head a good shaking while slowly getting to your feet,
 looking around, you ask yourself "Where am I?"

In a small room that is approximately 9 feet by 9 feet in width by 9 feet
high.  And without any doors!  "Whhhaaaaatttt?" you find yourself asking, in
mild shock.

Trying to remember what had just happened, you had just taken your appointed
seat at the 1st Annual Dumpster's Bakery Presidential Toast.  Looking rather
fetching in the More's 2 piece striped purple suit that you had picked up
earlier in the day for this event, you were careful in not having the suit's
arm sleeve brush against the butter as you reached for your glass of water
and ...... that's all that you're able to remember!

Now - never mind why you're here but,.how do you get OUT of here??
