West end of the beach
The west end of the beach at Portobello, in many ways the poorer cousin of
the more popular end, due in part to the further reach of the tide here
meaning less expanse of dry, golden sands. Nevertheless popular with
dog-walkers and metal detector enthusiasts, this end of the resort is home to
the varied pursuits of indoor bowling, five-a-side football, and drinking in
the newly refurbished "Tides Inn" pub/bar- a late night favourite for karaoke
enthusiasts. The western end of the beach is also home to Portobello's
repository of graffiti.

Middle of the beach

You are standing in the central area of the beach at Portobello. To the
north, over the glassy but dark waters of the firth of Forth, the rolling
hills of Fife stare back. Picking your way through the driftwood and seaweed,
you ponder which direction to follow as you recall summers past at this exact
spot - the tanned drinkers who used to roll out of the now-demolished
Grannies Attic bar, the hordes of children and dogs and their ensuing
detritus. The beach stretches east and west. To the south, the Arcade invites
you by smell - a heady cocktail of fried sugar and gambling awaits...

Scotmid (and Post Office) - entrance

You are standing outside the main supermarket in Portobello - unless evil
developers have their way, that is - but that is another story. For now this
building is what Portobello has to offer - the Scottish & Midland
Co-Operative society store, also known as Scotmid. It's a distant
evolutionary relative of the modern supermarket, much like the missing link
sought by Homo Sapiens. Sadly left behind by the ravages of modern
capitalism, Scotmid stands like a proud (and poor) victorian Scotsman
suddenly deposited on the streets of modern Manhattan - alien, confused,
nostalgic and ultimately facing extinction. They are doing quite a nice soup
pack today where you can buy a leek, small turnip and carrot for the price of
1/3 of a trendy take-away latte.
