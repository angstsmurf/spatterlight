# Jonathan Grimshaw: Space Tourist -- Ren, ADRIFT 4 (Writing Challenges Comp
# 2006).  WON.  The game has no score at all (every task is worth 0), so the
# only end-of-game marker is the final "WHOOOOOSH".
# No author walkthrough exists and the game has no hint system; the route was
# derived from SCR_DUMP_TASKS (124 tasks, 10 rooms, one EndGame action -- TASK
# 68, "enter dustbin" on the alien bridge).
# Two title-screen <waitkey>s.  The row sets SCR_SKIP_WAITKEY=1, so they are
# transparent; the blank fillers went out 2026-08-16.
# Act 1, the cabin.  Break the porthole and feed the W.O.R.C.S. turbine
# through it: the spanner sets the ship on fire, then the cardboard bed makes
# the smoke that brings the fire door down.  "move chair to door" first, or
# TASK 21 never runs and the door seals you in.
# Act 2, the lift.  EVENT 1 needs three turns after you arrive before TASK 3
# ("#second tannoy") puts the dark-suited man and the elderly gentleman in the
# room -- that is what "x map" / "x sign" / "wait" buy.  Steal the old man's
# sock and his sweets, combine them into a cosh, and club the CIA type: he
# drops the suitcase (prawn sandwich + sellotape), the sunglasses and a
# pistol.  "peel wall" gets the sheet of foil, and only after TASK 24 will
# TASK 6 let you push the ceiling panel out.
# Act 3, the ventilation shaft.  Boots, the margarine off the silver tray, and
# the toolbox behind the metal shard (space tool, blowtorch, cigarettes).
# "unfasten blowtorch" splits it into the flint lighter and the oxygen
# cylinder.  Take the mud -- it is the vacuum-cleaner sabotage.  Rubbing on
# the margarine (TASK 33) is the only way through the narrowing tunnel.
# Act 4, the dining area and gift shop.  Take the steel dustbin, empty the
# fishtank for the goldfish bowl (do NOT "sing" first -- TASK 41 locks TASK 40
# out).  In the shop, drop the mud: the maid's vacuum chokes on it and she
# puts the hose aside (EVENT 8, 1-3 turns), and EVENT 5 drops a Cittian dollar
# four turns after you walk in.  Hold the hose and TASK 87 makes her give up.
# Act 5, the escape bay.  The vending machine wants "type <letter> <number>"
# as two words -- "type a1" is not parsed.  One dollar buys all four Space
# Bars (TASKs 52/53/54/56 chain off each other), and TASK 55 wants all four in
# hand at once before the large-bodied woman peels off her gloves.
# TASKs 59-62, which were meant to take the bars away again, test "Space Bar
# is in - No room -" and so can never fire; the bars stay in your inventory
# and TASK 67 refuses to launch you while you hold any.  "drop all" is the way
# out: it drops the carried items but not the worn ones, so the space-suit,
# bowl, boots, gloves and sunglasses -- all five of TASK 67's worn
# requirements -- stay on, and only the sandwich, the oxygen tank and the
# foil-wrapped dustbin need picking back up.
# Act 6, the alien planet.  "in" is the cottage and "e" the cave; in the
# hangar "up" matches TASK 94 (back to the valley) before TASK 97, so the way
# aboard has to be spelled "enter space-ship".
# Act 7, the bridge.  Sit, then press the three buttons -- the third blows up
# Jupiter and turns the alien murderous (EVENT 11, one turn).  The mirrored
# chute bounces his own laser bolt back at him; his head unscrews into a
# second helmet, and the microwave prints the Space Bar that TASK 68 wants in
# your hand.  EVENT 12 launches the escape pods three turns after you step to
# the mirror, and TASK 64 will not let you re-load the chute until it has.
