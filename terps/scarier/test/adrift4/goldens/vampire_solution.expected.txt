The Vampire with a Conscience
Version 1.0
By Ole Olsen

You are standing by your window in the Oslo Plaza five star hotel. The city
seems quiet tonight, almost as if it's inhabitants know who's visiting and,
wisely, decided to stay at home. It's the night of the annual grand vampire
convention. You, the representative of the Wienna delegation, are a 500 year
old vampire yourself. No one younger have ever been elected grand vampire.
But you take no pride in your position. Unlike most undead you have no desire
for power. In fact, you hate your existence. You hate all of your evil kind.
You might say you're a vampire with a conscience, however rare that might be.
You have fought for your position for one purpose only: Revenge. Tonight
you'll meet the worlds grand vampires, including the most evil and powerful
of them, the grand master vampire Igor van der Linden. And then, you will
slay them all.

You examine your watch. It's 22:00. That gives you 2 hours to prepare for the
meeting at midnight. That should be just enough time to carry through your
plan.
