# 15 tasks, 40 rooms.  No author walkthrough exists (plover.net lists the game
# without one); this route was derived from SCR_DUMP_TASKS.
# Result: WON, 60 points -- every point the game can award.
# The status line claims "a maximum of 2400", but that is an author typo: the
# dump has exactly five point-awarding tasks and this route fires all five --
# open door +15 (TASK 0), shicknaw +5 (3), tie rope to rock +5 (6),
# push river button +20 (10), give water to unicorn +15 (13, EndGame win).
# The `score` on the second-to-last line reads 45, so the finale is worth 15.
# Ordering notes:
#  * The golden necklace in the oak dresser is the "special key" the front
#    door wants (RESTR type=0 v1=3 -- held by the player).
#  * The special herb grows on the red vine at the Tree Top, four rooms south
#    and up from the Hidden Trail; the wizard in the Lab trades it for the
#    blue crystal, which is what makes `shicknaw` work.
#  * `shicknaw` is the only way into the cave half of the map -- no exit
#    leads from the Cliff to the Mouth of cave -- so the healing water has to
#    be collected at the Cliff BEFORE saying the word.  (`wankish` is the
#    return trip, restricted to rooms 26-39.)
#  * Three deaths are one keystroke away and all are avoided here: `eat herb`
#    (TASK 1), `drink water` (12) and `give water to dragon` (14) each run an
#    EndGame(lose).  The unicorn is the correct half of the Lava room.
#  * `tie rope to rock` opens the descent from the Cave Entrance, and
#    `push river button` drains the river bed to open the way south.
