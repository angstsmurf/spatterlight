We are coming to get you!

by Richard Otter, version 1.0

It really is so much fun being a germ. Not only do you travel the world from
one flesh-sack to another, you get to meet your millions of relatives and
breed. You have never been that sure what infection you are, but you are
pretty sure it is something really nasty. Yes, you really can't beat being a
germ.

Only this morning you began a new adventure, when your flesh-sack sneezed you
onto the hand of another.  Unfortunately you couldn't get through the skin
and had nearly given up hope. When, your victim got a tiny cut on its hand
poor thing!

Your objective? Infect every location you can find and then escape.

How to infect? Find a weak point and 'infect' away.

Press ENTER to continue

The Throat
You are in a smooth, slippery tube with a shiny pink lining.  From here to
you can go to the ear, nose, mouth or stomach.
Millions of your relatives are here.

> x me
You are basically a ball with spikes sticking out.

> x relative
Loads and loads of your family, all multiplying and infecting. It is a
beautiful sight.

> s
You need to state which place you are going to.

> x tube
On either side of the throat you can see the tonsils.

It sounds like something is being eaten or maybe the flesh-sack is taking a
drink?

> x tonsils
The tonsils help to catch germs entering through the throat.  "Well, they are
going to catch something today!" you think to yourself.

> infect tonsils
They are now Infected!
