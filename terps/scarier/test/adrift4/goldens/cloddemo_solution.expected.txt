Please note:

Clod's Quest: The Dungeons of Zivulda is best played at
1280 x 1024 or greater resolution.

If your monitor is running at a lower resolution,
you may wish to change it before continuing.

Although it is not necessary to play,
you may wish to engage the map function of your ADRIFT Runner
under "Options"... "Display & Media"... in the "Layout" tab.

If used, the map should be positioned at the right-hand side of the screen.

You will not need the graphics window.

Introduction

Long ago in the kingdom of Brelsamore, the terrible sorceress Zivulda
demanded that the king relinquish the throne to her zombie overlord
Gridlorch.  When the king refused, Zivulda threatened that she would cover
the sun with Vile Darkness forever if the king did not change his mind within
a fortnight.

At the King's orders, Heroes of the Crown have issued forth from Wendellwich
Castle to stop Zivulda, but none of them have returned from her horrible
dungeons in Mount Gravinwort.

When no more heroes were left and the end of a fortnight drew nigh, the King
turned to the College of Wizardry for help.  Wizards, however, have
traditionally been unbothered by politics.  Even amidst events as threatening
the impending Darkness, the wizards have vowed to maintain their apolitical
stance.

And so it was that, with the clock ticking down to the sorceress's deadline,
the wizards chose to send Clod-- the College of Wizardry's Second Archmage's
apprentice's janitor's apprentice-- to delve into the dungeons of Zivulda and
save the day with nothing but a sword and what few items he could fit into
his tunic.

The sun still shines on Brelsamore... but for how long?
