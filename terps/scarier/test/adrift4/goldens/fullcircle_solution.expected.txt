Full Circle

"Wake up."

The words are spoken as if from far away.

"Wake up."

Somewhere in the darkness, for all about you is nothing but darkness, a light
shines.

"Wake up," says the light.

You awaken...

The darkness, the emptiness, is gone. You are lying in a glade at the side of
a statue. A cool wind blows through the glade, ruffling your hair, making you
shiver.
   You climb to your feet and look around. There is nothing here that is
familiar, that you remember. Have you been here before? For that matter,
where is this place? What is this place?
   "You have questions, many, many questions," says a voice. It is the same
voice from the dream (if dream it was), but its source is impossible to
discern. "I have the answers. But you need to find them for yourself."
   "Who are you?" you call, moving about the glade. "What am I doing here?"
   "You are here because it is fated that you are here, because you always
return here. Always. Come find me. I will be waiting."
   A fierce gust of wind blows and the voice is gone. You are alone in the
glade.

difficult to tell what that something might be.

passing of the seasons? Or stolen by thieves?

something, the right arm is pointing at something... acting on a hunch, you
go to the point on the ground they are indicating and find, buried just under
the surface, caked with soft mud, a helm. Brushing the worst of the mud off,
you pick it up.

> x helm

sturdy enough. While unlikely to stop a forceful blow, this at least ought to
turn it from a killing strike to merely a wounding one. There is an unusual
glass faceplate on the helm, unlike any you have seen before. It is currently
raised.

> lower faceplate

Cave
[Score: 2/50] [Locations visited: 2/43] [Decisions made: 8]

EXITS: East

This appears to once have been a smuggler's cave judging by the old wooden
crates, almost reduced to dust by the passing of the years, piled in one
corner. The word "moonshine" is visible on the side of many of them. The cave
itself is not large, though a mirror, affixed to the west wall, gives it the
impression of being quite a bit larger than it really is. A tapestry hangs to
one side of the mirror, depicting an ancient scene. There is a grate set into
the ground.

> x tapestry
The ancient scene is one you are familiar with though you cannot say where
from. Your childhood perhaps?
   It tells the tale of a man, never named, who has to cross the path of a
ferocious beast. Knowing the beast to abhor poison, he first thinks to coat
his weapon with it, but rejects this idea in favour of another: namely, to
drink the poison himself. Thereby ensuring that even if the beast kills him,
he will in turn kill it. As it happens, the beast, upon sensing the man has
poison coursing through his very body, flees in terror from him, thus
allowing him to drink the antidote he has concealed nearby. The antidote, you
seem to remember, is nothing more than common water.

> open crate
You gingerly lift the lid on one of the crates, expecting to see its contents
decayed away to little more than dust-
   So imagine your surprise when a snake, reddish gold in colour, rears up
hissing out of the crate and sinks its fangs into your wrist!
   You cry out and stagger back, shaking your arm frantically to try and
dislodge the snake. It clings to you stubbornly, like a leech, but eventually
you manage to throw it to the floor where it slithers away out of sight.
   The wound on your wrist oozes greenish ichor.
   You have been poisoned.

> x mirror
You see yourself looking back out, but the you in the mirror seems...
different than you remember. Were you always so thin? Your face so drawn?
Your hair so straggly?
   It is only as you turn away from the mirror that you catch sight of
something previously unseen: a mark, easily mistaken for a bruise, on your
shoulder. In the mirror it glows a brilliant red.
