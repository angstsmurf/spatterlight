Your Bedchamber

Your Bedchamber
You're in your bedchamber which is a large, sprawling room filled with a
hideous mismatch of furniture, clothing and junk. Quite why you need such a
large bedchamber is a mystery but it's the done thing what with you being a
noble and everything.

Sunlight through a coloured window illuminates the room, banishing shadows
away from your bed, desk, bureau, wardrobe, closet and shower.

Exits lie: in and down

> x bureau
   You're not really sure what it is that a bureau does (hence this seriously
lame description) but you're now a lord and you imagine that a bureau is the
sort of thing every lord should have.  A small bell is on the bureau.

> get bell
You take the small bell from the bureau.

> open wardrobe
   You gingerly touch the wardrobe door and, sure enough, a whole mass of
items fall out onto the floor. Actually, it turns out only to be two items
but it definitely seemed like more at the time.

> get crown
You take the jewelled crown.

> open closet
   You gingerly reach out and open the door - sure enough, there is a cascade
of useless junk within that falls all over the carpet. Don't you hate it when
that sort of thing happens?

> get trousers
You take your trousers.

> wear trousers
   You put on your trousers.

> get bear rug
You take the bear rug.

> get lump
You take the lump.

> down
   You move down.

Main Hall

Main Hall
You're in the main hall of Castle Stroppenheim. This a huge chamber which
looks to have been hacked out of the side of a cliff by a builder with more
enthusiam than taste. Walls lean at weird angles and the ceiling, assuming
there is one, is so far above you as to be hidden in the murky shadows that
seem a permanent fixture of your new home.

Dominating the centre of the main hall is a partially-finished statue of you
that, one day, will magically greet everyone who enters your domain.

There are a number of corridors and passageways leading away from here but
quite a few are blocked due to building work being underway.

Exits lie: north, south, east, west, northeast, southeast, southwest, up and
down

nod before deciding to promptly ignore you.

Courtyard

Courtyard
The main courtyard of Castle Stroppenheim isn't a very impressive site at the
best of times, which this most certainly isn't. The dead (and still steaming)
bodies of a couple of your servants the dragon lunched on don't add a lot to
the decor and there is a definite atmosphere of unease floating around (or
maybe that's just the roasted bodies again).

A pair of broken gates lead southwards out of the courtyard and into the big,
bad world. Off to one side are the stables which, while a little charred, are
at least upright.

Exits lie: north, south, west and up

Stimmons your bodyguard and Doris the maid are here.

> talk to stimmons
   "An honour to serve, sir," remarks Stimmons and gives you a proud salute.
That he does so with two fingers sticking up straight in your face is, you
are sure, just pure coincidence. "The dragon shall be caught and killed, sir,
the next time he sets foot, er paw, inside the castle, sir. Me and the lads
guarantee it, sir."
   You glance about the yard and notice a distinct lack of 'lads'. "Where are
the lads, Stimmons?"
   "Ran away, sir. Cowardly little sods! They said they didn't get paid
enough to fight a dragon. I tried pointing out they didn't get paid full stop
but that didn't seem to motivate 'em to stay, sir, I regret to say. So when
the dragon shows up, I shall take him on myself, sir."
   "And, er, if you fail, Stimmons?" you ask. "What then?"
   Stimmons frowns as if the idea had never occurred to him. "Well, sir, then
I guess you get eaten up, sir. But don't worry, sir, I've been practising.
See the two practice dummies I've set up in the corner of the yard?"
   "I see only one practice dummy, Stimmons."
   "Hmmm. Funny. I guess my double vision must be coming back. Anyway... I
got the first of the practice dummies beaten to a standstill and was close to
handling the second one when my bad leg gave in and I had to take a breather.
Damn asthma, sir."
   "Does it affect you all the time?" you ask, not liking the idea of
trusting your life to someone with breathing problems.
   "Oh no, sir," says Stimmons.
   You breathe a sigh of relief.
   "Only when I fight, sir."

Guardroom

Guardroom
The guardroom of Castle Stroppenheim once served as the castle's cat room and
the smell has lingered despite the cats having since been eaten by the
monstrous rats that infest your crypt. Nowadays, this room is occupied by a
couple of guards (your only guards as it happens) called Bill and Ben who are
to keeping their room tidy what dragons are to not eating nubile young
maidens. As such, while you are sure there is a floor in this room you've yet
to actually catch sight of it amidst the piles of unwashed clothes, broken
weapons, rusted armour and all round litter.

Exits lie: south

Bill the guard and Ben the guard are here.

Stables
You find yourself amidst several large smelly horses in the stables. You've
never liked the stables much because, unlike back home, the animals here are

taken off.

in here aside from the horses and, of course, the ever present smell of a

Constipated Badger

Constipated Badger
You're in a tavern, that much is clear from the sounds of fighting, vomiting
and people complaining over the poor quality of the beer. The Constipated
Badger is the sort of tavern your mother warned you to stay away well from.
One wrong move here and your life, money and virginity will all be gone in a
flash.

A bar runs along one wall at which is what appears to a female troll serving
drinks. Several rousty layabouts sit at the bar exchanging insults. In one
corner is a singer murdering some power ballad. In another corner a group of
foul-tempered dwarves (then again is there any other sort?) are playing
cards. A noticeboard hangs to one side of the bar.
