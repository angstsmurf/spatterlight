The Crooked Estate
        by Duncan Bowsman

No-one lives in the crooked estate, yet one may wander alone in it without
feeling alone. Something, one might feel, must lurk in this thing, on the
other side of the door or behind its terrible wallpaper-- but one would be
wrong. A different kind of haunting infests it.

I first came in search of a higher place, but the estate's staircase never
ends. Countless rooms fill the crooked estate, but they only occur at
intervals between ascending and descending staircases. Every room is an
identical gallery filled with peeling wallpaper and forgotten puppets. By
staying, one might infuse the estate with secrets banal or awful, yet the
stagnant place cannot change and yields nothing. Where once I could hear the
promise of a morbid whispering, I strain my ear for the slightest of echoes,
hearing nothing but the occasional... slow... soft... crackling of a faded,
artificial wallpaper in the cold, cobwebbed shadows.

Here everything is lost forever. There is no thought and no solution, but
only the lurking sense of a meticulous, blank watchfulness and slow, certain
decay...

Gallery.
An empty box of flaking wallpaper, marked by human abandonment. Torn sheets
of dust lie across the walls hung with twisted frames. In the centre lie dead
puppets. I could flee up or down the stairs. The door is closed and though I
feel vaguely safer for it, it does not calm my interior sense of bizarre
misplacedness.

I shiver.

Gallery.
I am in a room which I believe to be an empty gallery. I had a plan when I
arrived, but cannot say what has happened to it, nor how long I have haunted
these halls in total. All about me in the unsettling shadow lie scattered the
disused bodies of clothy puppets & ragged cobwebs. Upon the faded, leaning
walls are hung empty frames, and etched tiny, tiny words. A crooked stairway
leads into darkness. Also here is a door (closed).

> examine puppets
There is a sneaking suspicion of unreal life in the puppets strewn across the
floor. One may wonder if it was they who wrote the words in the dust upon the
wall in their tiny handwriting. When in eternity might they stir from beneath
the weight of their dusty cobwebs?

1 puppet's jaw has fallen open, giving it a vacantly morbid expression. What
are they trying to say?

> examine words
Tiny etchings in the wall, in uniform shapes, half-drawn in cobwebs.

The writing on the wall speaks of conflicted delusions... some parts suggest
the creature will come soon... others that it never will... some that it
already has, and all that are left are threadbare puppets, their empty
frames, and the choking weeds of the estate's foundation, which has always
been crooked.

> examine frames
Presumably once regular frames contorted into twisted wrecks of brass,
scourged by erosion and verdigris. I cannot remember if they once or ever
held anything at all.

> examine stairway
The stairway leans at strange, uneven & dizzying angles as it climbs away
into dark spaces I cannot see.

I once had a plan for this place. Now nothing, I feel, remembers it.

> examine walls
Hoary, wrinkled paper with weird, sprawling designs (here & there, they look
like wilted peacock feathers, over there some bulbous fungal growth or a
rotting wheel) covers over the walls. In a few spots,the wallpaper hangs in
ribbons, in others it is either faded or stained by mysterious dark blotches.

Empty, twisted frames hang on the walls. In the dust are written tiny words.

> examine shadows
The shadows make every surface look pitted & sinister.

> examine cobwebs
I see nothing special about the cobwebs, except that they are everywhere and
cover over everything and there is no getting rid of them ever no matter how
hard I try. They hide & fill the crevices in everything.

The damn wallpaper crackles softly, verminously.

> examine designs
Blemished with hideous inconsistencies... and those regularities that I can
conceive of strain at nauseous, fleeting things which I cannot properly name
or order in any significant way.

> examine ribbons
They hang off the wall like the wilted dead. Shadow conceals the surface of
the wall behind them.

Some vague thing brushes by in my mind, but slips by when I attempt to grasp
it.

> examine blotches
I can neither tell what made these stains in such odd shapes nor what the
stains signify. They just don't look healthy.

> examine jaws
They hang open... clothy, listless, dusty.

> examine crevices
Filled with the bony dust of cobwebbed cloth. Across the walls. Upstairs.
Forever.

> examine flakes
Crumbled, brittle and dry as old leaves.

> examine door
An ancient & little used door. Most of its pale paint has peeled off, and the

safer for it, it does not calm my interior sense of bizarre misplacedness.

I once had a plan for this place. Now nothing, I feel, remembers it.

> up
I ascend the stairway, feeling my way with my hands through utter blackness,
but after a tedious while of apparently normal progress I find that you must
have taken a misstep or wrong turn on its weirdly angled slats in the
impenetrable dark-- and this continues until the path of the staircase ceases

what seems to be the same gallery I left.

The puppets all seem to look blankly upward for a moment, as if rolling their
tiny eyes. The head of another seems to shift and stare at me expectantly
from the shadows.

Did it whisper something? Does it know my plan? Or was that just the
wallpaper...?
