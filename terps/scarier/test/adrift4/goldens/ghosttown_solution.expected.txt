Having received a puzzling letter at your home address a few days ago from a
law firm you've never heard of before, you find yourself here in the midst of
the wood, where a friendly truck driver dropped you off before continuing his
trip across the state. As you wait for the lawyer to meet you, you start to
reach in your pocket to read the letter for the umpteenth time although
you've read the contents so often they're committed to memory.
Were you wrong to come here? You hope not!   Press any key

Outside cabin
Withered leaves and rotten twigs crumble under your feet as you slowly pace
back and forth outside the old wooden cabin.
It looks likes like it has been here for an eternity and the years hasn't
passed it kindly. The single window is broken, and the remaining glass is too
dirty to even look through, and you  get a creepy feeling that it stares at
you like an empty eye socket. The door leading in is nothing more than
rotting planks of timber.
Big patches of greenish moss cover the logs that make up the sides of the
cabin, and the roof is hidden under layer upon layer of leaves and broken
branches.  Nature has once again claimed her ground, and a narrow path that
appears to lead around to the back of the cabin is blocked by dense bushes
and shrubbery.
Although hard to see, there is still a trace of a path leading north into the
cottage, and back south towards the road.

> open door
Grabbing the handle you pull at the door. With a heartening scream from the
rusty hinges the door slowly opens.
A smell of rot engulfs you as the door opens and you feel like vomiting for a
brief second. A light breeze that must have been heaven sent washes away the
foul odor and you can breathe freely again.
You can now go in.

cabin.
Daylight filters through what's left of the glass in what was once a window
and casts an eerie dusty light inside the room. Every step you take whirls up

There isn't much furniture left in here. Two wooden boxes supporting two

on the walls you see the sad remains of some old posters, once probably very

once made up the drawers.
Behind you a gaping hole in the wall leads east to the kitchen, and a ragged
blanket covers an exit to the north, leading into yet another room.
