Thanks to Dave, Toni, and Tyler for feedback and/or trying it out!

Please note:
This game is meant to be played using the ADRIFT Runner, full screen, and
Arial size 12 font.
Any other configurations may have unintended results.

Part One: The Rocket Launch

You're flying a rocket up three miles into the air.  Since fuel is very
expensive, and you're on a budget, you've ordered only the exact amount
you'll need for the ascent...

Complicating matters, that personal budget of yours has also significantly
limited the amount of fixing up you could do on your rocket, such that it can
only handle a small amount of fuel in play at any time, and it may or may not
go through fuel at a slightly unsteady rate.  So, given the budget and your
impatience and whatnot, instead of just programming the rocket to feed itself
brand new fuel from its large spare tanks into its little fuel tank
automatically when necessary, you decided to get the show on the road and
make the call to add it in remotely each time, as you see fit...

If it runs out of fuel before making it up all three miles, well... you
know...  It will be an interesting experience.  Less exciting to tell family
and friends about, but, hey, you're trying out your very own advanced flight
vehicle here!  No sense focusing on potential things that could go wrong now.
 Eyes to the future!  You're launching this shiny new rocket of yours!

Be careful:  You start off with a full tank, but the rocket will descend
quickly if it ever runs out of fuel, and it takes a bit for any fuel to
process and be useful every time it's added.

[Press enter to continue.]
