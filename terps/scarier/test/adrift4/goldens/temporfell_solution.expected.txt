On the Beach
You are lying on one of the hundreds of beaches on the ocean pleasure planet
Belneta.  Beautiful men and women stroll up and down the beach as far as the
eye can see chatting about this and that.  The calm bay water sparkles in the
sun paving the way to a glorious ancient castle standing in the distance on a
crag overlooking the water.  The beach stretches for miles to your left and
to your right.  Somewhere behind you, someone is playing relaxing music that
is just your style.  You don't think you've felt this good in your whole
life.
