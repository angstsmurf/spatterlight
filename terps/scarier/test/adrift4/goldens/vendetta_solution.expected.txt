Introduction

You are Colonel Jem Bitter, a soon-to-be-decorated officer of the European
Defence Force (EDF). It is now two months since you managed to
single-handedly uncover and eliminate a far-eastern terrorist plot to
detonate a nuclear weapon in London. Unfortunately, your heroic act was
somewhat overshadowed by the recent death of the King of England. His only
heir, Princess Natasha, is to be crowned queen in two days time. After the
coronation, you are to publicly receive your medal.

At the moment, you are staying in a hotel suite inside the luxurious Dragon
Lithoid; a gigantic, dragon-shaped condominium in the heart of London…

When Sally reaches you, she throws her arms around you and squeezes you

"Are you hurt?" you ask her. She shakes her head, trembling a little.
"No...I'm just a little shook up, I'll be okay." Sally replies, looking back

"I don't know about you Sally, but I think we ought to give the police the

Natalia is the real monster, but I was the man who created her? You ask
yourself. No, I could not be the one responsible; that thing that threatened
to kill Sally was not the clever and sweet-natured Natalia I used to know, or
was it? Was it all just a deception? Perhaps some undiscovered memory still
lurks inside my mind that will allow me to answer that question...
With that, you lead Sally out of the Lithoid, wrapping her in a rug that you
saw in one of the other rooms; Natalia makes no attempt to stop you. Outside,
the snow has stopped falling, but it is still thick underfoot. "Thank you,

"If I risked my life, it is because you are my friend and I don't like seeing
my friends suffer; yours was a life that bitch had no right to take." The icy
wind picks up a little, causing Sally to shiver. "When I was created, any
desire I had for female company was erased from my mind, as was any fear of
death. They said that making me incapable of love would make me a more
ruthless soldier, but there is something I don't think either the government
or those who created me understood," you tell her. Sally looks into you eyes,
a little confused. "The purpose of being a soldier is to protect the things
you love, but why would anyone seek to defend something they didn't love? How
can someone go through their life protecting what has no value? The answers
came back to me today when you told me about how much you loved to dance and
again when I saw Natalia blubbing over that monster of hers. Earlier, you
said that the greatest feeling you had ever felt was knowing that you had
entertained and inspired those watching you, something I have seen you pursue
with the same passion that I have seen from only one other person..."
"Who?" she asks. "Surely not-"
"Yes, Natalia." you reply. Sally seems surprised by your answer. "How on
earth could she possibly love that monster she had created? Because it was
hers. No doubt she spent endless hours designing it, creating it and training
it with as much love as a mother would give to raising her own child. That is
why she was so upset when I killed it and why she just lay there when I
called the police; I could have killed her and I don't think she would have
even tried to protect herself. By destroying what people value, you deprive
them of the things they love and the life they live, then their lives become
meaningless. I think even Natalia has learned that lesson, even if she did
learn it the hard way..." A smile appears on her face. She understands. In
the distance, you see several pairs of flashing blue lights speeding through
the sky toward you; it is the police.
"So why are you depriving me of the thing that I love, Jem?" She asks,
standing in the snow.
"Why ? What am I depriving you of?"
"You." she says. Before you can react, her arms encircle your neck and she
kisses you. For some reason, that moment seemed to last longer than any other
in your life. Even your previous one.
