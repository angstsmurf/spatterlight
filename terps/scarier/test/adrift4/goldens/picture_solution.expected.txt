Conversation with a Picture

You are in a art museum and have come into a small room with some paintings.
There is a wooden bench in the middle of the room. Your eyes are directed
toward a small picture hanging on the wall opposite the bench. It's a
genre-painting depicting everyday life in a Dutch home in the 17th century.

***

> sit on bench
You sit down on the wooden bench and hear a voice coming from the picture:
 "How nice to get some company. Let's talk."
You can ask the picture - or just p - about several things. Anytime you can
ask p about "topics", to get a list of appropriate subjects to discuss.

> ask picture about bird
"The bird in the cage is a parrot".

> ask picture about parrot
"I am a parrot!"

The title of the picture is "The Parrot's Cage".
