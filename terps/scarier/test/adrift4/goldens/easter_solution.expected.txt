It's the week before Easter, and you've popped out of your office one day on
your lunch break.  Your mission?  To assemble an Easter basket that will both
delight and mystify your 4-year-old son Max.

You enter a nearby candy store, and the shopkeeper hands you a festive Easter
basket.  "No charge," he says, smiling, and leaves you to your shopping.
