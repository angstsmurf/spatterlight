Prison life didn't agree with Clueless Bob Newbie - for a start he didn't
have access to a computer 24 hours a day so his usual output of new games was
cut down to a measly half a dozen or so a week. On top of that he had to put
up with the indignity of sharing a cell with a man who thought farting should
be considered a national pastime and, worse still, the prison warden didn't
like his games! Sure, the guy was also a despicable thug who delighted in
pounding prisoners into mush for any supposed insult but CBN expected that
kind of treatment and would have felt there was something wrong if he didn't
get it.

[more]

The final straw? An article in The Prison Herald denouncing text adventures
as a thing of the past. It was all Clueless Bob Newbie could do not to throw
himself on the prison authorities in a mad rage and cause them to implode
(such a thing had happened in one of his games so there was every reason to
assume it would happen in real life).

He had to escape. He had to get out. The world was crying out for his
freedom. Well, maybe not out loud but he was sure that someone, somewhere was
missing his colossal output of two dozen high quality games per week.

He had to escape. It shouldn't be that difficult. After all, he had written
no less than fifteen separate games about escaping from jail, one involving a
banana and another with an emphasis on short French monks with a penchant for
swearing (the details escaped him right now but it had been a classic game
for its time).

It should be a doddle to escape.

YOUR CELL

You're in the cell you share with Hoggins, an obnoxious fellow who would fart
for the Olympics if they had such an event. Fortunately he's not here right
now. You see his bed, the dusty patch in the corner where you spend your
nights, a shelf containing nothing, and a door to the north.

> look under bed
Just a few motes of dust and… hold on! What's this? You ferret under the bed
and come up with… a ferret!
   Quite what Hoggins has got a ferret under his bed for you don't know but
from writing several games about ferrets - Ferret PC: The Trials of Birdie,
Alesian Ferret and Dark Ferret - you're well acquainted with such creatures.

> hold nose
You cover your nose as best you can.

> x bed
Hoggins' bed: even with your nose covered, it's still a strain to study the
thing without being violently ill. But you persevere and come across, among
other things, a comb. After picking away a few bits of Hoggins' scalp from
between the tines, you slip it into your pocket (the comb, that is, not the
bits of Hoggins' scalp).

EXECUTIVE DINING HALL

Executive dining hall is the official title of this room but the inmates more
usually refer to it as "the craphole". It’s one long room with a table
running down the middle at which are crouched several dozen inmates. Your
cell lies to the south while the prison warden's office is northeast and the
exercise yard to the west.

   "Hey!" mutters an inmate. "That one's got a ferret!"
   Save me! pleads the ferret silently.
   Or maybe he doesn't and you've just started imagining things.

> throw ferret
The inmates throw themselves on the ferret in an orgy of despicable
ferret-murdering. The ferret catches your eyes as it is forced down the
throat of a particularly large-throated inmate - and then it is gone.
   Every inmate in the room looks at the large-throated fellow who gives a
dismayed cry as they barrel into him in a desperate attempt to have the
ferret for themselves.
   "Typical!" mutters a guard as he hurries from the warden's office. "Feed
'em bread 'n' water for sixteen years and then show 'em a ferret and they all
go crazy."
