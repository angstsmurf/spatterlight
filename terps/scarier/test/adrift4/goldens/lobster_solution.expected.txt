Attack of Doc Lobster's Mutant Menagerie of Horror

Tonight the menagerie will be unleashed!  The insane experiments of your
collaborator and servant have created a fantastic array of Frankenstein
monsters to invade the city and destroy it, once and for all.  You have yet
only to give your first creature a name, superpowers, and life.

Please enter the name of the first species to add to your menagerie.

For example:

HORSE
EARTHWORM
FOUR NOSTRILLED ANT-EATER

> HORSE
Species #1: the HORSE is dead.  Now it lies on the dissection table, ready
for monsterification.  Proper procedure requires you must now refer to it as
"the subject."

(press any key to continue)

Lobster's Lab
It is here, from within your tank, that you plot the return of havoc to the
world.

Before you is The Servant's dissection table, upon which the subject lies,
ready for monsterification.  Leaping lightning lashes light the laboratory.
Your immense, weirdly-shaped claws reach all over the lab, ready to pinch
anything.

Also here is the pyrogland, a rubber chicken, some rainbow sprinkles, the
reanimating serum and the envenomator.
The mad veterinarian cackles to himself, awaiting your choice of instruments.
"Servant, let the monsterification begin!"

Under your telepathic command, The Servant opens up the subject to receive
your augmentations...

> take scalpel
You grasp the scalpel with one of your mighty pincers.

> give scalpel to servant
You press the scalpel into The Servant's gloved hands.

> take sprinkles
You grasp the rainbow sprinkles with one of your mighty pincers.

> give sprinkles to servant
You press the rainbow sprinkles into The Servant's gloved hands.
The Servant sprinkles rainbow onto the subject.  The colours, the colours!

> take envenomator
You grasp the envenomator with one of your mighty pincers.

> give envenomator to servant
You press the envenomator into The Servant's gloved hands.
The Servant puts all the poison onto the subject.  He channels your own
(telepathic) maniacal laughter.

> take serum
You grasp the reanimating serum with one of your mighty pincers.

> give serum to servant
You press the reanimating serum into The Servant's gloved hands.
The Servant injects the reanimating serum into the subject and, with
bloodshot anticipation, watches it twitch.

"It's alive... it's ALIVE!"
