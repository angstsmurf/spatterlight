Smote
by Robert Street.

You are the god Jimmy. You need worshippers. You have just found a promising
solar system. You will smite the bug-like aliens until they worship you.

Water World
This world is mainly covered with water. The aliens do not live in the water
through. They live on blocks of ice, which float across the oceans. There is
another world to the east.

You can see a comparatively small block of ice where aliens live, just under
where you are looking down.

> get ice
You pick up the ice and many aliens fall in to the sea. Lots die, as they
cannot swim well. The survivors fear and worship you.

You have smote 1 world into submission.

> e
You move east.

Volcano World
This world is centred on a volcano. The aliens all live around it, not
realising the danger. The volcano looks like it is ready to burst and pop
open at the slightest opportunity. There are more worlds to the east and
west.

> e
You move east.

Desert World
This is a very dry, sandy world. It does not look like it has ever seen any
water. The aliens who live on this planet look hot and bothered. There is
another world to the west.

A pointy pyramid is in the desert.

> get pyramid
You take the pyramid.

> w
You move west.

Volcano World
This world is centred on a volcano. The aliens all live around it, not
realising the danger. The volcano looks like it is ready to burst and pop
open at the slightest opportunity. There are more worlds to the east and
west.

> pop volcano
You pop open the volcano using the pyramid. Lava immediately starts flowing
out, destroying lots of aliens. The survivors fear and worship you.

You have smote 2 worlds into submission.

> heat ice
You get some lava, which starts to melt the ice.

> e
You move east.

Desert World
This is a very dry, sandy world. It does not look like it has ever seen any
water. The aliens who live on this planet look hot and bothered. There is
another world to the west.

> put ice
The ice slowly melts and floods a section of the desert. Many aliens die. The
survivors fear and worship you.

Ha! Ha! Ha! You have smote all 3 worlds into submission. Jimmy is a mighty
god.
