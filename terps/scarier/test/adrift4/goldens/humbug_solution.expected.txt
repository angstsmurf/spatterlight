Bah Humbug!  I hate the school holidays.  Every year it's the same.  Mum
packs me off to boring old Attervist manor. "It'll be good for you," she
said, "Anyway, you can keep Grandad out of mischief".  There wasn't even
going to be a chance to explore the old windmill at the bottom of the garden
- not in this weather.  Suddenly the taxi I am sitting in lurches around a
familiar corner and up the long driveway to Grandad's home.  A drunken line
of beechwood trees lead up to the manor shrouded in a heavy blanket of snow.
The large black taxi splutters to a halt, and I step out onto the crunchy
snow.  With a snort the taxi driver, chewing on a matchstick, drawls my fare.

HUMBUG.  By Graham Cluley.  http://www.grahamcluley.com/

The winter breeze whips its way round my shoulders, as I dig into my pocket
for the money.  The taxi driver doesn't look at me as I pass him a twenty
pound note.  "I've got no change for that", he says looking straight ahead.

driver stuffs the note into his grubby jeans and drives off.  I pick up my
