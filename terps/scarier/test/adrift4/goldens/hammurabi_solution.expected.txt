by Ron Moore (based on Rick Merrill and David Ahl's version in BASIC)

of your primary resources, land and grain, you must keep your people happy
and steadily increase your land holdings.  To win, Sumeria's population must
exceed 200 and its acreage must exceed 1250.  Your population, acreage, and
grain are displayed at the bottom of the screen.  For management notes, use
the 'look' command.
