Cloak of Darkness
A basic IF demonstration.

Hurrying through the rainswept November night, you're glad to see the bright
lights of the Opera House.  It's surprising that there aren't more people
about but, hey, what do you expect in a cheap demo game...?

> w
You move west.
The walls of this small room were clearly once lined with hooks, though now
only one remains.  The exit is a door to the east.

> hang cloak
You hang the cloak on the hook.

> e
You move east.
You are standing in a spacious hall, splendidly decorated in red and gold,
with glittering chandeliers overhead.  The entrance from the street is to the
north, and there are doorways south and west.

> s
You move south.
The bar, much rougher than you'd have guessed after the opulence of the foyer
to the north, is completely empty.  There seems to be some sort of message
scrawled in the sawdust on the floor.

> read message
The message, neatly marked in the sawdust, reads... *** You have won ***
You scored 2 out of the maximum 2!
That is 100% of the game!
Well done - you scored maximum points!
