The Timmy Reid Adventure (The Jonny Reid Adventure - Part II)
All that you remember is looking back at Jon as you scramble to hold on...
You begin to plunge into the swirling image of the cottage.  Your ears start
to pop and you hear what sounds like ocean surf.  Your face is brushed with a
salty mist.  You fall for what seems an eternity...

Suddenly, all is bright as your limp carcass slams onto the hot pavement.
The exquisite pain confirms that you aren't dead!  Just before your world
goes black, you collect the strength to raise your head from the searing
blacktop.  Your eyes roll up into their sockets and you think you see the
cottage at Old Orchard Beach.  Darkness...

You took a nasty fall, but with some effort you manage to pick yourself up
and brush off.  You unconsciously start picking small pebbles from your cheek
and shoulder and discover that your shirt is stained with blood.  The sun
beats down on your head and neck causing you to start sweating.  Your ears
suddenly pick up a faint sound like a mosquito's buzz.  The sound gets
increasingly louder and you realize that it's not a mosquito but a scream and
it's coming from above you.  You shift your feet and look up just in time to
see a body hurling downwardly toward you.  Instinctively, you raise your arms
to protect yourself and crumple to the ground after being slammed by the body
of a small boy.  You sit up stunned and turn toward the boy.  You both scream
at the top of your lungs as you realize that the boy is your brother, Jon.

So begins "The Timmy Reid Adventure"
