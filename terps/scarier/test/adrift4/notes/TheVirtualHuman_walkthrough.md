# The Virtual Human — walkthrough (**WIN**)

- **Game:** `virtual.taf`, ADRIFT 4.00, Duncan Bowsman, July 2008, after
  Jørgen Leth's short film *The Perfect Human*. A questionnaire: the program
  describes a virtual room and a virtual human, and you define them with one-word
  answers.
- **Result:** win, the one `ACT type=6 v1=0` (TASK 127, answering the virtual
  human's closing question), which ends with *"today, too, i have experienced
  something i hope to understand in a few days."* MaxScore 0, no `ACT type=4`.
  15 answers.
- **Env:** none. **The blank lines carry the route.** Each one answers a `[Press
  any key to continue]` `<waitkey>` (12 in all, checked with
  `SCR_MARK_WAITKEY=1`). If you delete one, the pause eats the next answer.

## Answers

colour `blue`, feeling `calm`, sex `male`, has feelings `yes`, feels `happy`,
age `30`, another verb `sing`, wants `love`, shaves his `beard`, thinking of the
room `yes`, main course `fish`, because he wants love `yes`, drink `water`,
tasty `yes`, and "why is fortune so capricious?" `because`.

## Win marker

`to understand in a few days.` It appears once in the TAF, in the closing text.
