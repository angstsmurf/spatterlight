# Monster Island — walkthrough (**best reachable state**)

- **Engine:** ADRIFT, first person. A Frankenstein/Dracula monster-hunting
  comic adventure: a wrecked-ship beach area feeds into a jungle/cemetery/
  village area, which in turn gates a molten lead/silver bullet-casting
  puzzle, a generator/gas mechanic, and separate Frankenstein/vampire-bat/
  Dracula boss fights — confirmed via `SCR_DUMP_TASKS` to be a large,
  mostly self-contained second half of the game.
- **Result:** the walkthrough clears the beach/creature/gate obstacles and
  stops at the entrance to the old cemetery, the natural checkpoint before
  the shack/village/Frankenstein content. Wired as
  `monsterisland_solution.txt|MonsterIsland.taf|And the beginning of a village down the road to the south.`,
  no env.
- **Content note:** the walkthrough never reaches the game's actual ending,
  where an NPC's sexual orientation is revealed in a throwaway line — no
  sexual content, no minors anywhere on this route.

## The walkthrough


Salvages the wrecked ship (a first aid kit's flares, a harpoon), then wears
down the beach creature with six repeated harpoon throws until it dies,
dropping a skeleton key. The key unlocks the jungle-path gate south into the
old cemetery, where this walkthrough stops.
