# The View Is Better Here — walkthrough (**WIN**)

- **Game:** `the_view_is_better_here.taf`, ADRIFT 4.00, by Echo. Dallas,
  22 November 1963; you are Lee Harvey Oswald on the morning of the
  assassination.
- **Result:** win, the single `ACT type=6 v1=0`: *"YOUR TASK IS COMPLETED. YOU
  HAVE...WON?"*. 18 turns. MaxScore 0, no `ACT type=4`. The only other ending is
  TASK 44 (`v1=1`, "You waited too long ... GAME OVER"), which is the motorcade
  timer running out.
- **Env:** none.

## Route

1. Bedroom: `take parcel`, `w` (you leave the money and walk to Buell's).
2. `talk to linnie` (Buell comes out), `put parcel in back seat`, `get in car`.
3. Two blank lines. The first answers the drive's "Press any key to continue"
   `<waitkey>`, and the second is the turn the drive hands back. With only one
   blank line, `buy coke` is eaten.
4. Depository: `buy coke`, `take coke`, `drink coke`, `open fridge`, `take
   lunch`, `eat lunch`.
5. `u` to the sixth floor, `open window`, `open parcel`, `assemble rifle` (this
   starts the motorcade event), `look out window`, `z` ("Now is the time."),
   `shoot kennedy`.

## Win marker

`YOUR TASK IS COMPLETED. YOU HAVE...WON?`
