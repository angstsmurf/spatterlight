Blocking exits demo
This sample demonstrates the two ways of blocking the player's movement.

The exit north will only show up on the map when the movement is allowed, whereas the exit south will show up all the time.

It also demonstrates a "Fake" exit off to the east.

Start
This is the start location.  There is an exit north to "One", and an exit south to "Two".

Both exits will initially be blocked.  To allow the player to move north, type "one".  To allow the player to move south, type "two".  Exits are east and south.

> n
You cannot move North until you type "one".

> s
You cannot move South until you type "two".

> e
Going East from Start is strictly forbidden.

> one
You may now move North.  (The exit should now also show on the map)

> n
You move north.

One
An exit leads south.

> look

One
An exit leads south.

> s
You move south.

You may now move South.

Two
An exit leads north.

> look

Two
An exit leads north.

> n
You move north.
