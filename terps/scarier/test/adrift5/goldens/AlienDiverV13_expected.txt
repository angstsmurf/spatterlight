# Alien Diver #

Overview:

On a scouting mission in a far flung solar system, your ship malfunctioned at the worse time just as you were in the middle of sling shooting around a planet called Ochora S7 (mostly an ocean planet).

After an intense fight with the controls you managed to avoid hitting the atmosphere sideways and eventually crashed into the (thankfully) deep ocean.
Your crashed ship now lies on the bottom of an alien ocean. It needs repairs, and scans have shown a certain raw materials can be used to create the needed materials needed to do the repairs in order to fly again. Your ship has been built to travel underwater. You awake floating lifeless in the deep murky alien ocean, you can feel the ebb and tow of the currents. You expected to be in your ship but vaguely recall leaving it to inspect the outer hull for damage for your computer system is only operating at 40% but got swept away by a current and knocking your head.

You will need to find where the ship is. There was hull damage but your force field managed to protect the ship from getting flooded completely.
The raw materials needed are colored fragments which are found inside alien made cubes scattered over the ocean floor. The ship's integrity is slowly deteriorating and your Suit Oxygen depletes -1 unit of Oxygen per turn, if either reach zero it is game over.

Read on for more details

##-- Press any Key to Continue --##

The Details:
Find these Cubes and roll dice to match the cube power number to deactive/unlock it and then EXTRACT CARD a Blank card from within it.
These cards can be crafted into a specific color card that matches the color and power number of the active cube upon which it was craft upon. You cannot do this on a deactivated cube.
These Playable cards are like a fixed dice, whereby you can play Card where an active cube is present that matches its power number and unlock the cube.
If the card also matches the color of the cube you get a bonus.
If you fail your roll to deactive a cube in your many attempts you gain 1 Crafting Fragment automatically each.  These Craft Fragments are required to Craft a Blank Card.

Crafting a Blank card has a couple of requirements.
1) At least 5 Crafting Fragments. You can only carry 15.
2) An active cube (ie. You haven't deactivated its lock)
3) Command: craft card on cube

How to get Colored Fragmented needed to Repair the Crashed Ship:

When you play a card instead of a dice that matches the power-number of a cube AND its color you gain a Colored Fragment based on the color of the cube (Red,Blue,Green,Yellow); these types of Fragments are not used for Crafting but are needed to repair your damaged ship.

Need more Oxygen?
Crafting Fragments can also be used to be converted into Oxygen with the Convert Fragments to Oxygen (or Con Frag to Oxy for short) command. Colored fragments cannot be converted into Oxygen.  Converting fragments into oxygen will use all crafting fragments at 1:1 ratio and use up ALL your held Crafting Fragments.

There are some Dangers out there- Currently there are two kinds the Orkallion and the Sqviper Sea Predators. You can attack them or play a blank card if it has the attacking sea creatures name on it - this will activate a AR Holo-gram of its rival and scare it away. You will lose your blank card but the AR Holo-gram will remain there.

Main Commands Recap: Roll Die, Extract Card, Craft Card, Play Card on Cube, show cards, play a card on CreatureName (See abbreviated commands in COMMANDS)

Use command HELP (some tips of gameplay and how to use the map etc) and RULES (how to play the game) or COMMANDS to learn more at any time. See Status bar for your Stats.
