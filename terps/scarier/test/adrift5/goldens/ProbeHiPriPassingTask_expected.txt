Highest Priority Task
This game exercises HighestPriorityTask execution. Try "get on chair" and "get on rock".

Room
A plain room with a rock and a chair.  Also here is a rock.

> get on chair
You sit on the chair.

> stand
(on the chair)
You can't stand on the chair!

> get on rock
You can't lie on the rock!

> sit on rock
You can't sit on the rock!
