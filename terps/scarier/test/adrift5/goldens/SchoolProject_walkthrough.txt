# are Nolanus, dropped down a shaft into Hades and working west past Cerberus,
# the Graeae, the Minotaur and the three goddesses.  UNWINNABLE BY DESIGN --
# every puzzle is solvable, but the last room (Medusa's Den) carries an
# unconditional System LocationTrigger task whose only action is EndGame Lose,
# and the "the exit!" location has no inbound movement at all.  The joke ending
# says so out loud ("everyone knows Perseus killed the Medusa").
#

# Polydeuces (which is what actually opens the west door), the nemean lion hide
# out of the Ares statue, and the apple of discord onto a goddess's palms.
# Note the parser quirks this exercises: "apple" alone is not a known word (the
# object is named "apple of discord"), and the goddess statues answer to
