This is a small "game" that was made to show what can be done

Great Room
A large room with a TV and a sofa  Exits are south and west.

> x tv
Just an ordinary TV but this one is broken

> x sofa
A small brown sofa

> sit on sofa
You sit on the brown sofa.

> stand
(on the brown sofa)
You can't stand on the brown sofa!

> west
(getting off the brown sofa first)

> x chest
You see no such thing.

> open chest
Open what?

> look in chest
You see no such thing.

> east
There is no route to the east, only south and west.

> south
You move south.

Bedroom
A bed room with walnut paneling  Also here is a small, white envelope.  Exits are north and east.

> x envelope
A sealed envelope  The small, white envelope is currently closed.

> take envelope
Ok, you pick up the small, white envelope.

> open envelope
You open the small, white envelope.  Inside the small, white envelope is a Note.

> i
You are wearing nothing, and are carrying a small, white envelope.  Inside the small, white envelope is a Note.

> read note
You have won!!

*** You have won ***
