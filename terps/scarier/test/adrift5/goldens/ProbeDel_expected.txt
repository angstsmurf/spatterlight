Del Test
Exercises ADRIFT del (delete last character).

Unflushed / style / br: in-fragment delete before Glk.
Cross-message: a del with no glyph in its fragment deletes from the turn buffer (pSpace join + prior message), as the Runner does.

Commands: unflushed, flushed style, flushed br, cross, help.

Delete Lab
A quiet room for testing del. Type help for commands.

> help
Commands: unflushed, flushed style, flushed br, cross.

> unflushed
Unflushed: abc + del -- expect ab
Result: [ab]

> flushed style
Flushed style: a + bold x + del -- expect a (in-buffer delete across style marks)
Result: [a]

> flushed br
Flushed br: a + br + del -- expect a (newline removed in-buffer)
Result: [a]

> cross
Cross-message: parent 8[aZ + child three dels -- expect 8[a]END8
Result: 8[a]END8
