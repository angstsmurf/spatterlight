Untitled

This is a tutorial game for Holiday, Migrate or Bust.  Welcome!

You can interact with items by using these commands:

Examine
Push
Turn on / turn off
Read
Take
Unlock
Open
Ask
Give
Go north / south

Items are referred to by their last name only, e.g. "textbook" instead of "your copy of the OCR textbook".

bedroom
You are inside your bedroom at home.  It is full of all your favourite things, and also your bag full of college work.  A desk is in the corner, next to your bed.  You hear your parent/guardian, who we will refer to as ATM from now on, humming in the kitchen.    An exit leads north.

> x desk
The beautiful desk is made from wood, highly polished and intricately carved.  What a lovely room you have.   Your top-of-the-range tablet is on the large desk, and inside is a brass handled drawer.

> x tablet
You need to turn it on first.

> turn on tablet
You switch on the top-of-the-range tablet.

> x tablet
Lucky you; a brand new, high spec tablet lies gleaming on the desk.  ATM must be extremely generous.  I wonder what else you could ask for?

> x bed
Nothing unusual to see here.  A fluffy pillow is on the tidily made bed.

> x pillow
A mostly comfortable pillow, but it has something small and metallic inside.    Inside the fluffy pillow is a shiny key.

> look in pillow
A mostly comfortable pillow, but it has something small and metallic inside.    Inside the fluffy pillow is a shiny key.

> take key
(from the fluffy pillow)
Ok, you take the shiny key from the fluffy pillow.

> x drawer
The drawer is in the desk.  The brass handled drawer is currently locked.

> unlock drawer with key
You unlock the brass handled drawer with the shiny key.

> open drawer
You open the brass handled drawer.  Inside the brass handled drawer is your shiny red pre-Brexit passport, a crisp £20 note and a Doreen Massey photograph.

> x passport
Your lovely, slightly younger self stares back at you wearing a glum, passport-suitable expression.

> x note
J. M. W. Turner looks up at you, whilst her Majesty faces the floor.

> read note
There is nothing worth reading on the crisp £20 note.

> x photograph
There's an inscription on the back... " if history is about time, geography is about space".  So poetic.

> take passport
(from the brass handled drawer)
Ok, you take the shiny red pre-Brexit passport from the brass handled drawer.

> x bag
Your "insert whatever brand is coolest*" college bag.

*Sorry, I've no idea what this is: I'm 42.    The college bag is currently closed.

> open bag
You open the college bag.  Inside the college bag is your bunch of keys, your designer wallet, a screwed up piece of paper, your copy of the OCR textbook and a huge ringbinder.

> x paper
Looks like that's been at the bottom of your bag for a while.

> read paper
Dear parents/guardians,

We're very excited to announce that this year's Geography fieldtrip is to New York City... blah blah... staying in a hostel... blah blah... valuable fieldwork experience... blah blah... need a passport... blah blah... £500 for 5 nights, all inclusive...

Yours,
Sarah Graham
Geography Teacher and trip organiser

> take paper
(from the college bag)
Ok, you take the screwed up piece of paper from the college bag.

> x textbook
Bright yellow and every bit as exciting as it sounds.

> x ringbinder
Despite courses being digitally based, there's still so much paper!

> x wallet
So pretty.  The designer wallet is currently closed.

> x keys
House key, garage key, pool house key, private gym locker key, key for your Ferrari (joke)... that's a lot of keys for a teenager!

> north
You move north.

kitchen
A regular kitchen, really.  ATM is happily humming over by the stove, there's a sink over by the window and a vacuum propped up against the wall.  ATM is here.  An exit leads south.

> x atm
ATM is wearing nice clothes for an old person. Let's hope they're feeling generous today.

> kiss atm
"Aww, that's nice, but I still can't afford that holiday."

> push vacuum
Ah, the floor is looking much cleaner now.  ATM will be pleased.

> ask atm about holiday
"Sorry, I can't afford that right now!  Maybe if you helped out around the house more I might think about it..."

> show paper to atm
ATM seems suitably unimpressed.

> give paper to atm
"I'll fill this in immediately.  You'll need your passport too.  Please bring it to me."

> give passport to atm
"Yay! Pack your bags, you're off to the Big Apple!  I hope that hostel is Instagramable."

*** You have won ***
Hip hip hooray, you're off to the Big Apple!
