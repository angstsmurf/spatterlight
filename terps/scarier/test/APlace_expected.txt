A Place
You awaken in a room, with no idea of how you got here. There's nothing in the room but a single chair.

If you've never played a text adventure game before, here are some helpful commands: "take ", "go (north, south, east, west, up, down)", "examine ", "say hello".
You can usually make something happen by typing it in, ex.
"put rock into box."
Remember to ask characters about relevant objects in the area, ex. "ask guard about room".
Your goal is to get to the Victory! Room.
Have fun!
