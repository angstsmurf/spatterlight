Dark basement
You wake up in a dark, wet basement. Judging by the crushed support beams, partially caved-in ceiling and damaged walls, this basement has been abandoned for a long time. There is a thin stream of light coming in from a tiny hole in one of the walls. The light lets you see some barrels in the corner and a wooden box of sorts.

> x barrels
In the corner of the room, you see some barrels. They have a skull and crossbones drawn on them and appear to be leaking a dense, smelly fluid.

> sit on barrels
So, you read the description huh? Curious chap, aren't you?

As you sit on the barrels, you feel a strong force moving through your butt. Then, all of a sudden, you are launched through the ceiling of the basement, through the first floor of the house, then the second, then through the roof. You keep flying for a few minutes, then start falling. Hard ground moves closer and closer, and then... you land. WTF did you think would happen? It's called interactive FICTION for a reason! You get up and... you're home.
