
Riddle Run, v1.0 | Hayden A. Schiff

Welcome to Riddle Run! In this game, you'll have to solve five riddles to escape. You advance one room each time you solve a riddle. To answer a riddle you simply type in the answer. You have an unlimited amount of time and guesses, but you can win trophies at the end depending on how fast you are.

TROPHY GUIDE
Time - Award
Under 2 minutes - Gold Trophy
Under 3 minutes - Silver Trophy
Under 4 minutes - Bronze Trophy
Over 4 minutes - Participation Medal

The game will start as soon as you answer this question: What is your name?
