> How do I use the reporting system?
To report posts. The violation must be in the post you report. Evidence may be given in the report that the violation is true if it is not so obvious, such as saying "it started a few pages back" or similar, but do not report a random post to report a post on the same thread or page. 

 - To report threads. Use this when the first post in a thread or the entire thread is in violation of the rules. For example, a thread clearly requesting illegal and/or copyrighted materials such as not-normally-free software, or a thread which is full of inappropriate pictures. 

 - To report guilds. The guild should only be reported if there are TOS violations going on in it. 

 - to report journals. You should only be using this button if the journal has a violation in it. For example, it has inappropriate images in it.

[Report A Hacking] -  If you have been hacked, use the botton located in the main forums. If you cannot access your account the best thing to do is to create a new account or use another account to report your own hacking. You need to do this as soon as you can and be as informative as possible. This will better help the moderators deal with your situation. 

[Report A Scamming] - To report a scam. Use the botton(located in forums) to report a case in which you were scammed. If you see a thread that you think may be a scam, and have evidence to support your case, use the [Report Thread] button instead. The Report A Scamming button should be used if you yourself are scammed. 

[Report Abuse or Harassment] - To report someone who is abusing or harassing you(use the botton located in the main forums). Please do not use this button to report others being abused/harassed because the person you think is being harassed or abused may in fact not care at all, and situations like that are very hard to deal with.

> How do you become a mod?
Moderators are invited to the moderation team by Administrators, the creators and maintainers of Gaia. Generally, the Admins pick users that have been respectful and helpful in the past and who show an interest in making the site a better place. There's no set proccess to be beome a Mod.

> What is a Mod?
The term Mod is short for Moderator. We help the Admins with user disputes, try to keep the forums clean and organized, as well as dealing with some account issues such as Hacking/Scamming.


> What kinds of mods are there?
Dedicated Gaia Moderators: handle forum moderation, Art Arena approvals/disapprovals, Link List moderation, Gaia Guild Network moderation, and more. Send signature removal requests, Terms of Service questions, and other general forum questions to these moderators. 

Global Gaia Moderators: handle abuse and harassment reports, as well as oversee moderation throughout the site. 

Omnimoderators: handle account issues, including hacking and scamming reports. Talk to these moderators about problems you may have with your account, items, and inventories. 

Administrators: are the creators, programmers, and developers of Gaia Online. It's generally not recommended to PM the admins, but if you have a question about moderator conduct in particular, you should contact Jakobo, L0cke, or Ling. 

Burgundy names: are not moderators, but are Non-Playable Characters (NPCs) that inhabit and interact in the world of Gaia! They usually take part in Gaia's main storyline, and in some instances interact with users through posts/pms! Gaia's NPCs include but are not limited to Gambino and Gino. 

Blue names: are not moderators, but users on your friendslist. Grey names are not moderators, but users on your ignorelist. If you have a moderator on your friendlist or ignore list, however, it will override the mod-color, and show them as blue or grey to you.

> What is considered hacking?
Hacking is any unauthorized attempt to gain access to a member's account. Some hacking maybe done through clever computer know-how, but most hacking is very low tech -- mostly involving scams where members are coerced into giving away their account names and passwords. It's also possible to gain control of your account if you are logged in and leave your computer unattended for long periods of time. 

Generally people attempt to hack accounts in order to obtain Gaia Gold and items. If you suspect that your account has been accessed without your permission and see some unusual trading activity such as: 


Trades that you did not initiate or authorize 

Trades with members whose names you don't recognize 

Inability to access your account

> How do I purchase a Mc?
Step 1. Go to the GStore and select the Monthly Collectible Items banner. 

Step 2. After you've done that, click on the GET EM button. 

Step 3-A. You'll see the GET GAIA STUFF page. If you're making a purchase for yourself ignore the To: field. If you wish to increase the quantity, simply change the 1 to another amount. Then, select ADD USER. 

Step 3-B. You'll be shown the total amount of items you'll recieve and the total amount you'll pay. If that's the amount you wish to purchase, please select CONTINUE TO CHECKOUT. Otherwise, if you wish to change your order please change the quantity and then select update cart. Same principals applies to adding another user. You must select Update Cart. 

Step 4. Gaia's Step 2 is Select Payment Method. That's where you'll decide which method you'll use to pay the Monthly Collectibles you're about to purchase. Below is a few screen shots of how it would look.

> What is Gaia?
Gaia Online is a virtual space "hangout" geared towards young people, though we welcome members of all ages. Among many other features we provide games, forums, journals and a customizable on-line presence called an avatar - which allows members of our system to interact with each other within the virtual world of Gaia. 

Gaia comes complete with its own cast of heroes, villians, and ordinary, everday characters -- known as Non-Player Characters, or NPCs. These NPCs interact with one another in site-wide social events, in which memebers are encourage to participate.
