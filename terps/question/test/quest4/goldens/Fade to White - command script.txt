Fade to White -- command script
Derived from White.asl (ASL 210, QDK 1.0 Beta 5).

A demo again: there is no `playerwin`, the ending is simply the description of
the room `Escape!`, which has "END OF DEMO" printed at the end of it. That
string is the win marker, so the replay stops the moment that room is entered.

The whole game alternates between the hospital and a drug hallucination, and
each transition is scripted rather than walked:

  * WHITE LIGHT -- speaking to Voice 2 reveals Voice 3, and speaking to *her*
    is what puts you under (`goto <Hospital Bed>`).
  * The Lot 9 swap is the one real puzzle. Speaking to Dr. Cain reveals the
    Intrevenus, looking at the Intrevenus reveals the bag hanging from it, and
    taking the bag gives you `Lot 9 Bag`. In the bathroom you pour it away and
    refill it from the sink; lying down re-hooks the water bag, and `sleep`
    then takes you on to the Examination Table instead of the Stone Edifice.
  * Examination Table -- the Doctor reveals the Anesthesiologist, and speaking
    to him is the jump into the woods.
  * The woods end at the Eastern Edge: taking the Red Rose is what ends the
    hallucination and lands you in the Interrogation Room.
  * There, swallow the Blue Pill, then talk to Alan Stern. His `speak` script
    asks "Is the blue pill still on the table?" -- an `ask`, which the runner
    presents as a numbered menu, so the answer is the digit `2` (No), which by
    then is the truth. That gets his long speech and the move to Testing Room
    A; answering `1` only re-swallows the pill and you have to speak to him
    again.
  * Speaking to the Scientist reveals the Colors, and looking at them drops you
    into the Spectrum Maze.

The maze is seven rooms and the key is the mnemonic the game shows you on the
helmet and repeats through `hint`: ROYGBIV. Each room names a colour for each
of its exits, and the way through is to take the next colour of the rainbow in
turn -- red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet -- which is
north, southwest, east, northeast, west, southeast, south. Any other exit
throws you back to the first room.

Violet from room 7 is the way out. North from room 7 is the Head Chamber,
which is not on the way to anything -- the Blue Head is the demo's answer to
who you are, and every exit from that room dumps you back at maze room 1 --
so the seven maze moves are walked twice here: once to visit the head, once to
leave. Everything before the maze that is not one of the steps above (the
orderlie at the door, the sink, the black cat, the fork's other branch) is
included for the same reason, to cover more of the demo than the shortest path
does.
