I guess it didn't fit me very well huh?" An image comes to your mind, a memory of a stripper in, or rather coming out of, your wedding dress. "You know the police are after you, but, I feel I owe you one so, c'mon...." He leads you around the other side of the stadium to where there are a number of motorbikes. He kicks one to life and tells you to hold on tight.

Fans have emerged from the stadium too, and both sides are chanting for you- even some of the players are! This seems to annoy a small faction of the law, who stay behind to deal with them. The rest of the cops follow you.

"Left or right?!"

The police are a long way behind, and fall back even further after you go over a level crossing just before the 5.15 to Glasgow. Left or straight on?!" he shouts again.

Your mother is in tears, but isn't fighting with your soon to be father-in-law anymore, so that's good. Suddenly, one of your bridesmaids gives a shout as the groom finally arrives outside. The priest reappears and looks very relieved, if you'll pardon the expression. 
Quite unexpectedly your carefully constructed, lovingly thought-out, personally written vows have been used as a scorecard for someone's game of mah-jong, and then, equally unexpectedly, by the resident cat as litter. You want to cry, but your bridesmaids lead you away, and the groom enters.
He wears a gorgeous suit, shoes that go with it perfectly, and hay in his hair which does not go with anything, except stables. There are also some small cuts on his face and hands. Still, he looks more wonderful to you now than ever before.
It is a beautiful ceremony, despite your failure to remember your carefully constructed, lovingly thought-out, personally written vows that is matched by the failure of the groom to remember his carefully constructed, lovingly thought-out, personally written vows as well. You both end up laughing, which sets off your bridesmaids and the best man as well. The priest smiles.
Finally, you are married and leave the chapel, and if you hurry, you might make the boat on time!
