City of Blood
By Craig Dutton (c) 2012

Port Silvermoon is a den of thieves and murderers and ruled by Prince Velmaht, a tyrant who is rumoured to deal in the dark arts.  

After a long arduous journey you arrive at your friend's house in the Garden District.  You have known Meridam Trax all your life.  He raised you up when your parents were killed by Brigands in the Ballas Foothills, southeast of the city.  He is more than just a father-figure, he's your mentor and friend.

After breakfast the next day, you both talk about your adventures in his study.  In mid-conversation about the morals of gambling a gust of wind blows the curtains high up into the air and when they settle the sight is shocking!  Meridam lies slumped dead at his desk.  He has been murdered!

You look out from the window and see no sign of the murderer.  Who did this and why?  Your mission is to discover the reason and avenge Meridam's death.

The City of Blood might have claimed another victim but the truth will out or you will die trying!
