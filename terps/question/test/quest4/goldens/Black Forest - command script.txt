Black Forest (Part I)
Blackforest.asl, ASL 217, "created by QDK 1.01" / game info "Created with QDK 1.0
(Beta 5)", Devon Oratz, Silent Cities of The Mind, copyright 2000-2001.  The
declared game version is "Alpha".

Amnesia horror.  You come to in total darkness with something poking into your
ribs and no idea who you are; two flashbacks over the next hundred turns tell you
that you are a psychologist, and that is all you ever learn.  No score, no
timers, no randomness, no NPCs except a cat.  105 turns below, which is the whole
game plus both optional flashbacks.

This is Part I of two.  The last room, Black Gate, is a `playerwin` whose room
description is a note asking you to open the "Black Forest Part II" file with the
password 'cheese'; `define text <win>` is the single line "Please open file
'BlackForest2'".  Part II is not in this corpus, so this is the whole of the
shipped story.


THE OPENING

The first room contains one object, a Knob, and no exits.  TURN KNOB, PULL KNOB
and TAKE KNOB all do the same thing -- you were shut in a closet and the knob was
the doorknob -- and dump you into the Basement.


ACT ONE: THE HOUSE

  Basement       LOOK AT GLISTENING METAL   -> hides it, shows the Small Key
                 TAKE SMALL KEY
                 UNLOCK GATE DOOR WITH KEY  -> hides Gate Door, opens north
  Closet (east)  PULL LIGHT CORD            -> reveals Rope, Screwdriver,
                                               Flashlight (all three are
                                               `invisible` until the light is on)
                 TAKE ROPE / SCREWDRIVER / FLASHLIGHT
  Living Room    TAKE CANDLE
  Boiler Room    LIGHT BOILER WITH CANDLE   -> the shadow-being, flashback 1
  Dirty Bathroom TAKE METAL ROD             -> gives the Manhole Opener

The Rope and the Screwdriver are the two objects the rest of the game needs.  The
Flashlight is a pure red herring: "there are no batteries", and nothing in the
file ever refers to it again.


THE CANDLE INTERLOCK -- THE ONE THING THAT CAN LOCK YOU OUT OF CONTENT

The Basement's west exit is `if got <Candle> then goto <Boiler Room>`, and the
candle exists in exactly one window:

  * `~Internal Procedure 15` (TAKE METAL ROD) ends with
    `if got <Candle> then do <~Internal Procedure 18>`, which blows the candle out
    and `lose`s it.
  * The candle's own take tag is `if got <Manhole Opener> then msg <Holding the
    manhole opener in both hands, you can't also take the candle.>`.
  * And going to the Boiler Room at all costs the candle anyway --
    `~Internal Procedure 21` does `lose <Candle>` on the way out.

So the Boiler Room can be entered once, only after taking the candle and before
taking the metal rod, and the candle cannot be recovered afterwards (`lose` drops
it into the Boiler Room, but `hideobject` from the take script has already hidden
it, so it is not there to pick up).  Take the rod first and the best scene in the
game is gone for good.  This script does the boiler, then the rod.

That scene is worth the detour: lighting the gas summons a man-shaped thing
"sculpted of the primordial darkness itself", knocks you out, and plays
`define text <Shadow1>` -- your graduation from Harberry Medical College, licence
to practise psychology.  The only other flashback is LOOK AT TREES in Frost
Fusion (`~Internal Procedure 46`): shadow ichor lunges out of the ice into your
eyes and you see yourself signing a property deed for a psychotherapy practice in
Thorn Valley, Maine.  Both are one-shot -- 46 ends `hideobject <Trees>`.


ACT TWO: THE ROADS AND THE SEWER

The house's Backyard opens onto a road running east and west.  East is a
three-room dead end that exists only for atmosphere and for the game's title
image: Straight Road, Dark Vista, and The Edge, where the world has been sheared
off and the road stops in front of a wall of nothing.  Going south there is a
refusal, not a death ("the flapping of leathery wings can be faintly heard from
the abyss").  Note that the object is literally named `The Abyss`, so you have to
type LOOK AT THE ABYSS -- LOOK AT ABYSS finds nothing.

West is the way on:

  Dead End       OPEN MANHOLE WITH MANHOLE OPENER  -> `choose <Dark Hole>`
                 The selection has four choices and reappears every time you LOOK
                 AT HOLE.  2) Jump in is one of the game's five deaths.  4) Climb
                 Down runs `~Internal Procedure 28`, which finds no ladder and
                 then, `if got <Rope>`, offers "Will you use your rope to climb
                 down?" -- yes ties the rope off and drops you into the sewers.
  Sewer W        LOOK AT GRATE -> `choose <Sewer Grating>`, choice 4, Remove the
                 screws, then yes -> the grate comes off and west opens.

That grate choice is written

    choice <Remove the screws> if got <Screwdriver> then if ask <Will you unscrew
      the screws?> then do <~Internal Procedure 34> else msg <With what?, you
      can't take them out with your fingers.>

-- the `else` binds to the inner `if ask`, so "With what?" is what you get for
answering *no* while holding the screwdriver, and picking the choice without a
screwdriver at all prints nothing whatsoever.

Beyond the grate is a crawlway into a second, entirely separate road network
(Two-Lane Road, Concrete Wall, Road 2, Endless Road, The Dam).  Endless Road's
north exit points at Endless Road, which is the joke.  LOOK AT GRAPHITTI at the
Concrete Wall -- the misspelling is the object's real name -- is the game's one
outright genre nod: "The crimson king is coming!! All hail the unnamed!"


ACT THREE: UNDER THE ICE

  Surface Control  LOOK AT LEVER, yes    -> the floor is a freight elevator; it
                                            descends into a refrigerated room
  Frozen Forest    north, north
  Icy Stump        LOOK AT STUMP         -> reveals the Spiral
                   TURN SPIRAL           -> reveals the Depression
                   PUSH DEPRESSION, yes  -> the stump is a hatch; climb down
  Frost Chamber E  TAKE SHINY BLUE CRYSTAL  -> the Ice Stone
  Frost Chamber W  TAKE BLUE CRYSTAL        -> the Sleet Stone

Taking the second crystal shuts down the Frost Fusion globe, and from then on
every room in the forest prints a thawing message instead of its normal script.
Go back up the ladder and south twice: the Frozen Forest's south exit is
`if got <Sleet Stone> and got <Ice Stone> then do <~Internal Procedure 58>`, and
58 floods the elevator shaft, drops an emergency ladder and puts you on the dam
with the lake coming over the wall.

That is the endgame, and it is four rooms of one-way choices with only one safe
answer at each:

  Flooding Dam     north = drowned in a suction pipe        SOUTH
  Flooding Road    north = swept into the drainage tunnel
                   south = swept into the drainage tunnel   EAST
  Flooded Sewers   east  = the thing in the tunnel          SOUTH  -> Black Gate

Three of those four deaths are the same monster: the tentacle at the mouth of the
big drainage pipe in the Concrete Wall, which the graffiti warned you about
sixty turns earlier ("BEWARE THE BeASt, On our flesh, he shall feast").  With the
manhole death that makes five ways to die, none of them reachable by accident.


AUTHORING ODDITIES

  * QDK 1.0 had no way to share a script between two `command` lines, so eleven
    of the sixty-four procedures are byte-for-byte duplicates of another one:
    0/1 (turn vs pull knob), 6/7/8/9 (four phrasings of unlocking the gate),
    21/22, 25/26, 35/37, 47/49.  Procedures 19 and 20 operate on a `Gas Valve`
    object that is never defined anywhere in the file, and nothing calls them.
  * Both outdoor road rooms carry `alias <Road>`, and the room QDK named
    `Conrete Wall` keeps the typo with `alias <Concrete Wall>` papered over it.
  * `items <Small Key; Rope; Flashlight; Screwdriver; Candle; Manhole Opener; Ice
    Stone; Sleet Stone>` is the complete inventory of the game; `collectables`
    and `define text <intro>` are both empty.
  * `ask` renders as a 1) Yes / 2) No menu and the runner reads the answer with
    atoi, so a yes must be written `1` and a no `2` -- the spelled-out word "no"
    parses as 0, clamps to option 1 and takes the Yes branch.
