There were many others in the Quest forums that played a helpful role (small or large) including Al "MaDbRiT", and "I think I'm Dead" and even Alex, the Quest creator himself.

A gameplay warning, you will receive the occasional reminder to save the game before a challenging part (including the desert).  I advise you to save often because this game was closely modeled after the original whereas, you tend to be killed if you make the wrong mistake...
I did take the liberty of minimizing the amount of ways that you as the player can be killed so it won't be too bad.
In the desert if it becomes too challenging, type 'desert' and view the map I have created for you.

When talking to characters in the game, I have taken the extra steps to allow easier game control.  If you are talking to the toy shop owner, for example, you can simply say "talk to man".
In certain areas, there may be similarly named characters.  For example, there is a room where there is a busy clerk and a sales clerk.  If you say "talk to clerk", it will give you a menu of whom you'd like to address.
You will discover that long-named objects are for looks mostly.  For convenience, you can pick up the "silver coin" by typing "get coin" (or variations thereof).
This is PART ONE of this game.  It should last you a good while.  In the meantime, I will continue creating the rest of the game in a timely manner (hopefully!)
With that being said, enjoy!

After a lengthy discussion with the great wizard, Crispinopher (Crispin for short), Graham learns more about the evil wizard, Mordack.  
Crispin is unable to answer the obvious question, "Why?" but is almost as determined as Graham to find out.

And so...Graham sets off on his quest to find his family.  Accompanying him is Cedric, the wise-cracking owl, who will guide you occasionally.  Graham was given only two items by Crispin, a worn out magic wand and a bite of magical white snake (that he's already eaten) which helps him to understand the language of nature and animals.

"Good luck, King Graham!" says Crispin and shuts the front door behind Graham as he steps outside...
