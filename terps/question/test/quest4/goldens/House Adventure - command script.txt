House Adventure v1 (QDK Lite 4.02, ASL 400)
- raw command script for question_walkthrough_runner.
Game system: Quest 4.  Marker: "you get in the bath".

NOT A WIN -- a four-room sandbox (living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom) with
no goal, no score, no playerwin and no playerlose.  All there is to do is try
the appliances, each of which answers a custom verb through a property
("verb <flush>" in the game block + "properties <flush=...>" on the toilet).
The script is a tour that fires every such verb once and ends in the bathroom;
the marker is the bath's GET IN reply, the last thing on the tour.

Two lines deliberately show the game's own bugs, and print
"I can't see that here." in real Quest too:

  * PRESS POWER SWITCH ON COMPUTER -- the game also declares the shorter verb
    "press power switch" (for the TV), which is matched first and leaves
    "on computer" as the object name.
  * LAY ON BED -- likewise shadowed by the shorter verb "lay".
