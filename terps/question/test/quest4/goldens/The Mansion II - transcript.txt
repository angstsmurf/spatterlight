The Mansion II
------------------

After your uncle's untimely death, you explored his empty mansion to find the truth about his demise. You learned that your uncle's scientific skills were being abused by a sinister corporation developing an untraceable toxin for assassinations, and that when your uncle tried to destroy his work, he was silenced.
You were confronted by the corporation's agents, but fought back, killing one. Unfortunately you were struck by a tazer before you could escape from the mansion. Unconsciousness took you and you remember nothing until now. You have awoken in darkness, a bag on your head and handcuffs on your wrists...
