The Adventures of Koww the Magician (KOWW1.ASL) - walkthrough
Completes the game ("You fly up and over the chasm!").
You start carrying milk. The grappling-hook / duck-turd / purple-paint
chain is just the optional "secret feature" and is skipped here.
Start: Koww's Chasm.

east                                  (-> Zeke's Farm)
go to zeke's silo
give milk to zeke                     (he trades you a pitchfork)
out                                   (-> Zeke's Farm)
use pitchfork on haystack             (uncovers a jade statuette)
south                                 (-> Goblin Trail)
south                                 (-> Goblin Lair)
go to inside the goblin lair
give jade statuette to goblin king    (he gives you goblin spit)
out                                   (-> Goblin Lair)
north                                 (-> Goblin Trail)
north                                 (-> Zeke's Farm)
north                                 (-> Land of the NecroYaks)
north                                 (-> Ambush Point)
search                                (yaks take the spit, drop a wing feather)
south
south                                 (-> Zeke's Farm)
east                                  (-> Phoenix Mountain Pass)
east                                  (-> Phoenix Peak)
yes                                   (the Phoenix asks Yes/No; this game wants the word YES, not a number -> you have the wing feather -> fly scroll)
west
west                                  (-> Zeke's Farm)
west                                  (-> Koww's Chasm)
use fly scroll                        (fly over the chasm -> win)
