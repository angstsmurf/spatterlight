by Father Thyme (with DavyB)

You have received a strange email from your mysterious Uncle Barry. His computer was programmed to send it after he had not  logged on for a month.

He says that he needs help "to return," but from where he does not say. He reveals nothing else except that a full explanation will be in an envelope in the kitchen cupboard  of the  secluded cottage he moved into after his bereavement (his wife was trampled by a rhino on holiday in Africa).

He always spoke about a great discovery, but would never say what it was. You feel that this is the cause of his disappearance.  You decide to go and find him.

Still shaking Barry climbs down the tree. Seeing you, he begins to shake even more. "How did you get here," he blurts out, temporarily forgetting to thank you for saving his life.

You tell him the story and agree to celebrate your escapade in Tom Shakespeare's tavern. After all, you still have nine Elizabethan pennies to spend.

You then ask him about the black vehicle  further up the track but he knows nothing about it.

As you are about to head out you hear a strange buzzing noise behind you. Turning, you see a tall thin man in a woollen cloak and crudely hammered iron helmet. Strangely clashing with his period costume he carries a gleaming and efficient looking pistol in his right hand. With the other he displays an ornately engraved badge in a plastic case.

"TDCI Hark Jolfston of the Patrol," he announces in a strange accent. "I've been waiting to catch you together. On the laptop
You are sitting at home on your laptop. You seem to remember getting an emergency email from Uncle Barry but now can't find it.

You phone him to check and all seems fine. Coincidently, he is also struggling with a vague memory. He feels he may have made a technological breakthrough but now can't even recall what it was about!

Your brain is also telling you that you  met William Shakespeare and Queen Guinevere recently, both with a drinking problem.

Surely that must have been a dream?
