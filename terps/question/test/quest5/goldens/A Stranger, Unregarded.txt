Most such uses require high skills in the arts of magic, but it is best known as a weapon against werewolves. The second entry on the page reads:

Spells (Magic)
Spells are basic to the wizard's toolkit and exist in many forms. Some are words in the tongue of magical beings, with Elfen and Faerie tongues quite popular. Others are anagrams. Some may be associated with an object or place.

It is a peculiarity of spoken spells that their power lies less in the speaker than in the wizard or mage who first frames them. Thus the power of an anagram of the spell's action whose arrangement reflects the sound of one the magical tongues also describing or alluding to that action, depends not only on their combination but on the depth of knowledge of the crafter. Spells associated with an object or place are generally imbued in that object and can be very simple, as the power is called up from the object itself rather than being inherent in the words.

In general, spells are used by saying (or "casting") them. You must learn a spell before you can cast it, unless it is written where you are at the time.

The novice should beware, however, that the more powerful the spell the more dangerous its side effects can be.

Also see the entries in this Work on Anagrams, Homophonic Spells, Magical Tongues, Amulets and Rings of Power.
