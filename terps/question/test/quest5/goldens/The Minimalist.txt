This is a text-based adventure game, in the style of Zork and it's descendants.
In this game, you use simple one or two word commands to navigate the world. for example, you can type 'go north' 'north' or simply 'n' to move from your current area to the one north of you. you will also encounter south, east, west, up, down, in, out, and a number of others.
To interact with your world, you commonly use the commands 'look at X' 'use X' or 'speak to X' and in some situations, you will 'use X on Y' or similar combinations.
To define this game in a genre, it would be puzzle-mystery. Use lateral thinking and exploration to solve the puzzles and make it to the end!

This is Turbonerd Studio's second game, The minimalist.
Entry for the Ludum Dare competition under the theme 'Minimalism'
