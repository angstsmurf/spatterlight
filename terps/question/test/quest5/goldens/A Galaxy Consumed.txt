HUMANS: Humans are 2 meter tall, bipedal mammalians hailing from the world Earth, in orbit of the star Sol, within the Orion Arm. Diverse and proud of their accomplishments, they were the founding members of the Conjoined Species. They are also the only known species with a nudity taboo. In ancient times humans were persistence hunters, and even today their mechanical bodies are designed to be long-lasting.
Humans have +5% Maximum Energy.

MIDARIANS: Midarians are ~2 meter tall avians, covered in colorful feathers and with four eyes, hailing from the world Eerawiks, in orbit of the star Aerish, within the Perseus Arm. Their wings are broad but flightless, with flexible feathers that can double as fingers. Flighty and fierce, they joined the Conjoined Species after humans founded it. Midarians were ambush predators in prehistory, and even today their mechanical bodies have powerful springs and advanced threat-assessment calculators.
Midarians have +5% Speed.

TURLACS: Turlacs are ~3 meter long fish, though they more closely resemble crimson, three-eyed dolphins from Earth and possess poisonous flesh. They hail from the world of Calano, in orbit of the star Ora, within the Carina-Sagittarius Arm. Broad and strong, the Turlacs suffered a civil war when they found the Conjoined Species, and it was only after the war's resolution that they formally joined. To move among other species they use mechanical, water-filled suits around their bodies, lending them an extra layer of protection.
Turlacs have +5% Health.
