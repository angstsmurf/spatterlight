> take relic

**************************
Your spine tingles as you take the ancient, priceless relic into your posession. You peer into it, sensing the incredible power that it offers you. As you fixate on it, you are suddenly aware that you are not alone. The disembodied souls of a dozen ancient Sith Masters stand ready to serve you; Karnass Muur, Marka Ragnos, Exar Kun, Darth Malak, Darth Malgus... The incredibly menacing spirit of Darth Bane looks at you expectantly. "Why have you brought us here?" he snarls.
Initially, you feel almost overpowered by the fear threatening to consume you; but you calm yourself and realize that the spirits of the Dark Lords are bound to the relic and are therefore subservient to the wishes of its owner. You look into Bane's eyes and the eyes of each of the ancient Sith Masters one at a time. Each stares back at you with a mixture of malice and anticipation. Your fear transforms to loathing before you center yourself in the Force. The loathing then slowly changes  into pity, which then slowly rolls into compassion. “Compassion for the beings that have caused so much strife, anguish, and death in the galaxy for millennia?” you ask aloud to yourself.
Darth Malgus tilts his head inquisitively. You know that you could choose to wield the incredible power of the relic, but you have zero doubt that it would eventually lead you down the same path that these poor souls travelled in life. You see very clearly now that the way of darkness leads to complete crushing of one’s will and an eternity of hopeless wanderings through the shadows of black space.
So instead you place the relic on the volcano floor. You raise your lightsaber blade, point down. The Sith realize in horror what you are preparing to do. Not knowing what will happen to them if the relic is destroyed, they begin to plead pathetically for you to stay your hand. You look Darth Malak square in the eye and say with great sympathy, “I release you to your rest.”
You plunge your blue blade into the weapon, piercing even the ground beneath it. The Sith Lords become mere wisps of smoke and follow the steam up and out of the volcano opening far above.
Yoda comes out of stasis and walks solemnly up to you. “Done well you have,” he offers.
You smile down at the small, green Jedi. You exit the volcano with him, climb down its face, and flop down on the ground at the base. You have been through a lifetime of experiences in a mere day. You are exhausted.
You return to Corusant and within months you are assigned the rank of Master. As you live out your life, you uphold the tenants of the Jedi order with a level of honor and respectability that leaves behind a legacy of great reverence. Your name and all that you stood for live on well beyond your mortal life.
