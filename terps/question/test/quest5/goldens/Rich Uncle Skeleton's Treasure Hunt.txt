You are in the Foyer.

There is an impressive staircase on either side of the room leading to the floor above. You notice the paintings hanging on the wall, all portraits of members of the Skeleton family, spanning many generations past. The marble floor is so shiny not only can you see your face in it, but the detail of the ceiling mural also.

You suddenly remember the reason you are here, to pay your respects to your recently passed Rich Uncle Skeleton. How a skeleton can die is beyond me, but you should probably go and look for the family graveyard.

Dear Nephew,

If you are reading this then your old Rich Uncle Skeleton has kicked the bucket. I know I didn't leave you much inheritance, but you were always my favourite nephew, so I wanted you to have something that no one else can know about. I have a secret safe hidden somewhere in the mansion containing millions of dollars worth of money, jewellery and treasure.
