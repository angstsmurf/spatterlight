Welcome to GamesRadar's text version of Heavy Rain. This small story simulates the 'lizard' challenge from Quantic Dream's game. It's a proper text adventure, so you can play all the way through. Keep an eye out for secrets! Now, let's set the scene...

Ethan Mars wanders into an abandoned building, looking for clues to the whereabouts of his son, Shaun, who has been kidnapped by the Origami Killer.

How to play

1. It's a text adventure, so typing in stuff like 'look at lizard' or 'take lizard' will look at or take that object. If you want to use something on yourself type 'use xxx on me'.

2. Don't forget, there's a compass on your right, which lets you move into new rooms.

3. Keep an eye on the Room Objects on your right too. Once you've discovered them, certain objects will appear in this menu for you to interact with.

4. You can click on all useable objects to look at, take or do all manner of things to them.
