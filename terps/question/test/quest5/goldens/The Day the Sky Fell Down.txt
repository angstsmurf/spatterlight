The Day the Sky Fell Down

by Father Thyme

Last night the sky fell down. You have emerged from hiding into a world that has changed drastically.

The smooth surface of Central Park that you have known all your life looks very different. A vast rubble pile mars  its pristine whiteness. It has been many hours since the event. The rumbling lasted several minutes. No one dared leave Habitation. The disembodied voices that ruled daily life, issuing commands, education and instructions have fallen silent, leaving you and your fellow trainees leaderless and helpless.

You are considered rash, daring and foolhardy. An exception to your fellows still cowering under their beds. You alone no longer hear the voices, not since your strange accident two years ago. A thing you have never revealed to anyone for fear of the GODS overhearing.

Hunger and thirst have taken their toll, but fear of the unknown prevented an excursion outside. Finally you alone have dared to emerge from Habitation to face the outside world and seek an answer, and food and drink if possible.
