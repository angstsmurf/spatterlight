Island of the Magi
By Craig Dutton (c) 2012

The Magi Elementalists have lived on the island of Sinahr for many years. They help, guide, and even hinder the four elements of fire, air, earth and water to bring balance to the world.

However, there is unrest and the balance is shifting towards chaos. The Wizard Council of the Crystal Hand have sent you, an adventurer for hire, to the island and find out what is happening.

Two days later you arrive at Sinahr. You disembark the small craft that was rowed by two sailors from the 'Singing Mermaid' that sailed you here, on to a beach on the southside of the island.

The Elemental Tower where the Magi live is located on the northside of the island.

Good luck on your mission.
