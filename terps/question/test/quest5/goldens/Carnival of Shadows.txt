Julias Finch, aka Bongo the Clown was an old ex-con friend of Johnney's and together they murdered Alfonso Alberto, the real Ringmaster, so Johnney could take his place and hide out in full view. This happened two days ago during the first full moon.

Another werewolf attacked Johnney later that night and bit him. The infection didn't take effect until earlier tonight when he sadly clawed poor Billy to death. Madame Selene explained that beams of moonlight from a full moon is the only time Johnney 'transformed' and went back to human form when you first met him. Only just now when the night clouds cleared that Johnney went 'wolfman' again...

The Carnival eventually closed down, Bongo/Julias was arrested hours later trying to steal a car, Madame Selene is on hot pursuit of the original werewolf and closing in and Claudius is now retired at an animal sanctuary.  As far as you're concerned - case closed.
