A canabalistic monster who was once a human. Such embodiment of human greed, it was punished and turned into a monster.

It's emancipated, has pallid skin, and razor sharp teeth and claws for hunting. It's in a state of semi-hibernation but hunts well at night feasting on human flesh; never satisfying it's eternal hunger.

Now you know why the Townspeople have all vanished. They were all eaten!!! This creature must be destroyed.
