A Tiny Furry In A Huge World

As always, your alarm rings at precisely 7:00AM. And, as always, you are totally not ready to go to college. You were up way too late last night playing the latest Wolf Warrior game, and this morning, you are clearly going to pay for being irresponsible. You blame your parents for being so strict with their 9:00PM curfew back at home. Now that you're living by yourself, you have the freedom to stay up whenever you like, and man, is that freedom way too much for you sometimes. If only they'd have let you be more irresponsible, then you might be used to only getting three hours of sleep.

Regardless, you can't miss another day at class. Your attendance rate is already pretty damn low, you almost flunked your last test, and if you keep going at this rate, then, frankly, you're going to fail. With a truly titantic amount of effort, you push your duvet off of yourself and blearily slip on a plain green t-shirt, black jeans, and a pair of old trainers that should've been in the trash long, long ago. Grunting, you stretch your arms high above your head and let out a big, loud, yawn. There isn't any time for breakfast - the bus is here in twenty minutes - so you get your fuzzy butt right out of the door and head straight out of your dorm, going straight to the stop outside.

At the bus stop, you can barely keep your eyes open. On the bus, the gentle rumble of the engine almost puts you to sleep. Fortunately, you manage to stay awake. Pushing yourself through the grounds of the college, you finally make it to your lecture. You figure that you should sit up front so that your teacher actually knows that you came to class this time, but since you got here late, the only seat available is all the way in the corner of the lecture hall. The nice, cozy corner. The lights are even dimmer over there.

Shit. You're definitely going to fall asleep. Resigning yourself to your fate, you slump over toward the corner of class and park your butt down on the seat, leaning forward and setting your eyes upon your teacher. Ms. Matthews. She's a petite, white bunny, a svelte, mature and well spoken woman. Honestly, she's not really your type. You prefer the more buxom kind of lady, so you're not even going to be able to keep yourself awake by getting your horn on.

As her voice drones on and on, you find your eyes beginning to droop. There's only so many times you can shake your head or pinch your hip before you finally succumb to a sweet, blissful nap.

When you wake up, the world has changed massively. Literally.

Everything is now huge. You're on top of your desk, all of you. your now tiny body prone against the cheap, chipped wood. Grunting, you push yourself up into a seat, and take view of the now enormous lecture hall around you. By your own estimations, you're now about one, perhaps an inch and a half tall. What were simple seats and tables now look like distant buildings. The ceiling is now leagues above you, spread across the top of the halll like it's the very sky itself, seemingly stretching into infinity. Your pencil and notebook lay to the side of you, your notebook like a plot of land, and your pencil like one of the logs that you might use to build the house that you could put atop of this.

Swallowing nervously, you inch your way across to the edge of the table. Peering over it, you see a drop that's most definitely going to kill you. The legs of the table are too wide and smooth to be able to climb down.

You pinch yourself. This has to be a dumb dream, right? This is what you get for eating cheese in the middle of the night, right? As your claws scrape against your flesh, you feel a pang of pain. No. This is definitely real.

How on earth are you going to get yourself out of this? And furthermore, who did this to you? You guess it's time to find out.
