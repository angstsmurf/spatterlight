By Atomic Brainworks

You are a documentarian of the occult. But unlike others, you pride yourself in not just looking at the surface. Instead you infiltrate  these clandestine activities and reveal their innermost secrets. It can be dangerous and you have had several close calls. But in the process not only have you made some revealing documentaries, you have also had the opportunity to even save some innocent lives.

Your latest adventure takes you to the city of New Orleans. The topic is Voodoo. The African religion migrated to Haiti and then, after a slave revolt in the late 1700's, made it's way to the American city. True to your nature, you will not be satisfied at writing about the local tourist attractions like Marie Laveau's house of Voodoo. Instead you immediatly make your way to the New Orlean's underground. As you suspected there was much more then meets the eye. You discover a dark, veiled Voodoo culture that is still very active. After interacting with many shady characters and spending your money on more than a few bribes you learn of a location. The Riverfront Expressway. The very little known tunnel was built in the 60's right under downtown New Orleans. It was to handle six lanes of traffic from multiple points in the East all the way to the West bank. Due to much opposition from the French Quarter it never opened. Now you have a lead that some of the complex beneath is being used for other purposes. With anticipation you head towards the location, not realizing this would be your most dangerous adventure yet and the life you would have to save would be your own.

You wait until dusk. In the darkness you slip into a part of the tunnel that is being used for private valet parking. You go much further into the unused, long abandoned areas. You begin to question the reliablity of your source, the man who took your money in exchange for a crudely drawn map. You have gotten turned around many times and are unsure of what direction you are going in anymore. The only source of light is your cell phone. The condition of the walls and floor of the unused tunnel have deteriorated considerably. This footing is treacherous but you are too stubborn to go back. Suddenly a piece of the floor collapses and you tumble into darkness. It seems like you fall forever before hitting the ground with a sickening thud. Everything goes black.
You awake, not sure how long  you were lying there unconscious. You are in a candlelit chamber. The first thing you notice is a human skull lying on a small wooden table. Your cell phone is lying on the floor. It is smashed and useless.
