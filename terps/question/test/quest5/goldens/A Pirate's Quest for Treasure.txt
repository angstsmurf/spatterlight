A Pirate's Quest for Treasure
By Craig Dutton (c) 2012

You are Captain Jacob Diablo, dreaded pirate and scourge of the Western Seas. A challenge has been made by your rival Captain Ichabod Bloodaxe, scourge of the Eastern Seas and small furry animals.  Rumour has it that you're not so 'hot' anymore as a blood-thirsty pirate and he bets you to sail around the Inland Sea and find as much treasure worth 1500 gold coins or more. There's no time limit!

Challenge accepted! Can you defeat the mighty Cyclops? Successfully gamble in the pits of Port Clam? Fight wizards and defeat witches? Let's find out...

Also, each time you get some treasure put it in your strong-hold on your ship for safe-keeping. This will add to your Treasure Value. Any gold coins you acquire are recorded separately but are included in your total value of treasure.

If you lose, you become Bloodaxe's Cabin-boy for the rest of your days and vice versa.
