Hey guys. I had PTSD. I made a random game.
Not real, real, PTSD. But traumatic enough I had several nightmares afterwards. And lots of depression.
Anyways... I originally  made this to practice a combat code. Then I thought maybe the people who watch 'Harmful Opinions' (Gamergate figure, Twitch) would like it, but long story short, that isn't happening. So I just thought I could upload it in the adult section.

You're in Hell. First circle. Maybe the outer ring, I don't know.

Note: I'm changing a few elements of the game. I am changing room names, and moving a few rooms around. I just came up with a few neat ideas recently.
You are freshly dead. You are new to hell. You remember exactly how you died, and you can remember the memories of your llife like they were yesterday.
You just want to have a good time. Killing Satan sounds like fun!
It's all up to you.
In this game, you are a working class citizen in hell. The game represents the contant struggles of the average citizen in their life, such as survival, politics, work, alcohol, and sin. No, I did not just make that up. Otherwise Hillary Clinton would not be a harpy.
