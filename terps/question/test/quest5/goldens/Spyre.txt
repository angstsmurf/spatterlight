The Enchanted Grotto

by Sean DeArmond

Welcome to Spyre: the Enchanted Grotto!

Please enjoy yourself. Feel free to LOOK at anything, TALK to anyone, and TAKE anything that isn't nailed down. Most of all, have fun (preferably as a result of playing the game).

There are a total of 400 points to be earned.

You are in an Underground Hollow.

Your time spent wandering this less-traveled mountain has led you to an open crevasse that you can't help but be drawn into.

After a careful descent, your feet touch the damp floor.

Curiously you look around at the various flowers and fungus on the rock faces and notice two things. First, this place appears untouched by the presence of humans, as if very few people (if any) have ever set foot down here. Secondly, the deep hues of blueish-greens dotted with yellows and violets has a certain aesthetic you can't quite place. It's almost as if nature cultivated this cave with intention.

There is a small opening in the rock face towards the northwest.
