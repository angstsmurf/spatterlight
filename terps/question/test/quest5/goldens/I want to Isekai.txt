I want to Isekai

by Phurr

You used to love video games and isekai anime. So when truck-kun came for you... You tried to dodge out of the way of course. In the ensuing screams and sounds of crashing, twisting metal you have no idea if you dodged the truck or not. You just know when you wake up you're lying on a freshly dug grave in the forest. Spooky.

Could it be, though? Have you been transported to another world? You glance around. Trees! And so many! You've only ever seen those in fantasy stories. It must be true! Okay. Maybe that's not quite enough evidence. But you choose to believe it is until reality reasserts itself. As long as that doesn't happen you should be fine. Time to look around.
