Murder on the Hill
By Craig Dutton (c) 2012

Lord Augustus Mandrake is dead - murdered! You are Inspector Burton Strange of Scotland Yard and have been called in to investigate this grisly deed.

Lord Mandrake was found late this morning by his cook, Mrs Veronica Holborne, in the greenhouse at the back of Graven Hill House; Lord Mandrake's residence.  The estimated time of death was 10 o'clock this morning and there are a number of suspects:

Lady Mary Mandrake - His Lordship's wife.
Miss Olivia Mandrake - Lord and Lady Mandrake's daughter.
Mr Simon Reubens - Fiance to Olivia and a celebrity racing car driver.
Major Reginald Bolt - Old friend and retired army officer from the 1914-1918 World War.
Doctor Richard Matthews - Local General Practitioner and friend to Olivia.
Reverend Peter Potts - Family friend and vicar to a village on the other side of the district.
Mrs Veronica Holborne - The family cook.

All the suspects had stayed overnight due to a dinner party.

Press a key to continue.
In this murder mystery set in the 1930's you need to collect all incriminating evidence against one or more suspects. It is up to you to decide which clues are vital and which are not.

To speak to suspects you should ask them something. The following four questions should be asked as standard.

1) Ask suspect about where were you at the time of the murder.
2) Ask suspect about if he had any enemies.
3) Ask suspect about what was he like as a person.
4) Ask suspect about when did you last see him alive.

As new subjects come along you can then ask a suspect about them but only when they are mentioned by someone else first.

There are 3 types of evidence. Evidence you have heard from a suspect. Physical evidence that can be picked up and given to suspects for answers. Other physical evidence that cannot be removed from the scene.

The game will conclude with the Final dénouement; the gathering of all suspects together, the speculation of all the evidence gathered and your accusation of the murderer.

Good Luck, Inspector!
