Mystery of Silver Mountain
====================

Clustered in small vilages at the foot of Silver Mountain, the Sylvani were once peaceful and prosperous, governed by a wise Council of Elders.  All who knew of the Sylvani knew also of the Stone of Destiny.  The Stone gave a King or Queen the power to direct a person's destiny.

In the reign of Queen Pari Sylvan, a vicious Grarg army arrived intent on seizing the Stone.  Along with them came a powerful wizard, Magrarg.

Queen Pari and the Council used the Stone.  She entered the Silver Chamber and, placing her hands around the Stone, cried the magic words which would unlock its power.  A great thunderclap echoed as Magrarg joined in mental conflict with the Queen and the Elders.  One of the Elders collapsed under the strain and Magrarg howled in triumph.

Since then, the Sylvani have lived in terror as slaves of a merciless overlord, Grarg Obgan.  He uses the Stone to wreak destruction on the families and friends who dare oppose him.

Queen Pari, murdered by the Grarg left a baby son, Kylar.  He was raised by a wise hermit, his identity a closely guarded secret.  Before she died, Pari had locked in the mind of Kylar a secret: certain magic objects whose combined powers would overcome any evil force controlling the Stone of Destiny.

Kylar, all grown up, searched for these objects but to no avail.  One day, he was slain by a Grarg who coveted a brooch he wore -  a present from the Hermit.

Since then, the Elders cry out to anyone who will find these objects and stop the tyranny once and for all!
