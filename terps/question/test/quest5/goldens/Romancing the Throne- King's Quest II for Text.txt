Romancing the Throne: King's Quest II for Text

Please let me know if something doesn't work!
This is a remake of the classic Sierra adventure game Kings Quest II, Romancing the Throne.

To the Hardcore Sierra Players:

I have made some slight changes.  A couple of rooms are missing, to make the mapping (slightly) more straightforward.  I also tweaked the point system a touch, to fit with the text adaptation.  Everything vital to the story is here.  I have simplified a bit in some places (because I don't know how to require 2 objects to make something happen) but everything you needed before you still need now.

I have added Hagatha and the Enchanter, but I have not yet figured out how to work in the Dwarf, the Wolf, or the Fairy.  If you know how to do this, please let me know!

Most of the descriptions are the same as what is given in the game.  I was working off my memory and a slightly buggy (but still awesome!) version on sarien.net , so if there is something you loved in the game, and is missing in my adaptation, please let me know and I will try to work it in!
To Everyone Else:

Enjoy your taste of the Sierra Classic!

Thanks to Steve Lingle for creating King's Quest V for text.  You inspired me!
Sir Graham is now King of Daventry.  Under his wise rule, Daventry is prosperous and he is loved by his people.  However, loneliness had come upon the good king.  He searched high and low for the right maiden, but he remained alone.

One day, as King Graham sat upon his throne, an idea came to him.  He went to the magic mirror that hung on the wall and looked into its depths.

The mirror clouded and then cleared, revealing a vision of a tall quartz tower.  To reach it, he knew he must pass through a magic door in the nearby land of Kolyma.

The scene suddenly changed to show a beautiful girl locked within the tower!  Graham now knew what he had to do.  Swapping his golden crown for his old adventurer's cap, he set off to find the land of Kolyma and the beautiful maiden.
