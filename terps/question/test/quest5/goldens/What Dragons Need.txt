Character Select

Choose what race you wish to play as.

Human: Nobody really knows humanity's relation to the other mortals. The progenitors? An offshoot of one of the others? Sharing an ancestor? The answer is buried by history. Regardless, over the years humanity has shown its worth time and time again, and while they're nothing special in ability and temperament, they have come farther than would be expected for a race who only began recording history 8,000 years ago. Humans gain a minor bonus to experience gain.

Elf: While elves no longer have the ancient and vast empires that brought them glory, they are still a widely spread people covering all corners of the world. While it's no longer in practice today, a past history of internal eugenics means that the elves tend to be more magically inclined than their fellow races, for better or worse. Elves gain a minor magic bonus.

Orc: In ancient times, orcs formed many small tribes, barbarous and cruel, known for their ferocious strength and skill in battle. Those days are past now, and they live - mostly - in peace with the other races of the realm. While history is sprinkled with anti-orc sentiment, those days too are gone, and the orcish people live every day striving to show they are not their ancestors. Orcs gain a minor strength bonus.
