Project Sparkle

A dungeonpunk adventure game by Devon Cash

In the year 3678, MAGIC has overtaken the internal combustion engine as the primary source of power, all but eradicating the technology in the wake of the so-called MAGITEK boom.
 Cities are now built not on rivers or material resources, but convergent vortices of raw magical energy, known as GEYSERS.
This energy is shaped and manipulated into many forms, some benevolent, others malicious, all otherworldly and unpredictable.
You are a small town MAGECORPS certified technician currently tending to the local ESSENCE CRYSTAL of the equally small town DURHAM
You are at the front counter of your small store, it's closing time, and the setting sun throws spears of light into the large windows of your shop, bathing the aging wooden floor in orange light. The floor has been swept, the money counted and stored, and all is well in your small portion of existance. You have been working in this shop for quite some time, a faithful employee of MAGECORP, perveyors of magic items and services for 200 years. This shop sells MAGECORP licensed parts and supplys for their products as well as offering the maintenence services of a trained professional, which is you. You live in the adjoining apartment to the south, and maintain the town's energy source, located in the tower to the west.
You can go south, west or north.
