"CARRY ON LURK. GOOD LUCK! OH STOP DITHERING MAN. GET IN THERE!"

"Sigh! Yes Your Impatience, I'm going."

You enter the strange wooden cabinet. It is the size of a small room.

In the cabinet
The walls are strangely carved and drilled with holes of varied sizes. Copper rods are threaded through the holes in strange patterns joining metal and glass plates set in the walls and ceiling. A strange glow seems to come from nowhere. You look at the strange devices surrounding you. Many of them are kitchen implements, tableware and artisans tools twisted and altered to suit some strange purpose, by someone who made do with what was available. You are afraid to touch anything in case you may cause a malfunction. The copper rods buzz with energy. You have come across Prang's use of a magic he calls 'electrickery' before. It runs through metal wires and  causes severe tingling and shock to the system if touched.

The room suddenly falls silent, you seem to have arrived. You glance down at the odd button device you were given. This is not the time to press it. You must leave the cabinet and press on with your mission.
