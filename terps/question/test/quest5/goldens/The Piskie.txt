> ask piskie about piskies

> ask piskie about piskies
"I'm a Cornish Piskie. We're like ordinary pixies, but we tend to drink a lot!" he says. After looking quizzically at you, he continues,  "You look like a kind sort of person. Would you be prepared to help me?"

You think about it, and then, feeling that it might be interesting, agree to help him.

"Well, my friend," the piskie continues, "it's like this. My brother, admittedly a most irritating person even by piskie standards, eventually teased the local witch once too many times. She cast a spell on him, and he now lies down below in a deep coma.

"The spell to free him may well be found in her cottage. She herself is away on holiday, so the coast should be clear. If you could find the antidote and bring it here, you would earn the friendship of all us Cornish Piskies.

"Oh yes, and I've opened the way for you..."

Before you can ask him any questions, he says something that that you think sounded a bit like "blackberry", bows deeply and, clutching his carrot firmly, disappears into the hole.

You drop it.

As you drop the jug, blue smoke rises from it and wafts lazily into the rabbit hole. You imagine it drifting along the dark tunnels of the warren.

Obviously attracted by the aromatic smell emanating from the jug, the piskie appears at the entrance to the hole. Seeing the blue potion, his eyes light up. He grabs the jug eagerly.

"Thank you, thank you kind stranger," he cries, "But I must be honest with you, much though it do go against the grain of the Cornish Piskie. In fact, the spell cast on my brother was only temporary. It wears off tonight. What you've brought us is the infamous 'Piskie Reviver' - an amazing drink which guarantees a great party. And that's what we mean to have tonight to welcome my brother back.

"You may be sure that we'll not forget to drink your health!"

So saying, he scurries back into the hole.
Later that evening, before retiring, you decide to take a last stroll. As you pass the gate, you hear the sounds of merriment coming from somewhere underground.

Smiling, you head for home, and bed...
