Shop Shaper

by Light Writer

You are in the Main Hub.
Welcome to the Main Hub of Shop Shaper!  This is where you can find all the commands you'll need to know to play this game!
To go to your shop just type to shop.  To go to to the grocery story simply type to grocery store and you'll be transferred to the main entrance where you can easily find yourself at all the departments.  To go to the furniture store, simply, type to furniture store.
In your shop, you can type stock  ITEM, where item is the item you wish to stock.  If your shop isn't full, you will be asked to submit a price that must be greater than 0.  Depending on what pric you set it compared to its value will help determine how fast it sales.  To find out an object's value simply type value ITEM.
To remove an item from the shop type remove ITEM
Keep track of your money by looking in the stats on the right hand side of the screen.  This displays how much gold you have, your shop size, shop stock, and the amount of gold you need to upgrade shop.
To upgrade your shop type upgrade shop when you have enough gold to upgrade it to the next size.  Keep in mind you don't want to spend all your gold on upgrading if you run out of gold and don't have any things stocked, you lose.
Simply, type to hub to return to the Main Hub at any time.
