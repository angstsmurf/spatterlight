It was a peaceful time in Videoland; a universe of digital physics based on the diversity of video games on the Entropic Gaming System.  On a distant video-world in this universe; a group of prospectors placed some explosives on a collapsed pile of rocks, hooking them up to a detonator some distance away.  “Fire in the hole!” the explosives expert called out before pushing down on the detonation lever.  With a huge bang, the pile of rocks blew up into the air, clearing the way for the prospectors.  With a Geiger counter in-hand, one of the prospectors entered the cave, “Strange, our orbital readings picked up huge deposits of radioactive isotopes around here,” he sighed.  “Well someone messed up,” the leader of the prospectors grumbled just before the needle Geiger counter shot up into the red and let out a buzzing noise alerting them of the intense radiation.

“What is that!?” one of the prospectors gasped before he took aim with his fully automatic rifle and fired.  The projectiles did no damage to the orange hulking menace as it swung its metallic arm at the gunner as the rest of the prospectors made a run for their ship.  A tall, bipedal robot marched out of the cave and leveled a large Gatling gun at the prospectors.  Just before the robot got a lock-on, a small human blonde woman in a gilded armor midriff got in front and signaled the robot not to fire.  “Save your energy, Rapid-Fire, we have big plans for our coming out party!” the woman laughed.  “Yes, Queen Ishtar,” Rapid-Fire responded as he lowered his weapon, letting the prospectors jet off in their ship.

 “This planet will serve us in our new conquest of Videoland, but to start we need energy,” Queen Ishtar told her robotic minions.  “We can concentrate various sources of energy into portable Powerite Spheres,” Shockwave, a blue-colored robot, told the Queen.  “Excellent, start searching immediately!” Queen Ishtar ordered before turning to a red-colored robot, Skywarp, “Start searching for building materials, even if you have to smelt down the metals of this planet then do so,” she ordered.  “As you wish, my queen,” Skywarp bowed before ignited his leg-mounted thrusters and jetted off.  “Soon, I’ll have my revenge on the Game Master and the Deschain Royal Family,” Queen Ishtar laughed.  "Yeah, and maybe we can go to the mall," a pink feminine-like robot giggled.  Queen Ishtar sighed, "Just help Skywarp and Shockwave with the re-construction, Razor Edge," she ordered.

"I'll help cut up those mountians for stuff and things," Razor Edge shrugged as she morphed her lower arms into laser swords and activated her leg thrusters to follow Skywarp.

 Merlin launched a sphere of energy at a circular arc which turned into a warp zone!  Before Princess Valda could ask, a forceful wind gushed out of the warp zone and eventually three human figures emerged from it.  One of them was a less than middle-aged man in a button-down shirt and tie wearing glasses.  Another was a young girl with light brown hair wearing a pink shirt and jeans.  The last was a blonde guy about Valda’s age wearing a brown leather coat and forest camo pants.  “Three?” Valda asked, somewhat accepting the fact that Merlin just dragged some strangers from a distant world into the throne room of the Palace.  “The selection was supposed to choose one… but…” Merlin stopped as the little girl was trying to wake the teenage boy up, calling out his name, “Tyson!  Tyson!” she screamed as she saw blood starting to drip out of his nose.
