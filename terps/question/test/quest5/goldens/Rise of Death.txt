Al'Akir the Windlord
You can go east.

You feel like you're going to throw up. You'd stored so many souls within you that to expell them all, at so high a rate, makes it feel like you're being turned inside out. They just keep coming and coming, shattering around you in a halo and battering into the titanic elemental lord. The winds holding you aloft are weakening, and the tornado surrounding Al'Akir is dissipating. He's roaring and protesting, but shrinking and falling lower and lower. You're falling with him at a comfortable pace, past the clouds, past the structures his kind built, past the legions of air elementals staring in disbelief as their lord falls to the earth and you're still releasing souls.

Soon you're on the ground and Al'Akir is back to normal size, and only then does your onslaught finally end. You're still dizzy and twitching from stray lightning bolts, but Al'Akir is much worse off.

He's about to be even worse off.

You strike with your dark magic, bringing the curled-over elemental lord all the way down while crawling around to his head. You bend him over backwards and throw his scimitar out of reach, then pin his arms and head to the stoney land and push your telepathy in brutally hard."You," you say. "You work for me now." You open your many maws and siphon magic off of his defeated form, using it to grow even stronger. "When we're done here, you'll take your armies and battle whoever is leading the fire elementals, understood?"

Al'Akir's static eyes glare at you hatefully, but eventually he snarls, "Understood, creature. Release me at once!"

"In a moment," you say menacingly. "I just need to make sure you mean it."

"What do you mean... by... that... " Al'Akir asks, trailing off as you push your magic even deeper into his mind, and start rewriting his soul. Before long, Al'Akir is tied to your will utterly.

"There. Now you meant it," you say, crawling back and releasing the elemental lord. "Fire elementals. Don't kill their leader, kill their servants and keep me safe. Get to it."

Al'Akir looks down and you. He holds out a hand and his weapon flies into it. Then he bows his head. "Yes, master."

Dominion

After that, life is... surprisingly good. With Al'Akir under your  command, you finally get some actual semblence of security. You remain behind in their domain, occasionally reinforcing your mind control. Their elemental armies go forth and devastate the others, and with sacrificial rites offer you the souls of those they destroy. You grow to actually quite enjoy the soul offerings. The power, the rush of well being and fullness, the sharp electric flavor, the way they squirm inside of you...

You continue to grow in size and strength, occasionally collapsing the souls in order to grow ever larger, ever more powerful. You keep growing until you're the size of Al'Akir, then bigger than them. You continue to tune your weapons and body, adjusting yourself. It's getting harder to make large-scale changes with a body so massive, so you settle on having eight legs out from a center, filled with tentacles of three main varieties but with some less common ones as you see fit. There's armor, there's magic globules. You move your brain into a head and then attach that head to a long snake-like growth from the center of your body, and cover the head with a plethora of maws and fangs.

You take up residence at Al'Akir's domain, carving out an area specifically for you. With your height you can see the very curvature of the planet, and soon you'll venture out and see the entire thing. You are the size of a mountain, with dozens of souls, thousands of souls influxing every second, and the landscape never stays the same. It's always bucking and rolling, storms coming and going, mountains rising and falling... this is the life.

You crawl forward, dragging yourself over the stone plains of the world. As you do, you effortlessly crush the elementals you pass over and steal their souls. Some of them belong to Al'Akir, but what are they going to do about it? Nothing.

Reunion
You can go out.

It is! It's N'Zoth! It's the size of a mountain with millions of tentacles, some larger than others, but it's N'Zoth. The same purple plates with yellow undersides, the same suction cups, the same head with fanged maws stitched together in the middle, with the same red eye.

... red eyes. N'Zoth has three eyes now.

You push your telepathy to N'Zoth. "Well look at you!" you say excitedly. "What happened?! We all got separated so fast, I thought you were dead!" you babble, your entire body going slack with relief because your friend is alright, and if it's alright then the others might be alright too! "Where have you been, are the others alright, have you seen these elementals, have - "

"Yogg, Yogg!" it shouts, crawling over and bumping a larger tentacle against you. "Calm down. Ca-a-alm down. Everything's fine. So, let me explain..."

N'Zoth launches into a story, explaining how it was whisked away and washed up on a shore, forced to frantically battle against legions of Earth elementals. How it was forced to dip into searing heat, dizzying heights, and crushing water to avoid being ground to dust. Of devouring souls to grow stronger and larger, tapping into darker and darker powers until N'Zoth could finally bring an elemental lord to heel.

"Truly, you battled and defeated one of their lords?" you ask. "I have too," you explain. "Al'Akir, actually.'

"The others have too," N'Zoth says. "We were wondering where you vanished off to. Also explains why those elementals are acting more vicious these days."

"Small world," you joke.

"Small world," N'Zoth says with a grin in its voice. "Come on, let me introduce you to the others. They are at the north pole right now."

N'Zoth leads you north, past towering glaciers - that aren't so towering once you're the size of a mountain - and erupting supervolcanoes, all the way to the frigid pole where, as it said, C'Thun and Y'Shaarj are. Currently C'Thun is embroiled in what appears to be a magical puzzle created by Y'Shaarj, twisting and turning a complex cube made of billions of purple lines.

You spend some time catching up with the others on your journey, wrapping each other up into a giant, spikey betentacled ball. They're alive.

You're all alive, and so hideously, impossibly powerful that nothing can ever threaten your lives ever again. You can do whatever you want, whenever you want. Create fleshy servents to worship you, to sacrifice souls to you, create a weak and feeble race that can't grow strong and harvest them for souls and misery. You can have them build temples in your honor, and thrones the size of mountain ranges, all while you grow stronger and stronger, and the elementals wage bloody, endless war against each other like you were made to do against your own kind.

And that's exactly what you four do.

This entire world belongs to you four now.
