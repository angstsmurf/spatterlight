- a World of Warcraft story

by Enceladus

Watery Cave
You can go south.

Suddenly you think.

And therefore you are.

You are small, you can figure that out. After all, you can even make out the grain of your individual cells!

Your senses are primitive, as basic as you are young. You can get a general sense of direction, and light as well. It's easy for you to determine that you are in water, and the... things drifting over you are food particles. The water around you trembles, but it's hard to make out the size of your world. Could be infinite for all you know, and that thought... excites you.

Well. No time like the present! Time to explore this infinite world and see what it has to offer. Surely there's more than just neverending water in all directions.

Watery Cave
You can go south.

That thing just tried to kill you!

You'd killed it first, obviously. Thank goodness you decided to repurpose your excess material when you did. But still! You hadn't done anything to it. Was there any reason to its attack at all?!

Maybe. Maybe not.

Well, in the end it hardly mattered. The one you struck down resembled you closely, but was practically half your size. If you come across another such creature that's larger than you...

... you'd better start figuring out how to fight.

For starters though, your body is heavily damaged. Ectoplasm is leaking from your walls and one of your many tendrils hangs limply. The ocean around you is brimming with food though. Brimming with raw materials. It'd be prudent to absorb those and use the molecules to heal yourself.
