FAQ

How to put on a A* match?
Wrestling is a scripted sport (Don't tell me you didn't know that before!). To put on a good match, you need to follow the script given by your booker. Here are the few pointers:
-- If your opponent is to win, then put your ego aside and make sure that he wins!
-- Try to finish the match in the allotted time. If you finish the match within the allotted time (within 15 seconds of the end time), then you get a bonus point. Similarly, if you go way beyond the allotted time, you lose points. You should also not finish the match too early, or the fans will be disappointed.
-- Try to mix up your moves. Avoid being repetitive, because that will bore the heck out of the fans.

When the game ends?
When you reach 100 reputation, go bankrupt, or when nobody wants to hire you.

This game is annoying! The rules are impossible to follow...
Here are a few pointers to help you:
-- Try to avoid performing any move three times in a row, otherwise it will negatively affect the match rating. "Nothing" is an exception and can be performed any times you want.
-- "Pinfall" is not a move. It also doesn't cost any stamina to perform. However it comes under "3 times in a row" rule.
-- "Submission" is an offensive move. Performing it will add +1 to your "Offense Counter".
-- Be careful with performing "Offense" move twice in a row. If the opponent blocks the move the second time, you will be forced to perform it the third time. This is where "the opponent is not cooperating with me" thing comes. When you are in the ring trying to put on a good match, the miscalculations can happen. Your opponent has also has his own moves to worry about.
