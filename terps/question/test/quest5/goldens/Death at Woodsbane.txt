Death at Woodsbane

By Craig Dutton (c) 2015

News of Jerimiah Quill's murder had come as a shock. Not only was he the town of Woodsbane's Magistrate but his body was found with the head cleaved off!

The Devil had danced last night, that's for certain!

As the newly assigned Magistrate it's your job to find the culprit. His body lies locked in the courthouse cell for safe-keeping. The body was originally found in the Town Square early this morning.

Good hunting!
