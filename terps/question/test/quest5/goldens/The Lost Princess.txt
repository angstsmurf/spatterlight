You breathe in the cold night air as you look at the stars. A simple fireplace in the cabin is the only light in the dark night. It seems to beckon you back inside. But you hold your cloak tighter against the wind, remembering the first time that Father brought you here. He and Mother would come up here to trade with the roaming gypsies, with Mother's kin. Now you are the only one left to carry on the family trade, even though the people in the village think that thirteen is a bit young to be a real merchant. "Age doesn't matter," you say to yourself. You stopped being a child when you lost your family two years ago.

A crack in the west breaks your reverie. You look towards it, but see nothing in the moonlight. It might be someone lost in the cold. Or it could be a bear. You've never seen one, but the pelts you buy from the gypsies are large enough to worry you.
