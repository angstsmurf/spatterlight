You have always been looked down on and mistrusted by your village (except your loving parents), mainly because you were not born in the village. Your mother, unable to bear children, found you in the wilderness. The other reason is that when your mother found you, you were successfully using a hand-made wooden trowel to defend yourself from a badger, showing an intelligence and tenacity unnatural to a child of only a few months old. Today, you have the chance to finally become accepted by proving loyal to village traditions. You have reached the age of adulthood, and tonight there will be a celebration in your honor! That is, of course, if you survive the Temple Trials...

No one knows how old the temple is, who built it, or even what its original purpose was, but it is filled with strange symbols, dangerous traps, and ferocious creatures, or so you've heard. Supposedly, your people found it abandoned close to three centuries ago, and they've been using it for "tests of bravery" ever since. Today is your day to venture through the temple. Your parents and the village elder take you down an old path through the forest, until at last you reach the entrance. Your mother and father hug you and wish you luck before leaving you to your task. The elder remains nearby to see that you enter the temple.
