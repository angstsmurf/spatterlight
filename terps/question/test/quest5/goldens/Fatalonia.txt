Fatalonia

Welcome to the Kingdom of Fatalonia!

Here I will go over some of the basic tools you will need to employ to succeed here.

Firstly and most importantly, the main tool in your arsenal is your "Perception Magic". You can use this to find out about a variety of pertinent objects. Feel free to try it on the test object here. To use it simply type "Use Perception Magic on _ " where the underscore is the object or room you wish to potentially find out more about.

Please note that "Use Magic" and "Use Skill" also work and will save you time. (Also doesn't need to be capitalised, that's just aesthetically pleasing for me to read here).

You are also equipped with an "Attribute Stone", which will quickly show you your OWN stats, thus bypassing the need for you to type that out on yourself. It will show you your current Strength, Charisma, Dexterity and Intelligence levels. In this Concept build there are no tasks that require you to have a certain level of anything, however. As you perform tasks, you will gain EXP. For every 10 EXP you can use the Attribute Stone to convert into a Skill Point. These Skill Points can be used at any Skill trading point. The only one currently in the game is the Bookcase in Your Quarters.

You also have a "Coin Purse" which will show you your current... currency.

Finally, your "Note Pocket" is where you find any active quests. Once completed, they will be moved to your "Journal" in Your Quarters, so as to not clutter your "Note Pocket". As we are in the starting room, there are no Notes in your pocket, naturally.

Your Bag is where any regular items go (it's where you'll find the Attribute Stone).

Your Equipment is where any item actually physically equipped to you goes (like clothes and weapons). You may equip a weapon (when they are available) at any time, but your Throwing Knives are always equipped regardless and may have uses in certain situations.

FINALLY AND IMPORTANTLY. You can "lose" the game. If you break your clothes and you do not have the means to fix them, you will be transported to the nearest Tailor. If you do not have the money to fix them, you are essentially trapped. Also, if you cannot wear the clothes even if fixed because you are too fat, you will not be able to go back into public, and thus "lose". You can increase the size of your clothes, however, at the Tailors or if you pick up the Sewing Kit and get some extra material for whatever "class" of clothing you are wearing. At the moment there is only one attire, and you cannot get fat enough to break it in this build, but it is something to pay attention to.

You are also currently naked, incidentally and will need to equip the Guild Attire from your Wardrobe, firstly.

Once you are happy with the mechanics, please proceed to click "Out", to begin.
