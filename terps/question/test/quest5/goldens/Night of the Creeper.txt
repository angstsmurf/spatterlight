Night of the Creeper
By Craig Dutton (c) 2013

The village of Abbeymede, famous for its Abbey ruins, is now a ghost-village.  Strange supernatural deaths had driven away the residents and left nothing more than shadows and spectres for nearly four decades.

As an amateur Ghost Hunter you head for the village in hope of witnessing any supernatural sightings of your own.

However, this night will unravel a tale of dread and horror, and a cause for concern, as the true nature of the supernatural creature known locally as the Creeper comes to the fore.
