Fighting

by Quentin

Did you ever faced an enemy in a real combat? If your answer is NO, then read this book.

While in a fight, you allways should know your way out. You dont know anything about your opponent, maybe he will kill you with the next punch?
But in case your enemy is weaker than you, running away could be a bad decision.

-- Gameplay, you hit your enemy everytime you chose "fight". And your enemy will try to kill you.

The enemy will never be able to kill you instantly. Except your health is low.
But most enemies are tougher than you, keep that in mind.
Sometimes you just hit the weak spot of an enemy by pure luck.

You will not get informed about your enemies status. Allways remember: do NOT expect your opponent is going to show you how to kill it, just keep on fighting till the very end!

After you killed your opponent, you can chose to ignore it. That means, You will not be able to interact with it anymore.

Smithing - a how to guide for beginners

by Avolar Génor

So you want to get into blacksmithing? Of course you do, thats why you bought this book!
So how you start creating a weapon?

You need the material your new weapon is made of.

Now the fire: This is an important step! So be careful!
If the fire is too hot, you will destroy your blade as soon as you take it out of the fire! But on the other side, as You may gussed, if it is too cold, Your blade will deform like a piece of butter.

So take a few blades to make sure the temperature is perfect.
Now you lay the blade right into the fire, but not just on the coals! The full blade must be covered by the flames.
Let the blade rest in the fire until it is as hot as the forge. Do not touch the blade with your bare hands!

Now take the blade out of the forge and give it the right shape on the anvil. I guess you know how to handle a hammer.
If the metal cools down too fast, just heat it up again in the forge, then go back to the anvil.
Repeat that until your blade has the shape you wish.

Then put it into the forge for the final time, make sure the forge has the perfect temperature, because this time, it is highly important. When the blade is hot, "freeze" it in water.
That shock will make the metal even harder and more resistant to impacts.

But the blade still is not sharp, so You have to fix this. Sharpen the blade to give it its ultimate power.
 Of course, this is just a litte help for beginners, it takes years of experience to make a powerful blade. But by reading this book. You just made a first step on the long way to become the best blacksmith in the world.
