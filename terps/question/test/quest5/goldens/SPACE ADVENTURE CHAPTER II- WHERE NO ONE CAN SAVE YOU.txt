TIME PASSED...
You wake up with a nasty headache. Your vision is slurred and you feel like you are drunk. The first thing you see is Ken who appears to have difficult time staying up. Its then you noticed your hands are tied together. Your eyes manage to dial in seeing a disgusting figure!
A unfamiliar face sits before you. Presumably, he is a fat, bald man with a huge melted-like blob where his legs should be. He speaks in a rather deep and chilling voice.
"Finally, you've waken Mr. Winstein. It took me a LONG time to get you up as your friend was much more cooperative. My name is Dr....Slang Victoria Zealus. I am....WAS one of the greatest scientific minds of Velos and probably the best of the Confederation. I used to be a humble man until IT happened....You see despite my amazing inventions and theroies I was ridiculed by my fellow men, even when I helped design the Xeslovs blueprints a decade or two ago...Around that time when a prototype was finished I was taunted by the fellow scientists to test it even though I said it wasn't ready they used force on me to do it. The device exploded killing 5 crewmen and injuring 47 as well as melting my body below my waist. Despite, it was clearly their fault I WAS the one court martialed by the Confederation. They stripped me (literally per Confederation tradition) of my rank of scientist and banished me from the science community altogether. Since then I've sworn that one day I'll absolutely DESTROY the Confederation and become supreme leader of the NEBULA! My most recent plan involved paying the most dasterdly race in the nebula; Jaglins 2,500 Jacoins ($1,000,000.75 in Veloian money) to steal the finished Xeslovs device aboard the Arvaid II and use it to destroy Velos and the Confederation. It was the perfect plan....UNTIL YOU TWO NUMBSKULLS CAME AND SCREWED IT ALL UP!!! ARRRGH!!!" He then proceeds to rearrange a nearby monitor. Following this he wipes some sweat off his head with a handkerchief and regains his composure. "Sorry, about that I have a bit of a temper. Now for my new plan against the confederation is this!"
He turns behind and suddenly you see a rather huge ray gun!
"This is the BlasterX4. Once it makes contact with Velos it will cause an implosion that will suck the entire nebula in to parallel dimension 67. I'll revenge on those who wronged me!!!! HA HA HA!"
"Anyway, my plan for you morons his simple, You will be forced to work in my Gem Mines on the ferocious jungle planet of Hrkalis. A planet full of killer fauna and flora so its safe to say..ITS WHERE NO ONE CAN SAVE YOU!!! TAKE THEM AWAY!"
A small injection renders your body useless. You and Ken are dragged aboard separate ships... utter despair sets in as you blackout.
