The Order of the Swan

by Nomad

Yina woke up, not at once realising where she was. She was about to drift off back into the world of sleep when she realised that something was in fact wrong. She really didn't know where she was. She got up slowly from the creaky bed. It was completely unlike the luxurious bed she had slept in for every other day of her fifteen years.

Then she remembered. She had been alive. And then she'd died. Where was she? Why had she been sent back to this world and not to the underworld?
