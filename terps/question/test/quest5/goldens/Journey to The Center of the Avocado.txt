You are craving a consumable object that will appease your hunger. You reveal the contents of your refrigerator to commence your journey. Your eyes pounce on a dark green lump that is calling your name. You transport it to your table to prepare for said consumption, but as you attempt to open it, the avocado almost resists your efforts, and you are infuriated. Once more you attempt to open this avocado, but once more your efforts are rendered useless. What will you do?

You commence your quest to break this avocado. You search for a device that could allow you to complete this quest, and after searching your garage, you find a hacksaw. Your eyes glint and you internalize a deep hatred that you will soon manifest on a goddamn avocado. What the hell is wrong with you? What will you do?

> The Calming
Take the time to relax your anger

You realize that what you are doing is past the boundaries of human sanity, and you  begin to break down, crying hard and long. At this moment, a summary of your life begins to play. When you were little, you were a loser, and a nobody. This trend continued throughout your life. Your parents always made you feel like you were a burden, and your teachers and peers hardly ever noticed you. You were less inclined to participate, and throughout school, other people stronger than you felt your weakness, and they harassed you. Your grades were never good, and you can never keep a job. This was the moment in your life where you were going to succeed. And then you realize the pathetic nature in that. In the moment, a surge of self-hatred and self-pity overcome your actions, and somehow, you manage to kill yourself with a hacksaw. Congratulations.
