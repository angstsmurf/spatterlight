The Quest for Granny's Hat

by Sand Duce

You are Flick, a young jockey (or wench, you choose!) who is spending the summer with your grandparents at their charming cottage. One sunny morning, you were helping Granddad Oilcan to chop wood, while Granny Gumdrop visited the forest, to gather mushrooms.
But disaster struck! Granny returned...without her hat! Her favourite and most gorgeous of hats, gone! Missing! ...pilfered?

Only you can find out the truth! Put your Adventuring pants on, Flick, it's time to Find Granny's Missing Hat!
