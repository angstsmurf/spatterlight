Welcome to 'Magic Math Mine', an adventure designed for children from year 4 (age 8) upwards.

If you get stuck, please type 'help'.  Enjoy!

A small clearing
------------------------------------------------
There is a small creature here! He turns and stares at you as your torch shines upon him.

"who...are you?" you stammer in surprise.

The creature grins, and replies "Me an' my folk are known by many names: 'Dinkies', 'Knockers', 'Little Invisibles' or just 'Piskeys'. Keep away from the 'Buccas' though, mark my words!"

"Riddle me this," the Piskey continues, "if five humans have ten pasties, how many pasties do they have each?" (type the number in the box at the bottom)

Exits: north.
> 2
> 2

Correct! (Your score has gone up)
The Pisky grins at you.

"What, no pasties for the Knockers? Shame on you!" He snaps his fingers and disappears.

You continue down the tunnel. (Press a key to continue)

A mining station
------------------------------------------------
You can see a pasty.
You can go east or west.
There is a golden brown pasty here, it smells delicious! To take it, type "take pasty" or select the pasty in the objects panel, then click take.

There are lots of things you can do with objects in your inventory (backpack), try typing "look at pasty", or "eat pasty".
You can also give things to other characters, e.g. "give (thing) to (person)", throw things etc.
> take pasty
> take pasty
The pasty is still warm! You put it in your backpack (see inventory panel on right side of screen).

A tin seam
------------------------------------------------
The piskey won't look at you, he looks very unhappy. A gurgling noise is coming from his belly!
You can see a grumpy piskey.
You can go east.
> give pasty to grumpy piskey
> give pasty to grumpy piskey

"Thank you very much!" says the piskey. Would you like some of my tin? He gives you a bag full of rocks gleaming with black cassiterite (tin ore).

He clicks his fingers and you find yourself back outside the tin mine. You set off for home with a full backpack, hoping it might be pasties for dinner!
