Welcome to The Department, agent Smith.

This is your first assignment. It is, of course, pretty straightforward. If you succeed, we should have some more challenging missions for you.

A notorious spy named Kline is in town, and he's at the centre of something big. We don't know what. That's what we want to find out. We believe he has documents with him pertaining to his organisation and their plans.
