The realization that you are outside and away from home hits you as a thrum of dread strikes your chest, but you quickly recognize the memory. It's the release night of the exact game you were just playing. For the very first time since playing that series, you lined up for a midnight release. You couldn't tell if your heart was going crazy from anxiety or excitement.
Some voices cut into your thoughts and you focus in on the group in front of you. You don't recognize them; they're just other fans in line. It's unclear if they know each other, but their conversation about the game draws you in.
"Did you play the last game?"
"I did! I think it had my favorite protagonist. He really grew on me!"
"Ahh, good taste! His fighting style was so cool! I had some trouble with the controls on it. I'm still working through it."
"It took some getting used to! It's fun once you get it."
You're listening and you feel so on edge you're practically standing on your toes. You had just played the last game before coming and had a lot of thoughts on it too. This was your first time being surrounded by people who would know what you were talking about. The people are slightly facing you. They don't seem mean either.

Despite your panic, you hear yourself speak up. "The controls in that game are really different. I had to stop playing for a few months and come back to it."
The few people speaking paused and turned to look at you and for a horrible moment you were sure they were going to say something about interrupting them. But before the feeling could completely consume you, the first one lit up with a smile. "I feel that! Did you manage to get through it?"
Feeling more at ease and in your element now, you nod eagerly. "Just the other day. I had to make sure I finished before getting this game tonight."
They each nodded. "I hid from my social media for weeks so I wouldn't get spoilers," the next one said.
"Are you going to start playing tonight?" you ask. You don't remember the last time you felt this excited talking to someone about this. "I made sure to nap all day so I could stay up." You feel yourself laughing along with the others and your heart feels so full it could burst.
"Same! I even took off work for the next few days!" The others laughed at that and you laughed with them.
You settled into their circle as they let you join the conversation.
It passed the time quickly, and soon all of you were excitedly fidgeting as the line moved. You knew the first person had gotten their game when conversations were cut off by someone racing out of the store, game held above their head as they shouted a victory cry and sped to their car. The rest of the line shouted cheers along with him. A few people driving away after getting the game were blasting the opening theme song from their cars. You don't think you stopped smiling all night.
You don't even think you ever got each other's names, but you were friends in that moment all the same. Even the people you saw only for a passing moment, there was a connection between you just through your presence and mutual excitement. This series and moment had created something vital for you. It created a community. Even with those you did not actually know, even those you never even knew the name of, you were sharing important moments and feelings.
