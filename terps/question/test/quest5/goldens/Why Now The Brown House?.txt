by Anjan Chakrabarti

 On Wooden Bench.
You can go  Stone Pathway.
You sit on a wooden bench, read, and set down one newspaper after another.  Reading material is endless from where you are. You shift uneasily and wonder why you were. As the day approaches dusk, a monstrous fog emerges stealing away all the remaining light. You clutch your head to your knees and again wonder why you were.  As the morning approaches, the fog lifts and hopefully so do you.  Welcome to the adventure. Your objectives are two-fold. The first one being to explore as much of the house as you can. The second being to find the three pillars of achievment: the gold, silver, and bronze medals placed  somewhere in the house. Bring all medals to the  Center of the  Living Room.  Your first task is to find a way to get into the house.  Last but not least,  if examining is not giving you enough clues, try searching instead. GOOD LUCK!!!
